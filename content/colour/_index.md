The named colour palettes tartans are dyed against. Each palette belongs to a supplier — the Dictionary's own colour roles, the Tartan Register's standard shades, mill shade cards as they are captured — and each colour in it has a name and a reference, the thing quoted on an order.

Every palette is arranged as a wheel: an achromatic spine from white down to black, and hue families round the ring, each a ladder of the supplier's actual dyes from light to dark. The in-browser weaver navigates a palette one jog at a time — lighter or darker along a ladder (past its ends, white and black), or round the ring at the same lightness — so a tartan's colours can be varied a fraction and the effect seen at once.
