Each intensity gets its own colour wheel — light, mid and dark, banded by OKLab lightness (unlike the Register's, these codes carry no intensity of their own). Each wheel is a polar plot of OKLab's own dimensions: the angle is hue — red at the top, then clockwise through orange and brown, yellow, green, blue and purple, the wheel's jog order — and the radius is chroma, √(a²+b²) of the a and b columns below, with rings every 0.05. The near-achromatic shades gather at the centre, the saturated dyes push out. Hover a dot for its code, name and hex.
