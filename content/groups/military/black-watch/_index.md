**Type:** Military · **Part of:** [Military](/groups/military/)

The Black Watch — the 42nd, later the Royal Highland Regiment (RHR) — and the tartans recorded under its name: the Government sett it made famous, the RHR and Regimental variants, the Dress and the Piper's plaid. Grouped by name for now under the Military world; the true regimental setts can be distinguished from the fashion derivatives later.
