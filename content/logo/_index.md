---
title: "Match a tartan to your logo"
date: 2026-06-19
---

Have a logo or a set of brand colours? Drop the image below and the dictionary will read its
colours — setting the background aside — and give you two things:

- **Tartans from your colours** — a spread of setts woven straight from your palette, in your
  original colours and reduced to standard worsted shades, at 4, 8, 12 and 20 stripes. Open any
  one in the TTD to weave and tune it.
- **Closest existing tartans** — the recorded tartans nearest your logo by number of colour
  blocks, number of colours, and how close those colours sit to each tartan's palette.

It is a starting point, not a commission: a sett you could adopt as-is, or take to a weaver as
the basis for your own.
