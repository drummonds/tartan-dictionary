The most famous Lermontov was Mikhail (b1814) - a much revered poet and dissident who was killed in a duel in 1841. His standing in Russia was almost akin to that of Robert Burns. This tartan is based upon the MacDuff, drawing upon George Lermont's home county of Fife. The white lines on blue symbolise St Andrew, patron saint of both Russia and Scotland and celebrate the Lermontovs Scottish ancestry.
