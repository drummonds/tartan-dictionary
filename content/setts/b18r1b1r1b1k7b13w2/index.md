The **Tokyo Bluebells (Dance)** tartan is one sett proportion (`b18r1b1r1b1k7b13w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BRBRBKBW](/stripes/brbrbkbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1995**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Tokyo Bluebells](/tartans/tokyo-bluebells/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
