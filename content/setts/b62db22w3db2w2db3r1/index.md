The **North Tyneside Pipe Band** tartan is one sett proportion (`b62db22w3db2w2db3r1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBWBWBR](/stripes/bbwbwbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1996**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Tyneside](/tartans/tyneside/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
