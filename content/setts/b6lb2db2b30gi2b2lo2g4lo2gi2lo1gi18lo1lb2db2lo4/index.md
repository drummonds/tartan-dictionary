The **All Ireland Blue (Fashion)** tartan is one sett proportion (`b6lb2db2b30gi2b2lo2g4lo2gi2lo1gi18lo1lb2db2lo4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWBBGBYGYGYGYWBY](/stripes/bwbbgbygygygywby/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1997**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [All Ireland](/tartans/all-ireland/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
