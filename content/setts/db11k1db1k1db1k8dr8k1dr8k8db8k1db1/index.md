The **Wine Watch** tartan is one sett proportion (`db11k1db1k1db1k8dr8k1dr8k8db8k1db1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKBKBKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkbkbkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1970**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
