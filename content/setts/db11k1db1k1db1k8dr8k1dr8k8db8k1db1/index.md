This is the **Wine Watch** sett — one scale-free proportion (`db11k1db1k1db1k8dr8k1dr8k8db8k1db1`), colour order pattern
[BKBKBKBKBKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkbkbkbkb/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **~1970**.
