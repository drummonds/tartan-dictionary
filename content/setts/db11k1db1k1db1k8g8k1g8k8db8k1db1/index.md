The **Black Watch, A&S Highlanders** tartan is one sett proportion (`db11k1db1k1db1k8g8k1g8k8db8k1db1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGKGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgkgkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1725**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Black Watch Regimental](/tartans/black-watch-regimental/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
