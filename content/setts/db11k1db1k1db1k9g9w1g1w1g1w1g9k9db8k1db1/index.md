The **Baillie of Polkemmet** tartan is one sett proportion (`db11k1db1k1db1k9g9w1g1w1g1w1g9k9db8k1db1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGWGWGWGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgwgwgwgkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1937**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
