The **Harmony** tartan is one sett proportion (`db11ti3db4w3db3lo4db3dbi13t34dg3t4db3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBBWBYBBBGBB](/stripes/bbbwbybbbgbb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Harmony](/tartans/harmony/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
