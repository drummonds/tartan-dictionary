The **Stewart Old** tartan is one sett proportion (`db12k1g2k1g2k1db12r2k12r1k12r2g12k1db2k1g12`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKGKGKBRKRKRGKBKG](/stripes/bkgkgkbrkrkrgkbkg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1893**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
