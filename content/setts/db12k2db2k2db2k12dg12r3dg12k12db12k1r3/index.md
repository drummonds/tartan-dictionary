The **Murray of Atholl** tartan is one sett proportion (`db12k2db2k2db2k12dg12r3dg12k12db12k1r3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGRGKBKR](/stripes/bkbkbkgrgkbkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Murray of Atholl](/tartans/murray-of-atholl/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
