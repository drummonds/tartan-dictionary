The **MacKenzie** tartan is one sett proportion (`db12k2db2k2db2k12g12k1w2k1g12k12db12k1r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGKWKGKBKR](/stripes/bkbkbkgkwkgkbkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1778**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacKenzie](/tartans/mackenzie/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
