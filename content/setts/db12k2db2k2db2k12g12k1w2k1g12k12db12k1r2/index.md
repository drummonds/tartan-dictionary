This is the **MacKenzie** sett — one scale-free proportion (`db12k2db2k2db2k12g12k1w2k1g12k12db12k1r2`), colour order pattern
[BKBKBKGKWKGKBKR](/stripes/bkbkbkgkwkgkbkr/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **1778**.
