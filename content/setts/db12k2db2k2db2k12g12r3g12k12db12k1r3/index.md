This is the **Murray of Atholl** sett — one scale-free proportion (`db12k2db2k2db2k12g12r3g12k12db12k1r3`), colour order pattern
[BKBKBKGRGKBKR](/stripes/bkbkbkgrgkbkr/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
