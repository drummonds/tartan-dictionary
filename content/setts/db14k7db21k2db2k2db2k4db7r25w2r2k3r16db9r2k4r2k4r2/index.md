The **Club World** tartan is one sett proportion (`db14k7db21k2db2k2db2k4db7r25w2r2k3r16db9r2k4r2k4r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKBKBRWRKRBRKRKR](/stripes/bkbkbkbkbrwrkrbrkrkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1996**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Club World](/tartans/club-world/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
