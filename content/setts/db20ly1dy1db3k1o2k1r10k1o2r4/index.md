The **Blais (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`db20ly1dy1db3k1o2k1r10k1o2r4`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYGBKRKRKRR](/stripes/bygbkrkrkrr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1997**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Blais](/tartans/blais/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
