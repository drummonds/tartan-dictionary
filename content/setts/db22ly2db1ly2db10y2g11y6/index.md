The **Katsushika** tartan is one sett proportion (`db22ly2db1ly2db10y2g11y6`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYBYBGGG](/stripes/bybybggg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1995**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Katsushika](/tartans/katsushika/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
