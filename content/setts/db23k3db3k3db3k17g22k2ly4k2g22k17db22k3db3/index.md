The **Gordon 2** tartan is one sett proportion (`db23k3db3k3db3k17g22k2ly4k2g22k17db22k3db3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGKYKGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgkykgkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1793**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
