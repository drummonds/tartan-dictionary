The **MacLaren** tartan is one sett proportion (`db24k8dg8r2dg8k1ly2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKGRGKY](/stripes/bkgrgky/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacLaren](/tartans/maclaren/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
