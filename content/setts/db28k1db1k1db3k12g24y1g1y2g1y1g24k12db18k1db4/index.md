This is the **Gordon VS** sett — one scale-free proportion (`db28k1db1k1db3k12g24y1g1y2g1y1g24k12db18k1db4`), colour order pattern
[BKBKBKGGGGGGGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgggggggkbkb/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **1842**.
