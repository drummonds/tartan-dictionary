The **Lorne** tartan is one sett proportion (`db2k1g8k1g1k1g1k8db1k1db1k1db8k1g2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKGKGKGKBKBKBKG](/stripes/bkgkgkgkbkbkbkg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1871**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
