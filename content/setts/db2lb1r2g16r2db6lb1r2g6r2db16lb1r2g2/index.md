The **Glenorchy** tartan is one sett proportion (`db2lb1r2g16r2db6lb1r2g6r2db16lb1r2g2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWRGRBWRGRBWRG](/stripes/bwrgrbwrgrbwrg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1870**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Glen Orchy](/tartans/glen-orchy/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
