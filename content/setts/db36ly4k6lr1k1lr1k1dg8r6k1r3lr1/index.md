The **MacBeth** tartan is one sett proportion (`db36ly4k6lr1k1lr1k1dg8r6k1r3lr1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYKYKYKGRKRY](/stripes/bykykykgrkry/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacBeth](/tartans/macbeth/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
