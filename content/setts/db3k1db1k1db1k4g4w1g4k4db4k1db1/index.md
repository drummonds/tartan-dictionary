The **Lamont** tartan is one sett proportion (`db3k1db1k1db1k4g4w1g4k4db4k1db1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGWGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgwgkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Lamont](/tartans/lamont/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
