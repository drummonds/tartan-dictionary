The **MacMillan Hunting** tartan is one sett proportion (`db3ly1db12k4ly2k4dg8r2dg8r1`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYBKYKGRGR](/stripes/bybkykgrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1890**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacMillan Hunting](/tartans/macmillan-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
