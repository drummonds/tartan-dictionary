The **Carnegie** tartan is one sett proportion (`db3r1db1r2db6r1k6g6r2g1r1g2y1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BRBRBRKGRGRGG](/stripes/brbrbrkgrgrgg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1819**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
