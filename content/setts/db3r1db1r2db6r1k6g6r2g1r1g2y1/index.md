This is the **Carnegie** sett — one scale-free proportion (`db3r1db1r2db6r1k6g6r2g1r1g2y1`), colour order pattern
[BRBRBRKGRGRGG](/stripes/brbrbrkgrgrgg/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **~1715**.
