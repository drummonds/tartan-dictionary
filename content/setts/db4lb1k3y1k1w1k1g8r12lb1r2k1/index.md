The **MacLean of Duart 5** tartan is one sett proportion (`db4lb1k3y1k1w1k1g8r12lb1r2k1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWKGKWKGRWRK](/stripes/bwkgkwkgrwrk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1815**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacLean](/tartans/maclean/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
