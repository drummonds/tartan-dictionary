The **Roberts (Welsh Name)** tartan is one sett proportion (`db4lb20k3lb2k3lb20db24k3db2k3db24r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWKWKWBKBKBR](/stripes/bwkwkwbkbkbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
