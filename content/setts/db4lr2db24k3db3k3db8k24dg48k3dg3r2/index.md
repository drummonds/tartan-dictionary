The **Urquhart** tartan is one sett proportion (`db4lr2db24k3db3k3db8k24dg48k3dg3r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYBKBKBKGKGR](/stripes/bybkbkbkgkgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Urquhart](/tartans/urquhart/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
