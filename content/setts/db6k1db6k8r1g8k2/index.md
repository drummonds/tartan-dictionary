The **Fletcher** tartan is one sett proportion (`db6k1db6k8r1g8k2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKRGK](/stripes/bkbkrgk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
