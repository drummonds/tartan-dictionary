The **Scottish Rugby Union** tartan is one sett proportion (`db6k2db24k10g2m2g2m2g10k2lb3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKGRGRGKW](/stripes/bkbkgrgrgkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1991**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Scottish Rugby Union](/tartans/scottish-rugby-union/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
