The **Forbes** tartan is one sett proportion (`db8k1db2k1db2k6dg8k1lr2k1dg8k6db8k1db2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGKYKGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgkykgkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Forbes](/tartans/forbes-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
