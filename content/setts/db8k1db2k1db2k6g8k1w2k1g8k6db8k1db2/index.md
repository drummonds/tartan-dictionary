The **Forbes** tartan is one sett proportion (`db8k1db2k1db2k6g8k1w2k1g8k6db8k1db2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGKWKGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgkwkgkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1819**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Forbes](/tartans/forbes/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
