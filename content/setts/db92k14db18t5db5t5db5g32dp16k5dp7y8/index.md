The **Bavidge (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`db92k14db18t5db5t5db5g32dp16k5dp7y8`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBBBBBGBKBG](/stripes/bkbbbbbgbkbg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1998**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Bavidge](/tartans/bavidge/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
