The **Abercrombie** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg14lr1dg7k7db2k2db2k2db7`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGYG](/stripes/bkbkbkgyg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Abercrombie](/tartans/abercrombie/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
