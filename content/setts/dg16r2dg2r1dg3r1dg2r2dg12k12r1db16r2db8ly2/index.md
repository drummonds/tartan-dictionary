The **Cochrane** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg16r2dg2r1dg3r1dg2r2dg12k12r1db16r2db8ly2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGRGRGRGKRBRBY](/stripes/grgrgrgrgkrbrby/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Cochrane](/tartans/cochrane-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
