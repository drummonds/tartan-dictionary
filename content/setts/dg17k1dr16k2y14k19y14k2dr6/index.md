The **Borthwick** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg17k1dr16k2y14k19y14k2dr6`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKGKGKBKG](/stripes/bkgkgkbkg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Borthwick](/tartans/borthwick/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
