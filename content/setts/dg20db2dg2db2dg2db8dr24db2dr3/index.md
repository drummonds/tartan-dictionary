The **Lindsay** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg20db2dg2db2dg2db8dr24db2dr3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBBBGBGBG](/stripes/bbbbgbgbg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Lindsay](/tartans/lindsay/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
