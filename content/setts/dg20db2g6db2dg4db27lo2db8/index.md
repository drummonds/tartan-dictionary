The **Kinross** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg20db2g6db2dg4db27lo2db8`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BGBGBYBYBGBGBG](/stripes/bgbgbybybgbgbg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
