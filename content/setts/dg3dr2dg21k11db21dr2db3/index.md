The **MacThomas** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg3dr2dg21k11db21dr2db3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBBKGBG](/stripes/bbbkgbg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacThomas](/tartans/macthomas/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
