The **Celtic 2005 Sports Tartan** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg6dgi3dg22k2dg4k14dgi5k3ly5k3dgi20k4lo4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGGKGKGKYKGKY](/stripes/gggkgkgkykgky/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2005**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Celtic Football Club](/tartans/celtic-football-club/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
