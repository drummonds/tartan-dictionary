The **Dunblane** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg6y5w1dg2w1dg5w1dg2w1r15dp2w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGWGWGWGWRBW](/stripes/ggwgwgwgwrbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1822**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Dunblane](/tartans/dunblane/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
