The **Cameron of Erracht** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg8r1dg1r3dg16k16r1db16r3db8ly2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGRGKRBRBY](/stripes/grgrgkrbrby/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Cameron of Erracht](/tartans/cameron-of-erracht/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
