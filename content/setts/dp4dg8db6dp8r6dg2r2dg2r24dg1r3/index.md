The **MacDougall** tartan is one sett proportion (`dp4dg8db6dp8r6dg2r2dg2r24dg1r3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BGBBRGRGRGR](/stripes/bgbbrgrgrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacDougall VS](/tartans/macdougall-vs/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
