The **Smith** tartan is one sett proportion (`dr2k1g7k6db7t2db7k6g7k1lo2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKGKBBBKGKY](/stripes/bkgkbbbkgky/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1886**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Smith](/tartans/smith/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
