The **Desang** tartan is one sett proportion (`dr4wi2w8dg2w8k3wi2db4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWKWGWWB](/stripes/bwkwgwwb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
