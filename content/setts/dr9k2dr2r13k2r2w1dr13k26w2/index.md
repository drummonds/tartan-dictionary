The **Pride of Wales (Fashion)** tartan is one sett proportion (`dr9k2dr2r13k2r2w1dr13k26w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBRKRWBKW](/stripes/bkbrkrwbkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2008**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
