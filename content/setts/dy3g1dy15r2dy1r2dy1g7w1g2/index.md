This is the **Seton Hunting** sett — one scale-free proportion (`dy3g1dy15r2dy1r2dy1g7w1g2`), colour order pattern
[GGGRGRGGWG](/stripes/gggrgrggwg/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **~1930**.

Part of the [Seton Hunting](/tartans/s/se/seton-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its kin.
