The **Seton Hunting** tartan is one sett proportion (`dy3g1dy15r2dy1r2dy1g7w1g2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGGRGRGGWG](/stripes/gggrgrggwg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1930**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Seton Hunting](/tartans/seton-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
