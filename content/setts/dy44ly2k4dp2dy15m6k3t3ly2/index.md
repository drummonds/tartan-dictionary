The **Inches of Perth** tartan is one sett proportion (`dy44ly2k4dp2dy15m6k3t3ly2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GYKBGRKBY](/stripes/gykbgrkby/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1920**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Inches of Perth](/tartans/inches-of-perth/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
