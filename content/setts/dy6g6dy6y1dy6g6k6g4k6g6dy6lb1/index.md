This is the **Buchanan Hunting Clan Tartan** sett — one scale-free proportion (`dy6g6dy6y1dy6g6k6g4k6g6dy6lb1`), colour order pattern
[GGGGGGKGKGGW](/stripes/ggggggkgkggw/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **1850**.

Part of the [Buchanan Hunting](/tartans/b/bu/buchanan-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its kin.
