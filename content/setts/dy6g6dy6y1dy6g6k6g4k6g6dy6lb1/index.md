The **Buchanan Hunting Clan Tartan** tartan is one sett proportion (`dy6g6dy6y1dy6g6k6g4k6g6dy6lb1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGGGGGKGKGGW](/stripes/ggggggkgkggw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1850**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Buchanan Hunting](/tartans/buchanan-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
