The **Golden Broom** tartan is one sett proportion (`g12y3g6dr19k1r8k2lb4k2g19k1dr19k1r9`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGGBKRKWKGKBKR](/stripes/gggbkrkwkgkbkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2005**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Golden Broom](/tartans/golden-broom/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
