The **Cochrane** tartan is one sett proportion (`g16r2g2r1g3r1g2r2g12k12r1db16r2db8y2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBRBRKGRGRGRGRG](/stripes/gbrbrkgrgrgrgrg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Cochrane](/tartans/cochrane/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
