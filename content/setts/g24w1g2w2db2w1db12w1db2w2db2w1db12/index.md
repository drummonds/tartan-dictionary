The **MacDonald Lord of the Isles Hunting** tartan is one sett proportion (`g24w1g2w2db2w1db12w1db2w2db2w1db12`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWBWBWBWBWGWG](/stripes/bwbwbwbwbwgwg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacDonald, Lord of the Isles Hunting](/tartans/macdonald-lord-of-the-isles-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
