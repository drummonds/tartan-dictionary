The **Cumnock** tartan is one sett proportion (`g3db16k3dp2k45r1k2lo3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBKBKRKY](/stripes/gbkbkrky/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2011**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Cumnock](/tartans/cumnock/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
