The **Maitland** tartan is one sett proportion (`g3db8g3k4g9y2db2y2r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBGKGGBGR](/stripes/gbgkggbgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Maitland](/tartans/maitland/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
