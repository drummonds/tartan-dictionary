The **MacRae** tartan is one sett proportion (`g4r1g4r4db1r1db1r1db1r4db1r1db1r1db1r4w1r1db4r1db4r1w1r4g1r1g1r4g4r1g4`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGRBRBRBRBRBRBRWRBRBRWRGRGRGRG](/stripes/grgrbrbrbrbrbrbrwrbrbrwrgrgrgrg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1850**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacRae](/tartans/macrae-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
