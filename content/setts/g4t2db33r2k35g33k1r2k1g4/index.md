The **Lochaber** tartan is one sett proportion (`g4t2db33r2k35g33k1r2k1g4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBBRKGKRKG](/stripes/gbbrkgkrkg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1797**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Lochaber](/tartans/lochaber/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
