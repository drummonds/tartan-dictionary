The **Northern College (Ontario)** tartan is one sett proportion (`g5db3lb3g5w4dy2lb1dy2db1r1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBWGWGWGBR](/stripes/gbwgwgwgbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1983**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Northern College](/tartans/northern-college/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
