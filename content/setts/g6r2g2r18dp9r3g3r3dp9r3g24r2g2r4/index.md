The **Stewart of Killiecrankie** tartan is one sett proportion (`g6r2g2r18dp9r3g3r3dp9r3g24r2g2r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGRBRGRBRGRGR](/stripes/grgrbrgrbrgrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1930**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Stewart of Urrard](/tartans/stewart-of-urrard/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
