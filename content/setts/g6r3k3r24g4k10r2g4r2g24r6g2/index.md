The **MacNeish** tartan is one sett proportion (`g6r3k3r24g4k10r2g4r2g24r6g2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGRGRKGRKRG](/stripes/grgrgrkgrkrg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
