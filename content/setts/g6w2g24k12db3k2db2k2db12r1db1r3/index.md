The **Sutherland** tartan is one sett proportion (`g6w2g24k12db3k2db2k2db12r1db1r3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWGKBKBKBRBR](/stripes/gwgkbkbkbrbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1829**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Sutherland](/tartans/sutherland/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
