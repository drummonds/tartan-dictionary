This is the **Cameron of Erracht** sett — one scale-free proportion (`g8r1g1r3g16k16r1db16r3db8y2`), colour order pattern
[GBRBRKGRGRG](/stripes/gbrbrkgrgrg/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **1793**.
