The **Hebridean, South Uist** tartan is one sett proportion (`g8r1k1r8g1r8k1r1k8r1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRKRGRKRKR](/stripes/grkrgrkrkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Hebridean South Uist](/tartans/hebridean-south-uist/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
