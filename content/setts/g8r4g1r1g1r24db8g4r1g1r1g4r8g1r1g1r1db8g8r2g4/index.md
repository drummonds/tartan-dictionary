The **Matheson** tartan is one sett proportion (`g8r4g1r1g1r24db8g4r1g1r1g4r8g1r1g1r1db8g8r2g4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGBRGRGRGRGRGBRGRGRG](/stripes/grgbrgrgrgrgrgbrgrgrg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Matheson](/tartans/matheson/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
