The **Allon/Allan** tartan is one sett proportion (`g8w1g1r1g4k4db8k1db1k1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWGRGKBKBK](/stripes/gwgrgkbkbk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1996**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Allen](/tartans/allen/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
