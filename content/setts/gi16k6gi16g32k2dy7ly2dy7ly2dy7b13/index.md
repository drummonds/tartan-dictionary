The **Wagga Wagga** tartan is one sett proportion (`gi16k6gi16g32k2dy7ly2dy7ly2dy7b13`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BGYGYGKGGKG](/stripes/bgygygkggkg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
