The **Ogilvie (B&W) (Fashion?)** tartan is one sett proportion (`k12w2k12w2k6w2k7w6k2w6k2w2k6w2k6w2k2w12k2w12`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKW](/stripes/kwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Ogilvie](/tartans/ogilvie/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
