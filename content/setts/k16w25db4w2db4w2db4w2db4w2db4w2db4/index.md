The **Hanna** tartan is one sett proportion (`k16w25db4w2db4w2db4w2db4w2db4w2db4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWBWBWBWBWBWK](/stripes/bwbwbwbwbwbwk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1900**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Hanna](/tartans/hanna/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
