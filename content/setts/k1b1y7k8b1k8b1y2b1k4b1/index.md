The **Priest** tartan is one sett proportion (`k1b1y7k8b1k8b1y2b1k4b1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBGBKBKGBK](/stripes/bkbgbkbkgbk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Priest](/tartans/priest/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
