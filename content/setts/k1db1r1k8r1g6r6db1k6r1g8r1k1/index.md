The **Cumming** tartan is one sett proportion (`k1db1r1k8r1g6r6db1k6r1g8r1k1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KBRKRGRBKRGRK](/stripes/kbrkrgrbkrgrk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
