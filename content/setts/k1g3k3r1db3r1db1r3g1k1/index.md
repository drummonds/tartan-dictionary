The **MacInroy** tartan is one sett proportion (`k1g3k3r1db3r1db1r3g1k1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KGKRBRBRGK](/stripes/kgkrbrbrgk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1856**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacInroy](/tartans/macinroy/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
