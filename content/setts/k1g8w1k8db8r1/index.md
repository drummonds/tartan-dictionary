The **Syme** tartan is one sett proportion (`k1g8w1k8db8r1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KGWKBR](/stripes/kgwkbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1810**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Leslie Hunting](/tartans/leslie-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
