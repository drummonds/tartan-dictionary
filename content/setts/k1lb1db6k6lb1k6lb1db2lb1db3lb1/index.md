The **Clergy (Smith)** tartan is one sett proportion (`k1lb1db6k6lb1k6lb1db2lb1db3lb1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWBKWKWBWBW](/stripes/kwbkwkwbwbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1850**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Clergy](/tartans/clergy-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
