The **Border Bell** tartan is one sett proportion (`k1r1w1k1w1k1db1`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKWKWRK](/stripes/bkwkwrk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1986**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
