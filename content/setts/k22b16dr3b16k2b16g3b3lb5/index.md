The **Jethart** tartan is one sett proportion (`k22b16dr3b16k2b16g3b3lb5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BGBKBBBKBBBKBGBW](/stripes/bgbkbbbkbbbkbgbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1996**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Jethart](/tartans/jethart/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
