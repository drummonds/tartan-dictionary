The **Douglas** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2b2dg8db8lr1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KBGBY](/stripes/kbgby/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Douglas](/tartans/douglas/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
