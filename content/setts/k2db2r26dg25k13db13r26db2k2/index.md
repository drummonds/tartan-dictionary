same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KBRBKGRBK](/stripes/kbrbkgrbk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1831**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacNaughton](/tartans/macnaughton-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
