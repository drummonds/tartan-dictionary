The **MacKirdy** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2g12k11t12w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KGKBW](/stripes/kgkbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1950**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacKirdy](/tartans/mackirdy/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
