The **Kennedy** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2g2y1g3r1g2r1g12db4k3db3k3db3k3db4g24ri2`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KGGGRGRGBKBKBKBGR](/stripes/kgggrgrgbkbkbkbgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Kennedy](/tartans/kennedy-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
