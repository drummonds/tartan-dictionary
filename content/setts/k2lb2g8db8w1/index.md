The **Douglas** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2lb2g8db8w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWGBW](/stripes/kwgbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1819**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Douglas](/tartans/douglas-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
