The **MacAulay** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2r16g6r3g8w1`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KRGRGW](/stripes/krgrgw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1880**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacAulay](/tartans/macaulay/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
