The **Gipsy** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2r2k8r2w1r2db8r2k2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KRBRWRKRK](/stripes/krbrwrkrk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1840**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Gipsy](/tartans/gipsy/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
