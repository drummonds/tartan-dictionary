The **Scott** tartan is one sett proportion (`k31w2k4w1k1w1k1w1k1w1k3w3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWKWKWKWKWKW](/stripes/kwkwkwkwkwkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Scott](/tartans/scott/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
