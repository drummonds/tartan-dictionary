The **MacArthur** tartan is one sett proportion (`k32g6k12g30y3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGKGK](/stripes/ggkgk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacArthur](/tartans/macarthur-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
