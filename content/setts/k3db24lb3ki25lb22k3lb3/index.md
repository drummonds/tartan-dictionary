The **Strathclyde blue** tartan is one sett proportion (`k3db24lb3ki25lb22k3lb3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KBWKWKW](/stripes/kbwkwkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1993**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Strathclyde](/tartans/strathclyde/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
