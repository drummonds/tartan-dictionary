The **Angus** tartan is one sett proportion (`k3dr1k14db14dr1db1dr1db1dr2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBBBBBKBK](/stripes/bbbbbbkbk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1880**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Angus](/tartans/angus/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
