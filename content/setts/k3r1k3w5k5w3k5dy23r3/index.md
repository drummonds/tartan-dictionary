The **Southdown** tartan is one sett proportion (`k3r1k3w5k5w3k5dy23r3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KRKWKWKGR](/stripes/krkwkwkgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1985**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Southdown](/tartans/southdown/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
