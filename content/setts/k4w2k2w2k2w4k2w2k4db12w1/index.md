The **Napier** tartan is one sett proportion (`k4w2k2w2k2w4k2w2k4db12w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWKWKWKWKBW](/stripes/kwkwkwkwkbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1880**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Napier](/tartans/napier/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
