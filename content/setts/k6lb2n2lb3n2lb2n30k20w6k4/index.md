The **Nunavut** tartan is one sett proportion (`k6lb2n2lb3n2lb2n30k20w6k4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWBWBWBKWK](/stripes/kwbwbwbkwk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2008**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
