The **Scott (Green)** tartan is one sett proportion (`k8g3w3g3r3g12k8g6db12ly4k6w3k3g8`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GKWKYBGKGRGWGK](/stripes/gkwkybgkgrgwgk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Scott](/tartans/scott/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
