The **O'Keefe** tartan is one sett proportion (`k8r2k3ly2k2w3k2lo12y22r2y2k1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KGRGYKWKYKRK](/stripes/kgrgykwkykrk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1978**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [O'Keefe](/tartans/o-keefe/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
