This is the **Universal Ancient** sett — one scale-free proportion (`lb12dg2lb2dg2lb2dy8g8dy1`), colour order pattern
[GGGWGWGW](/stripes/gggwgwgw/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **~2016**.

Part of the [Universal](/tartans/u/un/universal-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its kin.
