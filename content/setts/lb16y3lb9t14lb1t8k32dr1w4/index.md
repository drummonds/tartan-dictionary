The **Wrens (WRNS)** tartan is one sett proportion (`lb16y3lb9t14lb1t8k32dr1w4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWBWBKBWBKBWBWGW](/stripes/gwbwbkbwbkbwbwgw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1995**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
