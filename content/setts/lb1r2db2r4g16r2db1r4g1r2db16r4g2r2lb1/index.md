The **MacIntyre** tartan is one sett proportion (`lb1r2db2r4g16r2db1r4g1r2db16r4g2r2lb1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WRBRGRBRGRBRGRW](/stripes/wrbrgrbrgrbrgrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacIntyre](/tartans/macintyre/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
