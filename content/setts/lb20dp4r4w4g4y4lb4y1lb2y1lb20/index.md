The **Yukon** tartan is one sett proportion (`lb20dp4r4w4g4y4lb4y1lb2y1lb20`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WBRWGGWGWGW](/stripes/wbrwggwgwgw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1965**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Yukon](/tartans/yukon/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
