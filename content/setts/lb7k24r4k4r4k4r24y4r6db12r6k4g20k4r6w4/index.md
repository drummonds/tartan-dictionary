The **Innes** tartan is one sett proportion (`lb7k24r4k4r4k4r24y4r6db12r6k4g20k4r6w4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WKRKRKRGRBRKGKRW](/stripes/wkrkrkrgrbrkgkrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1938**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Innes](/tartans/innes/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
