The **O'Brien** tartan is one sett proportion (`lo13g6y2g3y2g6lb3g2lb3g12r3g6`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGWGWGGGGGY](/stripes/grgwgwgggggy/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1995**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
