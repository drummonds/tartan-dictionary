The **North West Territories** tartan is one sett proportion (`lo4g2lo2g2lo2g24k2r16w9db3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWRKGYGYGY](/stripes/bwrkgygygy/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1968**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [North West Territories](/tartans/north-west-territories/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
