The **MacPherson Dress** tartan is one sett proportion (`lr3r1lr30k20lr3k9ly1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[YKYKYRY](/stripes/ykykyry/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacPherson Dress](/tartans/macpherson-dress/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
