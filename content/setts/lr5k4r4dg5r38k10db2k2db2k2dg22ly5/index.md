The **Boyd** tartan is one sett proportion (`lr5k4r4dg5r38k10db2k2db2k2dg22ly5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[YGKBKBKRGRKY](/stripes/ygkbkbkrgrky/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Boyd](/tartans/boyd/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
