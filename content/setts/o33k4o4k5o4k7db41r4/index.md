The **Kinnaird** tartan is one sett proportion (`o33k4o4k5o4k7db41r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KRKRKBRBKRKRKR](/stripes/krkrkbrbkrkrkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1996**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Kinnaird](/tartans/kinnaird/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
