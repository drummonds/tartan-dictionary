The **Scottish National Hunting** tartan is one sett proportion (`o66m3g3m3g16dr8g3dr3mi4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RBGBGRGRR](/stripes/rbgbgrgrr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1996**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Scottish National](/tartans/scottish-national/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
