The **Grant of Monymusk** tartan is one sett proportion (`r12g14r4g14r4g14r4db8r5db8r12g3r12`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RGRBRBRGRGRGR](/stripes/rgrbrbrgrgrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Grant of Monymusk](/tartans/grant-of-monymusk/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
