The **Stuart/Stewart of Bute** tartan is one sett proportion (`r12g6k1g2k1g1k6r24w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RGKGKGKRW](/stripes/rgkgkgkrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1840**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Stuart of Bute](/tartans/stuart-of-bute/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
