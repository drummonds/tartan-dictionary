The **KPMG** tartan is one sett proportion (`r12lo2db3k3db30k20dr6k10lo2dr4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYKBKBKBYR](/stripes/bykbkbkbyr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1996**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
