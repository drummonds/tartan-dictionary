The **Sabrettes** tartan is one sett proportion (`r15k5w2o7k4w8k2r26k5o2w2k7w2r5w2g2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWRWKWRKRKWKRWKR](/stripes/gwrwkwrkrkwkrwkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2001**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Sabrettes](/tartans/sabrettes/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
