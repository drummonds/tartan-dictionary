The **MacLachlan** tartan is one sett proportion (`r16k2r2k2r2k16db16dg3db16k16r16k2r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RKRKBGBKRKRKR](/stripes/rkrkbgbkrkrkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacLachlan](/tartans/maclachlan/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
