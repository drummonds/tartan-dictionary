This is the **MacLachlan** sett — one scale-free proportion (`r16k2r2k2r2k16db16g3db16k16r16k2r2`), colour order pattern
[RKRKBGBKRKRKR](/stripes/rkrkbgbkrkrkr/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **1831**.

Part of the [MacLachlan](/tartans/m/ma/maclachlan/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its kin.
