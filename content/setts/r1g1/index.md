The **Moncreiffe** tartan is one sett proportion (`r1g1`) recorded at 4 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GR](/stripes/gr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1819**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Moncreiffe](/tartans/moncreiffe/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
