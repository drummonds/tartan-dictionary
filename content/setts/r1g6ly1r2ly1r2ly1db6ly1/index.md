The **Stevenson** tartan is one sett proportion (`r1g6ly1r2ly1r2ly1db6ly1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RGYRYRYBY](/stripes/rgyryryby/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1980**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Stevenson](/tartans/stevenson/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
