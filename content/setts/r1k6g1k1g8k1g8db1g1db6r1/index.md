The **Davidson** tartan is one sett proportion (`r1k6g1k1g8k1g8db1g1db6r1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RBGBGKGKGKR](/stripes/rbgbgkgkgkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1842**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Davidson](/tartans/davidson/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
