The **MacQuarrie** tartan is one sett proportion (`r1lb1r6db3r1g6r1g6r6db1r1lb1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RWRBRGRGRBRW](/stripes/rwrbrgrgrbrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacQuarrie SM](/tartans/macquarrie-sm/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
