This is the **Farquharson** sett — one scale-free proportion (`r2db4k1db1k1db1k8g8y2g8k8db8k1r2`), colour order pattern
[RBKBKBKGGGKBKR](/stripes/rbkbkbkgggkbkr/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **1774**.
