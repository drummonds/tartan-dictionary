The **Plowman (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`r2dp17r2dg3r2dp2r20dg1ly1r1dg2r2dp18r2dg2r24dg3w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RBRGRBRGYRGRBRGRGW](/stripes/rbrgrbrgyrgrbrgrgw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1730**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Plowman](/tartans/plowman/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
