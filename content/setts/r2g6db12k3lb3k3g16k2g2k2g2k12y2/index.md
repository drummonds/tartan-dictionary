The **MacInnes** tartan is one sett proportion (`r2g6db12k3lb3k3g16k2g2k2g2k12y2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GKGKGKGKWKBGR](/stripes/gkgkgkgkwkbgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1908**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacInnes](/tartans/macinnes-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
