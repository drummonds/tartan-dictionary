The **Argyll** tartan is one sett proportion (`r2k1db8k8g8k1t2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKGKBKR](/stripes/bkgkbkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1798**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
