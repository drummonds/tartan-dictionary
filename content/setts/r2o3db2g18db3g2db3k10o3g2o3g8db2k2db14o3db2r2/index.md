The **Cooper (Clan)** tartan is one sett proportion (`r2o3db2g18db3g2db3k10o3g2o3g8db2k2db14o3db2r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RBRBKBGRGRKBGBGBRR](/stripes/rbrbkbgrgrkbgbgbrr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1950**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
