The **Mordente (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`r2w1g1r16g7r1w1g1w1r1g7w2r1g2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRWGRWGWRGRGWR](/stripes/grwgrwgwrgrgwr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1966**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Mordente](/tartans/mordente/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
