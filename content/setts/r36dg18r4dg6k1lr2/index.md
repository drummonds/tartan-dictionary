The **MacGregor** tartan is one sett proportion (`r36dg18r4dg6k1lr2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RGRGKY](/stripes/rgrgky/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacGregor](/tartans/macgregor/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
