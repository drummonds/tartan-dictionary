The **Park** tartan is one sett proportion (`r3db16k16g4k2g2k2g34r4g3r2g3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGRGKGKGKBR](/stripes/grgrgkgkgkbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1996**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Park](/tartans/park/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
