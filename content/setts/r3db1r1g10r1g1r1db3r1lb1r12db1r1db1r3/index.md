The **Grant D** tartan is one sett proportion (`r3db1r1g10r1g1r1db3r1lb1r12db1r1db1r3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RBRBRWRBRGRGRBR](/stripes/rbrbrwrbrgrgrbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Grant D](/tartans/grant-d/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
