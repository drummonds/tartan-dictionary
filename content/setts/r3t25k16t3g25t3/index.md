The **Flower of Scotland** tartan is one sett proportion (`r3t25k16t3g25t3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BGBKBR](/stripes/bgbkbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1990**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Flower of Scotland](/tartans/flower-of-scotland/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
