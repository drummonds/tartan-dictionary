This is the **Gow** sett — one scale-free proportion (`r4dg4r1db4r4`), colour order pattern
[GRBRBRGR](/stripes/grbrbrgr/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **~1815**.

Part of the [Gow](/tartans/g/go/gow/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its kin.
