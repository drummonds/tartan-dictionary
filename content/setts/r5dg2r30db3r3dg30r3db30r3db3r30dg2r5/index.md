The **Robertson D** tartan is one sett proportion (`r5dg2r30db3r3dg30r3db30r3db3r30dg2r5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RGRBRBRGRBRGR](/stripes/rgrbrbrgrbrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Robertson D](/tartans/robertson-d/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
