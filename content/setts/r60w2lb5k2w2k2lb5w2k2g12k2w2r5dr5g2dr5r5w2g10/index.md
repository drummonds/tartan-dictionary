The **MacBain** tartan is one sett proportion (`r60w2lb5k2w2k2lb5w2k2g12k2w2r5dr5g2dr5r5w2g10`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWRBGBRWKGKWWKWKWWR](/stripes/gwrbgbrwkgkwwkwkwwr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacBain](/tartans/macbain/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
