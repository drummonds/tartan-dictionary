The **Ellis Island (District)** tartan is one sett proportion (`r68k1g6lb4g1lb12w1g6dy1g24dy1g2dy3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGGGGGWWGWGKR](/stripes/ggggggwwgwgkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2010**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Ellis Island](/tartans/ellis-island/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
