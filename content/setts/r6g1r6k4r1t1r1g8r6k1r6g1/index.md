The **Nicolson (McIan)** tartan is one sett proportion (`r6g1r6k4r1t1r1g8r6k1r6g1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRKRGRBRKRGR](/stripes/grkrgrbrkrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1845**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Nicolson](/tartans/nicolson/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
