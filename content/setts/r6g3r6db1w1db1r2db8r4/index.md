The **Cameron of Lochiel** tartan is one sett proportion (`r6g3r6db1w1db1r2db8r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RBRBWBRGR](/stripes/rbrbwbrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1820**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Cameron of Locheil](/tartans/cameron-of-locheil/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
