The **Hay** tartan is one sett proportion (`r6g4y2g36r2g2r2g12r48g4r2k1r2w6`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RGGGRGRGRGRKRW](/stripes/rgggrgrgrgrkrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Hay](/tartans/hay/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
