The **Chisholm** tartan is one sett proportion (`r6w1r24db6g2db1g2db1g12r1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RGBGBGBRWR](/stripes/rgbgbgbrwr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1842**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Chisholm D](/tartans/chisholm-d/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
