The **MacDuff** tartan is one sett proportion (`r8db3k4g6r4k1r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RBKGRKR](/stripes/rbkgrkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacDuff](/tartans/macduff/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
