The **Welsh National** tartan is one sett proportion (`r8g3r4g44w4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGWGRGR](/stripes/grgwgrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1967**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Welsh National](/tartans/welsh-national/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
