The **MacBean** tartan is one sett proportion (`ri24w1db2lb1w1lb1db2w1k1g6k1w1ri2r2g1r2ri2w1g3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWRRGRRWKGKWBWWWBWR](/stripes/gwrrgrrwkgkwbwwwbwr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
