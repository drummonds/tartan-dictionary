The **Dundee, dress** tartan is one sett proportion (`ri36r2k16r2w19y4w2k2w2y4w12lb2db10g10`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBWWGWKWGWRKRR](/stripes/gbwwgwkwgwrkrr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Dundee Dress](/tartans/dundee-dress/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
