The **MacBain/MacBean** tartan is one sett proportion (`ri60w2lb5k2w2k2lb5w2k2g12k2w2ri5r5g2r5ri5w2g10`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWRRGRRWKGKWWKWKWWR](/stripes/gwrrgrrwkgkwwkwkwwr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
