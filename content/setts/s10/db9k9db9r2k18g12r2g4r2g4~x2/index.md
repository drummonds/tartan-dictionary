<li>undated — Newlands Family Tartan Tartan Number: 2175. Earliest known date: 1986 Designed for Willy Newlands - a journalist who wrote for The Field magazine and who lives (2004) at Lauriston Castle near Aberdeen. Walker is the maiden name of Willie Newland's wife. Note from Willy Newlands in August 2004 says 'based on a rather bloodstained bit if rag on a family cushion'). Mr Newlands also suggests calling it the "Falconers' Tartan" in memory of an ancestor who was a Royal Falconer at the court of King James IV at Linlithgow c.1495. (Sindex notes). Design based on Cameron of Erracht. Letter from Willie newlands 6th April 1988 asked that this now be named Newlands of Lauriston.
