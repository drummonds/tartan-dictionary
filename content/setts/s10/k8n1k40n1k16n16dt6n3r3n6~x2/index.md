Earliest known date: 01/01/1999 Inspired by the original works of Fenton Wyness, Dark Lochnagar tartan was designed to encapsulate Lochnagar in all its glory, including the Royal connections. Black & Grey are the primary colours of the tartan and were chosen to reflect 'The steep frowning glories of Dark Lochnagar', a line from Byron's verse. Purple was used for the Royal connections i.e. Queen Victoria's love of the district and Prince Charles book 'The Old Man of Lochnagar'.
