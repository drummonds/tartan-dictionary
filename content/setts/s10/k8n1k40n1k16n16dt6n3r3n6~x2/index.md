title: "Lochnagar Dark Fashion Tartan Tartan Number: 7327. Earliest known date: 01/01/1999 Inspired by the original works of Fenton Wyness, Dark Lochnagar tartan was designed to encapsulate Lochnagar in all its glory, including the Royal connections. Black & Grey are the primary colours of the tartan and were chosen to reflect 'The steep frowning glories of Dark Lochnagar', a line from Byron's verse. Purple was used for the Royal connections i.e. Queen Victoria's love of the district and Prince Charles book 'The Old Man of Lochnagar'. For the red we used a dye which we have called 'Red Granite' which Lochnagar has an abundance.'/Threadcount and colours aren't 100% original.
