## Provenance

James Logan recorded the **Ferguson** sett in 1831, on page 403 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> ½ green · 6 blue · ½ red · 6 black · 6 green · 1 black · 6 green · 6 black · ½ red · 6 blue · 1 green

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `G/4 B48 R4 K48 G48 K8 G48 K48 R4 B48 G/8`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
