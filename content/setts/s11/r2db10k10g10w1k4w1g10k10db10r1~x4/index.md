This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/tartans/r2db10k10g10w1k4w1g10k10db10r1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [RBKGWKWGKBR](/stripes/rbkgwkwgkbr/).

## Provenance

<figure class="logan-scan">
<a href="/sources/logan-1831/setts/rose.png"><img src="/sources/logan-1831/setts/rose.png" alt="Rose: Logan's printed table listing, scanned"></a>
<figcaption>Logan, The Scottish Gaël (1831), vol. II pp. 407, 408 — page-scan crop (1088,1864)–(1341,2227), (349,481)–(605,594)</figcaption>
</figure>

James Logan recorded the **Rose** sett in 1831, on page 407 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> ½ red · 5 blue · 5 black · 5 green · ½ white · 2 black · ½ white · 5 green · 5 black · 5 blue · 1 red

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `R/4 B40 K40 G40 W4 K16 W4 G40 K40 B40 R/8`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
