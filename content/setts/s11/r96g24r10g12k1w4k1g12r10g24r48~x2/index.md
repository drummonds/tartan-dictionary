## Provenance

James Logan recorded the **MacGregor** sett in 1831, on page 405 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> 12 red · 6 green · 2½ red · 3 green · ¼ black · 1 white · ¼ black · 3 green · 2½ red · 6 green · 24 red

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `R/96 G48 R20 G24 K2 W8 K2 G24 R20 G48 R/192`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
