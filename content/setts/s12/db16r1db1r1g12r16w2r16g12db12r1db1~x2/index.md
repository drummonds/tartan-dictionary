Earliest known date: 1893 'Old and Rare Scottish Tartans' (1893), contains a selection of forty five setts, woven in silk, of special interest and antiquity. The author was D.W. Stewart. Lord Lovat, 84 year old war veteran and chief of the Frasers of Lovat, died in March 1995. His grandson, Simon Fraser, became the 25th chief of the clan.
