Earliest known date: 1992 Designed by Tony Murray for an Albert (Bert) Riddoch of Bearsden, Glasgow as a personal tartan initially but it was then changed to usage by anyone of the name. Bert Riddoch (2002) is a private investigator involved in covert drugs investigations and 'highly thought of by the police'. Tartan based on Campbell of Breadalbane.
