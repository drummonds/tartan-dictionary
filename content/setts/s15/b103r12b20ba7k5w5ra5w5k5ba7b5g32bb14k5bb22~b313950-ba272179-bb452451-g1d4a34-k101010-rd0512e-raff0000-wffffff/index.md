Earliest known date: 3 May 2012 Designed by Marno and Mickey de Jong as a tribute to time spent in Scotland where their eldest son was born. This tartan is based on Spirit of Scotland tartan (STR ref #3865) with added colours red, white, blue and orange representing the national colours of the Netherlands.
