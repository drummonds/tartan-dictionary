<li>undated — Gayre Arisaidh Clan Tartan Tartan Number: 176. Earliest known date: pre 2003 Sindex notes that this sett is taken from an arisaidh. Although generally regarded as the usual modern form, it differs from the count registered with Lord Lyon. The Chief of the Gayres has declared that the Duthus of the clan is at Minard Castle, Loch Fyne, since oil related developments made the location at Tain unsuitable. Minard Castle contains a small museum of family history.
