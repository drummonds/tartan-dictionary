## Provenance

James Logan recorded the **Cumming** sett in 1831, on page 403 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> 1 azure · 1 black · 2 azure · 5 black · ¼ orange · 5 green · 2 red · ¼ white · 2 red · ¼ white · 2 red · 5 green · ¼ orange · 5 black · 2 azure · 1 black · 2 azure

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `A/8 K8 A16 K40 O2 G40 R16 W2 R16 W2 R16 G40 O2 K40 A16 K8 A/16`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
