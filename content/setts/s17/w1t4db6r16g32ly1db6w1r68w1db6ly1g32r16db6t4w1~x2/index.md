## Provenance

James Logan recorded the **Drummond** sett in 1831, on page 403 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> ¼ white · 1 azure · 1½ blue · 4 red · 8 green · ¼ yellow · 1½ blue · ¼ white · 17 red · ¼ white · 1½ blue · ¼ yellow · 8 green · 4 red · 1½ blue · 1 azure · ¼ white

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `W/2 A8 B12 R32 G64 Y2 B12 W2 R136 W2 B12 Y2 G64 R32 B12 A8 W/2`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
