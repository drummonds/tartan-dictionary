## Related setts

Setts a curator has related to this one.

- **derived-from**: [Shepherd](/tartans/n1t1/) — The Shepherd's Check is the black-and-white form of the Falkirk sett — the oldest surviving Scottish tartan (c. 3rd century AD), a simple 2:2 check recovered in undyed brown-and-white wool near Falkirk. Restated in dyed black and white it became the Border shepherds' plaid from which a long line of district checks descends.
- **parent-of**: [Haig Check](/tartans/k1w1k1w1b1/) — Haig is one of the Border district checks built on the Shepherd's Check.
