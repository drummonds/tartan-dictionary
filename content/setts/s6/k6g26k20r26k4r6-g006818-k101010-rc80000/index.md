Very close to the Lindsay tartan (704). Sometimes referred to as the MacCormick Dress. The sett is the same as Campbell (12). Pendleton could have based the MacCormick tartan on an existing Scottish tartan and just changing the colours. This appears to have been the basis for many of the Irish family tartans.
