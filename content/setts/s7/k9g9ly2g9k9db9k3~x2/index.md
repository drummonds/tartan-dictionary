- 01/01/1819 — Campbell of Breadalbane #3 (register-of-tartans, [record](https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=517))

- undated — Campbell of Breadalbane Clan Tartan Tartan Number: 1046. Earliest known date: 1810-15 The earliest reference to this pattern is called simply, Breadalbane. W. and A. Smith (1850) were the first to illustrate the sett in its present form. Wilson's of Bannockburn produced this pattern, the No. 64 or 'Abercrombie' in a variety of colours. (See Graham, MacCallum, Rollo.)
