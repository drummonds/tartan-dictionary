- undated — Menzies Hunting Clan Tartan Tartan Number: 894. Earliest known date: 1893 This sett is woven in various colours with the same basic design. The certified version is red and black. The hunting Menzies was recorded by D.W. Stewart in his book, 'Old and Rare Scottish Tartans' (1893), which contained a selection of forty five setts, woven in silk, of special interest or antiquity.
