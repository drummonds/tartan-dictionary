The family name originates from Farskane near Cullen in Banffshire and Faskins have been resident in Aberdeenshire for 400 years. This tartan is primarily for the use of the designer's immediate family, but is also available for others to use in any way they choose. The colours were chosen to represent the Aberdeenshire and Banffshire countryside. Designed online using the Croftweaver software at www.tartansauthority.com.
