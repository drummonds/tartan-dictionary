- undated — MacMillan Hunting Clan Tartan Tartan Number: 667. Earliest known date: (1906) The modern Hunting MacMillan incorporates red and yellow stripes from the ancient design with the greens and blues of the Vestiarium version. - J. Cant. If Cant's notes are good, then the Vestiarium reference would place the design much earlier - say 1842. - BU. This version which lacks a black stripe outlining the blue square is not generally used.
