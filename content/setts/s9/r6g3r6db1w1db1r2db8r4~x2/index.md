## Attestations

This cloth appears in 2 source records; the oldest owns this page.

- undated — Cameron of Lochiel (weddslist, [record](http://www.weddslist.com/cgi-bin/tartans/pg.pl?source=sts))
- undated — Cameron of Lochiel (Smith) Clan Tartan Tartan Number: 1398. Earliest known date: 1764 According to I.B. Cameron Taylor from a portrait in Achnacarry of the Gentle Lochiel painted by George Chalmers in 1764. He wrote, "Of the unknown Cameron tartans in existence today, the Chief's personal tartan, the Cameron of Locheil, is undoubtably the oldest."
