Colours: green and gold (sage green) represent Australia, the country where the designer’s ancestors settled and called home; the shades of blue represent the ocean and the designer’s intent to sail the oceans of the world; and most importantly, white stands for truth and justice, a common bond of the Bird family. The designer is happy for anyone sharing the same surname to wear the tartan. The designer, Andrew Bird, is happy to be contacted by persons interested in wearing the tartan.
