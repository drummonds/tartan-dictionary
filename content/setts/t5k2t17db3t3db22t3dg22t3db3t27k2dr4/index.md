The **Leith** tartan is one sett proportion (`t5k2t17db3t3db22t3dg22t3db3t27k2dr4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBBBBBGBBBKB](/stripes/bkbbbbbgbbbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1995**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
