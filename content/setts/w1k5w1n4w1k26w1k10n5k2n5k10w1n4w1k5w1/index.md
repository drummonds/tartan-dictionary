The **Clergy** tartan is one sett proportion (`w1k5w1n4w1k26w1k10n5k2n5k10w1n4w1k5w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WKWBWKBKBKWKWBWKW](/stripes/wkwbwkbkbkwkwbwkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
