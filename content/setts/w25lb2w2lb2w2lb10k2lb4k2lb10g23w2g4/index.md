The **Oliphant Dress (Clan)** tartan is one sett proportion (`w25lb2w2lb2w2lb10k2lb4k2lb10g23w2g4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWGWKWKWWWWWW](/stripes/gwgwkwkwwwwww/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2010**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Oliphant Dress](/tartans/oliphant-dress/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
