The **Nova Scotia (Province)** tartan is one sett proportion (`w2db20dg2g2dg2g4dg8y2r1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RGGGGGGBW](/stripes/rggggggbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1953**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Nova Scotia](/tartans/nova-scotia-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
