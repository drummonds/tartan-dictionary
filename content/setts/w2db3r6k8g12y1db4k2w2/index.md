The **National** tartan is one sett proportion (`w2db3r6k8g12y1db4k2w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WBRKGGBKW](/stripes/wbrkggbkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1934**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
