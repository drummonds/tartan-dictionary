The **Saskatchewan** tartan is one sett proportion (`w2k1g6dy11lo26r2lo1ly2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KGGYRYYYRYGGKW](/stripes/kggyryyyryggkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1961**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Saskatchewan](/tartans/saskatchewan/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
