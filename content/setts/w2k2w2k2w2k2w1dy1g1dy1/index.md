The **Burns Check** tartan is one sett proportion (`w2k2w2k2w2k2w1dy1g1dy1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGGWKWKWKW](/stripes/gggwkwkwkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1959**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Burns Check](/tartans/burns-check/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
