The **Baxter (Clan)** tartan is one sett proportion (`w2r16k1lb2k1y4k1lb2k1g16lb1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WGKWKGKWKRW](/stripes/wgkwkgkwkrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1856**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
