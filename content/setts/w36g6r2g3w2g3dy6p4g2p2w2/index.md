The **Strathyre Dress (Dance)** tartan is one sett proportion (`w36g6r2g3w2g3dy6p4g2p2w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGWGGBGBWBGBGGWGRGW](/stripes/grgwggbgbwbgbggwgrgw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1996**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Strathyre Dress](/tartans/strathyre-dress/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
