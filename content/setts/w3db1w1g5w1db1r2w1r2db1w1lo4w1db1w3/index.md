The **Womble** tartan is one sett proportion (`w3db1w1g5w1db1r2w1r2db1w1lo4w1db1w3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWGWBRWRBWYWBWBWYWBRWRBWGWBW](/stripes/bwgwbrwrbwywbwbwywbrwrbwgwbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1970**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
