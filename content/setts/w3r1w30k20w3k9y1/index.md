The **MacPherson 6** tartan is one sett proportion (`w3r1w30k20w3k9y1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GKWKWRW](/stripes/gkwkwrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacPherson Dress](/tartans/macpherson-dress-3/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
