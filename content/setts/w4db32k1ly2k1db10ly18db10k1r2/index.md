The **European Union (Fashion)** tartan is one sett proportion (`w4db32k1ly2k1db10ly18db10k1r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KBYBKYKBWBKYKBYBKR](/stripes/kbybkykbwbkykbybkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1997**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [European Union](/tartans/european-union/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
