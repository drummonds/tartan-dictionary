The **Menzies VS** tartan is one sett proportion (`w4r1w2r3w24dr5r3dr1r1dr1r20w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WRBRBRBWRWRW](/stripes/wrbrbrbwrwrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Menzies VS](/tartans/menzies-vs/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
