The **Frame** tartan is one sett proportion (`w4t14r1t1w1t1r1t14r14t1r1w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WBRBWBRBRBRW](/stripes/wbrbwbrbrbrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1982**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Frame](/tartans/frame/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
