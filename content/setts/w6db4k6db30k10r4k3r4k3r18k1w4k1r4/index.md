The **Harris (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`w6db4k6db30k10r4k3r4k3r18k1w4k1r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWKRKRKRKBKBWBKBKRKRKRKWKR](/stripes/kwkrkrkrkbkbwbkbkrkrkrkwkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1997**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Harris](/tartans/harris/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
