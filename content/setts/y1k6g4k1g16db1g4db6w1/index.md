The **MacKendrick** tartan is one sett proportion (`y1k6g4k1g16db1g4db6w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GKGKGBGBW](/stripes/gkgkgbgbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1842**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Henderson](/tartans/henderson/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
