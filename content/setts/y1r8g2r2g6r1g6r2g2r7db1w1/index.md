The **Bruce County** tartan is one sett proportion (`y1r8g2r2g6r1g6r2g2r7db1w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGRGRGRGRBW](/stripes/grgrgrgrgrbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1964**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Bruce County](/tartans/bruce-county/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
