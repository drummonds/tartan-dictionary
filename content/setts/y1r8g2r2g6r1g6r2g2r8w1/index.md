The **Bruce** tartan is one sett proportion (`y1r8g2r2g6r1g6r2g2r8w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGRGRGRGRW](/stripes/grgrgrgrgrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1797**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Bruce](/tartans/bruce/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
