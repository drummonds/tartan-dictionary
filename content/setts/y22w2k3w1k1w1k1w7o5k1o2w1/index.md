The **Waverley Check** tartan is one sett proportion (`y22w2k3w1k1w1k1w7o5k1o2w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWKWKWKWRKRW](/stripes/gwkwkwkwrkrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1984**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
