The **Grampian** tartan is one sett proportion (`y24r2y3db14dt24r2dt3db3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBRBBGRG](/stripes/bbrbbgrg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1995**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Grampian](/tartans/grampian/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
