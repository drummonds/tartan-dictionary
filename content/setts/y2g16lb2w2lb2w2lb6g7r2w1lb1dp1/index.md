The **Fredericton** tartan is one sett proportion (`y2g16lb2w2lb2w2lb6g7r2w1lb1dp1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BWWRGWWWWWGG](/stripes/bwwrgwwwwwgg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1961**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Fredericton](/tartans/fredericton/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
