This is the **Fredericton** sett — one scale-free proportion (`y2g16lb2w2lb2w2lb6g7r2w1lb1dp1`), colour order pattern
[BWWRGWWWWWGG](/stripes/bwwrgwwwwwgg/). It is recorded as **2 variants** — the same
cloth woven at different scales or shades, each its own page below; the oldest (★) anchors the sett.
First attested **1961**.
