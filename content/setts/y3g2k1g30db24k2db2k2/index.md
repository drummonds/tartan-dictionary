The **Johnston** tartan is one sett proportion (`y3g2k1g30db24k2db2k2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGKGBKBK](/stripes/ggkgbkbk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Johnston](/tartans/johnston/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
