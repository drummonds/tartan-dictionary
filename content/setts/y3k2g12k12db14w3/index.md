The **MacNeil 6** tartan is one sett proportion (`y3k2g12k12db14w3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GKGKBW](/stripes/gkgkbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [MacNeil of Barra](/tartans/macneil-of-barra/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
