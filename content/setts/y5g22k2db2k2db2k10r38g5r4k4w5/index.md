The **Boyd** tartan is one sett proportion (`y5g22k2db2k2db2k10r38g5r4k4w5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGKBKBKRGRKW](/stripes/ggkbkbkrgrkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1956**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [Boyd](/tartans/boyd/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
