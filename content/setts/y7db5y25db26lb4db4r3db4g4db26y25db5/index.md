The **O Savaaoo** tartan is one sett proportion (`y7db5y25db26lb4db4r3db4g4db26y25db5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BGBGBRBWBGBG](/stripes/bgbgbrbwbgbg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2008**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
