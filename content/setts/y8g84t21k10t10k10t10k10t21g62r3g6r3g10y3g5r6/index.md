The **King Edward VII** tartan is one sett proportion (`y8g84t21k10t10k10t10k10t21g62r3g6r3g10y3g5r6`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGBKBKBKBGRGRGGGR](/stripes/ggbkbkbkbgrgrgggr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~1910**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.

Part of the [King Edward](/tartans/king-edward/) tartan — the named design grouping this proportion with its other setts.
