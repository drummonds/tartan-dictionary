Every tartan in the dictionary is traceable to a **source**: a dataset of setts, colours or photographs, each with its own licence and provenance. The dictionary is *derived* data — these sources are the primary record.

When the same cloth is recorded by more than one source, the records **collapse** to a single page that lists every source as an *attestation*, and the **oldest record owns the page**. James Logan's 1831 survey is the earliest of them, so where Logan recorded a sett his is the owning record, with the later, adjusted versions linked from it.
