Catalogued paintings from the National Galleries of Scotland (nationalgalleries.org) that depict tartan cloth — dated primary witnesses to how a sett looked when painted. Per-painting metadata in paintings.jsonl; the painted cloth's sett is recovered by a manual reading informed by internal/unweave (see research/painting-to-sett.md). Setts pending recovery, so no variants are attested yet.
