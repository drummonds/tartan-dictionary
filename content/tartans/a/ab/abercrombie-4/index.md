A tartan of the [Abercrombie](/families/abercrombie/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBRKGWGRK](/stripes/kbrkgwgrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan Abercrombie sett whose blue and green central panels should be doubled; akin to Graham of Menteith and Wilson's No. 158.

The **Abercrombie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
