Its design is pattern [WBKWBRBRKBGRGW](/stripes/wbkwbrbrkbgrgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Heather Yellowley for the Aberfeldy & District Gaelic Choir's 2008 MOD appearance, then adopted as the district tartan.

The **Aberfeldy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
