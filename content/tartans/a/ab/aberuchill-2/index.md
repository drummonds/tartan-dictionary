A tartan of the [Aberuchill](/families/aberuchill/) family.
Its design is pattern [RKGBGGKY](/stripes/rkgbggky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Created for the Aberuchill Estate, south of Comrie where the rivers Ruchil and Earn meet.

The **Aberuchill** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
