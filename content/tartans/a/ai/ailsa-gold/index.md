A tartan of [Clan Ailsa](/clan/ailsa/).
Its design is pattern [WBWYWY](/stripes/wbwywy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

One of a series of dancer's tartans (Gold, Yellow and more) designed by Kirsty Anderson for House of Edgar's in-house collection.

The **Ailsa Gold** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
