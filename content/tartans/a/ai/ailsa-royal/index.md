A tartan of [Clan Ailsa](/clan/ailsa/).
Its design is pattern [BWBWBW](/stripes/bwbwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

One of a series of dancers' tartans designed by Kirsty Anderson for House of Edgar's in-house collection.

The **Ailsa Royal** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
