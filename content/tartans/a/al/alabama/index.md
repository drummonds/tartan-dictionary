A tartan of the [Alabama](/families/alabama/) family.

Its design is pattern [RWBWBRBRBRBBWBWBBBWBWBW](/stripes/rwbwbrbrbrbbwbwbbbwbwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2006 by Joseph Ryan Morrison for Alabama's first Tartan Day; colours symbolise state flags, skies, Gulf waters and Confederate heritage.

The **Alabama** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
