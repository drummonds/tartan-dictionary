Its design is pattern [GBBKWR](/stripes/gbbkwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Created 2010 for Alan Stone's family, its colours symbolise Stone origins in Cornwall, England, Ireland and American Civil War service.

The **Alan Stone** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
