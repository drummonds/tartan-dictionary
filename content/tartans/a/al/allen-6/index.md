A tartan of the [Allen](/families/allen/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGKBRBKBKBKBKBGBKGRGRGKBGBKBKBKBKBRBKGR](/stripes/grgkbrbkbkbkbkbgbkgrgrgkbgbkbkbkbkbrbkgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1997 for Christopher Allen Holler of Winter Park, Florida, for anyone named Allen.

The **Allen** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
