A tartan of [Clan Allen](/clan/allen/).

Its design is pattern [BRBRBBGWGBBGRG](/stripes/brbrbbgwgbbgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 2001 by Jerry M P Allen for use by his family and relations.

The **Allen Northumbrian** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
