Its design is pattern [BWWWBKRWRWRWR](/stripes/bwwwbkrwrwrwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 1975 from J.C. Thomson's idea for American St Andrews and Caledonian Societies; thirteen red-white stripes and starlike crossings evoke the US flag.

The **American Bi-Centennial** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
