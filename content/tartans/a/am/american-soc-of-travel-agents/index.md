Its design is pattern [GBRBBBBBBBBBRBGW](/stripes/gbrbbbbbbbbbrbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Kinloch Anderson for Scottish-trained US travel agents, based on Davidson tartan, celebrating ASTA's 1997 Glasgow conference.

The **American Soc of Travel Agents** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
