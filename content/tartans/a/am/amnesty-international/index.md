A tartan of [Clan Amnesty](/clan/amnesty/).

Its design is pattern [BRKRBWBRKYKRBK](/stripes/brkrbwbrkykrbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by James Scarlett for Amnesty International's 40th anniversary; colours represent its multi-racial, international work.

The **Amnesty International** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
