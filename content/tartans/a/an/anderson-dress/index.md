A tartan of the [Anderson](/families/anderson/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGRGRKGKGWGKGWRWR](/stripes/rgrgrkgkgwgkgwrwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress variant of the Anderson clan tartan; unmarked sample from the MacGregor-Hastie collection, likely 1930–1950.

The **Anderson Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
