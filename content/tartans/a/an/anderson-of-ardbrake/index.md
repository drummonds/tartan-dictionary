A tartan of the [Anderson of Ardbrake](/families/anderson-of-ardbrake/) family.

Its design is pattern [KRGBWRKGKGKWKBRWKGW](/stripes/krgbwrkgkgkwkbrwkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2006 by David Waterton-Anderson for the Ardbrake Andersons, featuring a red ground with traditional white-and-yellow tramline stripes.

The **Anderson of Ardbrake** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
