Its design is pattern [KRKRKRKRKRKRKRKWKWKWKWKW](/stripes/krkrkrkrkrkrkrkwkwkwkwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Used as both District and Family tartan; name means "The Only One," linked to Clan MacInnes and Dalriada.

The **Angus** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
