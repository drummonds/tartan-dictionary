A tartan of the [Annan](/families/annan/) family.
Its design is pattern [YBKYBYYYBY](/stripes/ybkybyyyby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Sett from Paton's collection, housed at the Scottish Tartans Museum, Comrie; likely assembled around the 1830s.

The **Annan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
