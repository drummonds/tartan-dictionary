Its design is pattern [YKBWBRYRY](/stripes/ykbwbryry/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Celebrates the Arbroath Smokie's European protected status; colours evoke Arbroath Abbey sandstone, the sea, and the smoked fish.

The **Arbroath Smokie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
