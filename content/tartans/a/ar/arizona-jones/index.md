A tartan of [Clan Arizona](/clan/arizona/).

Its design is pattern [GBKGKBKBKG](/stripes/gbkgkbkbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Welsh surname tartan for Jones and variants, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft for a vertical stripe.

The **Arizona Jones** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
