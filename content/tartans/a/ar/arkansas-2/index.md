A tartan of the [Arkansas](/families/arkansas/) family.

Its design is pattern [GBGBGGRGG](/stripes/gbgbggrgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Official Arkansas state tartan, approved 1998; green for the "Natural State," red settlers, white diamonds, black oil.

The **Arkansas** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
