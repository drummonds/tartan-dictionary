A tartan of the [Arran](/families/arran/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWBWKWKW](/stripes/bwbwkwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Modern district sett introduced by MacNaughtons of Pitlochry in 1982, its decreasing-stripe effect drawn from old Arran plaids.

The **Arran** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
