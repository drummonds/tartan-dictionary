A tartan of [Clan Asman](/clan/asman/).
Its design is pattern [GKGYWKWRYYY](/stripes/gkgywkwryyy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Phil Smith for David Asman, woven by Peter MacDonald; intended for day wear, "tan" denoting light brown.

The **Asman Day Tan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
