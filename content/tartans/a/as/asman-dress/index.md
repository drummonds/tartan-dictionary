A tartan of the [Asman](/families/asman/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGBBWKWRKR](/stripes/bgbbwkwrkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Dr. Philip D. Smith in 1989 for David I. Asman, an English armiger in New Jersey.

The **Asman Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
