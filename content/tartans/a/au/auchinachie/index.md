A tartan of the [Auchinachie](/families/auchinachie/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKBKRKWGWKBK](/stripes/gkbkrkwgwkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Family tartan designed for Peter Auchinachie, available to all of the name.

The **Auchinachie** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
