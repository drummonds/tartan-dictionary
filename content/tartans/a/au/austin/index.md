A tartan of the [Austin](/families/austin/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBGK](/stripes/bkbgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Sett recorded by Wilson's of Bannockburn (1819) as No. 75 or Austin; worn by the Keiths, hereditary Great Marischals of Scotland.

The **Austin** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
