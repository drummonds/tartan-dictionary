A tartan of the [Australia](/families/australia/) family.

Its design is pattern [WRYRYKYRYRYKYRYRW](/stripes/wryrykyryrykyryrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 1984 by Melbourne architect John Reid for a national competition, based on outback colours and Lachlan MacQuarrie's tartan.

The **Australia** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
