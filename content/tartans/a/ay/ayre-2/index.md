A tartan of the [Ayre](/families/ayre/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKGKGKBKWKBKGKGKRKGW](/stripes/gkgkgkbkwkbkgkgkrkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Wilsons' No. 038 (1819), adopted by David Ayre of Kilmarnock as a private family tartan.

The **Ayre** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
