A tartan of the [Ayrshire](/families/ayrshire/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBWBKGG](/stripes/bbwbkgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1988 by Dr Phil Smith for Boyd and Cunningham Societies; colours evoke Ayrshire's sun, land, coast and sea.

The **Ayrshire** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
