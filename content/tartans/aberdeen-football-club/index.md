Commissioned by Aberdeen Football Club using navy and gold from the team's away strip.

The **Aberdeen Football Club** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
