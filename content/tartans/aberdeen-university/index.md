Designed by the Weaver Incorporation of Aberdeen and Harry Lindley for the University's Quincentennial, using its armorial colours.

The **Aberdeen University** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
