Created for the Aberuchill Estate, south of Comrie where the rivers Ruchil and Earn meet.

The **Aberuchill** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
