Designed 2006 by Joseph Ryan Morrison for Alabama's first Tartan Day; colours symbolise state flags, skies, Gulf waters and Confederate heritage.

The **Alabama** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
