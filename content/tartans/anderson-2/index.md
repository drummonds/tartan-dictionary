A tartan of [Clan Anderson](/clan/anderson/).
Its design is pattern [BBBBBKYKYKWKBBKBBB](/stripes/bbbbbkykykwkbbkbbb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Anderson** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
