Clan tartan based on the Black Watch; sett registered with Lord Lyon in 1962.

The **Arbuthnott** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
