Official Arkansas state tartan, approved 1998; green for the "Natural State," red settlers, white diamonds, black oil.

The **Arkansas** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
