Designed early 20th century for Sir William Arrol, engineer behind the Forth Bridge and Arrol-Johnston motor company.

The **Arrol** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
