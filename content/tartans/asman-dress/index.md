Designed by Dr. Philip D. Smith in 1989 for David I. Asman, an English armiger in New Jersey.

The **Asman Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
