Based on Stewart of Atholl with colours from Aubigny-sur-Nère's crest, marking the Scotland–France Auld Alliance.

The **Aubigny Auld Alliance** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
