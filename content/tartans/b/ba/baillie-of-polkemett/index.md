A tartan of the [Baillie of Polkemett](/families/baillie-of-polkemett/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGWGWGWGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgwgwgwgkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1937 by A.W. Geddes for the Baillies of Polkemett; based on MacKenzie with three white lines.

The **Baillie of Polkemett** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
