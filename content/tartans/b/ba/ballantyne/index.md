Its design is pattern [BGBBG](/stripes/bgbbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Ballantyne** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
