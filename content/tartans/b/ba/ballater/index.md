A tartan of the [Ballater](/families/ballater/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBBBWKBKWB](/stripes/bbbbwkbkwb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Modern commercial design given a district name for marketing, not a true historical Ballater district tartan.

The **Ballater** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
