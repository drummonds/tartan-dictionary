A tartan of the [Ballintrae](/families/ballintrae/) family.

Its design is pattern [RBRGRRR](/stripes/rbrgrrr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Modern design given a district name to promote Scottish connections, not a historic district tartan.

The **Ballintrae** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
