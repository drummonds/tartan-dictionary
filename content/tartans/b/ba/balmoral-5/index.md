A tartan of the [Balmoral](/families/balmoral/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRBWKBWBWBKBR](/stripes/brbwkbwbwbkbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Royal tartan designed by Prince Albert in the early reign years; grey threads flecked to suggest granite.

The **Balmoral** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
