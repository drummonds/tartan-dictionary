Its design is pattern [RGRGRGRGW](/stripes/rgrgrgrgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Based on Rothesay, adopted by Northern India's Baluch Regiment under commanding officer Fitzgerald, later becoming the Fitzgerald tartan.

The **Baluchistan Fitzgerald Regimental** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
