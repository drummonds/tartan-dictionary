A tartan of [Clan Banff](/clan/banff/).
Its design is pattern [KWKBBBBY](/stripes/kwkbbbby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1992 for the District of Banff and Buchan, based on the Ogilvy sett, with landscape-inspired colours.

The **Banff and Buchan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
