A tartan of [Clan Bannockbane](/clan/bannockbane/).

Its design is pattern [BRBRWRRR](/stripes/brbrwrrr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Variation of an early 1970s trade sett, in a navy fashion colourway.

The **Bannockbane Navy** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
