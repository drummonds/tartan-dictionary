A tartan of [Clan Bannockbane](/clan/bannockbane/).
Its design is pattern [BYBYWYYY](/stripes/bybywyyy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Fashion pattern from the early 1970s, possibly designed by Donald Broun; unconnected to any Bannockbane place or family.

The **Bannockbane Orange Stripes** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
