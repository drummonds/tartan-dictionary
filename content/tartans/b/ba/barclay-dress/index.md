A tartan of the [Barclay](/families/barclay/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKGW](/stripes/gkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress version of the Barclay sett, derived from the hunting tartan in the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum; the name's most common tartan.

The **Barclay Dress** tartan is one sett, recorded at 3 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
