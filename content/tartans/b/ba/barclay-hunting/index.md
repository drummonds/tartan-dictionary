A tartan of the [Barclay](/families/barclay/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBGR](/stripes/gbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting variant of Barclay, first recorded in the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum.

The **Barclay Hunting** tartan is one sett, recorded at 3 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
