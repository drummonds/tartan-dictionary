Its design is pattern [GBGR](/stripes/gbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting variant of Barclay, first recorded in the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum.

The **Barclay Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
