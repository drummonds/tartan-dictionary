Its design is pattern [BKGWRKBKGKGKGKGK](/stripes/bkgwrkbkgkgkgkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed for the Golden Bones branch of Venice's Baseggio family; gold and blue from their arms, with Italian Tricolore stripes.

The **Baseggio** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
