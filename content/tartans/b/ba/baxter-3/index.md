A tartan of [Clan Baxter](/clan/baxter/).
Its design is pattern [BGKBKYKYKBKBWBKBKYKYKBKG](/stripes/bgkbkykykbkbwbkbkykykbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Recorded by Logan (c.1832) as "Buchanan"; named Baxter in Peter's 1856 Baronage of Angus & Mearns.

The **Baxter** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
