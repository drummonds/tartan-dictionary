A tartan of the [Baxter](/families/baxter/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGKBKYKYKBKBW](/stripes/bgkbkykykbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Documented in The Baronage of Angus and Mearns (1856).

The **Baxter** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
