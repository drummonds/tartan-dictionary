A tartan of the [Begg](/families/begg/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBKWKGBGR](/stripes/kbkwkgbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2012 for Melissa McNulty's Begg family, drawing on Macdonald and Caithness Sinclair setts; green for crofting, blue for sea, purple for heather.

The **Begg** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
