Its design is pattern [BBBGBY](/stripes/bbbgby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1997 by Peter MacDonald for the Swedish Belfrage family, originally Fife's Beveridge; based on Duke of Fife and Spens tartans.

The **Belfrage** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
