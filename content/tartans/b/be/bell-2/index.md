A tartan of the [Bell](/families/bell/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBWKWKGWRWGKWBKBK](/stripes/bkbwkwkgwrwgkwbkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1984 for Colonel William H. Bell, president of the Bell Family Association; commemorates a Border riding clan.

The **Bell** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
