A tartan of [Clan Bell](/clan/bell/).
Its design is pattern [RBKBKBBBBW](/stripes/rbkbkbbbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorates the Bell Rock Lighthouse's 200th anniversary; red and white flashes, dark North Sea blues, black for lives lost.

The **Bell Rock Lighthouse 200th Anniv Commemorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
