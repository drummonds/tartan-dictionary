A tartan of the [Benyon of Wales](/families/benyon-of-wales/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKGBKBGBGBGBGGK](/stripes/bkgbkbgbgbgbggk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh name tartan for surnames Enyon and Benyon, designed by Sheila Daniel at Cambrian Woollen Mill.

The **Benyon of Wales** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
