A tartan of the [Bermuda](/families/bermuda/) family.

Its design is pattern [WBWGBRW](/stripes/wbwgbrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 1962 by Peter Macarthur Ltd for Bermuda; colours represent its sky, sea, coral, and cedar trees.

The **Bermuda** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
