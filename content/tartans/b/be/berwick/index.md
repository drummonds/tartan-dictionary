A tartan of the [Berwick](/families/berwick/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGBKBKGKBBKBG](/stripes/bgbkbkgkbbkbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Won by Berwick High School pupil Alison Wilkinson in a 1981 competition to commemorate the town's history.

The **Berwick** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
