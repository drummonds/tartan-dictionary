A tartan of the [Bisset](/families/bisset/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGKGKYGBW](/stripes/rgkgkygbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1976 by the Scottish Tartans Society for Mrs E. Bisset, with colours symbolising the family shield and motto.

The **Bisset** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
