A tartan of [Clan Black](/clan/black/).

Its design is pattern [BWGRKBKGK](/stripes/bwgrkbkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Commissioned by the Campbells for the Black Hills Highland Games in Sturgis, South Dakota; unofficial state tartan.

The **Black Hills** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
