A tartan of [Clan Black](/clan/black/).
Its design is pattern [BKRKGKGKGKRBRWR](/stripes/bkrkgkgkgkrbrwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Reworked dark variant of the Scottish National tartan, woven by Marton Mills.

The **Black Scottish National** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
