A tartan of [Clan Black](/clan/black/).
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKBKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkbkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Combination of two shades of black, shown as black and dark grey to illustrate the sett.

The **Black Shadow** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
