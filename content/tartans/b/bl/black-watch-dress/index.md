A tartan of [Clan Black](/clan/black/).
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGKGKWBWBWBWBWKBKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgkgkwbwbwbwbwkbkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress variant of Black Watch, intended for highland dancing.

The **Black Watch Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
