A tartan of [Clan Black](/clan/black/).
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGKGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgkgkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dark blue-green-black sett worn by Highland Companies (1725) and Black Watch (1739); approved by the Duke of Argyll.

The **Black Watch Regimental** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
