A tartan of the [Blackstock](/families/blackstock/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGKRKRG](/stripes/ggkrkrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress tartan commissioned 1983 by Herbert Earl Blackstock of the US Clan Blackstock Society; designed by Bob Martin.

The **Blackstock Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
