Designed by Kirsty Anderson of The House of Edgar for ACS Clothing of Glasgow.

The **Blue Spirit** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
