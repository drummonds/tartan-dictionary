Its design is pattern [BKWKWRK](/stripes/bkwkwrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Now called 'Southern Bell', formerly known (possibly in error) as Bell of Blackethouse; no longer recognised by Chief Apparent.

The **Border Bell** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
