A tartan of [Clan Boucherville](/clan/boucherville/).
Its design is pattern [GBBBBWBGW](/stripes/gbbbbwbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1990 by three La Navette d'Art weavers for Boucherville, Quebec, using symbolic colours.

The **Boucherville Formal** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
