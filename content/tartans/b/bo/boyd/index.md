Its design is pattern [GGKBKBKRGRKW](/stripes/ggkbkbkrgrkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Compact form of the Boyd sett designed for Lord Kilmarnock in 1956; based on Hay and Stuart of Bute tartans.

The **Boyd** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
