A tartan of [Clan Braveheart](/clan/braveheart/).

Its design is pattern [KBKBKBKGRKRW](/stripes/kbkbkbkgrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Commemorates the 1995 film about William Wallace; originally designed for Ronnie Watt.

The **Braveheart Commemorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
