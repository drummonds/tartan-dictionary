A tartan of the [Brodie](/families/brodie/) family.
Its design is pattern [KRKGKR](/stripes/krkgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Brodie clan sett, first recorded in Smiths' 1850 manuscript; Aberdeenshire Brodies earlier wore Huntly District tartan.

The **Brodie** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
