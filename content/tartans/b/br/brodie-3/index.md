A tartan of the [Brodie](/families/brodie/) family.

Its design is pattern [GRBRKBWR](/stripes/grbrkbwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Clan tartan documented in Peters' 1856 'Baronage of Angus and Mearns' and the Vestiarium Scoticum manuscript; origin unknown.

The **Brodie** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
