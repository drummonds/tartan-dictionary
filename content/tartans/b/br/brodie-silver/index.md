A tartan of [Clan Brodie](/clan/brodie/).

Its design is pattern [RBGBRWR](/stripes/rbgbrwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Probable trade design based on Hunting Brodie, also called muted Brodie; appeared mid-20th century.

The **Brodie Silver** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
