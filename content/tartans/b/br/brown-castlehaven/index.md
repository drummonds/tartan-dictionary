A tartan of [Clan Brown](/clan/brown/).
Its design is pattern [KRBWKGKWKGRKRW](/stripes/krbwkgkwkgrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Plaid owned by Thomas Brown, born 1786 at Castlehaven near Stonehaven; preserved by his descendant Mme. Rolland de Maubeuge.

The **Brown Castlehaven** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
