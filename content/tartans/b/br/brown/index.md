A tartan of [Clan Brown](/clan/brown/).

Its design is pattern [BRBRBKRG](/stripes/brbrbkrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


From the 1850 Scott Adie collection of manufacturers' samples, representing tartans sold 1840–50.

The **Brown** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
