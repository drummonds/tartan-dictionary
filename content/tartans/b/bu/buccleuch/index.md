Its design is pattern [RGRBRKR](/stripes/rgrbrkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Fancy pattern dating c.1840, inspired by the works of Sir Walter Scott.

The **Buccleuch** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
