Its design is pattern [WGKWKGKGKWKRW](/stripes/wgkwkgkgkwkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Asymmetrical Buchanan clan sett, also known as Baxter; possibly altered by McIan from Logan's earlier symmetrical version and favoured by weaver Wilson.

The **Buchanan 9** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
