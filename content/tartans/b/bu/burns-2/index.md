A tartan of the [Burns](/families/burns/) family.
Its design is pattern [YBYBYY](/stripes/ybybyy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Modern family sett discovered by MacKinlay at Forsyth's, probably dating 1930–50; a separate Robert Burns check exists.

The **Burns** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
