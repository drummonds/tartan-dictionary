The **Fletcher** tartan is one sett proportion (`b11k3b3k3b3k11r3g13k3g13r3k11b11k3b3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKBKBKRGKGRKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkrgkgrkbkb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
