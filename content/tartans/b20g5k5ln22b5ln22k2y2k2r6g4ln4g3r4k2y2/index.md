The **Gayre Arisaidh** tartan is one sett proportion (`b20g5k5ln22b5ln22k2y2k2r6g4ln4g3r4k2y2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BGKWBWKYKRGWGRKY](/stripes/bgkwbwkykrgwgrky/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
