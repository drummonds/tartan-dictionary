The **Tartan Army** tartan is one sett proportion (`b22db2b4db2b4db8ln2db2ln2db10r5y2r5db10ln2db2ln2db8b18db2b4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BBBBBBWBWBRYRBWBWBBBB](/stripes/bbbbbbwbwbryrbwbwbbbb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
