The **Nicolson** tartan is one sett proportion (`b2r11g2r11g21r2k9ba2b11r11g2r11b2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRGRBBKRGRGRB](/stripes/brgrbbkrgrgrb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
