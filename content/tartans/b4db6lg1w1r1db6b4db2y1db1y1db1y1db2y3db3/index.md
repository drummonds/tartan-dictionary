The **de Baseggio (Golden Bones)** tartan is one sett proportion (`b4db6lg1w1r1db6b4db2y1db1y1db1y1db2y3db3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BBGWRBBBYBYBYBYB](/stripes/bbgwrbbbybybybyb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
