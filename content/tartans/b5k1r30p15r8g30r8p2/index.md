The **Shaw of Tordarroch** tartan is one sett proportion (`b5k1r30p15r8g30r8p2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKRBRGRB](/stripes/bkrbrgrb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
