The **Cheape** tartan is one sett proportion (`b6k1b1k1b1k6g6ba2g6k6b6k1b2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKBKBKGBGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgbgkbkb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
