The **Innes** tartan is one sett proportion (`b7k24r4k4r4k4r24ly4r6db12r6k4dg20k4r6lr4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKRKRKRYRBRKGKRY](/stripes/bkrkrkryrbrkgkry/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
