A tartan of the [Baillie of Polkemett](/families/baillie-of-polkemett/) family.

Designed 1937 by A.W. Geddes for the Baillies of Polkemett; based on MacKenzie with three white lines.

The **Baillie of Polkemett** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
