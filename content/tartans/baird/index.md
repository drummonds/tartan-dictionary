Its design is pattern [BGBGKBKB](/stripes/bgbgkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan Baird tartan first recorded in Johnston's 1906 work; originally triple red stripes, now usually purple.

The **Baird** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
