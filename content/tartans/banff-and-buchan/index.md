Designed in 1992 for the District of Banff and Buchan, based on the Ogilvy sett, with landscape-inspired colours.

The **Banff and Buchan** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
