Corporate tartan designed by Betty Davies of Scottish Fashion International, with differing warp and weft.

The **Bank of Scotland** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
