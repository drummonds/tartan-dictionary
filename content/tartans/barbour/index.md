Designed by Kinloch Anderson of Edinburgh for the linings of Barbour's wax jackets.

The **Barbour** tartan groups 6 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
