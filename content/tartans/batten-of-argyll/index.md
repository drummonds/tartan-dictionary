A tartan of the [Batten of Argyll](/families/batten-of-argyll/) family.

Designed for the Baddenach/Batten family of Argyll descent and the St Andrews Legion Pipes & Drums of Richmond, Virginia.

The **Batten of Argyll** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
