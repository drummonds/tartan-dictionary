Designed 2012 for Melissa McNulty's Begg family, drawing on Macdonald and Caithness Sinclair setts; green for crofting, blue for sea, purple for heather.

The **Begg** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
