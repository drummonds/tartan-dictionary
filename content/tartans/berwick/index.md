Won by Berwick High School pupil Alison Wilkinson in a 1981 competition to commemorate the town's history.

The **Berwick** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
