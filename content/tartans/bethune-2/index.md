Its design is pattern [WBYKYKWKGRKRW](/stripes/wbykykwkgrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Phil Smith; as MacBeth but with a light blue stripe added in the darker blue ground.

The **Bethune** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
