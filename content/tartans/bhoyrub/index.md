Its design is pattern [BGGBRGBKBKBW](/stripes/bggbrgbkbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Created for Iggy Bhoyrub's 2006 wedding, based on the flag colours of Scotland and Mauritius.

The **Bhoyrub** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
