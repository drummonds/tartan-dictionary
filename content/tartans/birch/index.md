Designed by Robin Birch of Connell Reid kiltmaker, Blairgowrie, for the Birch family.

The **Birch** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
