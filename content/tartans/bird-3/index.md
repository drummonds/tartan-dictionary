A tartan of [Clan Bird](/clan/bird/).
Its design is pattern [WBWGGWWBW](/stripes/wbwggwwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Andrew Bird as a legacy for the Bird family; green and gold for Australia, blue for oceans, white for truth.

The **Bird** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
