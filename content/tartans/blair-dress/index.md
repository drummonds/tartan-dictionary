Its design is pattern [BBBKGBGBGKWBW](/stripes/bbbkgbgbgkwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress version of the Blair tartan, white added to the existing sett; approved by the Clan Blair Society, 1988.

The **Blair Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
