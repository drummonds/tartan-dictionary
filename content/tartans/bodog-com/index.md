Corporate tartan in red, black and silver-grey; first named after a website and first for Costa Rica.

The **Bodog.com** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
