Its design is pattern [WBYKGKWKGGKGW](/stripes/wbykgkwkggkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Irish family pattern originating in Waterford Mill, later recorded at Pendleton Mill from the 'Clan Originaux'.

The **Bowling** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
