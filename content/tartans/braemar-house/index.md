Corporate tartan combining the red Hay-Leith and green Hunting Stewart, reflecting the Lords Kilmarnock's Hay and Stewart descent.

The **Braemar House** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
