Asymmetrical sett, also MacIntyre and Glenorchy; adopted by Buchan family c.1965 reflecting ancient Cumming kinship ties.

The **Buchan** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
