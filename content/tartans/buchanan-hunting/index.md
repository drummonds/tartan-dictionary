Hunting variant of the Buchanan clan tartan, recorded in the Scott Adie sample book c.1850.

The **Buchanan Hunting** tartan groups 8 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
