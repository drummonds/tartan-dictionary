A tartan of the [Burnett of Leys](/families/burnett-of-leys/) family.

Mentioned in the Public Register of All Arms and Bearings in Scotland but not defined.

The **Burnett of Leys** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
