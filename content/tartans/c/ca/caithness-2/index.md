A tartan of the [Caithness](/families/caithness/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRKBKRBYKYKYKYK](/stripes/brkbkrbykykykyk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Trudi Mann of Wick using Caithness colours, including the blue-grey of local flagstone.

The **Caithness** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
