A tartan of the [Caledonia](/families/caledonia/) family.
Its design is pattern [RBKBKBKGGRKRWR](/stripes/rbkbkbkggrkrwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Eighteenth-century district sett, Wilson's No 155, dubiously named "Caledonia" but resembling other Caledonia setts.

The **Caledonia** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
