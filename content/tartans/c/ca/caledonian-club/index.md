A tartan of [Clan Caledonian](/clan/caledonian/).

Its design is pattern [BBBBBBBBBBRB](/stripes/bbbbbbbbbbrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on Clan Campbell sett with Morrison red and Club-arms blue, navy and purple, for the Belgravia members' club founded 1891.

The **Caledonian Club** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
