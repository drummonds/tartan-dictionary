A tartan of [Clan Cameron](/clan/cameron/).

Its design is pattern [GKRBRKWRWRW](/stripes/gkrbrkwrwrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress variant of Cameron of Erracht, from the unmarked MacGregor-Hastie collection, likely dating 1930–1950.

The **Cameron Erracht Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
