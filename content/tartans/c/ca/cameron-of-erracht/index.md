A tartan of the [Cameron of Erracht](/families/cameron-of-erracht/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBRBRKGRGRG](/stripes/gbrbrkgrgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for the Queen's Own Cameron Highlanders, raised by Alan Cameron of Erracht in 1793; never adopted by the clan.

The **Cameron of Erracht** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
