A tartan of the [Cameron of Locheil](/families/cameron-of-locheil/) family.
Its design is pattern [RBRBWBRGR](/stripes/rbrbwbrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Chief's personal Cameron sett, dated from a 1764 portrait of the Gentle Lochiel at Achnacarry.

The **Cameron of Locheil** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
