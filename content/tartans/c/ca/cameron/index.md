A tartan of the [Cameron](/families/cameron/) family.

Its design is pattern [BRBRKGRGRG](/stripes/brbrkgrgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in the 1940s by J.G. MacKay of Portree, evoking the blue and yellow worn by Camerons at Killiecrankie (1689).

The **Cameron** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
