A tartan of [Clan Campbell](/clan/campbell/).
Its design is pattern [BKBKGKGKWBWKW](/stripes/bkbkgkgkwbwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress variant of Campbell, dated 1880, sampled at the Black Watch museum but never worn as regimental tartan.

The **Campbell Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
