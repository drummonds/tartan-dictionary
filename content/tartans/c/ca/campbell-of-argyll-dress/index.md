A tartan of the [Campbell of Argyll](/families/campbell-of-argyll/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGWGKWWWWWWWWWKGGGK](/stripes/bkbkbkgwgkwwwwwwwwwkgggk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress version of the Campbell of Argyll clan tartan, introduced 1906 in Whyte's "Tartans of the Clans and Septs of Scotland."

The **Campbell of Argyll Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
