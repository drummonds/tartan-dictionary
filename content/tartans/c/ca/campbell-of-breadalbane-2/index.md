A tartan of the [Campbell of Breadalbane](/families/campbell-of-breadalbane/) family.

Its design is pattern [KBKGYGK](/stripes/kbkgygk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Campbell of Breadalbane variant, dating to 1810-15, originally called simply "Breadalbane"; standardised by W. and A. Smith in 1850.

The **Campbell of Breadalbane** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
