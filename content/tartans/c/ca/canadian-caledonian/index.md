Its design is pattern [BGGRWRGRGW](/stripes/bggrwrgrgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Hastie-Cochrane and George MacBeth of Vancouver, a Canadian Caledonian district tartan dating from 1939.

The **Canadian Caledonian** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
