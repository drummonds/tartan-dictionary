Its design is pattern [YKGKWKY](/stripes/ykgkwky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1957 to match Lillian Crewe Walsh's poem praising Cape Breton, with colours symbolising steel, hills, and more.

The **Cape Breton** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
