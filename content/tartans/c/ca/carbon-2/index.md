A tartan of the [Carbon](/families/carbon/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKBBKWKWY](/stripes/bkbbkwkwy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Kinloch Anderson for Carbon Financial Partners Ltd, based on the Perthshire District tartan, using corporate greys, black, orange, and white.

The **Carbon** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
