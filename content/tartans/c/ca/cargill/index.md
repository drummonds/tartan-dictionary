Its design is pattern [BGBKGKGBGBGBGBGKGKBG](/stripes/bgbkgkgbgbgbgbgkgkbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Black, white and grey design known as Clergy or Beachan na Clerich; also called Cargill; from Clans Originaux, 1880.

The **Cargill** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
