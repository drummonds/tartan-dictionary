A tartan of the [Carnegie](/families/carnegie/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRBRBRKGRGRGG](/stripes/brbrbrkgrgrgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Variant of MacDonell of Glengarry, adopted by Lord Southesk in the 1715 rebellion; Glengarry's white becomes yellow.

The **Carnegie** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
