Its design is pattern [RWKGKWKRGRKRW](/stripes/rwkgkwkrgrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Commemorates the historic Carolina link; based on a c.1730 Royal Company of Archers coat fragment, reputedly Charles II's 1661 wedding ribbon sett.

The **Carolina** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
