A tartan of the [Cavalier](/families/cavalier/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKGKWKRBKBW](/stripes/bkgkwkrbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Catalan-themed design with red stripes for Guifre Pilos's bloodied fingers, plus white snow, green flora, and blue Mediterranean; first seen at the 1992 Barcelona Olympics.

The **Cavalier** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
