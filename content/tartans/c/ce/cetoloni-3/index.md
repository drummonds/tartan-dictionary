A tartan of the [Cetoloni](/families/cetoloni/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKGKRB](/stripes/bkgkrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed for Italian-born Franco Cetoloni, evoking Tuscan skies, Siena rooftops and golden sun in Sienna red.

The **Cetoloni** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
