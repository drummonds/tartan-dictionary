Its design is pattern [GGBGBGBBRKRW](/stripes/ggbgbgbbrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorates cooperation between Fulton County, Georgia and the Scottish Borders; red, white, black from the Atlanta Falcons.

The **Chattahoochee Commemorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
