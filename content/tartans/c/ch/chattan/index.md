A tartan of the [Chattan](/families/chattan/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRKWGWGRKRGWGWKR](/stripes/grkwgwgrkrgwgwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Variant of Clan Chattan sett with a brown stripe beside the yellow, absent from Lord Lyon's records.

The **Chattan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
