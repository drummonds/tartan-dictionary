A tartan of [Clan Cheape](/clan/cheape/).
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGWGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgwgkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Worn by the Tiroran branch of the Cheape family from the Glenfarg area, dated c.1906.

The **Cheape** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
