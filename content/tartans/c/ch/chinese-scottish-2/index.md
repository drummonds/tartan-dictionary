Its design is pattern [GRGRGBWBWBWBWBGRGR](/stripes/grgrgbwbwbwbwbgrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2006 by Heather Yellowley to mark Scottish-Chinese ties, blending Saltire blue with Chinese flag's red and yellow.

The **Chinese Scottish** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
