A tartan of the [Christie](/families/christie/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRKWR](/stripes/brkwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Christie family tartan, from two 1930 samples presented by Stewart Christie of Edinburgh; an azure variant exists.

The **Christie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
