A tartan of the [Cian](/families/cian/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBWBBGRBWBY](/stripes/bbwbbgrbwby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Irish family tartan registered with the Chief Herald of Ireland in 1983; usually woven in ancient colours.

The **Cian** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
