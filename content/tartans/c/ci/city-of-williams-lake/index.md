Its design is pattern [GKGGWBKBBWBBKBWGGK](/stripes/gkggwbkbbwbbkbwggk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by the Spinners & Weavers Guild for Williams Lake's 75th anniversary, March 2004; accepted by City Council.

The **City of Williams Lake** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
