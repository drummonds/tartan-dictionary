A tartan of the [Cleland](/families/cleland/) family.

Its design is pattern [KWGWR](/stripes/kwgwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on Douglas tartan, reflecting Clelands' role as hereditary foresters to the Douglases; designed 1989 for the Northern Territories Clan Clelland Association in Australia.

The **Cleland** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
