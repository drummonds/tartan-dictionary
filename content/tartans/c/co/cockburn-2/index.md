A tartan of the [Cockburn](/families/cockburn/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKGKGKBKWKBKGKGKR](/stripes/bkgkgkbkwkbkgkgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Sir William Cockburn mistakenly certified the MacKenzie sett as his own; modern version retains distinctive red, yellow and white stripes.

The **Cockburn** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
