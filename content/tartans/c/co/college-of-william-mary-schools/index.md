Its design is pattern [BGGGGGGGBWBGGGGGGGBK](/stripes/bgggggggbwbgggggggbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 2004 by Carol Worthley for the College of William & Mary, sold as a library fundraiser.

The **College of William & Mary Schools** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
