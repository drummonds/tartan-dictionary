A tartan of the [Colquhoun](/families/colquhoun/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKYBYBBBYBYKYGBGYK](/stripes/bkbkbkybybbbybykygbgyk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Recent design, notable as one of the very few asymmetrical setts.

The **Colquhoun Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
