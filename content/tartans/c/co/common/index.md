Its design is pattern [BRKGKBKR](/stripes/brkgkbkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Wilson's of Bannockburn variant of Black Watch, predating widespread clan naming; military version included a red stripe.

The **Common** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
