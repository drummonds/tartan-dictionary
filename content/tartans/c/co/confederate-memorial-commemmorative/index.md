Its design is pattern [WBWRYGYRYY](/stripes/wbwrygyryy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Dr. Philip Smith in 1995; grey for the Confederacy, with fields for infantry, artillery and cavalry.

The **Confederate Memorial Commemmorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
