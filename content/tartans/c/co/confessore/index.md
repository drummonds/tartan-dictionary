A tartan of the [Confessore](/families/confessore/) family.
Its design is pattern [KGKGYRYRYGKGKWK](/stripes/kgkgyryrygkgkwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1994 by Keith Lumsden for Mr Confessore of Naples; colours reflect Italian countryside tones.

The **Confessore** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
