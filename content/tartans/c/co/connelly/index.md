Its design is pattern [GGRGGBGW](/stripes/ggrggbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by James Connelly in 2009 for his daughter's 2010 wedding, with symbolic greens, purple, gold, white, and red.

The **Connelly** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
