A tartan of the [Conquergood](/families/conquergood/) family.

Its design is pattern [KWWWBWKW](/stripes/kwwwbwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 1982 to represent Canadian winter landscape and summer beaches; approved by the Conquergood family in 1990.

The **Conquergood** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
