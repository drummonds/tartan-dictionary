Dress family tartan; Gaelic name meaning "Blessed Copper" honouring Mrs Y Ch van Koperen-Benoist, green for oxidised copper, blue for Benoist.

The **Copar a'Beannichte Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
