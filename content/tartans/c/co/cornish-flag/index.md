A tartan of [Clan Cornish](/clan/cornish/).
Its design is pattern [KWKWKWKR](/stripes/kwkwkwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Cornish district tartan based on the St Piran flag; worn by a New York pipe band.

The **Cornish Flag** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
