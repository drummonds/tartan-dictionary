A tartan of the [Cornish](/families/cornish/) family.
Its design is pattern [KYGBKRKBGYKW](/stripes/kygbkrkbgykw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting variant of the Cornish National tartan, produced 1984 by Cornovi Creations, replacing yellow with green and azure with darker blue.

The **Cornish Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
