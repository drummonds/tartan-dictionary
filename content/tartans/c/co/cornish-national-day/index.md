A tartan of [Clan Cornish](/clan/cornish/).

Its design is pattern [KWGWR](/stripes/kwgwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Variant of the Cornish National tartan, proportionally adjusted; dates from 1984.

The **Cornish National Day** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
