A tartan of [Clan Cornish](/clan/cornish/).
Its design is pattern [RKWGKW](/stripes/rkwgkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1963 by Cornish poet E.E. Morton-Nance to commemorate the ancient kingdom of Cornwall.

The **Cornish National Small Set** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
