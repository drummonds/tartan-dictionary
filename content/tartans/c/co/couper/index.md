A tartan of [Clan Couper](/clan/couper/).
Its design is pattern [BWRGBGBKWBWGBKBWBR](/stripes/bwrgbgbkwbwgbkbwbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Woven by Peter MacArthur of Hamilton for the Couper family of Gogar, copied from an old shawl.

The **Couper** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
