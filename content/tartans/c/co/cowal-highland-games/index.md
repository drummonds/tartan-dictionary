Its design is pattern [BBBGBGBGGGBGBGBB](/stripes/bbbgbgbgggbgbgbb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 1994 for the centenary of Dunoon's Cowal Highland Gathering; muted blues and greens.

The **Cowal Highland Games** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
