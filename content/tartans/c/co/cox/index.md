A tartan of the [Cox](/families/cox/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWRBGBKBKBGBKBKGR](/stripes/bwrbgbkbkbgbkbkgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Personal tartan combining Bell and Marshall patterns with red, blue, white and purple symbolising the designer, his wife, marriage and happiness.

The **Cox** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
