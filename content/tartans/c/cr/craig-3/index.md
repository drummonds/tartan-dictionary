A tartan of the [Craig](/families/craig/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKWKWBWBWBWWKGKWWBWBWBWKWKBK](/stripes/bkbkwkwbwbwbwwkgkwwbwbwbwkwkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1957 by MacGregor Hastie for the clanless Craig family, colours based on craigs and rocks.

The **Craig** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
