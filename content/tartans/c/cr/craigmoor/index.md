Its design is pattern [GGGKRKBRK](/stripes/gggkrkbrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by MacGregor Hastie for the numerous, clanless Craig family, based on the colours of craigs and rocks.

The **Craigmoor** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
