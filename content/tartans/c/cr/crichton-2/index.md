A tartan of the [Crichton](/families/crichton/) family.

Its design is pattern [GYKBWBKGK](/stripes/gykbwbkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Commissioned 2008 by clan chief; based on MacGill tartan, with colours from the Creighton coat of arms.

The **Crichton** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
