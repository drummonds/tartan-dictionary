A tartan of [Clan Crieff](/clan/crieff/).

Its design is pattern [KGBBBBBBBW](/stripes/kgbbbbbbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2014 by Crieff Primary pupils and staff; claret, blue/white for sky and River Earn, green for new playgrounds.

The **Crieff Primary School** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
