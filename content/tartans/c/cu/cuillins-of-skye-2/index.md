A tartan of the [Cuillins of Skye](/families/cuillins-of-skye/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBGBBBBBGBRGRBGBBW](/stripes/bbgbbbbbgbrgrbgbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Asymmetric fashion tartan designed by Duncan MacDonald and Helen Marshall of Marton Mills.

The **Cuillins of Skye** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
