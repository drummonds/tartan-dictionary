A tartan of the [Culloden](/families/culloden/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKGKWBWR](/stripes/gkgkwbwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Derived from a coat worn at the 1746 Battle of Culloden, now displayed at Kelvingrove Museum, Glasgow.

The **Culloden** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
