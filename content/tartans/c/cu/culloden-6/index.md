A tartan of the [Culloden](/families/culloden/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKYKWBWR](/stripes/gkykwbwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Worn by a member of Prince Charles' staff at the 1746 battle; now a district tartan.

The **Culloden** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
