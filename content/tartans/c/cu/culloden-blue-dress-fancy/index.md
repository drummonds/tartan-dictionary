A tartan of [Clan Culloden](/clan/culloden/).

Its design is pattern [GBWWBWBW](/stripes/gbwwbwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress tartan by Edinburgh kiltmaker Hugh Macpherson, intended for dancing and informal occasions.

The **Culloden Blue Dress Fancy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
