A tartan of the [Culloden](/families/culloden/) family.

Its design is pattern [RBBWGWGW](/stripes/rbbwgwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Worn by a member of Prince Charles' staff at Culloden; now an established district tartan.

The **Culloden Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
