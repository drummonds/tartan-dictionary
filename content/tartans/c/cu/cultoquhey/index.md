Its design is pattern [RBKGY](/stripes/rbkgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Peter MacDonald circa 1990 as a Cook tartan; later became the Cultoquhey Hotel's house tartan.

The **Cultoquhey** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
