A tartan of the [Cumnock](/families/cumnock/) family.

Its design is pattern [GBKBKRKY](/stripes/gbkbkrky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on MacMillan hunting tartan honouring Cumnock Games founder; blue from town's lion rampant, orange and red for Lugar's iron furnaces, black for coal mines.

The **Cumnock** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
