A tartan of the [Cunningham](/families/cunningham/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRKRWRW](/stripes/brkrwrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress version with white ground, created by Edinburgh kiltmaker Hugh Macpherson c.1980 for dancing and informal wear.

The **Cunningham Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
