A tartan of the [Cunningham](/families/cunningham/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBKBWBW](/stripes/bbkbwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Invented dance fashion tartan, unrecognised by Clan Cunningham Association (USA).

The **Cunningham Dress** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
