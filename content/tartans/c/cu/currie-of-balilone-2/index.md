A tartan of the [Currie of Balilone](/families/currie-of-balilone/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKGGKWKWKWKWK](/stripes/gkggkwkwkwkwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Granted by charter in 1822 to Currie of Balilone by MacDonald, Lord of the Isles; sett based on Lord of the Isles.

The **Currie of Balilone** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
