A tartan of the [Cuthill](/families/cuthill/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGRGRGBBBBBBGBBBBBBGRGRG](/stripes/bgrgrgbbbbbbgbbbbbbgrgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2006 by Mr Cuthill, based on Lindsay tartan worn by his family since c1800 following the Cuthill–Lindsay marriage.

The **Cuthill** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
