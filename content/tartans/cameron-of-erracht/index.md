A tartan of the [Cameron of Erracht](/families/cameron-of-erracht/) family.

The **Cameron of Erracht** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
