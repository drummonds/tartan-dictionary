A tartan of the [Campbell of Loch Awe](/families/campbell-of-loch-awe/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBKGK](/stripes/kbkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Campbell of Lochawe and Campbell of Loch Awe are the same — a spelling variant of the Loch Awe place name. The spaced form is preferred, so the two fold into one family and tartan.

The **Campbell of Loch Awe** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
