A tartan of the [Campbell of Loudon](/families/campbell-of-loudon/) family.

The **Campbell of Loudon** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
