A tartan of [Clan Campbell](/clan/campbell/).

Personal tartan made for Captain Campbell of the Blythswood family.

The **Campbell** tartan groups 12 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
