Corporate dress tartan for the Canmore Highland Games, organised by the Three Sisters Scottish Festival Society.

The **Canmore Highland Games Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
