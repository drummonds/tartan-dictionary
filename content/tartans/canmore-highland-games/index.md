Honours Canmore's Scottish roots, King Malcolm Ceann Mór and Scots railway settlers in the Canadian region.

The **Canmore Highland Games** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
