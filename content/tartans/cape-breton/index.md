date: 2023-03-18

Designed in 1957 to match Lillian Crewe Walsh's poem praising Cape Breton, with colours symbolising steel, hills, and more.

The **Cape Breton** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
