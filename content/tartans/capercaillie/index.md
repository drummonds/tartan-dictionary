Designed in the colours of the woodland grouse; Lochcarron weave yields a 7% RSPB Scotland royalty.

The **Capercaillie** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
