Designed by the Caribou Islands fire hall ladies' auxiliary; red symbolises sunsets, lobsters, and fire trucks.

The **Caribou** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
