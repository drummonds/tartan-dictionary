A tartan of [Clan Carnegie](/clan/carnegie/).

Dress version with white substituted for a ground colour, by Edinburgh kiltmaker Hugh Macpherson for dancing and informal wear.

The **Carnegie Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
