Its design is pattern [WYGYBYBYBYBYBY](/stripes/wygybybybybyby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Catalan district tartan from 1991; red stripes evoke Catalunya's heraldry, with white snow, green flora, blue Mediterranean.

The **Catalan** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
