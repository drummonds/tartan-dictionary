Designed for Celtic F.C., launched at Parkhead in 1989; appeared at the 1990 World Cup in Italy.

The **Celtic F.C.** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
