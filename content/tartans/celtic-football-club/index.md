Designed by Claire Donaldson of House of Edgar in 2005, updating Celtic Football Club's tartan.

The **Celtic Football Club** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
