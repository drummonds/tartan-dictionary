Designed for Italian-born Franco Cetoloni, evoking Tuscan skies, Siena rooftops and golden sun in Sienna red.

The **Cetoloni** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
