A tartan of the [Cheape of Torosay](/families/cheape-of-torosay/) family.

Worn by the Tiroran branch of the Cheape family from the Glenfarg area, dated c.1906.

The **Cheape of Torosay** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
