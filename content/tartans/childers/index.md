Worn by 1st Battalion, 1st Gurkha Rifles for plaids, ribbons and bag-covers.

The **Childers** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
