Designed 2006 by Heather Yellowley to mark Scottish-Chinese ties, blending Saltire blue with Chinese flag's red and yellow.

The **Chinese Scottish** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
