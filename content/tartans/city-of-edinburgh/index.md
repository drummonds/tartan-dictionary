District tartan for the City of Edinburgh; asymmetric design, trialled June 2001, with a muted version woven in 1999.

The **City of Edinburgh** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
