A tartan of [Clan Clark](/clan/clark/).
Its design is pattern [KGKBKGKR](/stripes/kgkbkgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Clark** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
