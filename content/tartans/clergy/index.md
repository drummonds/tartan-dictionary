Its design is pattern [BGBKGKGBGBG](/stripes/bgbkgkgbgbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Green variation of the Clergy sett recorded by Smith in 1850.

The **Clergy** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
