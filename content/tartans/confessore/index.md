Designed in 1994 by Keith Lumsden for Mr Confessore of Naples; colours reflect Italian countryside tones.

The **Confessore** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
