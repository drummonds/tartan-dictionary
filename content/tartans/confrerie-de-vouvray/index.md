Created for the French Order of Wine Growers; red, yellow, maroon are its colours, green grid depicts Chenin Blanc vine rows.

The **Confrerie de Vouvray** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
