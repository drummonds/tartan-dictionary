Designed in 1982 to represent Canadian winter landscape and summer beaches; approved by the Conquergood family in 1990.

The **Conquergood** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
