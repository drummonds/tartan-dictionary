Its design is pattern [BBWBGGGGYBYGGGG](/stripes/bbwbggggybygggg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Madeleine Asselin for Contrecoeur, a small township in southern Quebec.

The **Contrecoeur** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
