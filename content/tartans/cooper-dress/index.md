Dress version of Couper of Gogar tartan, white replacing green; original woven circa 1886 by Peter MacArthur.

The **Cooper Dress** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
