Its design is pattern [GWGBGBBBR](/stripes/gwgbgbbbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Family tartan honouring Mrs Y Ch van Koperen-Benoist; green for oxidised copper, blue for the Benoists.

The **Copar a'Beannichte** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
