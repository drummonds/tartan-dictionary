Modern tartan reflecting the history and ancestry of Cordiners from Boddam, Aberdeenshire.

The **Cordiner** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
