Personal tartan combining Bell and Marshall patterns with red, blue, white and purple symbolising the designer, his wife, marriage and happiness.

The **Cox** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
