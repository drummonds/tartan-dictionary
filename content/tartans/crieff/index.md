Its design is pattern [RRGRGRBRGRGRR](/stripes/rrgrgrbrgrgrr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Large district sett from Crieff, Perthshire, famed cattle-droving tryst; recorded by Wilson's from 1793.

The **Crieff** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
