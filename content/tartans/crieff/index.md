Large district sett from Crieff, Perthshire, famed cattle-droving tryst; recorded by Wilson's from 1793.

The **Crieff** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
