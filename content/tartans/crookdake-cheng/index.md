Its design is pattern [BBWKGGGGGG](/stripes/bbwkgggggg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1994 by Phil Smith for the Scottish Tartans Society treasurer; yellow represents rice stalks.

The **Crookdake Cheng** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
