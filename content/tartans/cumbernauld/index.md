MacKenzie-based design with ancient green added to blue, black and red, representing the new town of Cumbernauld.

The **Cumbernauld** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
