Its design is pattern [GGKGWGW](/stripes/ggkgwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dancers' variant of Cunningham, woven by D C Dalgliesh of Selkirk.

The **Cunningham Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
