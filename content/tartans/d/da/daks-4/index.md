A tartan of the [Daks](/families/daks/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBBBYBBG](/stripes/gbbbybbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Daks** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
