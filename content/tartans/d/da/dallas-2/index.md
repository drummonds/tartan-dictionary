A tartan of the [Dallas](/families/dallas/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBBBWBBWGGWGGWBBW](/stripes/bbbbwbbwggwggwbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dallas family tartan, reportedly seen in the 1940s near Dundee; woven by Lochcarron and D.C. Dalgliesh.

The **Dallas** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
