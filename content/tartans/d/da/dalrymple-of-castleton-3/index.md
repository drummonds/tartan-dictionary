A tartan of the [Dalrymple of Castleton](/families/dalrymple-of-castleton/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGRGKGBWBGRBW](/stripes/grgrgkgbwbgrbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Reconstructed by Don Rankin from a 1720 portrait illustration; sample held in STS collection.

The **Dalrymple of Castleton** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
