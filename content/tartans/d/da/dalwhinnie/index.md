A tartan of the [Dalwhinnie](/families/dalwhinnie/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGWGWGWGR](/stripes/ggwgwgwgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Reconstructed by Don Rankin in 1982 from an illustration; sample held in STS collection.

The **Dalwhinnie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
