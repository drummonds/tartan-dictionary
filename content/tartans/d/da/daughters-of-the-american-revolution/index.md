Corporate tartan for the Daughters of the American Revolution; red and white stripes reflect the society's logo.

The **Daughters of the American Revolution** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
