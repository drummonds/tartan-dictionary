A tartan of the [Dauphinee](/families/dauphinee/) family.
Its design is pattern [RKWKBGBKWKR](/stripes/rkwkbgbkwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2012 by Andrew Dauphinee for his family, with colours from the family crest and Halifax/Nova Scotia flags.

The **Dauphinee** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
