A tartan of the [Davidson](/families/davidson/) family.
Its design is pattern [KGBGBR](/stripes/kgbgbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Reduced version of the more recent Davidson sett, basis for the Henderson tartan; omits earlier white stripe.

The **Davidson** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
