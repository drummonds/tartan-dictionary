A tartan of [Clan Davies](/clan/davies/).

Its design is pattern [GKBKBKBKGKBKW](/stripes/gkbkbkbkgkbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Welsh surname tartan (Davy, Davis, Day, David) woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill; differing warp and weft create a vertical stripe.

The **Davies** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
