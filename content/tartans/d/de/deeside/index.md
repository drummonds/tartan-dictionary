Its design is pattern [GBGBBBW](/stripes/gbgbbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


The **Deeside** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
