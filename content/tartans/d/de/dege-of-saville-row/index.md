A tartan of the [Dege of Saville Row](/families/dege-of-saville-row/) family.

Its design is pattern [GBGBBR](/stripes/gbgbbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Corporate tartan for Savile Row tailors Dege, reflecting their country and sporting heritage since 1865.

The **Dege of Saville Row** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
