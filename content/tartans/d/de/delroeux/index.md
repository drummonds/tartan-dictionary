Its design is pattern [BGYR](/stripes/bgyr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Jean Michael Delroeux to show his affinity with Scotland; colours drawn from his coat of arms and Scottish landscapes.

The **Delroeux** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
