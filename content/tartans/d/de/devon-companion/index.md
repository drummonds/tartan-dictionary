A tartan of [Clan Devon](/clan/devon/).

Its design is pattern [BBGBKGW](/stripes/bbgbkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Devon district tartan inspired by the Cornish St Piran sett, woven by Coldharbour Mill, accredited 1991.

The **Devon Companion** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
