A tartan of [Clan Devon](/clan/devon/).

Its design is pattern [BGWGRGG](/stripes/bgwgrgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Devon district tartan, created early 1980s following success of Cornish St Piran sett.

The **Devon Original** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
