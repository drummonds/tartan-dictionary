A tartan of the [Dogwood](/families/dogwood/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGYBYGBG](/stripes/bgybygbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Registered Dinwiddie clan tartan; Lord Lyon ruled in 1988 they were a sept of no other clan.

The **Dogwood** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
