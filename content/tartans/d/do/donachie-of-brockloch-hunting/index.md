A tartan of the [Donachie of Brockloch](/families/donachie-of-brockloch/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBGGGGGBGG](/stripes/gbgggggbgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Robertson-based hunting sett with red changed to green; Donachie is part of Clan Donnachaidh.

The **Donachie of Brockloch Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
