A tartan of [Clan Donachie](/clan/donachie/).
Its design is pattern [GRGRGRGRGR](/stripes/grgrgrgrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Simplified 2004 sett based on Robertson tartan, adopted by the Donachie of Brockloch Society.

The **Donachie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
