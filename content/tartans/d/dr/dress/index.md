A tartan of the [Dress](/families/dress/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWKBBWG](/stripes/bwkbbwg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Unofficial alternative for US Marines, colours echoing the Dress Blue uniform in matching ratios.

The **Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
