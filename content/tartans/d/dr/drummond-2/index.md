A tartan of [Clan Drummond](/clan/drummond/).
Its design is pattern [BRGRBRBRGRBR](/stripes/brgrbrbrgrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Worn by the Drummonds to meet George IV in Edinburgh, 1822; resembles a Grant pattern.

The **Drummond** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
