A tartan of the [Dryburgh](/families/dryburgh/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKBKBKBRGRG](/stripes/gkbkbkbrgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on Kerr and the Dryburgh coat of arms colours, matriculated for William J. Dryburgh.

The **Dryburgh** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
