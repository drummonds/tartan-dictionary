A tartan of the [Duffy](/families/duffy/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGGKWGG](/stripes/gggkwgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by G. Duffy and kiltmaker Connell Reid; colours reflect the Duffy crest—yellow lion on green, outlined black.

The **Duffy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
