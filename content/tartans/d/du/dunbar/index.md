A tartan of [Clan Dunbar](/clan/dunbar/).
Its design is pattern [KWKRKW](/stripes/kwkrkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Lowland family sett first appearing in the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum; a separate Dunbar district pattern was woven by Wilson's around 1850.

The **Dunbar** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
