A tartan of the [Duncan of Sketraw](/families/duncan-of-sketraw/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKGKWKBRBKGKGKGKR](/stripes/bkgkwkbrbkgkgkgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Modified from an unidentified 1930s tartan (No. 331) for John Duncan of Sketraw; approved by Clan Duncan Society.

The **Duncan of Sketraw** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
