A tartan of the [Dundas](/families/dundas/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGBKGRB](/stripes/bgbkgrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Originated in the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842), with green, black, blue military ground and twin red stripes.

The **Dundas** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
