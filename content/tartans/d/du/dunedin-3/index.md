A tartan of the [Dunedin](/families/dunedin/) family.
Its design is pattern [KRGRRRKWW](/stripes/krgrrrkww/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorates the 1848 Free Church of Scotland settlers who founded Dunedin, New Zealand, the "Edinburgh of the South."

The **Dunedin** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
