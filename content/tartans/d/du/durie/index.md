A tartan of the [Durie](/families/durie/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBBBBBGGG](/stripes/bbbbbbggg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1988 by Harry Lindlay of Kinloch & Anderson; based on Argyll & Sutherland Highlanders, with yellow facings and burgundy for French ties.

The **Durie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
