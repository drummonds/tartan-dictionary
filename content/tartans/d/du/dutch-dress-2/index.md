Its design is pattern [KBKWWR](/stripes/kbkwwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on Mackay tartan, reflecting the Mackay chiefs' Dutch barony; dress variant via simple colour change.

The **Dutch, dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
