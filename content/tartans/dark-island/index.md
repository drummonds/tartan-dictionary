Solid Sett tartan: ecru Jacquard-woven fabric, piece-dyed black, with the sett revealed through varied light-reflecting stitches.

The **Dark Island** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
