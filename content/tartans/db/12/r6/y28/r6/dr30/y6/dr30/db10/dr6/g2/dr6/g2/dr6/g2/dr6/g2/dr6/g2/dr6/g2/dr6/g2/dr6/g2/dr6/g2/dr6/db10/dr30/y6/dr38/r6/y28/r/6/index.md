The red, yellow and maroon are the colours of the french order and the grid pattern of green lines depict the rows of vines of the Chenin Blanc grape. The tartan was presented as a gift to the Grand Council at the 2nd Scottish Confrerie, held in Dundee in May 2003. See products available Copyright © Blair Urquhart, Comrie, 2015 with 34 stripes"
