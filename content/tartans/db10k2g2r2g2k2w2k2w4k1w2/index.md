The **Highfield Dress** tartan is one sett proportion (`db10k2g2r2g2k2w2k2w4k1w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKGRGKWKWKW](/stripes/bkgrgkwkwkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1982**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
