The **North Berwick (Dance)** tartan is one sett proportion (`db10r2db10r10dg2r2dg2r2dg10r1w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRBRGRGRGRW](/stripes/brbrgrgrgrw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
