The **Campbell** tartan is one sett proportion (`db11k1db1k1db1k8g8k1g8k8db8k1db1`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKBKBKGKGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgkgkbkb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
