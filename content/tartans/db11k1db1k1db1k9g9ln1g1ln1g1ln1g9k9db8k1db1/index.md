The **Baillie of Polkemmet** tartan is one sett proportion (`db11k1db1k1db1k9g9ln1g1ln1g1ln1g9k9db8k1db1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKBKBKGWGWGWGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgwgwgwgkbkb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
