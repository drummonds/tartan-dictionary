The **Durie (Clan)** tartan is one sett proportion (`db12dr1db1dr1db1dr4g12y1g2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRBRBRGYG](/stripes/brbrbrgyg/); each scale
below is its own sett page.

The design is said to be based on the Argyle & Southern Highlanders regimental tartan - the yellow is from the mess dress (military uniform evening wear) facings (lapels) and the burgundy represents the Durie family's French connections.
