The **Gordon** tartan is one sett proportion (`db12k2db2k2db2k12dg12ly2dg12k12db11k2db2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGYGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgygkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
