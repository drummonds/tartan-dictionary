The **Kilburnie** tartan is one sett proportion (`db12k2db2k2r3k2b6k2ln2k2b6k2y2k2db6k2r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKBKRKBKWKBKYKBKRKBKYKBKWKBKRKBK](/stripes/bkbkrkbkwkbkykbkrkbkykbkwkbkrkbk/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
