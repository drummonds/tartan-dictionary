The **Kilburnie** tartan is one sett proportion (`db12k2db2k2r3k2t6k2w2k2t6k2ly2k2db6k2r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKRKBKWKBKYKBKR](/stripes/bkbkrkbkwkbkykbkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1985**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
