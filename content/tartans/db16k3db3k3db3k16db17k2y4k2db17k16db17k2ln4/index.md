The **Fleming/Frisken/Flanders** tartan is one sett proportion (`db16k3db3k3db3k16db17k2y4k2db17k16db17k2ln4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKBKBKBKYKBKBKW](/stripes/bkbkbkbkykbkbkw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
