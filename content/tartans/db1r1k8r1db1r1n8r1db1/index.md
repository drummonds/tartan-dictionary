The **MacPherson Hunting** tartan is one sett proportion (`db1r1k8r1db1r1n8r1db1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRBRBRKRB](/stripes/brbrbrkrb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
