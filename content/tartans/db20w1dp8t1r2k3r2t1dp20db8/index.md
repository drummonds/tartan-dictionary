The **Custer (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`db20w1dp8t1r2k3r2t1dp20db8`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBBRKRBBWB](/stripes/bbbrkrbbwb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2004**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
