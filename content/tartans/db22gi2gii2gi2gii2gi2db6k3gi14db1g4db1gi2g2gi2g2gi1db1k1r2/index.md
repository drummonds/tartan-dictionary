The **Peter Pan** tartan is one sett proportion (`db22gi2gii2gi2gii2gi2db6k3gi14db1g4db1gi2g2gi2g2gi1db1k1r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BGGGGGBKGBGBGGGGGBKR](/stripes/bgggggbkgbgbgggggbkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1998**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
