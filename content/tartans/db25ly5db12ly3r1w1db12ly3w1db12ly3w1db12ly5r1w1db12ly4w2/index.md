The **Wanless (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`db25ly5db12ly3r1w1db12ly3w1db12ly3w1db12ly5r1w1db12ly4w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYBYRWBYWBYWBYRWBYW](/stripes/bybyrwbywbywbyrwbyw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
