The **Glen Orchy** tartan is one sett proportion (`db2t1r2g16r2db6t1r2g6r2db16t1r2g2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBRGRBBRGRBBRG](/stripes/bbrgrbbrgrbbrg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1870**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
