The **Lamont** tartan is one sett proportion (`db3k1db1k1db1k4dg4n1dg4k4db4k1db1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKBKBKGYGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgygkbkb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
