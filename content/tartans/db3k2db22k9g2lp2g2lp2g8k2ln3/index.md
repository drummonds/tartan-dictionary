The **Scottish Rugby Union** tartan is one sett proportion (`db3k2db22k9g2lp2g2lp2g8k2ln3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [KKKKGWGWGKW](/stripes/kkkkgwgwgkw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
