The **St. Giles Check** tartan is one sett proportion (`db3o1w1o25w1o1dp3`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BRWRWRB](/stripes/brwrwrb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1984**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
