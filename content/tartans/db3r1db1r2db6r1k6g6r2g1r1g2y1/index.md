The **Carnegie** tartan is one sett proportion (`db3r1db1r2db6r1k6g6r2g1r1g2y1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRBRBRKGRGRGY](/stripes/brbrbrkgrgrgy/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
