The **Arbuthnott** tartan is one sett proportion (`db4k1db1k1db1k4dg2lr1dg2db2dg2lr1dg2k4db5k1db1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGYGBGYGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgygbgygkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
