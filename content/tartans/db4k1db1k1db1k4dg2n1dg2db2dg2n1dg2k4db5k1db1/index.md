The **Arbuthnott** tartan is one sett proportion (`db4k1db1k1db1k4dg2n1dg2db2dg2n1dg2k4db5k1db1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKBKBKGYGBGYGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgygbgygkbkb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
