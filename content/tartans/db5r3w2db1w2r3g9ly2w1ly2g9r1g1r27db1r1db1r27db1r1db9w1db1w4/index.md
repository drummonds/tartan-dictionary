The **Hebrides North Uist** tartan is one sett proportion (`db5r3w2db1w2r3g9ly2w1ly2g9r1g1r27db1r1db1r27db1r1db9w1db1w4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BRWBWRGYWYGRGRBRBRBRBWBW](/stripes/brwbwrgywygrgrbrbrbrbwbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1814**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
