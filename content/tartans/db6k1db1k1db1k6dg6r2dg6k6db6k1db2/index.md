The **Murray** tartan is one sett proportion (`db6k1db1k1db1k6dg6r2dg6k6db6k1db2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBKGRGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgrgkbkb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
