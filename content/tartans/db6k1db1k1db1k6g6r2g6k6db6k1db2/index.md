The **Murray** tartan is one sett proportion (`db6k1db1k1db1k6g6r2g6k6db6k1db2`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKBKBKGRGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgrgkbkb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
