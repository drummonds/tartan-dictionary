Designed for the De Clercq family, based on Christian De Clercq's coat of arms granted by the Walloon Government.

The **De Clercq, Christian** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
