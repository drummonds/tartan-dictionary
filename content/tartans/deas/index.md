Sept of Clan Davidson; Deas recorded historically as an alias for Davidson.

The **Deas** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
