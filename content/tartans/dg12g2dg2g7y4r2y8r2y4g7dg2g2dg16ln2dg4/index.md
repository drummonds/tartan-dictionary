The **Confessore** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg12g2dg2g7y4r2y8r2y4g7dg2g2dg16ln2dg4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GGGGYRYRYGGGGWG](/stripes/ggggyryryggggwg/); each scale
