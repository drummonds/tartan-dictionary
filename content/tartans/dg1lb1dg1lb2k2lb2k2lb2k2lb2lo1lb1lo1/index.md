The **Glen Flesk** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg1lb1dg1lb2k2lb2k2lb2k2lb2lo1lb1lo1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWGWKWKWKWYWY](/stripes/gwgwkwkwkwywy/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1997**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
