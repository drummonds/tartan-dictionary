The **Glen Flesk** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg1n1dg1n2k2n2k2n2k2n2lt1n1lt1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GWGWKWKWKWYWY](/stripes/gwgwkwkwkwywy/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
