The **Hand (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg1n8na5n23na5db3na5dp3na5dg5na3ln1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GBRBRBRBRGRW](/stripes/gbrbrbrbrgrw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
