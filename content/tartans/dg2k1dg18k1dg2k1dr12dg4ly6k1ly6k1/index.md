The **MacMillan Ancient** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg2k1dg18k1dg2k1dr12dg4ly6k1ly6k1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GKGKGKBGYKYK](/stripes/gkgkgkbgykyk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
