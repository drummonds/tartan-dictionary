The **Dalwhinnie** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg70y6n28g56n5g11n5g11o12`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GYGGGGGGY](/stripes/gyggggggy/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
