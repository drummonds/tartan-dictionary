The **MacNab** tartan is one sett proportion (`dg8dr1dg1dr1dg1dr6r8dr1r8dr6dg7dr1dg1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBGBGBRBRBGBG](/stripes/gbgbgbrbrbgbg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
