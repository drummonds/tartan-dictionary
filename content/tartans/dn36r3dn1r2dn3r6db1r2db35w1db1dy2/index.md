The **Kirkcaldy Tartan Army** tartan is one sett proportion (`dn36r3dn1r2dn3r6db1r2db35w1db1dy2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRBRBRBRBWBYBWBRBRBRBR](/stripes/brbrbrbrbwbybwbrbrbrbr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
