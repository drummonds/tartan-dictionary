A tartan of [Clan Douglas](/clan/douglas/).
Its design is pattern [WBWBWW](/stripes/wbwbww/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Douglas Variation** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
