The **Meanwood McMain (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`dp30db10m5db10dp30db3dpi5db3dp30`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBBBBBRBB](/stripes/bbbbbbrbb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2005**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
