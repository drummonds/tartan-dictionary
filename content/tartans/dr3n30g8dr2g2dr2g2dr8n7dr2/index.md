The **Gray** tartan is one sett proportion (`dr3n30g8dr2g2dr2g2dr8n7dr2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BGBGBGBGGB](/stripes/bgbgbgbggb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
