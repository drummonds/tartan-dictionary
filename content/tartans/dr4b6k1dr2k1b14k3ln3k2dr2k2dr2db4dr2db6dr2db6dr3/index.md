The **Royal Canadian Air Force** tartan is one sett proportion (`dr4b6k1dr2k1b14k3ln3k2dr2k2dr2db4dr2db6dr2db6dr3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RBKRKBKWKRKRBRBRBR](/stripes/rbkrkbkwkrkrbrbrbr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
