A tartan of [Clan Drummond](/clan/drummond/).
Its design is pattern [RBRBRWRBRGRGRBR](/stripes/rbrbrwrbrgrgrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Drummond** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
