A tartan of the [Drummond of Perth](/families/drummond-of-perth/) family.
Its design is pattern [RWBGGRBWW](/stripes/rwbggrbww/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Perthshire pattern with Royal Stewart elements, reputedly worn as a cloak by Bonnie Prince Charlie during the 1745 rebellion.

The **Drummond of Perth** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
