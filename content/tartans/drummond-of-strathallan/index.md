A tartan of the [Drummond of Strathallan](/families/drummond-of-strathallan/) family.

Adopted by the 6th Earl of Airlie on his 1812 marriage to Clementina Drummond; also known as Ogilvie.

The **Drummond of Strathallan** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
