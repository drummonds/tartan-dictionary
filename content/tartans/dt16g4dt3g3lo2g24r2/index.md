The **St Andrews Links** tartan is one sett proportion (`dt16g4dt3g3lo2g24r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BGBGYGR](/stripes/bgbgygr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1997**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
