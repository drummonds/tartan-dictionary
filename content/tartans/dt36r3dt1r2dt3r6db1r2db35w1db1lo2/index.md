The **Kirkcaldy Tartan Army** tartan is one sett proportion (`dt36r3dt1r2dt3r6db1r2db35w1db1lo2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BRBRBRBRBWBY](/stripes/brbrbrbrbwby/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2006**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
