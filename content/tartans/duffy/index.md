Designed by G. Duffy and kiltmaker Connell Reid; colours reflect the Duffy crest—yellow lion on green, outlined black.

The **Duffy** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
