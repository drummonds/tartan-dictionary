Worn by Peregrine, 2nd Viscount Dunblane, in a 1729 portrait at Hornby Castle, Yorkshire.

The **Dunblane** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
