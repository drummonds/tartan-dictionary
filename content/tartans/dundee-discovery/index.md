Asymmetric corporate sett designed 1996 by Keith Lumsden for Dundee District Council, home of Scott's RRS Discovery.

The **Dundee Discovery** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
