One of Wilson's designs named after a town, possibly reflecting the order's origin or purchaser.

The **Durham** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
