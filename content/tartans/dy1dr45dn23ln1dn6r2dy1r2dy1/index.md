The **Arbroath Smokie** tartan is one sett proportion (`dy1dr45dn23ln1dn6r2dy1r2dy1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [YRBWBRYRY](/stripes/yrbwbryry/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
