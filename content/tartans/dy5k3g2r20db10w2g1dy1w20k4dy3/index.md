The **MacCulloch Dress (Name)** tartan is one sett proportion (`dy5k3g2r20db10w2g1dy1w20k4dy3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GKGRBWGGWKG](/stripes/gkgrbwggwkg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2000**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
