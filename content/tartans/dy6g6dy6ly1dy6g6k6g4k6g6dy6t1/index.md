The **Buchanan Hunting (Scott Adie)** tartan is one sett proportion (`dy6g6dy6ly1dy6g6k6g4k6g6dy6t1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BGGKGKGGYGGG](/stripes/bggkgkggyggg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1850**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
