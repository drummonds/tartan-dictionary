Its design is pattern [GGGKBKGKGKGGGKW](/stripes/gggkbkgkgkgggkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for the Maryland Irish/Gaelic police society, with green for Ireland's provinces, blue thin-blue-line striping, and Calvert-arms gold borders.

The **Eastern Shore Police Emerald Society** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
