A tartan of the [Edinburgh](/families/edinburgh/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGBBGBGWKW](/stripes/rgbbgbgwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

One of several dress tartans by Edinburgh kiltmaker Hugh Macpherson, intended for dancing and informal occasions.

The **Edinburgh Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
