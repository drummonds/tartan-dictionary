A tartan of [Clan Edinburgh](/clan/edinburgh/).
Its design is pattern [BRBRWRBR](/stripes/brbrwrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Based on the Drummond tartan, designed by Kinloch Anderson in Edinburgh Marketing's corporate navy, red and white.

The **Edinburgh Marketing** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
