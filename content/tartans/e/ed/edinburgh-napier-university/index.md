A tartan of [Clan Edinburgh](/clan/edinburgh/).

Its design is pattern [BWBWKGRY](/stripes/bwbwkgry/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Kinloch Anderson in 2009, based on the Clan Napier sett with colours from the university's coat of arms.

The **Edinburgh Napier University** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
