A tartan of [Clan Eidart](/clan/eidart/).

Its design is pattern [BRBRBRBBWBWB](/stripes/brbrbrbbwbwb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Trade tartan for The Scotch House, recorded by the Scottish Tartans Society between 1970 and 1990.

The **Eidart Scotch House** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
