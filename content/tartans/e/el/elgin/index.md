A tartan of the [Elgin](/families/elgin/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRBWKWY](/stripes/brbwkwy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Recorded by the Scottish Tartans Society between 1970 and 1990; district tartan for Elgin.

The **Elgin** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
