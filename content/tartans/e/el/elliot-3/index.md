A tartan of the [Elliot](/families/elliot/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBGBGB](/stripes/bbgbgb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Border clan tartan, first recorded by H. Whyte in 1906, uniquely coloured in maroon and blue.

The **Elliot** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
