Its design is pattern [GGGGGGWBGBGKR](/stripes/ggggggwbgbgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Matt Newsome for Americans descended from Ellis Island immigrants, commemorating National Tartan Day's 10th anniversary.

The **Ellis Island** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
