A tartan of the [Ellis](/families/ellis/) family.
Its design is pattern [KGKBKWKWKWKWKWKWKBKGKW](/stripes/kgkbkwkwkwkwkwkwkbkgkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan (Elias, Bellis, Bayliss) woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill, with differing warp and weft creating vertical stripes.

The **Ellis** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
