Corporate tartan based on Farquharson, honouring one of the company's founders.

The **Encyclopaedia Britannica** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
