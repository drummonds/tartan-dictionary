Its design is pattern [BBYGYYGYBBBYGYYGYBBBYGYYGYYGYYGYB](/stripes/bbygyygybbbygyygybbbygyygyygyygyb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Edinburgh Woollen Mills for the 1988 Korea Olympics, raising funds for the British Equestrian Olympic Appeal.

The **Equorian Olympic Commemorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
