A tartan of the [Erskine](/families/erskine/) family.

Its design is pattern [KRKRKR](/stripes/krkrkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


The **Erskine** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
