Its design is pattern [GWGWGW](/stripes/gwgwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Green variant of the Erskine clan tartan, recorded by the Scottish Tartans Society between 1970 and 1990.

The **Erskine** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
