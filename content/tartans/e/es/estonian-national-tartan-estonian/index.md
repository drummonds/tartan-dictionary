Its design is pattern [BGRGBKBKWKBKBKWKBK](/stripes/bgrgbkbkwkbkbkwkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2005 by Kirsty Anderson of the MacNaughton Group, Perth; colours taken from the Estonian national flag.

The **Estonian National Tartan Estonian** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
