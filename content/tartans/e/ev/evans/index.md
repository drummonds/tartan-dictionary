A tartan of the [Evans](/families/evans/) family.
Its design is pattern [RKBKBKBKBKW](/stripes/rkbkbkbkbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan (Ifan, Bevan, Jevons, Heavens), woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft for a vertical stripe.

The **Evans** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
