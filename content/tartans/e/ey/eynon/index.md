Its design is pattern [KGGKGKGKGKKKGKK](/stripes/kggkgkgkgkkkgkk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan (also Beynon), woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill; asymmetric warp/weft yields a predominant vertical stripe.

The **Eynon** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
