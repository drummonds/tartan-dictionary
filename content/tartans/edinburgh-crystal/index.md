Its design is pattern [BWKBR](/stripes/bwkbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Corporate tartan for Edinburgh Crystal, designed by employee Sandra Campbell before 1997.

The **Edinburgh Crystal** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
