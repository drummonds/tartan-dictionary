Designed by Kinloch Anderson in 2009, based on the Clan Napier sett with colours from the university's coat of arms.

The **Edinburgh Napier University** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
