Welsh surname tartan (Elias, Bellis, Bayliss) woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill, with differing warp and weft creating vertical stripes.

The **Ellis** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
