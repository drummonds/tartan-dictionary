Its design is pattern [GGGGYGGGGGGKRKG](/stripes/ggggyggggggkrkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Inspired by Ontario's Provincial Coat of Arms; colours drawn from its maple leaves, St George's cross, and heraldic animals.

The **Ensign of Ontario** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
