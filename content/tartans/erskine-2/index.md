Its design is pattern [BWBWBW](/stripes/bwbwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

One of several dress tartans by Edinburgh kiltmaker Hugh Macpherson, white substituted for a ground colour for dancing.

The **Erskine** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
