Renfrewshire clan tartan from the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum, nearly identical to Cunningham but lacking its white stripe.

The **Erskine** tartan groups 11 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
