A tartan of the [Falkirk](/families/falkirk/) family.
Its design is pattern [KWKWKWYGR](/stripes/kwkwkwygr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Jim McGeorge's 1989 competition-winning design for Falkirk, its colours echoing the ancient Falkirk cloth, foundries, waterways and crossroads.

The **Falkirk** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
