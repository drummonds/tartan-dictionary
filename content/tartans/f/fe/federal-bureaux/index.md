Its design is pattern [BRBWBW](/stripes/brbwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Kinloch Anderson in 1988 as "S.C.O.T.S."; later adopted for the FBI pipe band's kilts.

The **Federal Bureaux** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
