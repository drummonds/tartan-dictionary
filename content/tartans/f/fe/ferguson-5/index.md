Its design is pattern [GBRKGK](/stripes/gbrkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Ferguson of Balquhidder, named by D.C. Stewart to distinguish it from the Ferguson of Athol sett.

The **Ferguson** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
