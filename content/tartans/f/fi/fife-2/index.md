A tartan of the [Fife](/families/fife/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKGKRK](/stripes/gkgkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Created for the 1889 wedding of Princess Louise to Alexander Duff, first Duke of Fife; sett differs slightly from the modern district version.

The **Fife** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
