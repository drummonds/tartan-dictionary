Its design is pattern [WGRKWGRKRGGWKRRW](/stripes/wgrkwgrkrggwkrrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed c.1805-15 by Archibald Farquharson of Finzean to promote his claim to the Clan Farquharson chiefship, based on the Chattan Chief's sett.

The **Finzean's Fancy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
