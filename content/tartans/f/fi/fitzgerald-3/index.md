A tartan of the [Fitzgerald](/families/fitzgerald/) family.

Its design is pattern [RBRBKBWBW](/stripes/rbrbkbwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Variation of Robertson designed by Robert P. Fitzgerald of Philadelphia, first of four Fitzgerald tartans.

The **Fitzgerald** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
