A tartan of the [Fitzpatrick](/families/fitzpatrick/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGGGGKBKG](/stripes/gggggkbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Irish family tartan, originating from Waterford Mill before arriving at Pendleton Mill late 19th century.

The **Fitzpatrick** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
