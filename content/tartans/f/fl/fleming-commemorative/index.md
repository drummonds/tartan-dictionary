A tartan of [Clan Fleming](/clan/fleming/).

Its design is pattern [BKBKBKBKGKBKBKW](/stripes/bkbkbkbkgkbkbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Created for Scotland Flanders 2002 as a cultural exchange product.

The **Fleming Commemorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
