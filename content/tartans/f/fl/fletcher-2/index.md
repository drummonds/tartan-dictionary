A tartan of the [Fletcher](/families/fletcher/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKRGKGRKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkrgkgrkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Worn by all Scottish Fletchers; legend ties the clan to first settling Glen Orchy.

The **Fletcher** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
