A tartan of [Clan Fletcher](/clan/fletcher/).
Its design is pattern [BKBKRGR](/stripes/bkbkrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Distinguished by a red stripe replacing the usual black; Fletchers were arrow makers tied to Stewarts, Campbells, and MacGregors.

The **Fletcher C** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
