Its design is pattern [BGBKBR](/stripes/bgbkbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Tribute to Roy Williamson, based on Gunn tartan with cornflower blue and green for bluebell and thistle.

The **Flower of Scotland MINI** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
