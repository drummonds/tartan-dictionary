A tartan of the [Forbes of Druminnor](/families/forbes-of-druminnor/) family.

Its design is pattern [BYBBGGBWBGBBYBYB](/stripes/bybbggbwbgbbybyb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on an old rug owned by Hon. Peggy Forbes Semphill; sample woven by A. Stewart, 1968.

The **Forbes of Druminnor** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
