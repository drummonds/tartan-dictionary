A tartan of the [Forrester](/families/forrester/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRGGGRBW](/stripes/brgggrbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

General clan tartan adopted in 1987 for all bearing the Forrester name.

The **Forrester** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
