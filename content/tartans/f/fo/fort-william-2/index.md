Its design is pattern [GWYWKWKGKWK](/stripes/gwywkwkgkwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Recorded in the pattern books of Wilson's of Bannockburn, dating to at least 1819.

The **Fort William** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
