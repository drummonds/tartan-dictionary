Its design is pattern [WBWGWBWBWBWRWBWGWBW](/stripes/wbwgwbwbwbwrwbwgwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2007 from old earth religions, its colours represent the four elements, directions, and central spirit.

The **Four Quarters** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
