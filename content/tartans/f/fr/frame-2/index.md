Its design is pattern [GGRGGGRGRGRG](/stripes/ggrgggrgrgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed c.1982 by Donald B. Frame of Minnesota, combining British, Scottish and US flag colours.

The **Frame** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
