A tartan of the [Franconian](/families/franconian/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBGBGBGBGBRBGBGBGBGBGBBBGRGWGRGB](/stripes/bbgbgbgbgbrbgbgbgbgbgbbbgrgwgrgb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 1997 by Franconia's Highland Circle whisky drinkers, produced by Hugh MacPherson of Edinburgh.

The **Franconian** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
