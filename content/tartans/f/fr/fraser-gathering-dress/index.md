A tartan of [Clan Fraser](/clan/fraser/).
Its design is pattern [GWGBGGGBR](/stripes/gwgbgggbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1997 by Polly Wittering and Lady Saltoun for the Castle Fraser Gathering, for all Frasers.

The **Fraser Gathering Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
