A tartan of the [Fraser](/families/fraser/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGGGBGBGGGW](/stripes/rgggbgbgggw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting Fraser sett substitutes brown for the Clan red; designed by the Sobieski Stuart brothers for Lord Lovat's Inverness and Nairn militia.

The **Fraser Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
