A tartan of the [Fraser](/families/fraser/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBGBWBWBGBR](/stripes/bbgbwbwbgbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting Fraser variant with brown replacing the clan sett's red; designed by the Sobieski Stuart brothers for Lord Lovat's Inverness and Nairn militia.

The **Fraser Hunting Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
