A tartan of the [Fraser of Lovat](/families/fraser-of-lovat/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRBGRWRGRBRB](/stripes/brbgrwrgrbrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Fraser of Lovat clan tartan, recorded in D.W. Stewart's 1893 "Old and Rare Scottish Tartans."

The **Fraser of Lovat** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
