A tartan of the [Fraser](/families/fraser/) family.
Its design is pattern [RBRGRW](/stripes/rbrgrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Fraser** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
