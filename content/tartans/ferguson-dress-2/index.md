Dress variant of the Ferguson clan tartan, with dark blue (the correct colour) rather than black.

The **Ferguson Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
