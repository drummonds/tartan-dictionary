A tartan of the [Forbes of Druminnor](/families/forbes-of-druminnor/) family.

The **Forbes of Druminnor** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
