A tartan of [Clan Forbes](/clan/forbes/).
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGKWKGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgkwkgkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Forbes** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
