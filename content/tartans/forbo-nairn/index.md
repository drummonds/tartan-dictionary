Corporate tartan for Forbo Nairn Ltd, based on Nairn sett, honouring Michael Nairn's 1843 Kirkcaldy linoleum factory.

The **Forbo Nairn** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
