Its design is pattern [RBWBWYWWGW](/stripes/rbwbwywwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2004 by Arthur Mackie for Forfar, county town of Angus; colours drawn from the burgh's coat of arms.

The **Forfar** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
