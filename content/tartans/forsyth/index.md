Closely resembles Leslie tartan with white replacing yellow; documented in Jeffrie's 1918 family history.

The **Forsyth** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
