A tartan of [Clan Fraser](/clan/fraser/).

Dress tartan, probably designed for Highland dancing.

The **Fraser Arisaid** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
