A tartan of the [Galloway](/families/galloway/) family.

Its design is pattern [RBKR](/stripes/rbkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Galloway district 'everyday' sett, designed by Hannay and woven by Cree Mills, Newton-Stewart, c.1950.

The **Galloway** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
