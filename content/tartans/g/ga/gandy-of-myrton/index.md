A tartan of the [Gandy of Myrton](/families/gandy-of-myrton/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWKBWR](/stripes/bwkbwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for Professor Gandy as chief of the Gandys of Myrton; alludes to Earl of Cawdor tartan and the family arms.

The **Gandy of Myrton** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
