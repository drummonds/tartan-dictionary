Its design is pattern [RWRWKW](/stripes/rwrwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed for the film "Gangs of New York," evoking 1860s men's fashion of checks and frock coats.

The **Gangs of New York Fashion Check** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
