A tartan of [Clan Gayre](/clan/gayre/).
Its design is pattern [BGKWBWKGKRGWGRKG](/stripes/bgkwbwkgkrgwgrkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Taken from an arisaidh; differs from the count registered with Lord Lyon.

The **Gayre Arisaidh** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
