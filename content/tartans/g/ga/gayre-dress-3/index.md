A tartan of the [Gayre](/families/gayre/) family.
Its design is pattern [KWGWGRKWGWGWKGW](/stripes/kwgwgrkwgwgwkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress version of Gayre clan tartan; one of five recorded Gayre setts.

The **Gayre Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
