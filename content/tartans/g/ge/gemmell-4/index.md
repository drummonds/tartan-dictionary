A tartan of the [Gemmell](/families/gemmell/) family.

Its design is pattern [WKWKWKGKGKBWRW](/stripes/wkwkwkgkgkbwrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 2001 in traditional colours; copyright held by Thomas Kempsill Gemmell and Davina Creighton Gemmell.

The **Gemmell** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
