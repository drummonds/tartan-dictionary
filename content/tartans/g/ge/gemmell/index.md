A tartan of the [Gemmell](/families/gemmell/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBBBKGKGKBKBKB](/stripes/bbbbkgkgkbkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2001 for Thomas Gemmell, Dumfries; based on the 42nd sett honouring his father's WWII Military Medal.

The **Gemmell** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
