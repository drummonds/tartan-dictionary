Its design is pattern [GKGKGKWR](/stripes/gkgkgkwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorates Georgia's founding, blending Black Watch (Oglethorpe's Highland Company) and MacIntosh red; prominent at Stone Mountain Highland Games.

The **Georgia** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
