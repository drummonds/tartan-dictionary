Its design is pattern [BKYKBRKBKBKW](/stripes/bkykbrkbkbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 2006 by Rocky Roeger to honor German Americans; colors represent the American and German flags.

The **German American** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
