A tartan of the [Gillies](/families/gillies/) family.

Its design is pattern [GWGWKWGWKR](/stripes/gwgwkwgwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Clan name associated with Badenoch and the Hebrides, meaning 'servant of Jesus'.

The **Gillies Blue Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
