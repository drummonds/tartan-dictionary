A tartan of [Clan Glasgow](/clan/glasgow/).

Its design is pattern [BKBKBKBKBKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkbkbkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on Black Watch, marking 1992 merger of Westbourne (purple) and Glasgow Academy (blue) schools.

The **Glasgow Academy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
