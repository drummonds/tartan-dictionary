A tartan of [Clan Glen](/clan/glen/).
Its design is pattern [GYRGYGYGYGYGK](/stripes/gyrgygygygygk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Pre-1800 hand-spun, hand-woven fragment from Glen Affric peat, likely late 17th-century, with red, black indigo-dyed green stripes.

The **Glen Affric Fragment Artefact** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
