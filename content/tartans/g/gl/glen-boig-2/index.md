A tartan of [Clan Glen](/clan/glen/).
Its design is pattern [GBGGG](/stripes/gbggg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Modern district-style design named for Scottish connection, not a historic "use and wont" district tartan.

The **Glen Boig** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
