A tartan of [Clan Glen](/clan/glen/).

Its design is pattern [BGBGBRBG](/stripes/bgbgbrbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Modern district-style design using a Scottish-sounding name, lacking the historical "use and wont" of genuine district tartans.

The **Glen Erin** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
