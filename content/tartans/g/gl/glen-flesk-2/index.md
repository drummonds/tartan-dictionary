A tartan of [Clan Glen](/clan/glen/).

Its design is pattern [GWGWKWKWKWYWY](/stripes/gwgwkwkwkwywy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 1997 by Lochcarron for The Check Trading Co, Tokyo; based on the Burns Check.

The **Glen Flesk** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
