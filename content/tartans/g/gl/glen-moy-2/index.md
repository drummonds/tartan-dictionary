A tartan of [Clan Glen](/clan/glen/).
Its design is pattern [BWBRW](/stripes/bwbrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Trade tartan for the quiet, sheep-farming Glen Moy in the Angus glens, near Cortachy Castle.

The **Glen Moy** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
