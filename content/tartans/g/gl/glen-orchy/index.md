A tartan of [Clan Glen](/clan/glen/).
Its design is pattern [GKRGRBWRGRBRGK](/stripes/gkrgrbwrgrbrgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Glen Orchy district sett, dating from at least 1893, also associated with the MacIntyres who occupied part of the glen.

The **Glen Orchy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
