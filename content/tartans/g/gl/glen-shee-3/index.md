A tartan of [Clan Glen](/clan/glen/).
Its design is pattern [BGYGYGRG](/stripes/bgygygrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Possibly derived from a handmade Highland design procured for The Highland Society of London.

The **Glen Shee** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
