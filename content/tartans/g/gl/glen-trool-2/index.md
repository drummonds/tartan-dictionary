A tartan of [Clan Glen](/clan/glen/).
Its design is pattern [GRGBR](/stripes/grgbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Originated as a trade fashion sett, later adopted as a district tartan for Glen Trool in the Galloway Uplands.

The **Glen Trool** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
