Its design is pattern [BRWBWRGRWR](/stripes/brwbwrgrwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Brought from Glenaladale to Prince Edward Island in 1772, associated with MacDonald of Glenaladale.

The **Glenaladale** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
