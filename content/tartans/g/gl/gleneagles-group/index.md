Designed in 1989 for the Gleneagles Collection range of tartan goods.

The **Gleneagles Group** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
