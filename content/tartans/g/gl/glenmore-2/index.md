A tartan of the [Glenmore](/families/glenmore/) family.

Its design is pattern [WKBKWKGRKRW](/stripes/wkbkwkgrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


A Royal Stewart colour variation produced by many weaving mills.

The **Glenmore** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
