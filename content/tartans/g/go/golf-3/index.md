A tartan of the [GOLF](/families/golf/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBKRWKRKKWKBKWK](/stripes/kbkrwkrkkwkbkwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Created for Wonderland Publications' limited-edition book GOLF, capturing the spirit of the game.

The **GOLF** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
