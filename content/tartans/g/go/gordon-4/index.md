A tartan of the [Gordon](/families/gordon/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGGGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgggkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Government sett with a single yellow overstripe, chosen by the Duke of Gordon for his regiment.

The **Gordon 4** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
