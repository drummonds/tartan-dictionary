A tartan of the [Gordon of Esslemont](/families/gordon-of-esslemont/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGGGKBK](/stripes/ggggkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Gordon variant with three stripes, chosen by Esslemont when the Duke selected the single-stripe sett for his troops.

The **Gordon of Esslemont** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
