A tartan of [Clan Gordon (Duke of Gordon)](/clan/gordon/).

Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGGGGGGGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgggggggkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Three-stripe Gordon variant adopted by the Gordons of Esslemont, derived from Wilson's samples passed on by the Duke of Gordon.

The **Gordon VS** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
