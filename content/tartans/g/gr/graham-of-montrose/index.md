A tartan of the [Graham of Montrose](/families/graham-of-montrose/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBWKGK](/stripes/gbwkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Wilson's No. 64 'Abercrombie' pattern (c.1819); yellow variant becomes Breadalbane, red becomes MacCallum, white-striped version MacLaggan.

The **Graham of Montrose** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
