A tartan of the [Grampian](/families/grampian/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGBBRBBBRBBGR](/stripes/grgbbrbbbrbbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

District tartan reflecting the colours of the Grampian Mountains, introduced by MacNaughtons of Pitlochry in 1993.

The **Grampian** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
