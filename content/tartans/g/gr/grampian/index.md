A tartan of the [Grampian](/families/grampian/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBRBBGRG](/stripes/bbrbbgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


District tartan reflecting the colours of the Grampian Mountains, introduced by MacNaughtons of Pitlochry in 1993.

The **Grampian** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
