A tartan of the [Grant](/families/grant/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGRGKG](/stripes/bkbkbkgrgkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Adopted from the Black Watch around 1730 by clan cadets serving as Black Watch officers.

The **Grant Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
