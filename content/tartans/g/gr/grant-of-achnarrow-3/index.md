A tartan of the [Grant of Achnarrow](/families/grant-of-achnarrow/) family.
Its design is pattern [WBGBWKWBGWBW](/stripes/wbgbwkwbgwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

A deliberate Lochcarron mis-weave of Grant of Achnarrow (1983, count misread by Phil Smith); folded into the one Grant of Achnarrow tartan as a member sett.

The **Grant of Achnarrow** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
