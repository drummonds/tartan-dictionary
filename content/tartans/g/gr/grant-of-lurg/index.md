A tartan of the [Grant of Lurg](/families/grant-of-lurg/) family.

Its design is pattern [BRGRBR](/stripes/brgrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Pre-1859 family plaid owned by the Dunbars, originally unidentified, now attributed to Grant of Lurg.

The **Grant of Lurg** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
