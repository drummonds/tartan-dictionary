A tartan of [Clan Grant](/clan/grant/).

Its design is pattern [BBRGRGRBRWRBRBR](/stripes/bbrgrgrbrwrbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Ordered 1819 by Patrick Grant of Redcastle, Clan Grant chief; also adopted by the Drummonds.

The **Grant or New Bruce** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
