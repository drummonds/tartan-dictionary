A tartan of the [Grassi](/families/grassi/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBRKBKBKRKBKBK](/stripes/bbrkbkbkrkbkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Commemorates the 2009 wedding of Benjamin Grassi and Lorraine Porter Vaughan; colours match bridal bouquet and venue decor.

The **Grassi** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
