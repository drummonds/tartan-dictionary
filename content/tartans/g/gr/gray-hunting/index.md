A tartan of the [Gray](/families/gray/) family.
Its design is pattern [KGRBRBRBGWK](/stripes/kgrbrbrbgwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting variant of the Gray family tartan; an earlier Gray tartan was designed by Mrs G. Gray.

The **Gray Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
