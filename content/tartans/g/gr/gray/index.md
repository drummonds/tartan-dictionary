A tartan of the [Gray](/families/gray/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGRGRGRBRBRGRGRGBR](/stripes/bgrgrgrbrbrgrgrgbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Gray family sept of Clans Stewart or Sutherland; northern Grays held lands at Skibo, Sordell and Ardinish.

The **Gray** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
