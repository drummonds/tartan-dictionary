Its design is pattern [BRKBYBRBY](/stripes/brkbybrby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Worn by the Greater St Louis Firefighters Highland Guard in memory of Firefighter-Paramedic Ryan Hummert, killed in the line of duty 2008; red, black and gold are traditional fire colours, charcoal grey for smoke.

The **Greater St. Louis Firefighters** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
