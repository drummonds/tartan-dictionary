A tartan of the [Greenshields](/families/greenshields/) family.
Its design is pattern [KGGGGGKBKGGGK](/stripes/kgggggkbkgggk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Peter MacDonald in 1997 for Alan Greenshields of Glasgow, based on MacNichol with MacLeod yellow.

The **Greenshields** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
