Its design is pattern [BGYGBRBR](/stripes/bgygbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1996 by Lochcarron for Tartan & Tweeds of Gretna Green, based on the Johnstone clan tartan.

The **Gretna** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
