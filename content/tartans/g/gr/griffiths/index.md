A tartan of [Clan Griffiths](/clan/griffiths/).
Its design is pattern [YBBBBBRBBBY](/stripes/ybbbbbrbbby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill, with differing warp and weft creating a predominant vertical stripe.

The **Griffiths** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
