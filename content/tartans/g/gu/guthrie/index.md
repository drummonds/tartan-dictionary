A tartan of the [Guthrie](/families/guthrie/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKRKRKBRBKRKRKGK](/stripes/gkrkrkbrbkrkrkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan tartan from the Barony of Guthrie in Angus; name possibly derived from Scandinavian prince Guthrum.

The **Guthrie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
