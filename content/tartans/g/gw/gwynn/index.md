A tartan of the [Gwynn](/families/gwynn/) family.

Its design is pattern [KGKGKGKR](/stripes/kgkgkgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Gwynn family tartan designed 2003 by Paul Gwynn of Texas, based on the Welsh national with added family colours.

The **Gwynn** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
