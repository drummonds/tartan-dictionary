The **Pike (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`g10k3g3k20dp3k5g3k20g3k3g10`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GKGKBKGKGKG](/stripes/gkgkbkgkgkg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
