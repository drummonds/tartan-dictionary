The **Rotary International** tartan is one sett proportion (`g15r3t15r8g15ly2db4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYGRBRG](/stripes/bygrbrg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1993**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
