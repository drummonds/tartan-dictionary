The **Copar a'Beannichte (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`g20g6dg15dt5dg2dt15o4dt10r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GGBGBRBRBRBGBGGG](/stripes/ggbgbrbrbrbgbggg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2004**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
