The **Crantock** tartan is one sett proportion (`g24p3g3p3g3p7dg20r3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GBGBGBGR](/stripes/gbgbgbgr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
