The **Scottish Canals (Corporate)** tartan is one sett proportion (`g2db1db29db2g9db26ly2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBBGBYBGBBBG](/stripes/bbbgbybgbbbg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2001**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
