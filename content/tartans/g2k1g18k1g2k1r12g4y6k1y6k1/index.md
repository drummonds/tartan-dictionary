The **MacMillan Ancient** tartan is one sett proportion (`g2k1g18k1g2k1r12g4y6k1y6k1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GKGKGKRGYKYK](/stripes/gkgkgkrgykyk/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
