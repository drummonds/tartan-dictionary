The **Dogwood** tartan is one sett proportion (`g3dr12g12lt5dr1ln25g2dr1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GRGRRWGR](/stripes/grgrrwgr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
