The **MacGiboney Dress** tartan is one sett proportion (`g5lb5t1lb1ly1lb1t1lb5g5w1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GWBWYWBWGW](/stripes/gwbwywbwgw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1999**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
