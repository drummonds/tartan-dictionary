Created c.1857 for the Glasgow Celtic Society, founded to promote Gaelic culture and which codified shinty rules in 1879.

The **Glasgow Celtic Society** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
