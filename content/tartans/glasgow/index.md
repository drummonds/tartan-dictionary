Glasgow district tartan from the Sindex cards, now woven by House of Edgar in their Old and Rare collection.

The **Glasgow** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
