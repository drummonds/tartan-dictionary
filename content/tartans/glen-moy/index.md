Trade tartan for the quiet, sheep-farming Glen Moy in the Angus glens, near Cortachy Castle.

The **Glen Moy** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
