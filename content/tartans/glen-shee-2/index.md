Possibly derived from a handmade Highland design procured for The Highland Society of London.

The **Glen Shee** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
