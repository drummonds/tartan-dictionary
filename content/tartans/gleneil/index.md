Spoof tartan designed by J B Renwick of Lochcarron for Gleneil Publishers' fictional clan book; Johnston with blue changed to red.

The **Gleneil** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
