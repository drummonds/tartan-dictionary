Designed by Lochcarron of Scotland in 1993 for Glengoyne Distillery.

The **Glengoyne Distillery** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
