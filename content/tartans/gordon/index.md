A tartan of [Clan Gordon (Duke of Gordon)](/clan/gordon/).

Pre-1819 Gordon family tartan, also called 'Old Huntly' and often used as Huntly District tartan.

The **Gordon** tartan groups 10 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
