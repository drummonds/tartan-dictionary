Its design is pattern [RGKBKBY](/stripes/rgkbkby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1993 by Marjorie Warren for North Carolina's Grandfather Mountain Highland Games, its colours symbolising the saltire, granite, meadows, and clan ceremonies.

The **Grandfather Mountain Games** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
