A tartan of the [Grant of Monymusk](/families/grant-of-monymusk/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGRGRGRBRKRGR](/stripes/rgrgrgrbrkrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Old tartan in the Cockburn Collection; combines elements of the Huntly group of tartans.

The **Grant of Monymusk** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
