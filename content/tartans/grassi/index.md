Commemorates the 2009 wedding of Benjamin Grassi and Lorraine Porter Vaughan; colours match bridal bouquet and venue decor.

The **Grassi** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
