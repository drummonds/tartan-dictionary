A tartan of [Clan Gwyn](/clan/gwyn/).
Its design is pattern [WKBKBKBKBKW](/stripes/wkbkbkbkbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan for Gwyn/Wynn, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft for a vertical stripe.

The **Gwyn** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
