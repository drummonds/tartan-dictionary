Gwynn family tartan designed 2003 by Paul Gwynn of Texas, based on the Welsh national with added family colours.

The **Gwynn** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
