Its design is pattern [GBWBWBWBWGWW](/stripes/gbwbwbwbwgww/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by James Hand for his marriage to Gail Wheatley; green for the Irish Hand name, blue and purple for Scottish ties.

The **Hand** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
