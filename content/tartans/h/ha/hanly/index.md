Its design is pattern [GGGGGGGGGWKGKGKG](/stripes/gggggggggwkgkgkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Created 2009 for the Irish-origin Hanly family, with colours from their coat of arms and a green RCAF-inspired stripe.

The **Hanly** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
