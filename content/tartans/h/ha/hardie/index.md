A tartan of the [Hardie](/families/hardie/) family.
Its design is pattern [GWGBRBGWGR](/stripes/gwgbrbgwgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2001 by Paul Hardie of Ecclesmachan for the Hardie family, who previously wore MacHardie tartan.

The **Hardie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
