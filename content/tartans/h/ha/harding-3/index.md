A tartan of the [Harding](/families/harding/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWRBRBBG](/stripes/bwrbrbbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Personal design uniting textile, silver, jewelry and leatherwork, inspired by Scottish craft heritage.

The **Harding** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
