A tartan of the [Harding](/families/harding/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBRBRWBWRBRBBG](/stripes/bbrbrwbwrbrbbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Created by Richard Scott Harding in 2006 as a personal tartan for his family.

The **Harding** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
