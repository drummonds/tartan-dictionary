A tartan of [Clan Hawick](/clan/hawick/).

Its design is pattern [GRGBWBGBWBGBWBGRGWRWBWBGBWBGBWBGR](/stripes/grgbwbgbwbgbwbgrgwrwbwbgbwbgbwbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Andrew Elliot as Hawick's official district tartan; a Cockburn variation, launched February 1996.

The **Hawick Corporate** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
