Clan Hay design from the Sobieski Stuart brothers' 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum.

The **Hay** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
