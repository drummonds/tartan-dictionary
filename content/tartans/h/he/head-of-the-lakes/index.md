Its design is pattern [BBWBWBGBWG](/stripes/bbwbwbgbwg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1996 to commemorate the 25th anniversary of Thunder Bay, Ontario, which adopted it.

The **Head of The Lakes** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
