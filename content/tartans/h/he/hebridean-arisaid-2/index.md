A tartan of [Clan Hebridean](/clan/hebridean/).
Its design is pattern [BKBWWBBKWKBBWWBK](/stripes/bkbwwbbkwkbbwwbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Modern blue dance-fashion variant of the Hebridean arisaid pattern.

The **Hebridean Arisaid** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
