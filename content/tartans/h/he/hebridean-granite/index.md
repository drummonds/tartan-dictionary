A tartan of [Clan Hebridean](/clan/hebridean/).
Its design is pattern [RWRKRKBWBKRKRW](/stripes/rwrkrkbwbkrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for a House of Edgar collection, evoking a gray morning suit.

The **Hebridean Granite** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
