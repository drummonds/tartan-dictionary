Its design is pattern [BKBGBKBBBKBBBKBBBKBGBKBW](/stripes/bkbgbkbbbkbbbkbbbkbgbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Scott Heddle for his 2003 wedding at St Magnus Cathedral, Orkney; adopted as the Heddle family tartan.

The **Heddle** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
