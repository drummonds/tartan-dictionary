Its design is pattern [BWBWGW](/stripes/bwbwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Produced 1968 by Anderson's of Edinburgh for Captain Charles Hepburn, from an existing design.

The **Hepburn** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
