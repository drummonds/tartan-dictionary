A tartan of [Clan Highland](/clan/highland/).

Its design is pattern [WRWRWKGYW](/stripes/wrwrwkgyw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Corporate tartan for McMullin Kilts, Aberdeenshire; greens, blues and burgundy evoke Highland glens, hills, burns and heathers.

The **Highland Glen** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
