A tartan of [Clan Highland](/clan/highland/).
Its design is pattern [BRKRKRKBWBWBKRKRKR](/stripes/brkrkrkbwbwbkrkrkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Colours reflect granite hills between Perth, Inverness and Royal Deeside; a universal tartan for those without clan connections.

The **Highland Granite Weavers** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
