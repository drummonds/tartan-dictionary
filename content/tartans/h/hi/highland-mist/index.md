A tartan of [Clan Highland](/clan/highland/).
Its design is pattern [BGBGYGBBYBGBRBBGBGYGBRBRBGBGYGBBGBRBBBBYBGBRB](/stripes/bgbgygbbybgbrbbgbgygbrbrbgbgygbbgbrbbbbybgbrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2002 by Claire Donaldson of House of Edgar for Harrisons of Hamilton's exclusive retail and kilt-hire use.

The **Highland Mist** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
