A tartan of [Clan Highland](/clan/highland/).
Its design is pattern [WKBBKBBKY](/stripes/wkbbkbbky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Lochcarron in 1996 for Highland Park Distillery, using the company's main logo colours.

The **Highland Park** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
