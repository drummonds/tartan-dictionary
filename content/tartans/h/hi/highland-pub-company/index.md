A tartan of [Clan Highland](/clan/highland/).
Its design is pattern [BKBRY](/stripes/bkbry/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Polly Wittering of The House of Edgar in 1996 for the Highland Pub Co, part of Scottish & Newcastle Breweries.

The **Highland Pub Company** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
