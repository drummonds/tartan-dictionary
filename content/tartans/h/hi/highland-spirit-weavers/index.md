A tartan of [Clan Highland](/clan/highland/).
Its design is pattern [KBKBKBKW](/stripes/kbkbkbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for T J Matthews of Blantyre; purple mountains, dark night and silver evoking Scottish freedom and pride.

The **Highland Spirit Weavers** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
