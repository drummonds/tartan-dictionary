A tartan of the [Hislop](/families/hislop/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKBKBYGKGKGKGKGBGBGKGKGKGKGYBKBKBG](/stripes/bkbkbygkgkgkgkgbgbgkgkgkgkgybkbkbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Commissioned 1992, based on Brodie tartan with elements from Hawick-area family setts; Hyslop spelling known in Galloway.

The **Hislop Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
