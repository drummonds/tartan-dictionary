A tartan of [Clan Holden](/clan/holden/).
Its design is pattern [RGKYKYKY](/stripes/rgkykyky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Reproduces the upholstery pattern from 1978 Holden Monaro GTS car seats.

The **Holden Monaro** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
