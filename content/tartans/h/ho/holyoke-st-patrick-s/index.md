Its design is pattern [RBGBGBGBGW](/stripes/rbgbgbgbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2002 by Gerald Healy and Ralf Hartwell Jnr for Holyoke, Massachusetts' St Patrick's Committee, blending US, Irish, and local school colours.

The **Holyoke St Patrick's** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
