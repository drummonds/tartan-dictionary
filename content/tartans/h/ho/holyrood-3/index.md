A tartan of the [Holyrood](/families/holyrood/) family.

Its design is pattern [KBYBWBBGBGW](/stripes/kbybwbbgbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Corporate tartan for Holyrood, the Queen's official Scottish residence; sample presented by Lochcarron Weavers in 1980.

The **Holyrood** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
