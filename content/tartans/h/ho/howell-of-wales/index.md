A tartan of the [Howell of Wales](/families/howell-of-wales/) family.

Its design is pattern [BRGBRBGBGBGBGGR](/stripes/brgbrbgbgbgbggr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Welsh surname tartan for Howells and Powell, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft for a vertical stripe.

The **Howell of Wales** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
