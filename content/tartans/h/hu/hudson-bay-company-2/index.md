A tartan of [Clan Hudson](/clan/hudson/).
Its design is pattern [RBGBKBGBKWKR](/stripes/rbgbkbgbkwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Corporate tartan designed by Gordon Kirkbright for the Hudson Bay Company, dating from 1984.

The **Hudson Bay Company** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
