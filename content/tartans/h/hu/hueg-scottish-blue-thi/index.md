A tartan of [Clan Hueg](/clan/hueg/).

Its design is pattern [RGKBGBGBWBGRKRW](/stripes/rgkbgbgbwbgrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed to celebrate the designer's love of Scotland and perceived similarities between Scots and Bavarians.

The **Hueg Scottish Blue Thi** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
