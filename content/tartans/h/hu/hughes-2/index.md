A tartan of the [Hughes](/families/hughes/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGBGBKW](/stripes/ggbgbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill; asymmetric warp/weft creates a vertical stripe.

The **Hughes** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
