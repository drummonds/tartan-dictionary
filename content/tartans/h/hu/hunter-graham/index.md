A tartan of [Clan Hunter](/clan/hunter/).
Its design is pattern [KBBRKGWGK](/stripes/kbbrkgwgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Combines Graham of Montrose and Hunter setts to commemorate the 2009 wedding of Adeline Hunter and Stuart Graham.

The **Hunter Graham** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
