A tartan of the [Hunter of Hunterston](/families/hunter-of-hunterston/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGWBRBGBGBGBRBWGRGY](/stripes/grgwbrbgbgbgbrbwgrgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Authorized by the Clan Society in 1985 for the Hunter of Hunterston chiefly line.

The **Hunter of Hunterston** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
