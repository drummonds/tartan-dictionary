A tartan of the [Hutcheson](/families/hutcheson/) family.
Its design is pattern [GYGYGRGBGR](/stripes/gygygrgbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2000 by Colin Hutcheson to mark his family's Borders textile career; based on Douglas.

The **Hutcheson** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
