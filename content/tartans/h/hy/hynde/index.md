A tartan of the [Hynde](/families/hynde/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBGBWBWBKBW](/stripes/gbgbwbwbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Hynde** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
