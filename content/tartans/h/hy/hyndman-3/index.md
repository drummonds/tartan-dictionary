A tartan of the [Hyndman](/families/hyndman/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGRBGWGBGGBBKB](/stripes/bgrbgwgbggbbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1992 for C.P. Hyndman; colours reflect his arms, Inniskilling Fusiliers ties, and Paisley-Ulster roots.

The **Hyndman** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
