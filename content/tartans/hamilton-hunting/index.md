A tartan of [Clan Hamilton](/clan/hamilton/).
Its design is pattern [BGBGW](/stripes/bgbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting variant of the Hamilton clan tartan.

The **Hamilton Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
