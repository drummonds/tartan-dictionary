Derivative of the Hannay tartan, created for J A Hannah of Balquhidder in 2005, substituting pale blue for white.

The **Hannay** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
