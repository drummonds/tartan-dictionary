Designed 2001 by Paul Hardie of Ecclesmachan for the Hardie family, who previously wore MacHardie tartan.

The **Hardie** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
