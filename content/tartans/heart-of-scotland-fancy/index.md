Designed in 2000 by Ruthven Milne of Piob Mhor, Blairgowrie; fancy tartan with a duplicated name.

The **Heart of Scotland Fancy** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
