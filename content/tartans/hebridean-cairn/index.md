Wedding-gray fashion design for House of Edgar; same sett as 6822 but accentuated by face-up/back-up weaving and a darker weft.

The **Hebridean Cairn** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
