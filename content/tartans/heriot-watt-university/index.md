Created in 1996 for the 30th anniversary of Heriot Watt University's charter.

The **Heriot Watt University** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
