Designed 2002 by Claire Donaldson of House of Edgar for Harrisons of Hamilton's exclusive retail and kilt-hire use.

The **Highland Mist** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
