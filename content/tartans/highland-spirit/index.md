Designed for T J Matthews of Blantyre; purple mountains, dark night and silver evoking Scottish freedom and pride.

The **Highland Spirit** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
