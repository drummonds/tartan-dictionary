Its design is pattern [BGBKRKRK](/stripes/bgbkrkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Border clan tartan first appearing in the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum, sharing the Grey Douglas scheme but differing in colour.

The **Home** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
