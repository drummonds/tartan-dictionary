Designed in 2000 by Colin Hutcheson to mark his family's Borders textile career; based on Douglas.

The **Hutcheson** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
