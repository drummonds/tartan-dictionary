Its design is pattern [GGKBGRKWGWKRGBKG](/stripes/ggkbgrkwgwkrgbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for the 150th anniversary of an 1840 Scots philanthropic society; colours reflect Illinois's flag, Chicago sports teams, and the St Andrew's cross.

The **Inches of Perth** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
