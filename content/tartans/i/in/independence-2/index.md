A tartan of the [Independence](/families/independence/) family.

Its design is pattern [KYBYKYWYWYKY](/stripes/kybykywywyky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 1996 by Donald Fraser for New Scots for Independence; launched at an SNP conference.

The **Independence** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
