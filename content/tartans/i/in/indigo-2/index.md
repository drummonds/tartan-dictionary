A tartan of the [Indigo](/families/indigo/) family.

Its design is pattern [BWKWKWBKBKBKBKBWKWKW](/stripes/bwkwkwbkbkbkbkbwkwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Corporate tartan for UK denim weaver Indigo Blue Works, designed to promote products in Japan; launched in Pitlochry.

The **Indigo** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
