A tartan of the [Innes](/families/innes/) family.

Its design is pattern [WKRKRKRGRBRKGKRW](/stripes/wkrkrkrgrbrkgkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Accepted in recent years as the tartan of the Inneses of Moray; recorded 1938.

The **Innes** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
