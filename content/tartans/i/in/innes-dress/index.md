A tartan of the [Innes](/families/innes/) family.
Its design is pattern [RWKGKWBWGWKRKRKW](/stripes/rwkgkwbwgwkrkrkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Inglis variation of MacIntyre, with yellow replacing the green stripe; from MacKinlay's collection.

The **Innes Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
