A tartan of [Clan Inverness](/clan/inverness/).

Its design is pattern [KBKRBKBW](/stripes/kbkrbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed c.1996 by Polly Wittering of the House of Edgar for Inverness Caledonian Thistle F.C.

The **Inverness Caledonian Thistle Football Club** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
