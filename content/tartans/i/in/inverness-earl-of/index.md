A tartan of [Clan Inverness](/clan/inverness/).

Its design is pattern [RBGBGBGR](/stripes/rbgbgbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Used by James Logan in 'The Scottish Gael' (1831) to demonstrate his thread-recording method; first published tartan illustration.

The **Inverness Earl of** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
