A tartan of the [Iowa](/families/iowa/) family.

Its design is pattern [RGGKGBKW](/stripes/rggkgbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed for Iowa with colours symbolising sky, fields, soil, snow, barns, earth and corn; state-adopted 2004.

The **Iowa** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
