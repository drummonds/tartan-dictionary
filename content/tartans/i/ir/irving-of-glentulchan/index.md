A tartan of the [Irving of Glentulchan](/families/irving-of-glentulchan/) family.
Its design is pattern [GWKWWWKWGR](/stripes/gwkwwwkwgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1987 by John Irving, combining Irvine and Malcolm setts for the Irving family of Glentulchan near Perth.

The **Irving of Glentulchan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
