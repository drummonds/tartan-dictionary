Its design is pattern [WGWYGGWGGWGWGGGWGWGGWGGYWGWY](/stripes/wgwyggwggwgwgggwgwggwggywgwy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Skye district tartan, designed via worldwide competition in 1992, its colours depicting the misty island's landscape.

The **Isle of Skye** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
