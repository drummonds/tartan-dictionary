Designed for the 150th anniversary of an 1840 Scots philanthropic society; colours reflect Illinois's flag, Chicago sports teams, and the St Andrew's cross.

The **Inches of Perth** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
