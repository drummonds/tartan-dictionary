MacIntyre variant with yellow replacing the green stripe; from James MacKinlay's collection, registered 1955.

The **Inglis** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
