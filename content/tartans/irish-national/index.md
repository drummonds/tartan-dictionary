One of a series of Irish District tartans designed by Polly Wittering of House of Edgar with John and Joan Nisbet.

The **Irish National** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
