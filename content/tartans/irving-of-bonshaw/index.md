A tartan of the [Irving of Bonshaw](/families/irving-of-bonshaw/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGBGG](/stripes/ggbgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed c. 1992 by the Scottish Tartans Society for Border Clan Irving of Bonshaw, distinguishing it from Clan Irvine.

The **Irving of Bonshaw** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
