A tartan of the [Jahore](/families/jahore/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWGBG](/stripes/bwgbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Reputedly presented to the Sultan of Jahore by Queen Victoria during his Balmoral visit, c.1890.

The **Jahore** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
