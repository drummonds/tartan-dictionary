A tartan of the [James of Wales](/families/james-of-wales/) family.
Its design is pattern [KYBBYBBBBBKBBKBKBBKBBBBBYBBY](/stripes/kybbybbbbbkbbkbkbbkbbbbbybby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan for Welsh, Jacob, Jago and variants, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft.

The **James of Wales** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
