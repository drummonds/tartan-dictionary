A tartan of the [Jenkins](/families/jenkins/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRGRGRBRBW](/stripes/brgrgrbrbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan (Jenks, Jenkin, Seincyn) woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill; differing warp and weft create predominant vertical stripe.

The **Jenkins** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
