A tartan of the [Jones](/families/jones/) family.

Its design is pattern [RWGGKBW](/stripes/rwggkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Peter MacDonald in 1997 for Mrs Ros Jones of Mull, for all of the name; charity fundraiser.

The **Jones** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
