A tartan of the [Jrgensen of Taasingee](/families/jrgensen-of-taasingee/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBGBBBBGBBBBGBBGBBW](/stripes/gbgbbbbgbbbbgbbgbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Danish family tartan with central green framed by autumn forest, sea, and violet sunset of Taasinge.

The **Jrgensen of Taasingee** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
