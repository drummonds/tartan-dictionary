Its design is pattern [BGKBGBYBGBKG](/stripes/bgkbgbybgbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Polly Wittering of House of Edgar for Aberdeen Junior Chamber of Commerce, for the 1995 Junior Chamber International Conference.

The **Junior Chamber International** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
