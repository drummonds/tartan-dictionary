Its design is pattern [WRBWGWBRWRBWYWBRW](/stripes/wrbwgwbrwrbwywbrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Jacobite-era tartan dating to c.1815, worn by clanless Scots as an alternative to district tartans.

The **Jacobite** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
