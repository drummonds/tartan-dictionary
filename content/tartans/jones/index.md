Welsh surname tartan for Jones and variants, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft for a vertical stripe.

The **Jones** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
