Designed by Polly Wittering of House of Edgar for Aberdeen Junior Chamber of Commerce, for the 1995 Junior Chamber International Conference.

The **Junior Chamber International** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
