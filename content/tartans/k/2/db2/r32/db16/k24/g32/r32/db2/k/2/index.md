The MacNaughton tartan is also recorded by William and Andrew Smith in their 'Authenticated Tartans of the Clans and Families of Scotland' (1850). Other works contain a commonly reproduced error. The tartan closely resembles the MacDuff, which may bear out the claim that the MacNaughtons were originally a Moray tribe transplanted by Malcolm IV. The MacNaughton tartan is worn by the 'Vale of Athol' pipe band. See products available Copyright © Blair Urquhart, Comrie, 2015 with 9 stripes"
