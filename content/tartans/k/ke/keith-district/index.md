A tartan of [Clan Keith](/clan/keith/).
Its design is pattern [BRBRBKGYGKBKBKBKGYGKBRBR](/stripes/brbrbkgygkbkbkbkgygkbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Councillor Linda Gorn for the Keith district, formalised by House of Edgar.

The **Keith District** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
