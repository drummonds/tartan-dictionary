A tartan of [Clan Keith](/clan/keith/).
Its design is pattern [GKGKGKBK](/stripes/gkgkgkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Personal tartan for Keith McCormick: blue for New Brunswick's Nashwaak salmon river, green for forest, black for his barrister's gown.

The **Keith McCormick** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
