A tartan of the [Kennedy](/families/kennedy/) family.
Its design is pattern [KGGGRGRGBKBKBKBGR](/stripes/kgggrgrgbkbkbkbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan tartan first recorded by MacIan in his 1847 "Clans of the Scottish Highlands," co-authored with James Logan.

The **Kennedy** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
