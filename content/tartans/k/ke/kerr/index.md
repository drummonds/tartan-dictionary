A tartan of the [Kerr](/families/kerr/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKGKGKRKRK](/stripes/gkgkgkrkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Border clan of supposed Viking-French descent; sett first appeared in the doubtful Vestiarium Scoticum.

The **Kerr** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
