A tartan of [Clan Kerry](/clan/kerry/).
Its design is pattern [YBGBKBGBKBGBKBY](/stripes/ybgbkbgbkbgbkby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

One of Polly Wittering's Irish district tartans for House of Edgar, with soft warm colours evoking the county.

The **Kerry Irish County** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
