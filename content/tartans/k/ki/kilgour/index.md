A tartan of the [Kilgour](/families/kilgour/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGBRBG](/stripes/bgbrbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Kilgour family sett recorded by Peter MacDonald in 1983; structurally similar to the Carrick-associated Kyle/Kile tartan.

The **Kilgour** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
