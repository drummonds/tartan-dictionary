A tartan of [Clan Kinloch](/clan/kinloch/).

Its design is pattern [KYKYKYKYGGGY](/stripes/kykykykygggy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Douglas Kinloch Anderson in 2002 as a fashion tartan for Japanese and Korean markets.

The **Kinloch Anderson Check** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
