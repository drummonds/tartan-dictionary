Its design is pattern [RKBKBKGKR](/stripes/rkbkbkgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorates the Danish Klappert family of Odsherred; colours evoke Nordic winter, sea, Danish origins, and Viking-Scottish ties.

The **Klappert Original** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
