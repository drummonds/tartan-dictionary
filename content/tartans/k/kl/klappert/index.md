Its design is pattern [BKBGRGBKB](/stripes/bkbgrgbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2011 for the Klapperts, a Danish family celebrating their Scottish affinity and Nordic-Viking heritage.

The **Klappert** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
