A tartan of the [Knox](/families/knox/) family.

Its design is pattern [GYWYKYWYBKYGY](/stripes/gywykywybkygy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on the MacFarlane, designed by Keith Lumsden of the Scottish Tartans Society for D L Knox of Lancashire.

The **Knox** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
