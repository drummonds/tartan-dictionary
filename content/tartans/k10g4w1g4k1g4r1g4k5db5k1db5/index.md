The **Lloyd of Dolobran (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`k10g4w1g4k1g4r1g4k5db5k1db5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKGRGKGWGK](/stripes/bkbkgrgkgwgk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1950**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
