The **Nicolson** tartan is one sett proportion (`k10r12g3r12k2r12g3r12g20r2k8b2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKRGRGRKRGRK](/stripes/bkrgrgrkrgrk/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
