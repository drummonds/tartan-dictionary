The **Cumming** tartan is one sett proportion (`k1dr1dg8dr1k6db1dr6dg6dr1k8dr1db1k1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [KBRKRGRBKRGRK](/stripes/kbrkrgrbkrgrk/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
