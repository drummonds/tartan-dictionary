The **Rob Roy** tartan is one sett proportion (`k1r1`) recorded at 8 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KR](/stripes/kr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1704**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
