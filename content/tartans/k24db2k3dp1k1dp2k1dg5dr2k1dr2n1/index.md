The **Braveheart Warrior** tartan is one sett proportion (`k24db2k3dp1k1dp2k1dg5dr2k1dr2n1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [KBKBKBKGRKRW](/stripes/kbkbkbkgrkrw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
