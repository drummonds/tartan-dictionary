The **Collister (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`k26n2k2n2k2n10w10n6db10o5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [KBKBKBWBBY](/stripes/kbkbkbwbby/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
