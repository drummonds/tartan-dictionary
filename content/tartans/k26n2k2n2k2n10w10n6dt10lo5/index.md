The **Collister (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`k26n2k2n2k2n10w10n6dt10lo5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBKBWBBYBBWBKBKBK](/stripes/bkbkbwbbybbwbkbkbk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2005**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
