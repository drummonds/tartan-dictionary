The **Buchan** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2db2r2k24r2dg6r6db2k6r2dg27r2k2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KBRKRGRBKRGRK](/stripes/kbrkrgrbkrgrk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
