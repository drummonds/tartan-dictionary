The **MacNicol D** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2r10dg2r10dg13r2k6b2k8r10dg2r10k2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KRGRGRKBKRGRK](/stripes/krgrgrkbkrgrk/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
