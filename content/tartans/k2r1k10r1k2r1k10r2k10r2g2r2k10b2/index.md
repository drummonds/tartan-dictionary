The **Hebridean** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2r1k10r1k2r1k10r2k10r2g2r2k10b2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BKRGRKRKRKRKRK](/stripes/bkrgrkrkrkrkrk/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
