The **Erskine (Black and White)** tartan is one sett proportion (`k2w1k9w9k1w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWKWKW](/stripes/kwkwkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1980**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
