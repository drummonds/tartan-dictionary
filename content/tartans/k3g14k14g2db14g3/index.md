The **MacKay** tartan is one sett proportion (`k3g14k14g2db14g3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GBGKGK](/stripes/gbgkgk/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
