The **Lendrum (B&W)** tartan is one sett proportion (`k7w6k1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWKW](/stripes/kwkw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1842**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
