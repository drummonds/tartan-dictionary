The **Langtree** tartan is one sett proportion (`k86n6k4ln3k3r3k3n22lt14k3lt6ln4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [KGKWKRKGRKRW](/stripes/kgkwkrkgrkrw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
