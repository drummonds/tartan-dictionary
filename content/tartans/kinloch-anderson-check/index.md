Designed by Douglas Kinloch Anderson in 2002 as a fashion tartan for Japanese and Korean markets.

The **Kinloch Anderson Check** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
