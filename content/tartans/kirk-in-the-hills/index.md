Created for Kirk in the Hills church, Bloomfield Hills, Michigan, marking its 50th anniversary in 1997.

The **Kirk in the Hills** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
