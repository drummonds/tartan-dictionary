Its design is pattern [GBRKWW](/stripes/gbrkww/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for general use; blue and white for the Saltire, red and yellow from William Kirkcaldy's shield.

The **Kirkcaldy** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
