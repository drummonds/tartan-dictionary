A tartan of the [Lachance](/families/lachance/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWGBGBG](/stripes/bwgbgbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorates the 2012 Queen's Diamond Jubilee Medal; colours drawn from the Lachance Arms.

The **Lachance** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
