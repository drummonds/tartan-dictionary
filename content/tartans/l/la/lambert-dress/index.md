A tartan of the [Lambert](/families/lambert/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGBRKGWGKRBGBK](/stripes/bgbrkgwgkrbgbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Charles Lambert via Scotweb Tartan Designer to celebrate his family's Irish ancestry.

The **Lambert Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
