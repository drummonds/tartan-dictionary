A tartan of the [Lamont](/families/lamont/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGW](/stripes/bkbkbkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan Lamont tartan, like Campbell of Argyll but with white lines on the green; dated 1810-15.

The **Lamont** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
