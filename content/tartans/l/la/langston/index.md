Its design is pattern [KGWGWGKGWGKRGRGRGR](/stripes/kgwgwgkgwgkrgrgrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 1999 by Blair Urquhart for LCDR Michael W. Langston of Greenville, USA; features teal accents.

The **Langston** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
