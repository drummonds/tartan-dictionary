A tartan of the [Langtree](/families/langtree/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBKWKBKBRKRW](/stripes/kbkwkbkbrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Stewart colour variant marketed by Selfridge's, London; designed and woven by Pendleton Woolen Mills, Oregon.

The **Langtree** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
