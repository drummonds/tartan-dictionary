Its design is pattern [KBGKGKGBGKBKGKBK](/stripes/kbgkgkgbgkbkgkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Family tartan for the Large family, created as a 2009 wedding gift.

The **Large** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
