A tartan of the [Lauder](/families/lauder/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBGKGR](/stripes/gbgkgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Possibly designed by Sir Thomas Dick Lauder, a friend of the Sobieski brothers; dates from 1842.

The **Lauder** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
