A tartan of the [Leach](/families/leach/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWKWGBKBW](/stripes/bwkwgbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Family tartan for the Leach name, historically given to physicians; wearable by all spelling variants.

The **Leach** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
