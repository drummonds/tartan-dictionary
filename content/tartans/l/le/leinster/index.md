A tartan of the [Leinster](/families/leinster/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGGGKGBKGKBKGR](/stripes/ggggkgbkgkbkgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

One of the Irish district tartans marking Scots-Irish cultural links; Leinster's principal city is Dublin.

The **Leinster** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
