A tartan of the [Leith](/families/leith/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBBBBBGBBBKB](/stripes/bkbbbbbgbbbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1995 for Tall Ships event; based on Robertson tartan honouring Leith shipbuilders Henry Robb, red line marking Claret Trade.

The **Leith** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
