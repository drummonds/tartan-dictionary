A tartan of the [Lennox](/families/lennox/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBBBWKW](/stripes/bbbbwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Purple dress variant of the ancient Lennox sett, derived from a 16th-century portrait of the Countess of Lennox.

The **Lennox Purple Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
