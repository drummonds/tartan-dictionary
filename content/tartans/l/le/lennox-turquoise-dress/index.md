A tartan of [Clan Lennox](/clan/lennox/).
Its design is pattern [WBWBWKW](/stripes/wbwbwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Turquoise dress variant of the ancient Lennox sett, derived from D.W. Stewart's reproduction of a lost 16th-century Countess of Lennox portrait.

The **Lennox Turquoise Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
