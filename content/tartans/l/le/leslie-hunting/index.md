A tartan of the [Leslie](/families/leslie/) family.

Its design is pattern [KGWKBR](/stripes/kgwkbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Worn by George, 14th Earl of Rothes (d.1841); shown by Smibert and W & A Smith (1850), resembles Duncan but with broad black.

The **Leslie Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
