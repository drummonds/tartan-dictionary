A tartan of the [Lewis](/families/lewis/) family.
Its design is pattern [BYGYGYBYGYGY](/stripes/bygygybygygy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan for Lewis and variants, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft for a vertical stripe.

The **Lewis** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
