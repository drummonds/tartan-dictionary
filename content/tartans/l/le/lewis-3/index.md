A tartan of the [Lewis](/families/lewis/) family.

Its design is pattern [RWRW](/stripes/rwrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dancer's tartan from House of Edgar's in-house collection, designed by Kirsty Anderson.

The **Lewis** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
