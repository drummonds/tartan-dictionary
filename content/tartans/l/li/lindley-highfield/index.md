Its design is pattern [BKGGWBG](/stripes/bkggwbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Family tartan of the Lindley-Highfields of Ballumbie Castle, colours drawn from the Head of Family's armorial bearings.

The **Lindley-Highfield** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
