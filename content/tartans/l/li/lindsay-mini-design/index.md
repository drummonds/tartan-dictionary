A tartan of [Clan Lindsay](/clan/lindsay/).

Its design is pattern [GBGBGBRBR](/stripes/gbgbgbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Recognisable by its distinctive colour; sett resembles Stewart of Athol but with dark blue replacing black.

The **Lindsay MINI Design** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
