A tartan of the [Little of Morton Rig Family/Clan](/families/little-of-morton-rig-family-clan/) family.

Its design is pattern [KWKWKBKBKY](/stripes/kwkwkbkbky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 1991 by Dr. Pat Little for the Clan Little Society, incorporating Wallace and Shepherd sett elements.

The **Little of Morton Rig Family/Clan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
