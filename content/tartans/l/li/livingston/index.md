Highland Livingston clan sett from Lismore and Western Argyll; resembles MacDonell of Keppoch.

The **Livingston** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
