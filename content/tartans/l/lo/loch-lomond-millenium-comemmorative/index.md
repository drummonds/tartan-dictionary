Its design is pattern [GGKBGBGBKGK](/stripes/ggkbgbgbkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Claire Donaldson for House of Edgar to commemorate the millennium; woven by Lochcarron.

The **Loch Lomond Millenium Comemmorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
