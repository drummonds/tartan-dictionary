Its design is pattern [GKRKGKRBWGWBRKGKRK](/stripes/gkrkgkrbwgwbrkgkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Pre-1800 district tartan from Wilson's pattern book, possibly a Napoleonic-era Fencibles design; specimen held at Fort William.

The **Lochaber** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
