A tartan of [Clan Lochaber](/clan/lochaber/).
Its design is pattern [BRBRBWB](/stripes/brbrbwb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

District tartan from Lochaber, home of Clan Cameron and Clanranald MacDonalds; recorded by 1797.

The **Lochaber Old** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
