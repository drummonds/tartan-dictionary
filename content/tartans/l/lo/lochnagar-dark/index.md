A tartan of [Clan Lochnagar](/clan/lochnagar/).
Its design is pattern [BRBBBRBRRR](/stripes/brbbbrbrrr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Black and grey design evoking Byron's "Dark Lochnagar," with purple for Royal links and "Red Granite" red.

The **Lochnagar Dark** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
