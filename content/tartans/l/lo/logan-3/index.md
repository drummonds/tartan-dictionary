A tartan of the [Logan](/families/logan/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKRGKBRBRBR](/stripes/gkrgkbrbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan tartan dated to 1831, also listed under Logan, possibly an alternative name for MacLennan.

The **Logan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
