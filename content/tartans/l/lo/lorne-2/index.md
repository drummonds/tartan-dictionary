A tartan of the [Lorne](/families/lorne/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKGKGKGKBKBKBKG](/stripes/bkgkgkgkbkbkbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Lorne** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
