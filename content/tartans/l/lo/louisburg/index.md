Its design is pattern [BGWB](/stripes/bgwb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1994 by Edith MacIntyre for the Nova Scotian seaside town; colours evoke fog, sea, sun, and sky.

The **Louisburg** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
