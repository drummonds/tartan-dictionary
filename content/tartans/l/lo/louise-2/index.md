A tartan of [Clan Louise](/clan/louise/).
Its design is pattern [BRGKGKGKBKBKBKG](/stripes/brgkgkgkbkbkbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Created for the 1871 wedding of the Marquis of Lorne to Princess Louise; companion to the Lorne sett.

The **Louise** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
