A tartan of the [Lowry](/families/lowry/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKBGBYB](/stripes/bkbgbyb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


One of three similar designs for Laurie, Lawrie and Lowry, by Peter MacDonald for Iain Laurie.

The **Lowry** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
