Its design is pattern [RWRGKGKGBW](/stripes/rwrgkgkgbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2002 by Linda Clifford for Christina Lyons' parents' golden anniversary, combining Irish greens with Croatian red and white.

The **Lyons** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
