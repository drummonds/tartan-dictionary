Its design is pattern [WKGRGKGKWKW](/stripes/wkgrgkgkwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Adopted by the combined Councils of Labrador in 2010 to celebrate the region's Scottish heritage.

The **Labrador** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
