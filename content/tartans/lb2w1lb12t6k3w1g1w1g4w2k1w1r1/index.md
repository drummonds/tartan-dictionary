The **Diana Princess of Wales Memorial** tartan is one sett proportion (`lb2w1lb12t6k3w1g1w1g4w2k1w1r1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RWKWGWGWKBWWW](/stripes/rwkwgwgwkbwww/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1997**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
