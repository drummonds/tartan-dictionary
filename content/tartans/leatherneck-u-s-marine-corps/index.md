Its design is pattern [GRGRGBGR](/stripes/grgrgbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Madam Leask and the Scottish Tartans Society for the U.S. Marine Corps.

The **Leatherneck U.S.Marine Corps** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
