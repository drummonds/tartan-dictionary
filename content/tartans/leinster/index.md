One of the Irish district tartans marking Scots-Irish cultural links; Leinster's principal city is Dublin.

The **Leinster** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
