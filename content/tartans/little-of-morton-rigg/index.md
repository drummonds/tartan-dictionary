A tartan of the [Little of Morton Rigg](/families/little-of-morton-rigg/) family.

Designed 1991 by Dr. Pat Little for the Clan Little Society, incorporating Wallace and Shepherd sett elements.

The **Little of Morton Rigg** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
