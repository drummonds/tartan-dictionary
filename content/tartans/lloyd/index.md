A tartan of [Clan Lloyd](/clan/lloyd/).

Welsh surname tartan (Lloyd, Floyd, Flood) woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill; asymmetric warp/weft creates a predominant vertical stripe.

The **Lloyd** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
