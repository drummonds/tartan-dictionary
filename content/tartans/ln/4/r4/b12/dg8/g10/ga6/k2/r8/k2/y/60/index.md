She used colours representing the various industries of the region; agriculture, salt mining, car building anf fishing. Azure blue symbolizes the sky and the water. The tartan was formally adopted by the Corporation of the County of Essex in 1984, and approved by the Leamington Council. The tartan is accredited by the Scottish Tartans Society.
