Earliest known date: 19th century The 'Dress' version of James Logan's 'Royal Stewart' which D.C.Stewart compared to 200 year old silk scarf with the remark, \"that the design had been well maintained\". In this pattern the blue is joined to the black and the yellow and white are of equal size. The red stripe on the white, not present here, signifies a 'Victoria' sett. The Dress Stewart tartan alongside its 'formal' partner, the Royal Stewart, is known throughout the world as a symbol of Scotland.
