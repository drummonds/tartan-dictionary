The **Lasting** tartan is one sett proportion (`ln2p7ln2b6ln2y4ln2p4ln2y4ln2g19ln2p7ln2p7ln2r19ln4r3b2r3ln2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [WBWBWYWBWYWGWBWBWRWRBRW](/stripes/wbwbwywbwywgwbwbwrwrbrw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
