The **Cornish, hunting** tartan is one sett proportion (`ln5k26y2dg24db7k3r3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RKKGYKW](/stripes/rkkgykw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
