The **Rennie** tartan is one sett proportion (`ln6k1g29k23p27g3p3g3p3g4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GBGBGBKGKW](/stripes/gbgbgbkgkw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
