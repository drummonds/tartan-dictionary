The **Kazakhstan Relic** tartan is one sett proportion (`lo5db5k3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBY](/stripes/bkby/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2000 BCE**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
