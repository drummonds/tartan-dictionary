Clan tartan dated to 1831, also listed under Logan, possibly an alternative name for MacLennan.

The **Logan and MacLennan** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
