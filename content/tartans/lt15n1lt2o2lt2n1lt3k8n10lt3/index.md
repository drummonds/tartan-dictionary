The **Annan** tartan is one sett proportion (`lt15n1lt2o2lt2n1lt3k8n10lt3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RGKRGRYRGR](/stripes/rgkrgryrgr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
