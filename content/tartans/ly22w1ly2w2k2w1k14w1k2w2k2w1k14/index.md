The **Thain, dress** tartan is one sett proportion (`ly22w1ly2w2k2w1k14w1k2w2k2w1k14`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[KWKWKWKWKWYWY](/stripes/kwkwkwkwkwywy/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1940**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
