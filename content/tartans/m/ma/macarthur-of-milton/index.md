A tartan of the [MacArthur of Milton](/families/macarthur-of-milton/) family.

Its design is pattern [GBGKBK](/stripes/gbgkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Older of two MacArthur setts, linking the clan with the Campbells.

The **MacArthur of Milton** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
