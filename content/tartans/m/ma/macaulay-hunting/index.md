A tartan of [Clan MacAulay](/clan/macaulay/).
Its design is pattern [GKWKGKGR](/stripes/gkwkgkgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting sett matching M'Intyre North's 1881 count from Logan; shortened form resembles MacGregor tartan.

The **MacAulay Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
