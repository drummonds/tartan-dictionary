A tartan of the [MacBean](/families/macbean/) family.

Its design is pattern [GWRBGBRWGWWWWWKR](/stripes/gwrbgbrwgwwwwwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress variant of MacBean, based on Scarlett's Meta MacBean with added black guards and red replacing black lines on alternate white bands.

The **MacBean Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
