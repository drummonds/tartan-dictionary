A tartan of [Clan MacBean](/clan/macbean/).

Its design is pattern [KWWWWWGWRBGBRWGWRBGBRWGWWWWWR](/stripes/kwwwwwgwrbgbrwgwrbgbrwgwwwwwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Clan tartan for MacBean/MacBain of Kinchyle, recorded by Lord Lyon as a minor variation on McIan's 1847 sett, reflecting Mackintosh links.

The **MacBean, Meta** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
