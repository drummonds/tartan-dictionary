Its design is pattern [BGBGBWBGBWBGBKB](/stripes/bgbgbwbgbwbgbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2000 for a McCall family wedding in Aberdeen; open to all of the name.

The **MacCall** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
