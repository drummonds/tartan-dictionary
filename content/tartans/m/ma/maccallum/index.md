A tartan of the [MacCallum](/families/maccallum/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBKGRGK](/stripes/kbkgrgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Argyllshire clan sett reconstructed circa 1832–1843 from elders' recollections, later found to deviate considerably from the recovered original.

The **MacCallum** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
