A tartan of the [MacConnell](/families/macconnell/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGBWGBGBGY](/stripes/bgbwgbgbgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on MacDonald Hunting without the black, for McConnell use alongside the MacDonald.

The **MacConnell** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
