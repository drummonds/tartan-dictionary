Its design is pattern [GKBKGKW](/stripes/gkbkgkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2007 by Keith McCormick of Canada for his family, Hebridean Scots and a sept of MacLaine of Lochbuie.

The **MacCormick** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
