A tartan of the [MacCulloch](/families/macculloch/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKGRBWGGWKG](/stripes/gkgrbwggwkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Peter MacDonald in 2000 for a US client; for all of the name MacCulloch and variants.

The **MacCulloch Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
