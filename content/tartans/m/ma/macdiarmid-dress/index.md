A tartan of the [MacDiarmid](/families/macdiarmid/) family.

Its design is pattern [RGKWKGWRW](/stripes/rgkwkgwrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress variant from Paton's collection (c.1830), housed at the Scottish Tartans Museum, Comrie.

The **MacDiarmid Dress** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
