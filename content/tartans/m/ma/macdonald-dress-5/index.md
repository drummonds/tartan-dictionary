A tartan of the [MacDonald](/families/macdonald/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRBRBRBRBRKWBWBRBWBWRKGRGRGRGRGKR](/stripes/brbrbrbrbrkwbwbrbwbwrkgrgrgrgrgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan MacDonald sett delivered to the Highland Society of London, 1815–1822, signed and sealed by the chief.

The **MacDonald Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
