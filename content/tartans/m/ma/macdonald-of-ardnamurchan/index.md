A tartan of the [MacDonald of Ardnamurchan](/families/macdonald-of-ardnamurchan/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKRKRKR](/stripes/gkrkrkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan tartan from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842), the sole recorded source for many tartans still used today.

The **MacDonald of Ardnamurchan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
