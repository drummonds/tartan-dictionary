A tartan of the [MacDonald of Clanranald](/families/macdonald-of-clanranald/) family.

Its design is pattern [BRBRBRKWGRGRG](/stripes/brbrbrkwgrgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Clan tartan distinguished by two white lines; sett from Lord Lyon records, matching Smith (1850) and Grant (1886).

The **MacDonald of Clanranald** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
