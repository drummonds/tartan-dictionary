A tartan of the [MacDonald of Denovan](/families/macdonald-of-denovan/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBBRBRKGRGBG](/stripes/bbbrbrkgrgbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1992 by Captain V.J. MacDonald-Evans for Lairds of Denovan; later opened to all Denovans and MacDonalds of Dunyveg.

The **MacDonald of Denovan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
