A tartan of the [MacDonald of Glenaladale](/families/macdonald-of-glenaladale/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWRBRWRGRBW](/stripes/bwrbrwrgrbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Said to have belonged to Alexander MacDonald of Glenaladale at Culloden; taken to Canada on emigration in 1772.

The **MacDonald of Glenaladale** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
