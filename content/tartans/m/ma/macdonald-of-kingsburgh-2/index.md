A tartan of the [MacDonald of Kingsburgh](/families/macdonald-of-kingsburgh/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGYRWGYGY](/stripes/rgyrwgygy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Recorded by D.W. Stewart from a relic worn by Prince Charles Edward Stuart in 1746.

The **MacDonald of Kingsburgh** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
