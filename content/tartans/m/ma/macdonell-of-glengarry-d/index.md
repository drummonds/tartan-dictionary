A tartan of the [MacDonell of Glengarry D](/families/macdonell-of-glengarry-d/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRBRBRKGRGRGW](/stripes/brbrbrkgrgrgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan tartan recorded by W & A K Johnston in 1906, with an earlier Glengarry-certified sample held by the Highland Society of London.

The **MacDonell of Glengarry D** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
