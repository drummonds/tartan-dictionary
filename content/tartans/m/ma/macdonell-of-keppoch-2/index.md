A tartan of the [MacDonell of Keppoch](/families/macdonell-of-keppoch/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGRWBRGRBR](/stripes/grgrwbrgrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Sett published in D.W. Stewart's "Old and Rare Scottish Tartans" (1893).

The **MacDonell of Keppoch** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
