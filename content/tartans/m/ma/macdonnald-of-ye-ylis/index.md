A tartan of the [MacDonnald of ye Ylis](/families/macdonnald-of-ye-ylis/) family.
Its design is pattern [RBKGKGKGW](/stripes/rbkgkgkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

First appeared in the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum; differs from earlier Clan Donald setts, not generally regarded as a clan tartan.

The **MacDonnald of ye Ylis** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
