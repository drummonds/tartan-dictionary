A tartan of the [MacDougall](/families/macdougall/) family.
Its design is pattern [RBRRGBRRRBBWRBRR](/stripes/rbrrgbrrrbbwrbrr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **MacDougall** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
