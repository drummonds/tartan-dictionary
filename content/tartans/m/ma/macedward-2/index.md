Its design is pattern [BBKBKBGBYB](/stripes/bbkbkbgbyb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Unmarked sample from the MacGregor-Hastie collection, likely dating between 1930 and 1950.

The **MacEdward** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
