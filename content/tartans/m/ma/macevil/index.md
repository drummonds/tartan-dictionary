Its design is pattern [BKBKBKWB](/stripes/bkbkbkwb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2010 by Rocky Roeger for Clan MacEvil, a group of friends; colours symbolise dark side, royalty, charity, bloodline.

The **MacEvil** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
