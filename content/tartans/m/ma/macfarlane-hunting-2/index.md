A tartan of the [MacFarlane](/families/macfarlane/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKGWRKRWKBKRWK](/stripes/gkgwrkrwkbkrwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Hunting variant based on Logan's 1831 count with red changed to green; MacFarlanes originated around Arrochar, western Scotland.

The **MacFarlane Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
