A tartan of [Clan MacFarlane](/clan/macfarlane/).
Its design is pattern [KWK](/stripes/kwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Black and white MacFarlane (Lendrum) design from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842).

The **MacFarlane VS** tartan is one sett, recorded at 3 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
