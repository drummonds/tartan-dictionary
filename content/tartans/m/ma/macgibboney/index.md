A tartan of the [MacGibboney](/families/macgibboney/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBWBKBKGKBKGWGK](/stripes/kbwbkbkgkbkgwgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2000 by Donald Suttie Smith for Greg McGibonney, descendant of a Scottish family that emigrated via Ireland in the 1760s.

The **MacGibboney** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
