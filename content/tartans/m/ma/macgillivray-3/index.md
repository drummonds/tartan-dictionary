A tartan of the [MacGillivray](/families/macgillivray/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGRGRBRGRBRBR](/stripes/grgrgrbrgrbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan Chattan-related sett, recorded by 1831; Wilson's version adds a black stripe centring the red square.

The **MacGillivray** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
