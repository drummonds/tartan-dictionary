A tartan of the [MacGlashan](/families/macglashan/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWRBBWGRBRGWYWBR](/stripes/bwrbbwgrbrgwywbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1982 by US historian Dr Philip Smith; MacGlashan is associated with Clan MacKintosh or Clan Stewart.

The **MacGlashan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
