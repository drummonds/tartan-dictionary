A tartan of [Clan MacGregor (Clan Gregor)](/clan/macgregor/).
Its design is pattern [KR](/stripes/kr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Bold red-and-black check; specimen in Highland Society of London collection certified by Sir John MacGregor Murray, 1815-16.

The **MacGregor** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
