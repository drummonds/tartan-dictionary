A tartan of the [MacGregor of Glen Strae](/families/macgregor-of-glen-strae/) family.

Its design is pattern [GRGRG](/stripes/grgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


MacGregor hunting sett from the Vestiarium Scoticum; Glenstrae in Campbell country was central to the clan's history and proscription.

The **MacGregor of Glen Strae** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
