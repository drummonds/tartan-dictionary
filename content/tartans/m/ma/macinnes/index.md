Its design is pattern [WGRGRGRGRBRKGKRW](/stripes/wgrgrgrgrbrkgkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Recorded by Adam in 1908, attributed to the "Onich Grocer"; its green structure mirrors the Innes red tartan.

The **MacInnes** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
