A tartan of [Clan MacIntyre](/clan/macintyre/).

Its design is pattern [GBRBGW](/stripes/gbrbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Hunting tartan of Clan MacIntyre, from the unmarked MacGregor-Hastie collection of the Scottish Tartans Society.

The **MacIntyre L** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
