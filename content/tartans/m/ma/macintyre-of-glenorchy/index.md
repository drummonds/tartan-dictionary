A tartan of the [MacIntyre of Glenorchy](/families/macintyre-of-glenorchy/) family.

Its design is pattern [WRBRGRBRGRBRGRW](/stripes/wrbrgrbrgrbrgrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Smiths' version, also called MacIntyre of Whitehouse, differs from Lyon's record but is most commonly available today.

The **MacIntyre of Glenorchy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
