A tartan of [Clan MacIver](/clan/maciver/).
Its design is pattern [KRKRW](/stripes/krkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Introduced by H. Whyte in 1906; sept of Campbell, MacKenzie, or Robertson depending on region.

The **MacIver** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
