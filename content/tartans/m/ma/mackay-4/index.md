A tartan of the [MacKay](/families/mackay/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGBGKBG](/stripes/bgbgkbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dark green and blue clan sett resembling Gunn, suggesting shared territorial origin; sealed by Chief in 1816.

The **MacKay** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
