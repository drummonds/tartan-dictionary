A tartan of [Clan MacKean](/clan/mackean/).
Its design is pattern [KGKGKBKWKBKGKGKR](/stripes/kgkgkbkwkbkgkgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1995 for silk squares woven by D C Dalgliesh; likely a New Zealand MacKean personal tartan.

The **MacKean dress Family/Clan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
