A tartan of [Clan MacKinnon](/clan/mackinnon/).
Its design is pattern [RGGW](/stripes/rggw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting variant of the MacKinnon clan tartan, count derived from a 1908 letter by MacKinnon of MacKinnon.

The **MacKinnon Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
