A tartan of the [MacKinnon](/families/mackinnon/) family.

Its design is pattern [GGGRGGW](/stripes/gggrggw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Hunting variant of the MacKinnon sett from the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum, with red changed to brown; registered 1960.

The **MacKinnon Hunting** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
