A tartan of the [MacKintosh](/families/mackintosh/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGKRWR](/stripes/rgkrwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Appears in the Vestiarium Scoticum as Mackintosh; resembles the Dunbar sett, also of Vestiarium origin.

The **MacKintosh 6** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
