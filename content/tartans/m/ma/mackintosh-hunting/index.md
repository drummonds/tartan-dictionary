A tartan of the [MacKintosh](/families/mackintosh/) family.

Its design is pattern [BRGRBGG](/stripes/brgrbgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Hunting sett for Clan MacKintosh, recorded in Lyon Court Book; a modern arrangement per D.C. Stewart.

The **MacKintosh Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
