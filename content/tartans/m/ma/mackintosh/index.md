A tartan of the [MacKintosh](/families/mackintosh/) family.

Its design is pattern [RKWGWGGRKRGGWWKRGGW](/stripes/rkwgwggrkrggwwkrggw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Showy pattern worn exclusively by the MacKintosh chief, noted by Logan and illustrated by the Smiths in 1850.

The **MacKintosh** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
