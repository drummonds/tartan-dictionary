Its design is pattern [KGKBW](/stripes/kgkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Simple green tartan from Anderson's of Edinburgh; family a sept of the Stuarts of Bute.

The **MacKirdy** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
