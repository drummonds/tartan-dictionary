A tartan of the [MacLaine of Lochbuie](/families/maclaine-of-lochbuie/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBBKY](/stripes/bbbky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting variant introduced early 20th century by H. Whyte; noted for unusually heavy use of blue.

The **MacLaine of Lochbuie Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
