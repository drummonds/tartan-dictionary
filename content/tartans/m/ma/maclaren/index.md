Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGRGKG](/stripes/bkbkbkgrgkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Differs from Ferguson only by a yellow line; produced by Wilsons pre-1820 as 'Regent', renamed MacLaren around 1822.

The **MacLaren** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
