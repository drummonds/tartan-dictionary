A tartan of the [MacLaurin of Brioch](/families/maclaurin-of-brioch/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKGRGKG](/stripes/bkgrgkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress MacLaren variant designed by I.G. Campbell MacLaren, replacing blue ground with white and centring a blue stripe; approved 1981.

The **MacLaurin of Brioch** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
