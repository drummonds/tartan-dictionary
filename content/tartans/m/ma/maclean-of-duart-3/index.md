A tartan of the [MacLean of Duart](/families/maclean-of-duart/) family.
Its design is pattern [KRWRGKWKGKW](/stripes/krwrgkwkgkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

MacLean of Duart clan tartan dating to 1810-15, mathematically mirroring Royal Stewart with reversed thread counts and colours.

The **MacLean of Duart 3** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
