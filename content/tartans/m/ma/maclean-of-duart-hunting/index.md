A tartan of the [MacLean of Duart](/families/maclean-of-duart/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKWKGKGK](/stripes/gkwkgkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Hunting tartan for MacLean of Duart, possibly from a 1587 charter; proportions devised by the Sobieski Stuarts.

The **MacLean of Duart Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
