A tartan of the [MacLeod](/families/macleod/) family.

Its design is pattern [BRBRBRBRGRBRBRGRGRBRG](/stripes/brbrbrbrgrbrbrgrgrbrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on Norman MacLeod's 1747 Ramsay portrait sett, with a yellow stripe added by Ruairidh MacLeod; approved 1982.

The **MacLeod** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
