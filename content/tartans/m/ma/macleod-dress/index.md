A tartan of [Clan MacLeod](/clan/macleod/).
Its design is pattern [KGKGR](/stripes/kgkgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Yellow field with black stripes; described by Sir Thomas Dick Lauder to Walter Scott in 1829.

The **MacLeod Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
