A tartan of the [MacLeod of Gesto](/families/macleod-of-gesto/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGGGKBKR](/stripes/bkbkbkgggkbkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Pre-1850 sett resembling a Wilson's of Bannockburn sample recorded for the 1851 Great Exhibition, now held in Stirling.

The **MacLeod of Gesto** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
