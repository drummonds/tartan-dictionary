A tartan of the [MacMaster](/families/macmaster/) family.

Its design is pattern [KBGBGWGBGBKW](/stripes/kbgbgwgbgbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Phil Smith in 1986 for all MacMaster, MacMasters and related names.

The **MacMaster** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
