Its design is pattern [RGWGR](/stripes/rgwgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


The **MacNab 4** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
