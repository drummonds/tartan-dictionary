A tartan of the [MacNab](/families/macnab/) family.

Its design is pattern [GRBGBRK](/stripes/grbgbrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Black Watch structure with translated colours, transforming a subdued sett into a striking one; recorded by Logan, 1831.

The **MacNab** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
