A tartan of the [MacNicol](/families/macnicol/) family.

Its design is pattern [RGRKBKRGRKR](/stripes/rgrkbkrgrkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Variation deduced from McIan's 1845–7 drawing; woven by Inverness Tweed Mill in 1869.

The **MacNicol** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
