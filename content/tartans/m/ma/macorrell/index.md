Its design is pattern [BGBGBGWGWGGGWGWG](/stripes/bgbgbgwgwgggwgwg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Resembles the MacDonald, Lord of the Isles sett, suggesting links to MacDonnell or MacDonald.

The **MacOrrell** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
