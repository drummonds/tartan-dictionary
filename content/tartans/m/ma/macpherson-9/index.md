A tartan of the [MacPherson](/families/macpherson/) family.

Its design is pattern [RBRGGKBKBKBRWKR](/stripes/rbrggkbkbkbrwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Clan tartan from Grant's 1886 collection; reduced red ground reflects Victorian trend toward compact setts; worn by Victoria Police pipe band.

The **MacPherson 9** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
