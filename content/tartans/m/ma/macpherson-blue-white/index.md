A tartan of [Clan MacPherson](/clan/macpherson/).

Its design is pattern [GBWBWRW](/stripes/gbwbwrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress MacPherson variant, a modern trade design popular with country dancers; supplied by Edinburgh kiltmaker Hugh Macpherson around 1980.

The **MacPherson Blue/White** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
