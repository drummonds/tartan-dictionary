A tartan of [Clan MacPherson](/clan/macpherson/).
Its design is pattern [RKWRBKBKBKGGWBW](/stripes/rkwrbkbkbkggwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress variant of MacPherson, popular with dancers; samples supplied to the Scottish Tartan Society around 1980.

The **MacPherson Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
