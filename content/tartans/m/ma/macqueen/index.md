A tartan of [Clan MacQueen](/clan/macqueen/).
Its design is pattern [GKRKRK](/stripes/gkrkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan MacQueen tartan, first published in the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842), likely derived from MacDonald and Mackintosh setts.

The **MacQueen** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
