A tartan of the [MacRae](/families/macrae/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGRBRBRBRBRBRBRWRBR](/stripes/grgrbrbrbrbrbrbrwrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Paton's "Red MacRae," a red clan tartan associated with the Macraes; the Conchra branch's tartan is blue and white.

The **MacRae** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
