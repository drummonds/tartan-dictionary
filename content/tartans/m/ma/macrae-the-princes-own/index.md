A tartan of [Clan MacRae](/clan/macrae/).

Its design is pattern [GRGRKRKRKRKRKRKRWRBRBRWRGRGRGRGRGGGRGW](/stripes/grgrkrkrkrkrkrkrwrbrbrwrgrgrgrgrgggrgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


One of two MacRae setts in the Highland Society collection, presumably sealed by the Chief around 1815.

The **MacRae The Princes Own** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
