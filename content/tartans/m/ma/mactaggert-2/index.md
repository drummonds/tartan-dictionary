A tartan of the [MacTaggert](/families/mactaggert/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKGWGWGKBRBRB](/stripes/bkgwgwgkbrbrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Sept of Clan Ross, name meaning 'son of the priest'; tartan first published by Johnston's of Edinburgh, 1906.

The **MacTaggert** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
