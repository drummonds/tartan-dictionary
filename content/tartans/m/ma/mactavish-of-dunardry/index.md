A tartan of the [MacTavish of Dunardry](/families/mactavish-of-dunardry/) family.
Its design is pattern [WKWGYWW](/stripes/wkwgyww/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Modern MacTavish dress clan tartan re-creating Dunardry design, substituting white for the yellow of the MacLachlan tartan.

The **MacTavish of Dunardry** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
