A tartan of the [MacWilliam](/families/macwilliam/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGKRBRBR](/stripes/ggkrbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Sutherland clan tartan recorded pre-1880 in Paris's "Clans Originaux"; similar in style and colour to MacKay.

The **MacWilliam** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
