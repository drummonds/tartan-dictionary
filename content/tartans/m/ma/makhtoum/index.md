Its design is pattern [RGRGRW](/stripes/rgrgrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Based on Cameron; designed in 1977 for Dubai's ruler Shaikh Rashid Bin Saeed al-Maktoum and the Dubai Pipe Band.

The **Makhtoum** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
