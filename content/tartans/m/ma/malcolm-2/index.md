A tartan of the [Malcolm](/families/malcolm/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGBKGKGKWKGKBRB](/stripes/bgbkgkgkwkgkbrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Symmetrical version recorded by Wilson's of Bannockburn (1847); gold and azure stripes echo the Malcolm family arms.

The **Malcolm** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
