A tartan of the [Malcolm](/families/malcolm/) family.

Its design is pattern [KGKYKBKGKBRBRBKWBWBWBWBWKWBWBWBWBWK](/stripes/kgkykbkgkbrbrbkwbwbwbwbwkwbwbwbwbwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress variant from 1890s, based on Wilson's 1847 sett; gold and azure derive from Malcolm armorial bearings.

The **Malcolm Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
