A tartan of the [Manitoba](/families/manitoba/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGRGWGW](/stripes/grgrgwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Canadian provincial tartan for Manitoba, designed by Hugh Kirkwood Rankine, given Royal Assent in 1962.

The **Manitoba** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
