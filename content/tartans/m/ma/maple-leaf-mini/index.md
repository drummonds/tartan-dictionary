Its design is pattern [GGGGRGRGRGRG](/stripes/ggggrgrgrgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by David Weiser to evoke maple leaves turning from summer to autumn; now Canada's national tartan.

The **Maple Leaf MINI** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
