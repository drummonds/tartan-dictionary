A tartan of the [Mar](/families/mar/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRGRBR](/stripes/brgrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Recorded by petition of the Countess of Mar to settle the proper Mar District sett; possibly pre-1850, designer unknown.

The **Mar** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
