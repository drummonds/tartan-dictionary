A tartan of the [Marchmont](/families/marchmont/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKGKBGBKGKBK](/stripes/bkgkbgbkgkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1997 by Keith Lumsden for personal blankets and clothing.

The **Marchmont** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
