Its design is pattern [BKWRBRBWBWBRBRBWBWBR](/stripes/bkwrbrbwbwbrbrbwbwbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed for Martha De Laurentiis, using Italian and American flag colours to symbolise her American-Italian family.

The **Martha De Laurentiis** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
