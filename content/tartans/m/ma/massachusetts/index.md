A tartan of the [Massachusetts](/families/massachusetts/) family.

Its design is pattern [BWBRBRGBWBGBG](/stripes/bwbrbrgbwbgbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Official tartan of Massachusetts, signed by Governor Romney in 2003; blue, green, tan, red represent the state's geography and harvests.

The **Massachusetts** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
