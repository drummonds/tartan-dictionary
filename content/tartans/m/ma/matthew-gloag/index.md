Its design is pattern [BBGKBKBKBGBKBKBKGB](/stripes/bbgkbkbkbgbkbkbkgb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1996 by Gillian Kirkwood for Perth whisky blenders Matthew Gloag & Son, makers of Famous Grouse.

The **Matthew Gloag** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
