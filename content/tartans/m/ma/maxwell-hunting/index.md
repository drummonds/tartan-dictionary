Hunting variant of the Maxwell tartan, a Lowland family from Glasgow and south-west Scotland.

The **Maxwell Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
