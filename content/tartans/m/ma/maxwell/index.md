A tartan of the [Maxwell](/families/maxwell/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGRKRGR](/stripes/rgrkrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Lowland family tartan first published in the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum; Maxwells hail from Glasgow and southwest Scotland.

The **Maxwell** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
