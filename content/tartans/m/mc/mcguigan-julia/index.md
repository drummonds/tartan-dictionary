Personal tartan based on MacNeil of Colonsay, with softened green, red and white stripes; McGuigan is a sept.

The **McGuigan, Julia** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
