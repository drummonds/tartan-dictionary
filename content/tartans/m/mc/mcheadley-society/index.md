Corporate tartan for a private American gentlemen's golf club, adapted from Boyd with green favoured.

The **McHeadley Society** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
