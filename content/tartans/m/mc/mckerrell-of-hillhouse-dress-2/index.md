A tartan of the [McKerrell of Hillhouse](/families/mckerrell-of-hillhouse/) family.

Its design is pattern [GWBWBW](/stripes/gwbwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress version of McKerrell of Hillhouse Hunting, designed 1996 by Madam McKerrell, substituting red for yellow.

The **McKerrell of Hillhouse Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
