A tartan of the [Mead of Poetry](/families/mead-of-poetry/) family.

Its design is pattern [BWWYBBBBBBBW](/stripes/bwwybbbbbbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Inspired by Norse mythology's poetic mead of inspiration; design derived from a blanket.

The **Mead of Poetry** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
