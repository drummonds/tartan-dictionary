A tartan of the [Meeson](/families/meeson/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGBBBK](/stripes/bgbbbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Hunting variant; black and gray originally woven with melange or marled yarn.

The **Meeson Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
