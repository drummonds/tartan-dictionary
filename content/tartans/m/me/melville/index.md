A tartan of the [Melville](/families/melville/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBGWG](/stripes/bkbgwg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Also known as Oliphant and Melville, recorded in Wilson's notebook in 1847, with samples in several collections.

The **Melville** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
