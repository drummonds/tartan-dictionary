A tartan of [Clan Menzies](/clan/menzies/).
Its design is pattern [GRGRGRGR](/stripes/grgrgrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting variant of Menzies, recorded by D.W. Stewart in 'Old and Rare Scottish Tartans' (1893).

The **Menzies** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
