A tartan of the [Mercer](/families/mercer/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBBGBBRB](/stripes/bbbgbbrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Dr Phil Smith for Charles Mercer of North Carolina; wearable by all of the name.

The **Mercer** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
