Its design is pattern [GRGR](/stripes/grgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Associated with the Forbes and Innes clans; the Middletons of Cromarty included WWI agriculturalist Sir Thomas Middleton.

The **Middleton** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
