A tartan of the [Minnesota](/families/minnesota/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKWKBGKWG](/stripes/bkwkbgkwg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Official Minnesota state tartan, dress variant, designed by Mark Osweiler of St Paul.

The **Minnesota Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
