A tartan of the [Minnesota](/families/minnesota/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGKRBKWK](/stripes/ggkrbkwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Official Minnesota state tartan, designed by Mark Osweiler of St Paul.

The **Minnesota** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
