A tartan of the [Mississippi](/families/mississippi/) family.
Its design is pattern [KGBKGRKGGYGYW](/stripes/kgbkgrkggygyw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2005 by Karen M Green for the Mississippi Gulf Coast Scottish Games, seeking state tartan status.

The **Mississippi** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
