A tartan of the [Mitchell](/families/mitchell/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBKGKGKBBY](/stripes/bbkgkgkbby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Named for General Billy Mitchell as the US Air Force pipe band tartan; sett also known as Russell, Hunter, and Galbraith.

The **Mitchell** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
