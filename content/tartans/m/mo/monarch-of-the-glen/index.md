Its design is pattern [BBGBRBBBBRBGBRBBGBGYGB](/stripes/bbgbrbbbbrbgbrbbgbgygb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Claire Donaldson of House of Edgar, named after the popular television series.

The **Monarch of the Glen** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
