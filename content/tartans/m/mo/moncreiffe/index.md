Its design is pattern [GR](/stripes/gr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Adopted by Sir Iain Moncreiffe in 1957 from an old MacLachlan red-and-green sett, deemed fitting to the clan's ancient history.

The **Moncreiffe** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
