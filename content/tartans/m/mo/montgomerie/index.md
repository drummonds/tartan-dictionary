A tartan of the [Montgomerie](/families/montgomerie/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBGBGBGBGBGG](/stripes/gbgbgbgbgbgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Montgomerie artifact dating to c.1815; incomplete sett with a broad mid-green stripe narrower in the weft.

The **Montgomerie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
