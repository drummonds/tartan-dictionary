A tartan of the [Moran](/families/moran/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBKWGKKBKKGWKBBW](/stripes/bbkwgkkbkkgwkbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2001 by Mark Moran as a copyright-free blue alternative to the restricted green Moran tartan, reserved for family use.

The **Moran** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
