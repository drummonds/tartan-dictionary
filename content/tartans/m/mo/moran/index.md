Its design is pattern [BRGRGBGW](/stripes/brgrgbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Variant of Moran Blue created for the 2003 wedding of Julie Drummond and Ian Moran.

The **Moran** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
