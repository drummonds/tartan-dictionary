A tartan of [Clan Morris](/clan/morris/).

Its design is pattern [GBGBGBGW](/stripes/gbgbgbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Welsh surname tartan for Morris and variants, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft.

The **Morris** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
