A tartan of the [Morrison](/families/morrison/) family.
Its design is pattern [KGKGBR](/stripes/kgkgbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Morrison clan pattern found in a family bible during demolition of a Lewis Black House in 1935.

The **Morrison** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
