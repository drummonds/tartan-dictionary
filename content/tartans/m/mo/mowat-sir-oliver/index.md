A tartan of [Clan Mowat](/clan/mowat/).

Its design is pattern [BBYBYBGBGBGBGBYBYBG](/stripes/bbybybgbgbgbgbybybg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Commemorates Sir Oliver Mowat (1820–1903), Premier of Ontario and a Father of Canadian Confederation.

The **Mowat, Sir Oliver** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
