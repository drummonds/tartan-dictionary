Its design is pattern [YBBGGY](/stripes/ybbggy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorates the 2012 NCLEES conference hosted in London; greens for Ireland, blues for police and Thames, yellow for pipers' saffron.

The **MPS Emerald Society** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
