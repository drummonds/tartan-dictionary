A tartan of the [Mungall](/families/mungall/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRYRYRBGRKGKRGY](/stripes/bryryrbgrkgkrgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Personal tartan commemorating William de Mungall's signing of the Ragman's Roll; first produced in ancient colours.

The **Mungall** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
