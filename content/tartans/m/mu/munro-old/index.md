A tartan of [Clan Munro](/clan/munro/).

Its design is pattern [BRBRGRBRGRBRBR](/stripes/brbrgrbrgrbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Plaid found at Culloden battlefield (1745), part of the MacGregor Hastie Collection.

The **Munro Old** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
