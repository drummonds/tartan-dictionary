A tartan of the [Munster](/families/munster/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGKGKWGKRWGWGWRW](/stripes/rgkgkwgkrwgwgwrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Irish district tartan for Munster, produced by Lochcarron in 1997 to mark Scots-Irish historical links.

The **Munster** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
