A tartan of the [Murray](/families/murray/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGRGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgrgkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Simplified version of Murray of Atholl, with red stripe added by Lord Murray to the Black Watch sett.

The **Murray** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
