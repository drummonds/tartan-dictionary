title: "MacAlpine"
date: 2023-03-18

Black-and-green hunting design with yellow lines, first recorded 1908; resembles hunting MacLean but for the Siol Alpin clan.

The **MacAlpine** tartan groups 5 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
