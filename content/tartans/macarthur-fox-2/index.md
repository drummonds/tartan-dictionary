Its design is pattern [BGKGK](/stripes/bgkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Older of two MacArthur setts, linking the clan with the Campbells.

The **MacArthur-Fox** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
