A tartan of [Clan MacArthur](/clan/macarthur/).
Its design is pattern [GYGKGK](/stripes/gygkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Green and blue clan sett sharing the MacDonald, Lord of the Isles form, reflecting the MacArthurs' hereditary piper links.

The **MacArthur** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
