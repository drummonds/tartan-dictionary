Hunting sett matching M'Intyre North's 1881 count from Logan; shortened form resembles MacGregor tartan.

The **MacAulay Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
