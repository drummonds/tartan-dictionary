Documented in Smith brothers' 1850 'Authenticated Tartans', drawing on George Hunter's pre-1822 Highland survey.

The **MacCoul** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
