A tartan of the [MacDonell of Keppoch](/families/macdonell-of-keppoch/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGBGBGBGBKBKBKB](/stripes/bgbgbgbgbkbkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **MacDonell of Keppoch** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
