A tartan of the [MacGregor of Balquhidder](/families/macgregor-of-balquhidder/) family.

Published by James Logan in 'The Scottish Gael' (1831), one of the earliest clan tartan records.

The **MacGregor of Balquhidder** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
