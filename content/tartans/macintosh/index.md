Sample labelled "MacIntosh" rather than the usual "MacKintosh" spelling.

The **MacIntosh** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
