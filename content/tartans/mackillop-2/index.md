Its design is pattern [BRGRBBRBRG](/stripes/brgrbbrbrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Sept of MacDonald of Keppoch, closely resembling that clan's tartan.

The **MacKillop** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
