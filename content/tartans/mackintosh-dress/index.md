A tartan of [Clan Mackintosh (Clan Chattan)](/clan/mackintosh/).

Dress variant of the MacIntosh clan tartan, from an old Scott Adie pattern book.

The **MacKintosh Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
