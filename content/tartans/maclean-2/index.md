A tartan of [Clan Maclean](/clan/maclean/).
Its design is pattern [BWKGKWKGRWRK](/stripes/bwkgkwkgrwrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **MacLean** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
