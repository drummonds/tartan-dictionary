A tartan of [Clan Maclean](/clan/maclean/).

Dress variant of MacLean of Duart, in burgundy.

The **MacLean Dress Burgundy** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
