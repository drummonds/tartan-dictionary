A tartan of the [MacLeod of Raasay](/families/macleod-of-raasay/) family.
Its design is pattern [KRKRK](/stripes/krkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Likely derived from the Vestiarium Scoticum, post-1829; thread count from the Provost MacBean Collection sample.

The **MacLeod of Raasay** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
