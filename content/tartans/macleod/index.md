A tartan of [Clan MacLeod](/clan/macleod/).

Based on Norman MacLeod's 1747 Ramsay portrait sett, with a yellow stripe added by Ruairidh MacLeod; approved 1982.

The **MacLeod** tartan groups 8 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
