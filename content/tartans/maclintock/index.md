Clan tartan documented from 1842, recorded in the MacGregor Hastie collection and Clans Originaux.

The **MacLintock** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
