Its design is pattern [GKGKGKRGGKGK](/stripes/gkgkgkrggkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Documented by Logan (1847), the ancient MacMillan form differs radically from the modern, and resembles Buchanan.

The **MacMillan** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
