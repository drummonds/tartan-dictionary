A tartan of the [MacNeil of Colonsay](/families/macneil-of-colonsay/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGWGKBK](/stripes/bgwgkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **MacNeil of Colonsay** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
