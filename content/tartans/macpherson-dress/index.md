A tartan of [Clan MacPherson](/clan/macpherson/).

Modern trade design, one of many Dress MacPherson variants popular with country dancers.

The **MacPherson Dress** tartan groups 13 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
