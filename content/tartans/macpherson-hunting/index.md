A tartan of [Clan MacPherson](/clan/macpherson/).

Grey-ground hunting variant of the MacPherson clan tartan, recorded from 1850; clan version has white ground.

The **MacPherson Hunting** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
