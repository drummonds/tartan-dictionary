A tartan of [Clan MacRae](/clan/macrae/).

Early 19th-century hunting sett; Wilson's specimen held at the Smith Institute, Stirling.

The **MacRae Hunting** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
