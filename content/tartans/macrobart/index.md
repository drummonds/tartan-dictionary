Its design is pattern [BKGWGW](/stripes/bkgwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress version for MacRobart family use, designed by Michael Owen Holder in 1997.

The **MacRobart** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
