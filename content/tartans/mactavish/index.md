A tartan of [Clan MacTavish](/clan/mactavish/).
Its design is pattern [WKWBRW](/stripes/wkwbrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Thomson sett (Thomson being anglicised MacTavish); came into use around 1950, some versions substituting black for the mid-blue stripe.

The **MacTavish** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
