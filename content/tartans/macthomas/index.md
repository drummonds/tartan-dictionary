Its design is pattern [BBBKGRG](/stripes/bbbkgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by David Thomas in 1975, registered with Lord Lyon 1979; woven by Strathmore Woollen Co., Forfar.

The **MacThomas** tartan groups 6 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
