Designed in 2005 by FE Maguire at House of Tartan, Comrie; based on MacQuarrie, for all Scottish Maguires.

The **Maguire** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
