Dress variant from 1890s, based on Wilson's 1847 sett; gold and azure derive from Malcolm armorial bearings.

The **Malcolm Dress** tartan groups 6 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
