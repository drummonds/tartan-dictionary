Seven-colour Manx National Tartan designed 1958 by Patricia McQuaid, its hues representing Isle of Man's landscape.

The **Manx National** tartan groups 6 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
