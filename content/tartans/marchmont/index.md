Designed in 1997 by Keith Lumsden for personal blankets and clothing.

The **Marchmont** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
