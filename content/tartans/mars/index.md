Its design is pattern [BGBKGKBKGBW](/stripes/bgbkgkbkgbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Based on the Hay Clan tartan, with blue signifying Royal Family and Royal Engineers service, and black for Highland regimental service.

The **Mars** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
