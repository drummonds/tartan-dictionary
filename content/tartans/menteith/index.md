Its design is pattern [GWGKBK](/stripes/gwgkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Mid-19th century district tartan from the upper Forth, resembling Graham of Menteith but with white replacing the azure stripe.

The **Menteith** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
