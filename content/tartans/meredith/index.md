Welsh surname tartan for Meredith and variants, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft.

The **Meredith** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
