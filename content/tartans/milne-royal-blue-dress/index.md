A tartan of [Clan Milne](/clan/milne/).

Dance version of Milne tartan in royal blue, reputedly a personal design.

The **Milne Royal Blue Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
