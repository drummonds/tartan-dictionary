Montgomerie artifact dating to c.1815; incomplete sett with a broad mid-green stripe narrower in the weft.

The **Montgomerie** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
