Its design is pattern [BGBGBGRGBGB](/stripes/bgbgbgrgbgb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

A Morgan/MacKay sett from the Sobieski Stuarts' Vestiarium Scoticum (1842); also recorded as a Welsh-surname Morgan woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft for a vertical stripe.

The **Morgan** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
