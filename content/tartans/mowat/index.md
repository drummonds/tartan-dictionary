Clan Mowat design with muted sky blue and dark pine green, linked to National Trust's Kincardine & Deeside branch.

The **Mowat** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
