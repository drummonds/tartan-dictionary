Designed by Peter MacDonald for the Mull Rugby Club, at the suggestion of Mrs Ros Jones of Aros.

The **Mull Rugby Club** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
