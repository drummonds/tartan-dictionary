Personal tartan commemorating William de Mungall's signing of the Ragman's Roll; first produced in ancient colours.

The **Mungall** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
