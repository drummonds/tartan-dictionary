A tartan of [Clan Munro](/clan/munro/).

Munro variant from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842), one of many tartans first recorded by the Sobieski Stuart brothers.

The **Munro** tartan groups 7 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
