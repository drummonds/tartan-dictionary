Personal tartan based on Blair Atholl sett, with white stripes for the Murrays' three silver stars and heraldic black-and-white.

The **Murray-Hetherington** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
