A tartan of the [Murray of Elibank](/families/murray-of-elibank/) family.

Murray of Elibank clan tartan from the MacGregor-Hastie collection, an unmarked sample dating circa 1930–50.

The **Murray of Elibank** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
