A tartan of the [Nairn](/families/nairn/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGBKBKGKBKY](/stripes/bgbkbkgkbky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Appeared c.1930 in Anderson of Edinburgh's collection; worn by the Spencer-Nairn family, resembling Hunting Brodie without the yellow.

The **Nairn** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
