Its design is pattern [GRWBWR](/stripes/grwbwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2009 by Mark Wright (USA) to honour US National Defense Service Medal recipients, based on the medal's ribbon colours.

The **National Defense** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
