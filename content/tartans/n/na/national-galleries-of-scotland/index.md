Its design is pattern [KGKBRBR](/stripes/kgkbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Based on Black Watch; three claret stripes represent the three galleries, echoing Playfair's original National Gallery colour scheme.

The **National Galleries of Scotland** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
