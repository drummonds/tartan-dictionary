Its design is pattern [WBRKGGBKW](/stripes/wbrkggbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by the National Association of Scottish Woollen Manufacturers in 1934.

The **National** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
