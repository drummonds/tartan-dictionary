A tartan of the [Neumann](/families/neumann/) family.

Its design is pattern [KRGKGBGGGKGRK](/stripes/krgkgbgggkgrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed for Marcus Neumann's family and German pipe smokers; green and blue for Scottish landscape, red, black and gold for German origins and pipe tobaccos.

The **Neumann** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
