A tartan of [Clan Newlands](/clan/newlands/).
Its design is pattern [BKBRKGRGRG](/stripes/bkbrkgrgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1986 for journalist Willy Newlands of Lauriston Castle, based on Cameron of Erracht; also called "Falconers' Tartan."

The **Newlands** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
