A tartan of the [Newmill](/families/newmill/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBBBBBY](/stripes/bbbbbby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Corporate design for Johnstons of Elgin mill shop refurbishment, based on house-style colours using their 'Antique' range.

The **Newmill** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
