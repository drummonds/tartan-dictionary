Its design is pattern [GWKRWRWRKW](/stripes/gwkrwrwrkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Sett from the Vestiarium Scoticum's Mackintosh; resembles the Vestiarium's Dunbar; Nisbets trace to Berwickshire's barony of Nisbet circa 1160.

The **Nisbett Rose Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
