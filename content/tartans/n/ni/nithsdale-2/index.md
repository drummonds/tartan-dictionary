A tartan of the [Nithsdale](/families/nithsdale/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBBBBBBGBB](/stripes/bbbbbbbgbb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


District tartan for the Nith valley in Dumfriesshire, designed by Arthur Galt for Councillor John Hannay.

The **Nithsdale** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
