Its design is pattern [BKBKBKWKW](/stripes/bkbkbkwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2012 by Peter Nocken and Jessica Brosch; colours and stripes symbolise family members and love of Scotland.

The **Nocken** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
