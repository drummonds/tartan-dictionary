Its design is pattern [KBRWGGGGK](/stripes/kbrwggggk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Evelyn B Halliday in 1963 to commemorate the naming of Ontario's Nor Westers Mountain Range.

The **Nor Westers** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
