A tartan of [Clan North](/clan/north/).
Its design is pattern [BRBRGRGRGRW](/stripes/brbrgrgrgrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Donald Fraser in 1990 for North Berwick Pipe Band dancers' visit to Maine, USA.

The **North Berwick** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
