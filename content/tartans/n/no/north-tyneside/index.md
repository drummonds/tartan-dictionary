A tartan of [Clan North](/clan/north/).
Its design is pattern [BBWBWBR](/stripes/bbwbwbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1996 for pipe band uniforms, based on Earl of St Andrews with added red linking to McGregor.

The **North Tyneside** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
