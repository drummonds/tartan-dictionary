The **Grey Watch** tartan is one sett proportion (`n25k4n4k4n4ln20k5ln20k20n4k4n4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GKGKGWKWKGKG](/stripes/gkgkgwkwkgkg/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
