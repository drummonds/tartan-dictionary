The **Balmoral** tartan is one sett proportion (`n2r1n8lp2k2n1lp1n1lp4n2k1n1r1`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RRKRRRRRKRRRR](/stripes/rrkrrrrrkrrrr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
