The **Devon, Original** tartan is one sett proportion (`n5g4ln1g4t4dg4y1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GGWGRGY](/stripes/ggwgrgy/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
