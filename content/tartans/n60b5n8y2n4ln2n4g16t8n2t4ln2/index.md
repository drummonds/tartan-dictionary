The **Stuart/Stewart Silver** tartan is one sett proportion (`n60b5n8y2n4ln2n4g16t8n2t4ln2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GBGYGWGGGGGW](/stripes/gbgygwgggggw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
