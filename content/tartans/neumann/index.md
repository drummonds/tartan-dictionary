Designed for Marcus Neumann's family and German pipe smokers; green and blue for Scottish landscape, red, black and gold for German origins and pipe tobaccos.

The **Neumann** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
