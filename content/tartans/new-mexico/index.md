Official New Mexico state tartan, designed 1995 by Ralph L. Stevenson; blue, green, red and gold symbolise sky, vegetation, peoples and minerals.

The **New Mexico** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
