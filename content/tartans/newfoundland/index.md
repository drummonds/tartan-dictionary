Canadian provincial tartan; colours reflect the 'Ode to Newfoundland', recorded by Lord Lyon in 1973.

The **Newfoundland** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
