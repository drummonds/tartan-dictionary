Corporate design for Johnstons of Elgin mill shop refurbishment, based on house-style colours using their 'Antique' range.

The **Newmill** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
