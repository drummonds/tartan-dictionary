A tartan of [Clan Nicolson](/clan/nicolson/).

The **Nicolson MacNicol** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
