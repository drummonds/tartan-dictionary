District tartan for the Nith valley in Dumfriesshire, designed by Arthur Galt for Councillor John Hannay.

The **Nithsdale** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
