Designed 2012 by Peter Nocken and Jessica Brosch; colours and stripes symbolise family members and love of Scotland.

The **Nocken** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
