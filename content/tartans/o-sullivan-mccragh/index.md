Designed by Chris Aitken for Geoffrey (Tailor) Highland Crafts in June 1994.

The **O'Sullivan McCragh** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
