Its design is pattern [GBKBKBKGWGWGKGKG](/stripes/gbkbkbkgwgwgkgkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1985 by Jerry O'Connor of Keltic Klassics, New Jersey, as "Royal na Connaught"; woven by House of Edgar.

The **O'Conner** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
