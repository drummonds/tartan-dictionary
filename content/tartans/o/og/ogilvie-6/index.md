A tartan of the [Ogilvie](/families/ogilvie/) family.
Its design is pattern [KWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKWKW](/stripes/kwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Most complex of all tartans, with 91 colour changes per James Logan; linked to Drummonds of Strathallan by 1812 marriage.

The **Ogilvie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
