A tartan of the [Ogilvie](/families/ogilvie/) family.

Its design is pattern [BYBKGKGR](/stripes/bybkgkgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Hunting variant of the Ogilvie clan tartan, once restricted to family use but now woven commercially.

The **Ogilvie Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
