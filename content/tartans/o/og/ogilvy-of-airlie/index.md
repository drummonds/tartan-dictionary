A tartan of the [Ogilvy of Airlie](/families/ogilvy-of-airlie/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGBGWGKRKRKRKGWGKRWRWRKGWWWGBRKRWBWR](/stripes/bgbgwgkrkrkrkgwgkrwrwrkgwwwgbrkrwbwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Most common form of the Ogilvy tartan, dated 1830; an enormously complex pattern with one sett spanning the loom width.

The **Ogilvy of Airlie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
