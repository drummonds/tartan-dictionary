Its design is pattern [BWRBYGYBG](/stripes/bwrbygybg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Based on Ohio's flag and state seal, with stripe widths reflecting Ohio's date of admission to the Union.

The **Ohio** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
