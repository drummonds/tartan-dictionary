A tartan of the [Oilmens](/families/oilmens/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKRKRKRK](/stripes/gkrkrkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Genuinely asymmetrical corporate design by Don Barkwell, dating from before 1992.

The **Oilmens** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
