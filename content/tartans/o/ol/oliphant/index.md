A tartan of the [Oliphant](/families/oliphant/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKBGWG](/stripes/bkbgwg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Clan tartan also called 'Oliphant and Melville'; from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842), provenance disputed.

The **Oliphant** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
