A tartan of the [Oliphant](/families/oliphant/) family.
Its design is pattern [GWGBKBKBGW](/stripes/gwgbkbkbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan tartan also called 'Oliphant and Melville'; from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842), provenance disputed.

The **Oliphant** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
