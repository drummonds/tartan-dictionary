A tartan of [Clan Ontario](/clan/ontario/).

Its design is pattern [GBBWBGG](/stripes/gbbwbgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Six colours represent nickel-bearing rocks, snow, sky and lakes, gold, forests and fields, and the Indian Nation.

The **Ontario Northern** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
