A tartan of [Clan Ontario](/clan/ontario/).

Its design is pattern [BRBWBKBGBKBGW](/stripes/brbwbkbgbkbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2009 by David Brown and Fiona Hall (Lochcarron) for the Ontario Provincial Police, under exclusive contract.

The **Ontario Provincial Police** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
