A tartan of the [Orkney](/families/orkney/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBGKBGBGBKGBKY](/stripes/kbgkbgbgbkgbky/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Ronnie Hek for the Westry Knitters; chosen by public vote as Orkney's official district tartan.

The **Orkney** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
