A tartan of the [Orman](/families/orman/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBGBGBKBK](/stripes/bbgbgbkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2012 by Geoffrey Highland Crafts for Kevin Orman, of Midlothian descent; colours reflect personal preference.

The **Orman** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
