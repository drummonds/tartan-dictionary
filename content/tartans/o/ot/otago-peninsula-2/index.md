Its design is pattern [BBBGBGBBGWRBBBGBBBGBGBBBGBBBRWGBBGBGBBBG](/stripes/bbbgbgbbgwrbbbgbbbgbgbbbgbbbrwgbbgbgbbbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for the Otago Peninsula Trust, Dunedin; colours represent local wildlife like salmon pink and kingfisher blue.

The **Otago Peninsula** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
