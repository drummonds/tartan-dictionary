The **Glasgow Dress (Dance)** tartan is one sett proportion (`o16r3lb15r18w15o3lb3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RRWRWRW](/stripes/rrwrwrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1984**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
