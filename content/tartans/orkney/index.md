Designed by Ronnie Hek for the Westry Knitters; chosen by public vote as Orkney's official district tartan.

The **Orkney** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
