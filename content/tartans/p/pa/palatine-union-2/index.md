Its design is pattern [BKBKBKWWRWWKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkwwrwwkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 2004 for the Seattle wedding of Traepischke Graves and Steve Lalor; Palatine references both a Scottish name and a Seattle district.

The **Palatine Union** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
