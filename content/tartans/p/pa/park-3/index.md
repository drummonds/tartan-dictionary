A tartan of the [Park](/families/park/) family.

Its design is pattern [GRGRGKGKGKBR](/stripes/grgrgkgkgkbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by William D. Park in 1996 for himself and family; wearable by others of the name.

The **Park** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
