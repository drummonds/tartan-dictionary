A tartan of the [Parker](/families/parker/) family.

Its design is pattern [BRBWBWBWBGBRWBWRBGBWBWBWBR](/stripes/brbwbwbwbgbrwbwrbgbwbwbwbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Las Vegas resident David Parker, based on the American flag.

The **Parker** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
