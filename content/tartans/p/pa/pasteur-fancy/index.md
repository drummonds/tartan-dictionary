Its design is pattern [BYBG](/stripes/bybg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Pattern derived from the shawl in a pastel portrait of Louis Pasteur's mother, drawn when he was 13.

The **Pasteur Fancy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
