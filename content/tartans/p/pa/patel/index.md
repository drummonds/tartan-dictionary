Its design is pattern [GGBGBGRBW](/stripes/ggbgbgrbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2013 for the Patel family, using colours of Gujarat, India's "Jewel of the West."

The **Patel** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
