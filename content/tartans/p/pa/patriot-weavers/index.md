Its design is pattern [KBKBKBW](/stripes/kbkbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for those proud of Scots connections; influenced by the Douglas tartan, honouring patriot Sir James (Black) Douglas.

The **Patriot Weavers** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
