A tartan of the [Pennsylvania](/families/pennsylvania/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGBGBGKBGBKBGBKGBGB](/stripes/bgbgbgkbgbkbgbkgbgb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 1992 by Wm. H. Johnston, based on the Black Watch worn at the 1763 Battle of Bushy Run near Pittsburgh.

The **Pennsylvania** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
