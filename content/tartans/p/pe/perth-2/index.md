A tartan of the [Perth](/families/perth/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRBRGWGWGRBRWR](/stripes/brbrgwgwgrbrwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Derived from Dominique Dupra's 1739 portrait of the Duke of Perth; white stripe may be yellow.

The **Perth** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
