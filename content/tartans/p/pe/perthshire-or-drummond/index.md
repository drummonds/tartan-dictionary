Perthshire district sett, akin to Drummond clan tartan; recorded c.1819 by Wilson's of Bannockburn as "Perthshire Rock and Wheel."

The **Perthshire or Drummond** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
