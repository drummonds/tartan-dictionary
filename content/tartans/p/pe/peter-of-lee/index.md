A tartan of the [Peter of Lee](/families/peter-of-lee/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBGKGRGKGBKBG](/stripes/bkbgkgrgkgbkbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Harry Lindley in 1988 for E. Leslie Peter; accredited by the Scottish Tartans Society.

The **Peter of Lee** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
