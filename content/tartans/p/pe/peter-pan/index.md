A tartan of [Clan Peter](/clan/peter/).
Its design is pattern [BGGGGGBKGBGBGGGGGBKR](/stripes/bgggggbkgbgbgggggbkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Lochcarron for Great Ormond Street Hospital, beneficiary of Barrie's donated Peter Pan rights.

The **Peter Pan** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
