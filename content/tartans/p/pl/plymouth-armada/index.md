Its design is pattern [BRBBBBWBBBBW](/stripes/brbbbbwbbbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Commemorates the 400th anniversary of the 1588 defeat of the Spanish Armada.

The **Plymouth Armada** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
