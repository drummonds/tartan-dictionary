A tartan of the [Polaris](/families/polaris/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGGGBGGGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkgggbgggkbkb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1964 for US Polaris submarine base at Holy Loch, the first ship to bear its own tartan.

The **Polaris** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
