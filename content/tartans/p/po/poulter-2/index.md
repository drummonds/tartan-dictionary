A tartan of the [Poulter](/families/poulter/) family.
Its design is pattern [RKRKGGGKRKRKR](/stripes/rkrkgggkrkrkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

One of four corporate colourways designed for golfer Ian Poulter's fashion range, woven in polyester/viscose.

The **Poulter** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
