Its design is pattern [WBWBWBYWYBWBW](/stripes/wbwbwbywybwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Pink colourway of corporate tartan for golfer Ian Poulter's fashion range; woven in polyester/viscose.

The **Poulter Pink** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
