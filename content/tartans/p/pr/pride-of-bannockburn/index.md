Its design is pattern [BKGBRGRBGKBWBWB](/stripes/bkgbrgrbgkbwbwb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Fashion design originally Dalgleish's "Scotland the Brave," renamed by Lochcarron, then given a white stripe by Tartan Ribbon.

The **Pride of Bannockburn** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
