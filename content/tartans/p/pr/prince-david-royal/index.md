Its design is pattern [GGYGGYGGGGGGGGY](/stripes/ggyggyggggggggy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed circa 1930 for the personal use of the Duke of Windsor, known as "David" in childhood.

The **Prince David Royal** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
