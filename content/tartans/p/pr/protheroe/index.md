A tartan of [Clan Protheroe](/clan/protheroe/).
Its design is pattern [BGBYBGBBGBGG](/stripes/bgbybgbbgbgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan for Protheroe and variants, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft.

The **Protheroe** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
