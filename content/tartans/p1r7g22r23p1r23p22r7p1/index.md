The **Lovat or Fraser** tartan is one sett proportion (`p1r7g22r23p1r23p22r7p1`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRBRBRGRB](/stripes/brbrbrgrb/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
