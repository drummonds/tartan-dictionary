The **Lions** tartan is one sett proportion (`p25b1ln1k1y1p3ln1y5p4k1b2p2ln1b1ln1p2b2p2k2ln5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BBWKYBWYBKBBWBWBBBKW](/stripes/bbwkybwybkbbwbwbbbkw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
