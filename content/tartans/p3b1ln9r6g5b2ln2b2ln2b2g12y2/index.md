The **Fredericton** tartan is one sett proportion (`p3b1ln9r6g5b2ln2b2ln2b2g12y2`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BBWRGBWBWBGY](/stripes/bbwrgbwbwbgy/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
