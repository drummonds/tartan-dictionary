Possibly designed by John Robertson around 1930; resembles Gordon of Abergeldy but with a red stripe; Aberdeenshire family.

The **Paton** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
