Derived from Dominique Dupra's 1739 portrait of the Duke of Perth; white stripe may be yellow.

The **Perth** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
