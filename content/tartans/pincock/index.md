Designed 2010 for Dougie Pincock, music school director; based on the Anderson tartan, his mother's maiden name.

The **Pincock** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
