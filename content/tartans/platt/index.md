Its design is pattern [GBGGRGBGBRGBGGR](/stripes/gbggrgbgbrgbggr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Modern pattern appearing near the end of the Provosts collection, arranged in order received.

The **Platt** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
