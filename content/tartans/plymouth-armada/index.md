Commemorates the 400th anniversary of the 1588 defeat of the Spanish Armada.

The **Plymouth Armada** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
