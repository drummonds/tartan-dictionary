Its design is pattern [GYGKWYG](/stripes/gygkwyg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Derived from the Maxwell sett of the Vestiarium Scoticum; Pollocks were Lowland feudal dependents of the Maxwells.

The **Pollock** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
