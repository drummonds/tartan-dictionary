Its design is pattern [BYBWBW](/stripes/bybwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2005 by Clair Hunter of House of Edgar for the Pipers' Cove Pipe Band, New Jersey.

The **Port Authority of NY & NJ** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
