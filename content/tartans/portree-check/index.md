Its design is pattern [RBRYRWRBBRBW](/stripes/rbryrwrbbrbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for the Porteous Family Association; submitted by association president Captain Barry Porteous.

The **Portree Check** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
