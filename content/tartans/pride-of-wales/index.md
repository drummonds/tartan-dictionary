Modern Welsh dress tartan, designed for weddings and evening functions, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill for Wales Tartan Centres.

The **Pride of Wales** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
