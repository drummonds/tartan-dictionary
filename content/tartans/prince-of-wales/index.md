Issued by Viyella; a flawed Rothesay pattern (omitting a white line) renamed Prince of Wales rather than withdrawn.

The **Prince of Wales** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
