Its design is pattern [GGKBKWKBKGKGKG](/stripes/ggkbkwkbkgkgkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2009 by Strathmore Woollen Co for Alan Proctor of Forfar, Angus, as a Proctor family tartan.

The **Proctor** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
