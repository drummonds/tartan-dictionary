Its design is pattern [GRGRYRYRYR](/stripes/grgryryryr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Named for Queen Alexandra, with colours drawn from the Provincial Coat of Arms; unregistered with Lord Lyon.

The **Queen Alexandra** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
