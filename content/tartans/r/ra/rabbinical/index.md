A tartan of the [Rabbinical](/families/rabbinical/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKRKBKWKBGRGBW](/stripes/bkrkbkwkbgrgbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Rabbinical** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
