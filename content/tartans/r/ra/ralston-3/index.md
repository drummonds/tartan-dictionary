A tartan of the [Ralston](/families/ralston/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGGGGBBBBBG](/stripes/bggggbbbbbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorates the 60th birthday of Michael R. Ralstin of Ohio; serves the Ralston name and spelling variants.

The **Ralston** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
