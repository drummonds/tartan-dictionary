A tartan of [Clan Rangers](/clan/rangers/).
Its design is pattern [BKBWBRBWBKBKBWBRBWBK](/stripes/bkbwbrbwbkbkbwbrbwbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Chris Aitken in 1994 as the dress version of Rangers F.C.'s corporate tartan.

The **Rangers F. C. Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
