Its design is pattern [BRBKBKBKBKBRBKBKBKBK](/stripes/brbkbkbkbkbrbkbkbkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1989 by Glasgow kiltmakers John MacGregor as the first football club tartan, launched at a Rangers match.

The **Rangers F. C.** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
