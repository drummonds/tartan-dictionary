A tartan of the [Rathmore](/families/rathmore/) family.
Its design is pattern [RBRBRBRBWGWR](/stripes/rbrbrbrbwgwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Rathmore** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
