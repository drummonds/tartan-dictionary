A tartan of [Clan Rattray](/clan/rattray/).
Its design is pattern [GKRBRBRBW](/stripes/gkrbrbrbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan tartan of the Rattrays, followers of the Murrays of Atholl, descended from 13th-century Adam de Rattrieff; seat at Craighall, Blairgowrie.

The **Rattray** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
