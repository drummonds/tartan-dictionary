Its design is pattern [GBGBGBBWBBRBRBRWBRW](/stripes/gbgbgbbwbbrbrbrwbrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Arbroath's official district tartan, adopted 2012, named for the town's "Red Lichtie" nickname.

The **Red Lichtie** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
