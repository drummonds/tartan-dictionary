A tartan of the [Reid](/families/reid/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGBBBGWGBBWBBGBGB](/stripes/bgbbbgwgbbwbbgbgb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Robertson modification worn by Clan Reid, recorded pre-1991 in Dalgleish files.

The **Reid** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
