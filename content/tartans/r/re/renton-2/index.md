A tartan of the [Renton](/families/renton/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBBKKKBBKKKKKBKRKR](/stripes/kbbkkkbbkkkkkbkrkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Personal design based on the Berwick tartan, in muted autumn shades, for the Renton family of that area.

The **Renton** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
