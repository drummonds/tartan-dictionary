A tartan of the [Richard of Wales](/families/richard-of-wales/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGBGBBGBWBGB](/stripes/bgbgbbgbwbgb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Welsh surname tartan (Richards, Pritchard) woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill; asymmetric warp/weft gives a predominant vertical stripe.

The **Richard of Wales** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
