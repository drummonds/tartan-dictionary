A tartan of the [Riddoch](/families/riddoch/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKRG](/stripes/bkbkbkrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed circa 1992 by Tony Murray for Bert Riddoch of Bearsden; based on Campbell of Breadalbane.

The **Riddoch** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
