A tartan of the [Rikaco](/families/rikaco/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWBRBRBGWR](/stripes/bwbrbrbgwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Rikaco** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
