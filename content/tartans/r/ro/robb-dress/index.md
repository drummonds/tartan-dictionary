A tartan of the [Robb](/families/robb/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRGBRGRB](/stripes/brgbrgrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Peter MacDonald in 1994 for Martin Robb of Carroglen, Comrie, Perthshire.

The **Robb Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
