A tartan of the [Robb](/families/robb/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRGBKYGRB](/stripes/brgbkygrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1994 by Peter MacDonald for Martin Robb of Carroglen, Comrie, Perthshire; wearable by anyone named Robb.

The **Robb** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
