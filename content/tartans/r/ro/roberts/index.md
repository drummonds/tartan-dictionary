A tartan of [Clan Roberts](/clan/roberts/).
Its design is pattern [BWKWKWBKBKBR](/stripes/bwkwkwbkbkbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill; differing warp and weft create a predominant vertical stripe.

The **Roberts** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
