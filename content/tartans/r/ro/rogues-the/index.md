Its design is pattern [GKBR](/stripes/gkbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Steven Randall Wothke for his Texas piping and drumming band The Rogues, formed 1994.

The **Rogues , The** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
