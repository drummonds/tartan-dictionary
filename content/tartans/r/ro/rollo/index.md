Its design is pattern [BKBKGGGKGRGK](/stripes/bkbkgggkgrgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for Lord Rollo in 1946; resembles Campbell of Breadalbane, reflecting shared Perthshire roots.

The **Rollo** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
