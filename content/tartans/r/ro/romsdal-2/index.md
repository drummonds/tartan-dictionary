A tartan of the [Romsdal](/families/romsdal/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBRBR](/stripes/gbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Norwegian district tartan from Romsdal, west coast region with unique tartan tradition linked to 1612 Scottish soldiers.

The **Romsdal** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
