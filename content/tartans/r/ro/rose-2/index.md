A tartan of the [Rose](/families/rose/) family.

Its design is pattern [GRBRBRBRW](/stripes/grbrbrbrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


From the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842); regarded by Stewart as a dress sett, with a hunting version recorded by Logan.

The **Rose** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
