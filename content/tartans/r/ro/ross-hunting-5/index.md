A tartan of the [Ross](/families/ross/) family.
Its design is pattern [GWGWGWGKGKGRGR](/stripes/gwgwgwgkgkgrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting variant of Clan Ross, recorded from an 1850 MacGregor-Hastie sample with a light green overcheck.

The **Ross Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
