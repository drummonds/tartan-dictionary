A tartan of the [Rothesay](/families/rothesay/) family.
Its design is pattern [GBYKGYG](/stripes/gbykgyg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

First appeared in the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842) as "Prince of Rothesay"; once a Royal tartan worn by Edward VII as a child.

The **Rothesay** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
