A tartan of the [Rothesay](/families/rothesay/) family.
Its design is pattern [WGRGRGRGRGRGRGWGW](/stripes/wgrgrgrgrgrgrgwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Hunting version introduced in 1906 in Johnston's "Tartans of the Clans and Septs of Scotland."

The **Rothesay Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
