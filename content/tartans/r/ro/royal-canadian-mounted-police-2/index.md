Its design is pattern [BBBWGBBRY](/stripes/bbbwgbbry/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Violet Holmes; official RCMP tartan, weft red replacing orange giving six colours.

The **Royal Canadian Mounted Police** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
