Its design is pattern [KRKBRGY](/stripes/krkbrgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1994 by Jack Dalgety for Royal Marines' Condor Depot, adapting the Angus tartan with altered overcheck.

The **Royal Marines Condor** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
