Best-known Scottish tartan, of the Royal House of Stewart; first published by Logan in 1831.

The **Royal Stewart MINI Design** tartan is one sett, recorded at 3 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
