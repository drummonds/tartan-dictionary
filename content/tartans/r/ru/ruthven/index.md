A tartan of the [Ruthven](/families/ruthven/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGRBGW](/stripes/rgrbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Named in the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum; family held the Barony of Ruthven in Angus and earldom of Gowrie.

The **Ruthven** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
