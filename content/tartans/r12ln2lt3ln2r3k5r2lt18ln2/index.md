The **Ballater** tartan is one sett proportion (`r12ln2lt3ln2r3k5r2lt18ln2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RWRWRKRRW](/stripes/rwrwrkrrw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
