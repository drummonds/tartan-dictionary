The **Stewart/Stuart, Royal #2** tartan is one sett proportion (`r18db2k3ly1k1w1k1g4r2k1r1w1`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RBKYKWKGRKRW](/stripes/rbkykwkgrkrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1800**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
