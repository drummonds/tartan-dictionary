The **MacQuarrie SM** tartan is one sett proportion (`r1b1r6db3r1dg6r1dg6r6db1r1b1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BRBRGRGRBRBR](/stripes/brbrgrgrbrbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
