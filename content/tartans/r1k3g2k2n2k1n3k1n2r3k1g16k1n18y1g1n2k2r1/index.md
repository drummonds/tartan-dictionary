The **Craig** tartan is one sett proportion (`r1k3g2k2n2k1n3k1n2r3k1g16k1n18y1g1n2k2r1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RKGGYGKGKRGKGKGKGKR](/stripes/rkggygkgkrgkgkgkgkr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
