The **Seaforth Estate Check** tartan is one sett proportion (`r1o1k1o1y1o1k1o1y1`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRKRGRKRR](/stripes/grkrgrkrr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1990**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
