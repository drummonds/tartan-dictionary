The **Donachie** tartan is one sett proportion (`r24g2r2g40r25g2r2g2r2g20`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GRGRGRGRGR](/stripes/grgrgrgrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2004**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
