The **Leach (1995)** tartan is one sett proportion (`r24lb1k3lb1g14r8k3dp3lb2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RWKWGRKBW](/stripes/rwkwgrkbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1995**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
