The **MacLachlan #4** tartan is one sett proportion (`r24ln2y3g16k16ln2y3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RWYGKWY](/stripes/rwygkwy/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
