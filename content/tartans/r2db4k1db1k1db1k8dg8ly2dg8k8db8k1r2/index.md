The **Farquharson** tartan is one sett proportion (`r2db4k1db1k1db1k8dg8ly2dg8k8db8k1r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RBKBKBKGYGKBKR](/stripes/rbkbkbkgygkbkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
