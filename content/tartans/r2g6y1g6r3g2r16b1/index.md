The **Burnett** tartan is one sett proportion (`r2g6y1g6r3g2r16b1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRGRGYGR](/stripes/brgrgygr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
