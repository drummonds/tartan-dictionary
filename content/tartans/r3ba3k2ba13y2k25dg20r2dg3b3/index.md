The **Loch Freuchie** tartan is one sett proportion (`r3ba3k2ba13y2k25dg20r2dg3b3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BGRGKYBKBR](/stripes/bgrgkybkbr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
