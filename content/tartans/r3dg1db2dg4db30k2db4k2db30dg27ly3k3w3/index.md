The **Joss** tartan is one sett proportion (`r3dg1db2dg4db30k2db4k2db30dg27ly3k3w3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBGBKBKBGYKWKYGBKBKBGBGR](/stripes/gbgbkbkbgykwkygbkbkbgbgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1999**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
