The **Mantle (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`r3g2r3g18k14w2db16g3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBWKGRGR](/stripes/gbwkgrgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2006**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
