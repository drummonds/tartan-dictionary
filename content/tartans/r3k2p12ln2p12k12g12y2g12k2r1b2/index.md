The **Rust** tartan is one sett proportion (`r3k2p12ln2p12k12g12y2g12k2r1b2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRKGYGKBWBKR](/stripes/brkgygkbwbkr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
