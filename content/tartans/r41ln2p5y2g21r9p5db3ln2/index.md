The **Perthshire, or Drummond** tartan is one sett proportion (`r41ln2p5y2g21r9p5db3ln2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RWBYGRBBW](/stripes/rwbygrbbw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
