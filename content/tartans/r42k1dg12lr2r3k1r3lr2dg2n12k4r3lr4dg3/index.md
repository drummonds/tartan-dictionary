The **MacFarlane** tartan is one sett proportion (`r42k1dg12lr2r3k1r3lr2dg2n12k4r3lr4dg3`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GYRKBGYRKRYGKR](/stripes/gyrkbgyrkrygkr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
