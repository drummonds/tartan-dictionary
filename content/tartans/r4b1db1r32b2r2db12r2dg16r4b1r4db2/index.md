The **MacGillivray** tartan is one sett proportion (`r4b1db1r32b2r2db12r2dg16r4b1r4db2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BRBRGRBRBRBBR](/stripes/brbrgrbrbrbbr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
