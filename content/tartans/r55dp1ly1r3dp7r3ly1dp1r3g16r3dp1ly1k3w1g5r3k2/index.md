The **MacFarhadian (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`r55dp1ly1r3dp7r3ly1dp1r3g16r3dp1ly1k3w1g5r3k2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYRBRYBRGRBYKWGRKRGWKYBRGRBYRBRYBR](/stripes/byrbrybrgrbykwgrkrgwkybrgrbyrbrybr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2003**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
