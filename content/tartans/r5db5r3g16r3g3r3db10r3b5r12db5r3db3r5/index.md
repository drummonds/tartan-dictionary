The **Grant of Ballindalloch** tartan is one sett proportion (`r5db5r3g16r3g3r3db10r3b5r12db5r3db3r5`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RKRGRGRKRBRKRKR](/stripes/rkrgrgrkrbrkrkr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
