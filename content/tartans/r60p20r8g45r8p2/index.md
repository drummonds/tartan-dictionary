The **Caledonian** tartan is one sett proportion (`r60p20r8g45r8p2`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BRGRBR](/stripes/brgrbr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
