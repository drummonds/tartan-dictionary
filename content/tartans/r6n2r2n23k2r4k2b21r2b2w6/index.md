The **IRPA** tartan is one sett proportion (`r6n2r2n23k2r4k2b21r2b2w6`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RBRBKRKBRBW](/stripes/rbrbkrkbrbw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
