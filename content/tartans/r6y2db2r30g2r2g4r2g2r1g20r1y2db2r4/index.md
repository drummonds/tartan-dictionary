The **All Ireland Red** tartan is one sett proportion (`r6y2db2r30g2r2g4r2g2r1g20r1y2db2r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GBRGRGRGRGRGBRBGRGRGRGRGRBGR](/stripes/gbrgrgrgrgrgbrbgrgrgrgrgrbgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1997**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
