The **MacDuff** tartan is one sett proportion (`r8db3k4g6r4k1r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RBKGRKR](/stripes/rbkgrkr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
