The **Munro** tartan is one sett proportion (`ra24y1db1ra3g16ra3db1y1ra3db6ra3y1db1ra16g2r2g2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [GRGRBYRBRYBRGRBYR](/stripes/grgrbyrbrybrgrbyr/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
