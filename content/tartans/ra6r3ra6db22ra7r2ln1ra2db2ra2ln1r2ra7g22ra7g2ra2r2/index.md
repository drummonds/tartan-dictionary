The **MacColl Hunting** tartan is one sett proportion (`ra6r3ra6db22ra7r2ln1ra2db2ra2ln1r2ra7g22ra7g2ra2r2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RRGRGRRWRBRWRRBRRR](/stripes/rrgrgrrwrbrwrrbrrr/); each scale
