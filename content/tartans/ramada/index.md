Its design is pattern [GRRGRBBRBRBBRGRBBR](/stripes/grrgrbbrbrbbrgrbbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Corporate tartan re-created from a Telfer Dunbar collection artifact, featuring an enhanced bleaching effect typical of aged silk fabrics.

The **Ramada** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
