date: 2023-03-18

Designed 1989 by Glasgow kiltmakers John MacGregor as the first football club tartan, launched at a Rangers match.

The **Rangers F. C.** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
