Its design is pattern [RGGRKW](/stripes/rggrkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Robert Reekie for his sister Charlene, honouring her dedication to Reekie heraldry; colours symbolise family virtues.

The **Reekie, Charlene** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
