Speculative 'Old Superfine' sett from 1847; suggestion it was designed for silk weaving deemed unlikely.

The **Ritch** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
