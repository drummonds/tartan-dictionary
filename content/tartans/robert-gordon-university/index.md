Designed by Mike King of Philip King Kiltmakers, Aberdeen, for Robert Gordon University.

The **Robert Gordon University** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
