Its design is pattern [BYGRWRG](/stripes/bygrwrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for Rotary International, launched at the 1997 Rotary World Convention in Glasgow.

The **Rotary** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
