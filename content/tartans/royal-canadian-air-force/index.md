Royal Canadian Air Force regimental tartan in dark blue, light blue and maroon, based on the Anderson sett.

The **Royal Canadian Air Force** tartan groups 7 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
