Named in the 1842 Vestiarium Scoticum; family held the Barony of Ruthven in Angus and earldom of Gowrie.

The **Ruthven** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
