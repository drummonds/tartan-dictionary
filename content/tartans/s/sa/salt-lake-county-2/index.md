Designed for Salt Lake County, Utah, founded 1850; publicly accepted 18 September 1996.

The **Salt Lake County** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
