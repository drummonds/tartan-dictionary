A tartan of the [Saorsa](/families/saorsa/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBBBKGB](/stripes/bbbbkgb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 2012 for the Fellowship of the Thistle; "Saorsa" means freedom in Gaelic, colours symbolising Scotland's landscape, saltire, thistle and union.

The **Saorsa** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
