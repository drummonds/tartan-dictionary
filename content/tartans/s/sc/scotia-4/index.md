Its design is pattern [BBWYBBBG](/stripes/bbwybbbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by James Allan of East Kilbride in 1850, later reconstructed by David Easton as a national tartan; never popular.

The **Scotia** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
