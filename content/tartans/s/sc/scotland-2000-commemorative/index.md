A tartan of [Clan Scotland](/clan/scotland/).
Its design is pattern [BGBGBGWW](/stripes/bgbgbgww/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Arthur Mackie of Strathmore Woollen Co., Forfar, to commemorate the millennium.

The **Scotland 2000 Commemorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
