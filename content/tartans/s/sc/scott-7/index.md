A tartan of the [Scott](/families/scott/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKWKGBGKGRGWGKGWGRGKGBGKWK](/stripes/gkwkgbgkgrgwgkgwgrgkgbgkwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Clan tartan, also called Green Scott; chief is the Duke of Buccleuch and Queensberry, based in Selkirk.

The **Scott** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
