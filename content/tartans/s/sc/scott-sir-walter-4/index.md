A tartan of [Clan Scott](/clan/scott/).
Its design is pattern [RWBWKWKWKWKWBW](/stripes/rwbwkwkwkwkwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Sir Walter Scott in 1822 for his private use as a Lowland shepherd's plaid.

The **Scott, Sir Walter** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
