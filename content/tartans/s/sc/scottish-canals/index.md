Its design is pattern [GBBBGBG](/stripes/gbbbgbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Corporate tartan designed by Claire Donaldson of House of Edgar for British Waterways Board; renamed twice, becoming Scottish Canals in 2003.

The **Scottish Canals** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
