Its design is pattern [BBBBBBWBWBBBBB](/stripes/bbbbbbwbwbbbbb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Based on Grampian with maroon and lavender for heather and thistles; designed for Gavin Kiltmakers of Fraserburgh's hire range.

The **Scottish Highlander Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
