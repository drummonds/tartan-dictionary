Its design is pattern [BWBRBKGKGKGBWR](/stripes/bwbrbkgkgkgbwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1994 by McCalls of Aberdeen, adapting the 1934 'National' tartan; registered patented design No. 601292.

The **Scottish National Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
