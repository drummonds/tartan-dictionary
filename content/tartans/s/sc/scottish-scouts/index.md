Its design is pattern [BBBBBBRBRBBBBBBBRBRBBBBB](/stripes/bbbbbbrbrbbbbbbbrbrbbbbb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Based on MacLaren, honouring a MacLaren estate donor; red checks denote Rovers and Scouts, yellow the Wolf Cubs.

The **Scottish Scouts** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
