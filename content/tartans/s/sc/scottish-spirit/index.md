Its design is pattern [BRBBKBKBKBKW](/stripes/brbbkbkbkbkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Lochcarron for ACS Clothing; named "Scottish Spirit" after "Highland Spirit" was unavailable.

The **Scottish Spirit** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
