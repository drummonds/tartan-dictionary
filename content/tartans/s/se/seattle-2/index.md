A tartan of the [Seattle](/families/seattle/) family.

Its design is pattern [GWBRGRBWGWBRGRBWGY](/stripes/gwbrgrbwgwbrgrbwgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Honors Seattle with emerald green, Aegean blue, primrose pink for rhododendrons, white for mountains and summer sun.

The **Seattle** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
