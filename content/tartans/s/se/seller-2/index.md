A tartan of the [Seller](/families/seller/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKWGBGBGRWRW](/stripes/gkwgbgbgrwrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on the Sillar family's connection with the Isle of Arran and Clan Stewart.

The **Seller** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
