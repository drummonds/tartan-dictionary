Its design is pattern [KGKGRBY](/stripes/kgkgrby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed for the 2008 wedding of Nathalie Selvon and Alex Bruce, blending Ancient Bruce red and green with Mauritian flag colours.

The **Selvon-Bruce** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
