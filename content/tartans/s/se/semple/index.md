A tartan of the [Semple](/families/semple/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKBKGWGKBKBKBR](/stripes/bkbkbkgwgkbkbkbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 1996 by Iain Semple of Tamworth for anyone of the name; resembles Sempill #2420.

The **Semple** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
