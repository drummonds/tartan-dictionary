A tartan of the [Shaw of Carolina](/families/shaw-of-carolina/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKGRBWGKGKBKBWBRBKBK](/stripes/gkgrbwgkgkbkbwbrbkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Based on a c.1800 cloth fragment and blanket worn by Mary Irvine for husband John Shaw.

The **Shaw of Carolina** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
