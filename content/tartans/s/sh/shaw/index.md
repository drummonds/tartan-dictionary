A tartan of [Clan Shaw](/clan/shaw/).
Its design is pattern [GKBKBRBKBK](/stripes/gkbkbrbkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Shaw clan sett, published by McIan c.1845 from a Black Watch–derived error; later superseded by a Mackintosh/Clan Chattan–based design.

The **Shaw** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
