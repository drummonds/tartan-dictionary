A tartan of the [Shepherd](/families/shepherd/) family.
Its design is pattern [GWGW](/stripes/gwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Black-and-white check from cloth found at Falkirk in a coin jar buried c.260 A.D.; woven today as Shepherd.

The **Shepherd** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
