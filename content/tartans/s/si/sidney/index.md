A tartan of the [Sidney](/families/sidney/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKWKBRBR](/stripes/bkwkbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Natural light/dark wool check found at Falkirk in a Roman coin jar (c. 260 AD); woven today as the Shepherd tartan.

The **Sidney** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
