A tartan of the [Sikh](/families/sikh/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGRKRKRKRKRGBG](/stripes/bgrkrkrkrkrgbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Commissioned by A.J. Singh in 1999 marking his family's Scottish arrival and Sikhism's tercentenary; for any Sikh.

The **Sikh** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
