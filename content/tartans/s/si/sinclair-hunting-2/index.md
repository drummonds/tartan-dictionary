Its design is pattern [GRGKWKR](/stripes/grgkwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Green hunting sett; Sinclairs wore green at Flodden, where all but the drummer fell.

The **Sinclair Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
