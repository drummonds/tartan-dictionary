A tartan of the [Singh](/families/singh/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBBGYGWB](/stripes/bbbgygwb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Created in 1999 for those bearing the name Singh; proposed by Sirdar Iqubal Singh, Lord of Butley.

The **Singh** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
