A tartan of the [Skinner](/families/skinner/) family.
Its design is pattern [BRKYKR](/stripes/brkykr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Wallace variant with blue substituted for the black overcheck, worn by Dundee-born John Skinner; now worn by all Skinners.

The **Skinner** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
