A tartan of the [Smith](/families/smith/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKGKKKGKBW](/stripes/bkgkkkgkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Gow Hunting sett with dark blue stripe changed to azure; Gow is Gaelic for Smith, meaning metalworker.

The **Smith** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
