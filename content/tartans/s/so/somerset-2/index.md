A tartan of the [Somerset](/families/somerset/) family.
Its design is pattern [BYRWKWKWKWKWRYBG](/stripes/byrwkwkwkwkwrybg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for Somerset, with colours symbolising its rivers, peat, abbeys, Cheddar pinks, and hills.

The **Somerset** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
