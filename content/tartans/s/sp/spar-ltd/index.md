Its design is pattern [GKBKBKBKGWGR](/stripes/gkbkbkbkgwgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for UK grocery chain Spar's 1997 Scottish conference, launched at Blair Castle, Perthshire.

The **Spar Ltd** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
