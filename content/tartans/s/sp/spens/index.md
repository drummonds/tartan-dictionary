Its design is pattern [RWBWGRBWW](/stripes/rwbwgrbww/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dating from c.1815, resembles Perthshire District sett; linked to a Highland Society of London specimen.

The **Spens** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
