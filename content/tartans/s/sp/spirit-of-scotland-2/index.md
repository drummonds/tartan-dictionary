Its design is pattern [BBBBBBBGBKBKBGBBBBBB](/stripes/bbbbbbbgbkbkbgbbbbbb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2002 by Michael King, Aberdeen; adopted by National Trust for Scotland for Fyvie Castle.

The **Spirit of Scotland** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
