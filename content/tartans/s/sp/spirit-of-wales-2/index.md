Its design is pattern [BKBBBKBBBBBKBBBKBW](/stripes/bkbbbkbbbbbkbbbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2005 by Claire Donaldson of House of Edgar for the exclusive use of Dyfed Menswear, Llanelli.

The **Spirit of Wales** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
