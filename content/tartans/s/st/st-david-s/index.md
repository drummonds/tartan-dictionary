Its design is pattern [GRGRGW](/stripes/grgrgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh national tartan launched 2002 at Cardiff Castle, honouring St David; woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill.

The **St. David's** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
