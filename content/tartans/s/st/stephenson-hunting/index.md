A tartan of the [Stephenson](/families/stephenson/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKGKWKWKGKBKG](/stripes/bkgkwkwkgkbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Hunting variant based on an old un-named sett in Bolingbroke and Jones of Norwich records, pre-1870.

The **Stephenson Hunting** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
