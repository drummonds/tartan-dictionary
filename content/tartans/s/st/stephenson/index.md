A tartan of the [Stephenson](/families/stephenson/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBGBYKBBBGKGBBBKYBGB](/stripes/bbgbykbbbgkgbbbkybgb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed c. 1975 by Jack Dalgety and R C Stephenson from a pre-1870 un-named sett in Bolingbroke and Jones' records.

The **Stephenson** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
