A tartan of the [Stevenson](/families/stevenson/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGYBYBYBYBYBYBYG](/stripes/bgybybybybybybyg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Family tartan recorded 1980 for the Stevensons; Charles Stevenson of Glasgow emigrated to America in 1861.

The **Stevenson** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
