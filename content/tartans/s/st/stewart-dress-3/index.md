A tartan of the [Stewart](/families/stewart/) family.

Its design is pattern [WBWKWKGKWKGRKRW](/stripes/wbwkwkgkwkgrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress version of Logan's Royal Stewart, with blue joined to black and equal yellow and white; worldwide symbol of Scotland.

The **Stewart Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
