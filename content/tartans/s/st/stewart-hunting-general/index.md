A tartan of [Clan Stewart](/clan/stewart/).
Its design is pattern [BGBKBKGRGKGKGKGKGGGKBK](/stripes/bgbkbkgrgkgkgkgkgggkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

First recorded in Wilson's of Bannockburn pattern books; unusually repeats the full sett rather than mirroring it.

The **Stewart Hunting General** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
