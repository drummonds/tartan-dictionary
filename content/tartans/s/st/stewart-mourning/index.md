A tartan of the [Stewart](/families/stewart/) family.

Its design is pattern [KBKBKBKBKWK](/stripes/kbkbkbkbkwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Black-and-white mourning variant of Stewart tartan, first recorded in the 1880 Paris publication 'Clan Originaux'.

The **Stewart Mourning** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
