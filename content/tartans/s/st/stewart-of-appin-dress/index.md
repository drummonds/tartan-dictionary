A tartan of the [Stewart of Appin](/families/stewart-of-appin/) family.
Its design is pattern [GWWKWGRBRGRGRWKR](/stripes/gwwkwgrbrgrgrwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dress variant for Stewarts of Appin, descended from Dugald; worn by Stonehaven pipe bands.

The **Stewart of Appin Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
