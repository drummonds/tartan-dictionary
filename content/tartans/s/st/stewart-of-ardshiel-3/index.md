A tartan of the [Stewart of Ardshiel](/families/stewart-of-ardshiel/) family.

Its design is pattern [GRRKRKWRKRWKRGRRKW](/stripes/grrkrkwrkrwkrgrrkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Earliest 1822 record of Stewart of Ardshiel; resembles Stewart of Appin with red and blue interchanged.

The **Stewart of Ardshiel** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
