A tartan of the [Stewart of Atholl](/families/stewart-of-atholl/) family.
Its design is pattern [KRKGKGKGKGKGKRKR](/stripes/krkgkgkgkgkgkrkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Prepared for the Vestiarium Scoticum but unpublished; later claimed as a '45 relic and published by D.W. Stewart in 1893.

The **Stewart of Atholl** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
