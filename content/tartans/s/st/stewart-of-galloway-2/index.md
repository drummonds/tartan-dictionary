A tartan of the [Stewart of Galloway](/families/stewart-of-galloway/) family.

Its design is pattern [GGRGKWKGKWRG](/stripes/ggrgkwkgkwrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Clan tartan recorded c.1820 from a specimen at the Smith Institute, Stirling.

The **Stewart of Galloway** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
