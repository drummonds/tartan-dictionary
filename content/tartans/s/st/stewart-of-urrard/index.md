A tartan of the [Stewart of Urrard](/families/stewart-of-urrard/) family.

Its design is pattern [GRGRBRGRBRGRGR](/stripes/grgrbrgrbrgrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Documented from a pre-1930 kilt owned by a Stewart Society member; threadcount recorded by Mackinlay.

The **Stewart of Urrard** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
