A tartan of [Clan Stewart](/clan/stewart/).
Its design is pattern [BKGKGKBRKRKRGKBKG](/stripes/bkgkgkbrkrkrgkbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Worn by the Appin Stewarts, allies of Clan MacColl and distinct from the Royal Stewart; echoed in the Ardshiel branch tartan, worn by Stonehaven pipe bands, and also recorded as Stewart of Bute.

The **Stewart Old** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
