A tartan of [Clan Stewart](/clan/stewart/).

Its design is pattern [RWKGKWKGRKRW](/stripes/rwkgkwkgrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Royal Stewart variant with reduced red square; reputedly worn by Prince Charles Edward at Holyrood, now adopted by several pipe bands.

The **Stewart Prince Charles Edward** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
