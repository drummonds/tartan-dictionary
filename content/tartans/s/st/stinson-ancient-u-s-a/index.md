A tartan of [Clan Stinson](/clan/stinson/).

Its design is pattern [BRBGGKWRWBK](/stripes/brbggkwrwbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Documented by Mackinlay from a pre-1930 kilt owned by a Stewart Society member.

The **Stinson Ancient U.S.A.** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
