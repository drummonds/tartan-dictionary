A tartan of [Clan Stirling](/clan/stirling/).
Its design is pattern [RGRWRKRYGY](/stripes/rgrwrkrygy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Produced by Wilson's of Bannockburn c.1847 for the Caledonian Society; now the district tartan for the Stirling-Bannockburn area.

The **Stirling and Bannockburn** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
