A tartan of the [Strachan](/families/strachan/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBKGKGKRKGKGKBKR](/stripes/kbkgkgkrkgkgkbkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1987 by A.G. Murray as a personal tartan, now available to all of the name Strachan.

The **Strachan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
