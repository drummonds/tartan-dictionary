Its design is pattern [RWGRWWKBWGWKW](/stripes/rwgrwwkbwgwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Scott Baughman for the Stratford Police Pipe Band, Ontario; blue, white, gold for the city, red and black for police service.

The **Stratford Police Pipe Band** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
