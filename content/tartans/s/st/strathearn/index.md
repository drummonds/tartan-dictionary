A tartan of the [Strathearn](/families/strathearn/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGRGGGRGRGRGGRGRGGRGRGRGGGRGR](/stripes/grgrgggrgrgrggrgrggrgrgrgggrgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Said worn by Edward, Duke of Kent and Strathearn; adopted by Comrie Pipe Band.

The **Strathearn** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
