A tartan of the [Strathspey](/families/strathspey/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWKWBWKWB](/stripes/bwkwbwkwb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Black Watch variation from a 1794-5 Strathspey Fencible waistcoat, attributed to General James Grant of Ballindalloch.

The **Strathspey** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
