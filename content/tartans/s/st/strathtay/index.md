A tartan of the [Strathtay](/families/strathtay/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGRRRRBRBGWGW](/stripes/bgrrrrbrbgwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

District tartan for the Tay valley, its colours drawn from autumn foliage and the river's blue-green waters.

The **Strathtay** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
