A tartan of the [Strathtummel](/families/strathtummel/) family.
Its design is pattern [BKBKKKWKGKGKGK](/stripes/bkbkkkwkgkgkgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

District tartan for the Tummel valley, echoing summer heather, mountains, ferns, and enduring snowfields.

The **Strathtummel** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
