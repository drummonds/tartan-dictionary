A tartan of the [Stuart of Bute](/families/stuart-of-bute/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGKGKGKRW](/stripes/rgkgkgkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Family tartan from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842); often seen in maroon, though the Marquis of Bute favours red.

The **Stuart of Bute** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
