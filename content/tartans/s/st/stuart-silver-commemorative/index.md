A tartan of [Clan Stuart](/clan/stuart/).

Its design is pattern [BWBGBWBGGBGW](/stripes/bwbgbwbggbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed for Queen Elizabeth II's 1977 Silver Jubilee, but never officially adopted.

The **Stuart Silver Commemorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
