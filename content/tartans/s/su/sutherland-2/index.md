A tartan of the [Sutherland](/families/sutherland/) family.
Its design is pattern [GWGKBKBKBRBRBRBKBKBKGW](/stripes/gwgkbkbkbrbrbrbkbkbkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dark Sutherland clan sett; 1618 letter ordered red and white lines removed for sept harmony.

The **Sutherland** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
