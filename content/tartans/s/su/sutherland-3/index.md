A tartan of the [Sutherland](/families/sutherland/) family.

Its design is pattern [GWGKBKBKBRBR](/stripes/gwgkbkbkbrbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dark Sutherland clan sett; 1618 letter ordered red and white lines removed for sept harmony.

The **Sutherland** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
