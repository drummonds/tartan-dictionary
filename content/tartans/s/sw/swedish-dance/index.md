Its design is pattern [BYKYKYKGBBW](/stripes/bykykykgbbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed and woven by Peter McDonald to represent a Scottish-Swedish connection, available for general use.

The **Swedish Dance** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
