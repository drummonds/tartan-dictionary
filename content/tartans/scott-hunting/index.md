Green-based hunting sett of Clan Scott, also called Green Scott; recorded from 1906.

The **Scott Hunting** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
