Designed by Sir Walter Scott in 1822 for his private use as a Lowland shepherd's plaid.

The **Scott, Sir Walter** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
