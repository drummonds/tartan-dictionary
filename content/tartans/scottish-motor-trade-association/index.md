Corporate tartan for the Scottish Motor Trade Association, established 1903.

The **Scottish Motor Trade Association** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
