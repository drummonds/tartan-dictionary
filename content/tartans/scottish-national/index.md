Hunting variant of the Scottish National universal tartan, designed 1996 by Michael King of Philip King Ltd, Aberdeen.

The **Scottish National** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
