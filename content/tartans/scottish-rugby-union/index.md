Its design is pattern [BKBKGRGRGKWKGRGRGKBK](/stripes/bkbkgrgrgkwkgrgrgkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for the Scottish Rugby Union, featuring jersey navy, thistle green and lilac, and short white.

The **Scottish Rugby Union** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
