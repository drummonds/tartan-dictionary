Designed by Donald C. Stewart in 1970; displayed at a Scrymgeour gathering at Dudhope Castle, Dundee, 1971.

The **Scrymgeour** tartan groups 4 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
