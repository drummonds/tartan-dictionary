Based on the Sillar family's connection with the Isle of Arran and Clan Stewart.

The **Seller Sillar** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
