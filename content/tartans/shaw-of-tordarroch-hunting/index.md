A tartan of the [Shaw of Tordarroch,](/families/shaw-of-tordarroch/) family.

Hunting variant of Shaw of Tordarroch, with sage green ground; also called 'Green Shaw of Tordarroch'.

The **Shaw of Tordarroch Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
