Launched 1996 for the opening of American computer firm Simple Technology's circuit-board plant in East Kilbride.

The **Simple Technology** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
