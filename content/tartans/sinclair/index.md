A tartan of [Clan Sinclair](/clan/sinclair/).

Sinclair clan tartan, a minor variation on the Cockburn specimen (1810-15), depicted in a portrait of Alexander, 13th Earl of Caithness.

The **Sinclair** tartan groups 9 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
