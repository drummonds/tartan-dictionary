Wallace variant with blue substituted for the black overcheck, worn by Dundee-born John Skinner; now worn by all Skinners.

The **Skinner** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
