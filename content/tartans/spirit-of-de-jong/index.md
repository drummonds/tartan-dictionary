Its design is pattern [BKBGBBKWRWKBBYB](/stripes/bkbgbbkwrwkbbyb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 2012 by Marno and Mickey de Jong, based on Spirit of Scotland with Dutch national colours added.

The **Spirit of de Jong** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
