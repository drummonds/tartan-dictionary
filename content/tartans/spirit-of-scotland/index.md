Designed 2002 by Michael King, Aberdeen; adopted by National Trust for Scotland for Fyvie Castle.

The **Spirit of Scotland** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
