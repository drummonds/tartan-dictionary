Designed in 2005 by Claire Donaldson of House of Edgar for the exclusive use of Dyfed Menswear, Llanelli.

The **Spirit of Wales** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
