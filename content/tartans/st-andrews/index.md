Designed by Robin Elliot of Elliots of Selkirk for St Andrews Ltd of Edinburgh.

The **St Andrews** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
