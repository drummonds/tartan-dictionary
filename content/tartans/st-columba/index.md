Designed by Peter MacDonald for St. Columba's Church, Mull, marking the 1400th anniversary of St. Columba's death.

The **St. Columba** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
