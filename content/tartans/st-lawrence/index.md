Its design is pattern [KBGBBBBBBBBYKBGKR](/stripes/kbgbbbbbbbbykbgkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **St. Lawrence** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
