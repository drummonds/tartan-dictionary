A tartan of [Clan Stewart](/clan/stewart/).

Black-and-white mourning variant of Stewart tartan, first recorded in the 1880 Paris publication 'Clan Originaux'.

The **Stewart Mourning** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
