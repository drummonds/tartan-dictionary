A tartan of the [Stewart of Appin](/families/stewart-of-appin/) family.

Hunting sett for Clan Stewart of Appin; brown likely originally black, similar to early 19th-century count.

The **Stewart of Appin Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
