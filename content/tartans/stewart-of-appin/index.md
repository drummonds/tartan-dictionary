A tartan of the [Stewart of Appin](/families/stewart-of-appin/) family.

Worn by the Appin Stewarts, allies of Clan MacColl and distinct from the Royal Stewart; echoed in the Ardshiel branch tartan, worn by Stonehaven pipe bands, and also recorded as Stewart of Bute.

The **Stewart of Appin** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
