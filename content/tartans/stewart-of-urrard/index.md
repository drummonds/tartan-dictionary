A tartan of the [Stewart of Urrard](/families/stewart-of-urrard/) family.

Documented from a pre-1930 kilt owned by a Stewart Society member; threadcount recorded by Mackinlay.

The **Stewart of Urrard** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
