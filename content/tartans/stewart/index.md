A tartan of [Clan Stewart](/clan/stewart/).
Its design is pattern [KBWKGKWKGRKRW](/stripes/kbwkgkwkgrkrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Black variant of Stewart, c.1830, worn by Royal Scots Dragoon Guards and Grampian Police pipe bands.

The **Stewart** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
