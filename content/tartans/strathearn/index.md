Said worn by Edward, Duke of Kent and Strathearn; adopted by Comrie Pipe Band.

The **Strathearn** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
