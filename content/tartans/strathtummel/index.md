District tartan for the Tummel valley, echoing summer heather, mountains, ferns, and enduring snowfields.

The **Strathtummel** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
