Its design is pattern [GBGWGGBGBWBGBGGWGBGW](/stripes/gbgwggbgbwbgbggwgbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Dance-oriented district tartan from 1996, possibly designed as a Dancers' Fancy.

The **Strathyre Dress** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
