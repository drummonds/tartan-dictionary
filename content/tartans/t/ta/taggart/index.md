A tartan of the [Taggart](/families/taggart/) family.
Its design is pattern [RGKRKGBRKRKRBWKRKWR](/stripes/rgkrkgbrkrkrbwkrkwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for personal use and woven by Elliots of Selkirk.

The **Taggart** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
