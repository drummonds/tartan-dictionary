Its design is pattern [RWRKBGBKBKBKBW](/stripes/rwrkbgbkbkbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Marks the centennial of the Republic of China (Taiwan), commissioned by Scotland's Taiwanese community; based on Caledonian sett with Mackay and Maxwell elements honouring missionaries, using flag colours.

The **Taiwan Scottish** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
