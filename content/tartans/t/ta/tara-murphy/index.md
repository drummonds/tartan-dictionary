Its design is pattern [KGRGGKWKGKRK](/stripes/kgrggkwkgkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Irish family pattern, colour variation of O'Keefe; likely originated at Waterford Mill before reaching Pendleton late 19th century.

The **Tara Murphy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
