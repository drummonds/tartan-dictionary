A tartan of the [Taylor](/families/taylor/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKGRGBGY](/stripes/gkgrgbgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Lt Col Iain Cameron Taylor, resembling Vestiarium Scoticum Cameron; coral stripe; recognised by Cameron of Lochiel.

The **Taylor** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
