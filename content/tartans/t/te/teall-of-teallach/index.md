A tartan of the [Teall of Teallach](/families/teall-of-teallach/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRKWGBKWK](/stripes/grkwgbkwk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Adopted by the Scottish Tartans Society honouring chairman Dr Gordon Teall for years of service.

The **Teall of Teallach** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
