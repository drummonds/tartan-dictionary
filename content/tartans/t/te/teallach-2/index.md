A tartan of the [Teallach](/families/teallach/) family.
Its design is pattern [GRGBGWGRY](/stripes/grgbgwgry/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed for Dr Gordon Teall of Teallach, chairman of the Scottish Tartans Society.

The **Teallach** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
