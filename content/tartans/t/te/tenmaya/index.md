Its design is pattern [WBBGBGBW](/stripes/wbbgbgbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed 1996 for Tenmaya Department Store Ltd, Okayama, Japan; woven by Archie Small of Selkirk for a British promotion.

The **Tenmaya** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
