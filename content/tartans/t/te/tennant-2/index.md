A tartan of the [Tennant](/families/tennant/) family.
Its design is pattern [KGBR](/stripes/kgbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Tennant family tartan, likely designed by Captain Tennant's father; family headed by Lord Glenconner.

The **Tennant** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
