Its design is pattern [RBWBGRGBGW](/stripes/rbwbgrgbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Official Tennessee state tartan recorded 1999; colours symbolize agriculture, Smoky Mountains, the iris, veterans' sacrifices, and the state's three divisions.

The **Tennessee State** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
