Its design is pattern [RBKWKR](/stripes/rbkwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1958 for Lord Thomson of Fleet from a mid-19th-century Moy Hall sample; also suits MacTavishs and Thompsons.

The **Thompson** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
