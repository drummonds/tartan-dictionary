A tartan of [Clan Thompson](/clan/thompson/).
Its design is pattern [RYKWKW](/stripes/rykwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Camel variation of blue Thomson, designed 1967 by Scotty Thompson; often confused with the Burberry Check.

The **Thompson Camel** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
