A tartan of [Clan Thompson](/clan/thompson/).
Its design is pattern [RBWGBGR](/stripes/rbwgbgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Possibly designed by Councillor John Hannay; earliest known example from Oregon.

The **Thompson Navy** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
