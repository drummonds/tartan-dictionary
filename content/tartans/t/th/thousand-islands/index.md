Its design is pattern [BWYKWRWGRW](/stripes/bwykwrwgrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Grindstone Island weaver Emily Post in the 1970s; light blue for skies and granite, dark blue for rivers, green for trees, orange for rainbows.

The **Thousand Islands** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
