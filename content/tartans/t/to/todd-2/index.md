A tartan of the [Todd](/families/todd/) family.

Its design is pattern [BGKGWGWGR](/stripes/bgkgwgwgr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Variation of Tweedside Green Hunting with red pivot, created for Gregory V Todd; Todd is Border dialect for fox.

The **Todd** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
