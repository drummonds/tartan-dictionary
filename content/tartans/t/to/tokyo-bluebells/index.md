Designed by Donald and Fiona Fraser for a Tokyo-based Scottish Country Dance club.

The **Tokyo Bluebells** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
