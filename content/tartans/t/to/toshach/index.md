A tartan of the [Toshach](/families/toshach/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGGWGGGGWGWGRGGGGWGGGW](/stripes/gggwggggwgwgrggggwgggw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Derived from an heirloom rug of a family descended from Sir Walter Scott's reputed librarian; Toshach is an early Celtic chiefly title.

The **Toshach** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
