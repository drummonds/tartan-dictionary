Its design is pattern [BKGKYB](/stripes/bkgkyb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Private family tartan designed 1997 by Peter MacDonald for Kenneth Tough, based on Tribe of Mar with grey added for salmon farming.

The **Tough** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
