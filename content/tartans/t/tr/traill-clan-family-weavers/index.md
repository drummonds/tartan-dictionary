A tartan of [Clan Traill](/clan/traill/).
Its design is pattern [BGGGBKG](/stripes/bgggbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2002 for descendants of the Traills of Blebo, in Orkney and elsewhere.

The **Traill Clan/Family Weavers** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
