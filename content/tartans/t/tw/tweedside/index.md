A tartan of the [Tweedside](/families/tweedside/) family.
Its design is pattern [KRKRWRWRK](/stripes/krkrwrwrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Named by Wilson after the Tweedside district; a hunting version has recently been revived.

The **Tweedside** tartan is one sett, recorded at 2 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
