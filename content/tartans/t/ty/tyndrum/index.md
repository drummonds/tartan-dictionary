Its design is pattern [BKBKBKYKBK](/stripes/bkbkbkykbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Tyndrum** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
