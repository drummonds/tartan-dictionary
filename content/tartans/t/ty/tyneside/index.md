Its design is pattern [BBBBBBGBGBBBB](/stripes/bbbbbbgbgbbbb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Intended as a regimental tartan but rejected by the War Office; adopted instead as a district tartan.

The **Tyneside** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
