The **Thompson/Thomson/MacTavish** tartan is one sett proportion (`t2r12db2t6k6t1`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKBBRB](/stripes/bkbbrb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
