The **Ogilvie Hunting** tartan is one sett proportion (`t30ly3t4k25y26k3y3r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYBKGKGR](/stripes/bybkgkgr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2000**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
