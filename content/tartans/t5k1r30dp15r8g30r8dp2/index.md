The **Shaw of Tordarroch Red (Dress)** tartan is one sett proportion (`t5k1r30dp15r8g30r8dp2`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BKRBRGRB](/stripes/bkrbrgrb/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1971**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
