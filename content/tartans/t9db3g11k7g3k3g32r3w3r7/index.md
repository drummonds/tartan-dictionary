The **Lyons** tartan is one sett proportion (`t9db3g11k7g3k3g32r3w3r7`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BBGKGKGRWR](/stripes/bbgkgkgrwr/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **2002**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
