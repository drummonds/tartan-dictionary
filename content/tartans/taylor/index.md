Designed by Lt Col Iain Cameron Taylor, resembling Vestiarium Scoticum Cameron; coral stripe; recognised by Cameron of Lochiel.

The **Taylor** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
