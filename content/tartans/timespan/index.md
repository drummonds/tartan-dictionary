Corporate tartan based on MacKay of Strathnaver, created for Helmsdale's Timespan Heritage Centre.

The **Timespan** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
