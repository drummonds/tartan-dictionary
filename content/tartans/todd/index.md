Variation of Tweedside Green Hunting with red pivot, created for Gregory V Todd; Todd is Border dialect for fox.

The **Todd** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
