---
title: "Toshach"
date: 2023-03-18
---

Derived from an heirloom rug of a family descended from Sir Walter Scott's reputed librarian; Toshach is an early Celtic chiefly title.

The **Toshach** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
