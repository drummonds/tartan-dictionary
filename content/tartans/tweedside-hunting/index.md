Its design is pattern [BGKGWGWGK](/stripes/bgkgwgwgk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Most popular Tweedside district tartan; a 20th-century variation of Wilson's design.

The **Tweedside Hunting** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
