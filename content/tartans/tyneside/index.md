Designed in 1996 for pipe band uniforms, based on Earl of St Andrews with added red linking to McGregor.

The **Tyneside** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
