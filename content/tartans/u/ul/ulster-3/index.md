A tartan of the [Ulster](/families/ulster/) family.
Its design is pattern [GKGBGKGKBKYK](/stripes/gkgbgkgkbkyk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Irish district tartan from Ulster; originally listed as MacNeil, reidentified by weaver Ken Dalgliesh in 1999.

The **Ulster** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
