A tartan of the [Ulster](/families/ulster/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKGKGKGKRK](/stripes/gkgkgkgkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Ulster district tartan, reconstructed from c.1590–1650 cloth found in peat at Dungiven, County Derry, in 1956.

The **Ulster** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
