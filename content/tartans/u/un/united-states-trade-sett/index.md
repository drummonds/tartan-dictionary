Its design is pattern [BBYBRBBBY](/stripes/bbybrbbby/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 1989 to celebrate American tourism in Scotland; colours from both nations' flags and the Atlantic.

The **United States Trade sett** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
