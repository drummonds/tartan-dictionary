A tartan of the [Universal](/families/universal/) family.
Its design is pattern [GGGWGWGW](/stripes/gggwgwgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Asymmetric design celebrating American tourism in Scotland, with colours from both nations' flags and the Atlantic Ocean.

The **Universal** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
