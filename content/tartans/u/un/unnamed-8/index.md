Its design is pattern [GKGKGKBKWKBKGKGKRKGW](/stripes/gkgkgkbkwkbkgkgkrkgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Pre-1900 pattern woven by Bolingbroke and Jones of Norwich; resembles the Cockburn and 71st Highland Regiment setts, later adopted by the Ayre family.

The **Unnamed 8** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
