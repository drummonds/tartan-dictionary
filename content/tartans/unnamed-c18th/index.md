Silk sett from D.W. Stewart's "Old and Rare Scottish Tartans" (1893), valued for antiquity.

The **Unnamed C18th** tartan groups 9 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
