Its design is pattern [GWBW](/stripes/gwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed in 2009 in remembrance of Jean Alexander Varrie, for the Varrie family's Scottish heritage.

The **Varrie Commemorative** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
