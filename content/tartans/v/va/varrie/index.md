Its design is pattern [GWBW](/stripes/gwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed in 2009 in remembrance of Jean Alexander Varrie; blues for rivers and oceans crossed, yellow for land, white for peace.

The **Varrie** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
