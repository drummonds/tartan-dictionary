A tartan of the [Vaughan](/families/vaughan/) family.

Its design is pattern [WKGKYKYKYKW](/stripes/wkgkykykykw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Welsh surname tartan woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill; warp and weft differ, producing a predominant vertical stripe.

The **Vaughan** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
