A tartan of the [Vermont](/families/vermont/) family.
Its design is pattern [GYGBGRGY](/stripes/gygbgrgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed by Lilias MacBean Hart in 1994 with Andrew Elliot for Vermont's Quaigh Shop.

The **Vermont** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
