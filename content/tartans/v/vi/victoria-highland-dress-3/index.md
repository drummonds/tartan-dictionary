A tartan of [Clan Victoria](/clan/victoria/).

Its design is pattern [RWBWKGKWKGRGRWRGRGKWKGKWBW](/stripes/rwbwkgkwkgrgrwrgrgkwkgkwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Late Victorian child's Highland dress sett, a 'Victoria' variant favoured by Queen Victoria, also called Royal Stewart Dress.

The **Victoria Highland Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
