Designed by Lilias MacBean Hart in 1994 with Andrew Elliot for Vermont's Quaigh Shop.

The **Vermont** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
