A tartan of the [Walker](/families/walker/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKRKRKRKRGRG](/stripes/gkrkrkrkrgrg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Maroon-red Walker family tartan submitted by R.W. Hawks in 1991, available for any Walker to use.

The **Walker** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
