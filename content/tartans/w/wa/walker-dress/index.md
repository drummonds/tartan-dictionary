A tartan of the [Walker](/families/walker/) family.

Its design is pattern [GBRBRBRBWRWR](/stripes/gbrbrbrbwrwr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Dress variant for the Walker family, submitted by R.W. Hawks in 1992 for general Walker use.

The **Walker Dress** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
