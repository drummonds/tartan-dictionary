A tartan of the [Wallace](/families/wallace/) family.

Its design is pattern [GKRK](/stripes/gkrk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Clan tartan from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842), the sole recorded version of many tartans still used today.

The **Wallace** tartan is one sett, recorded at 4 scales — the same proportion woven
finer or broader. The top row is the unit proportion; each scale row is one weaving of it.
