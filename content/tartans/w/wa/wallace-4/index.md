A tartan of the [Wallace](/families/wallace/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBBGWGBB](/stripes/bbbgwgbb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Variant of the Wallace sett derived from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842).

The **Wallace** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
