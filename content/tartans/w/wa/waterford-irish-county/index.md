A tartan of [Clan Waterford](/clan/waterford/).
Its design is pattern [GGGBKR](/stripes/gggbkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

One of Polly Wittering's Irish District series for the House of Edgar, with soft warm colours evoking the county.

The **Waterford Irish County** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
