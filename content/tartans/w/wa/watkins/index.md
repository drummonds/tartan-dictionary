A tartan of the [Watkins](/families/watkins/) family.

Its design is pattern [GBBBWBGBGGGBGBBGBGG](/stripes/gbbbwbgbgggbgbbgbgg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Welsh surname tartan (Walters, Watts, Gwatkin, Watkiss) woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill, with differing warp and weft creating a vertical stripe.

The **Watkins** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
