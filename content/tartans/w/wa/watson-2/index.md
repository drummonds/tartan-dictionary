Its design is pattern [BKBRBKGGGGGGGKBRBK](/stripes/bkbrbkgggggggkbrbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Designed 1950 for the Clan Watson Society, based on Gordon and Hunting MacRae tartans; linked to Aberdeenshire.

The **Watson** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
