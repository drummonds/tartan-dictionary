Its design is pattern [KGRGKGY](/stripes/kgrgkgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Created by a 1967 Cardiff society to assert Welsh nationhood; colours from the Welsh flag, design echoing MacDonald.

The **Welsh National** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
