A tartan of [Clan West](/clan/west/).
Its design is pattern [YGBGWRWKRGRBGY](/stripes/ygbgwrwkrgrbgy/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Adapted from the West Virginia Shawl, adopted by the state senate in 2008 as West Virginia's official tartan, its colours symbolising state wildlife, landscape, and resources.

The **West Virginia** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
