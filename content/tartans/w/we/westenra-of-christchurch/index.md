A tartan of the [Westenra of Christchurch](/families/westenra-of-christchurch/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBKBKBKRWBK](/stripes/kbkbkbkrwbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorates NZ soprano Hayley Westenra's 2006 visit to Scotland.

The **Westenra of Christchurch** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
