Its design is pattern [GRBGBGKGKRBW](/stripes/grbgbgkgkrbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed by Kenneth Dalgliesh as a variant of Pride of Scotland, with an added red line for McCalls' wedding tartan range.

The **Western Isles** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
