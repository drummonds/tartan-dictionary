A tartan of the [Westfalia](/families/westfalia/) family.
Its design is pattern [GWGWBR](/stripes/gwgwbr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Corporate tartan for a German dairy machinery company, woven as a worsted scarf.

The **Westfalia** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
