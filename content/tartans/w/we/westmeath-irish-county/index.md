A tartan of [Clan Westmeath](/clan/westmeath/).
Its design is pattern [BBYGYGBBGGGBBG](/stripes/bbygygbbgggbbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

One of Polly Wittering's Irish county series for House of Edgar, with soft warm colours evoking the county.

The **Westmeath Irish County** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
