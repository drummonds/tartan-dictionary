A tartan of [Clan Westwood](/clan/westwood/).
Its design is pattern [BKGGGKGKGKGKGKGKGKBKBKBKRGRKG](/stripes/bkgggkgkgkgkgkgkgkbkbkbkrgrkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Vibrant design woven by Lochcarron in 1994 for fashion designer Vivienne Westwood, with sett over 18 inches.

The **Westwood Metropolitan 1** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
