A tartan of the [Whitworth](/families/whitworth/) family.

Its design is pattern [BKBKBKBGRKRGBKGBKG](/stripes/bkbkbkbgrkrgbkgbkg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Reputedly cut from Prince Charles's '45 plaid, but likely a c.1790 commercial copy of a "Prince's Plaid."

The **Whitworth** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
