Its design is pattern [BKBKBKBGRKRGBKGBKGKBGKBGRKRGBKBKBK](/stripes/bkbkbkbgrkrgbkgbkgkbgkbgrkrgbkbkbk/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Reputedly cut from Prince Charles's '45 plaid, but likely a c.1790 commercial copy of a "Prince's Plaid."

The **Whitworth** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
