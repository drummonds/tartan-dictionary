A tartan of the [Wicks](/families/wicks/) family.

Its design is pattern [BBGGGBBG](/stripes/bbgggbbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Designed for the 2003 Aberdeen marriage of Christopher Wicks and Nicola Bundle.

The **Wicks** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
