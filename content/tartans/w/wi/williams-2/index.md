A tartan of the [Williams](/families/williams/) family.
Its design is pattern [KBKBW](/stripes/kbkbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Welsh surname tartan for Williams and variants, woven at Cambrian Woollen Mill with differing warp and weft for a striped effect.

The **Williams** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
