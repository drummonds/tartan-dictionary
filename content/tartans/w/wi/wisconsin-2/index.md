A tartan of the [Wisconsin](/families/wisconsin/) family.
Its design is pattern [BBKGGGKBBRB](/stripes/bbkgggkbbrb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Official Wisconsin state tartan, chosen 2002 and legislated 2008, with colours symbolising the state's history, industries, and culture.

The **Wisconsin** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
