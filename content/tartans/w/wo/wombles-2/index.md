A tartan of the [Wombles](/families/wombles/) family.
Its design is pattern [BWYWBRWRBWGWBWBWGWBRWRBWYWBW](/stripes/bwywbrwrbwgwbwbwgwbrwrbwywbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

The **Wombles** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
