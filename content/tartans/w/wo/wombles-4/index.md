A tartan of the [Wombles](/families/wombles/) family.
Its design is pattern [WBRBYBWBW](/stripes/wbrbybwbw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Variant of the Jacobite tartan, patented by Wombles International for the television puppet characters.

The **Wombles 4** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
