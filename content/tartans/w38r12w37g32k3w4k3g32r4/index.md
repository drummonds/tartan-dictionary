The **MacDiarmid Dress** tartan is one sett proportion (`w38r12w37g32k3w4k3g32r4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[RGKWKGWRW](/stripes/rgkwkgwrw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1830**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
