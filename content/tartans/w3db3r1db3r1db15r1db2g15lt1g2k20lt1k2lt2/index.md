The **Joseph Linn Family (Monohon 2012) (Personal)** tartan is one sett proportion (`w3db3r1db3r1db15r1db2g15lt1g2k20lt1k2lt2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [RKRKGRGBRBRBRBW](/stripes/rkrkgrgbrbrbrbw/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
