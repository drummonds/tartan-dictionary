The **Womble** tartan is one sett proportion (`w4db8w1db1g6db3r6db1w4`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WBRBGBWBW](/stripes/wbrbgbwbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2016**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
