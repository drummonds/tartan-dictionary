The **Chattan, Chief of Clan** tartan is one sett proportion (`w4ly12r8k8t32w4ly7r7k2r7ly7w4g32w2k4r60w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WRKWGWYRKRYWBKRYW](/stripes/wrkwgwyrkrywbkryw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1819**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
