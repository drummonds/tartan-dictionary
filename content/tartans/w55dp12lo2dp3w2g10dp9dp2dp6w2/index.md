The **Stewart Dress, Purple (Dance)** tartan is one sett proportion (`w55dp12lo2dp3w2g10dp9dp2dp6w2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[BYBWGBBBWBBBGWBYBW](/stripes/bybwgbbbwbbbgwbybw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **~2004**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
