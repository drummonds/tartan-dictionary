The **Wombles #4** tartan is one sett proportion (`w5db2w1g8w1db2m2w1m2db2w1lo8w1db2w5`) recorded at 3 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[WBWGWBRWRBWYWBW](/stripes/wbwgwbrwrbwywbw/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1970**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
