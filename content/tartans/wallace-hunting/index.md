Hunting variant of Wallace clan tartan, sourced from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842).

The **Wallace Hunting** tartan groups 3 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
