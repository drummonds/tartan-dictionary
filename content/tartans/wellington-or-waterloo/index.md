Its design is pattern [WGKWRW](/stripes/wgkwrw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Commemorative tartan named from Wilson letters of 1821 and 1824, honouring Wellington or Waterloo.

The **Wellington or Waterloo** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
