Clan tartan whose name means "cave," likely originating from caves beneath MacDuff Castle.

The **Wemyss** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
