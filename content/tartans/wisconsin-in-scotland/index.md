Its design is pattern [BGBBBRWRBBBGBWBGBBBRWRBBBGBG](/stripes/bgbbbrwrbbbgbwbgbbbrwrbbbgbg/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Possibly designed by Morgan Clifford for University of Wisconsin's cultural study centre at Dalkeith, south of Edinburgh.

The **Wisconsin in Scotland** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
