A tartan of the [YMCA](/families/ymca/) family.

Its design is pattern [RWBBKBKWBWKW](/stripes/rwbbkbkwbwkw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.


Variation of the Clergy tartan, encoding the YMCA's 1844 founding date and global membership.

The **YMCA** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
