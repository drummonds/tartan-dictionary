A tartan of the [Young](/families/young/) family.
Its design is pattern [BGRBBGWB](/stripes/bgrbbgwb/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Derived from the 1726 Christina Young blanket, retaining its purple-yellow-orange box check on a Douglas-style green and blue ground.

The **Young** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
