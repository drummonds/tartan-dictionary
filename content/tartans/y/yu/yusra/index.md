A tartan of the [Yusra](/families/yusra/) family.
Its design is pattern [RBKBWKR](/stripes/rbkbwkr/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

Personal tartan using Malaysian flag colours (navy, red, yellow, cream) to represent the Yusra family's country of origin.

The **Yusra** tartan is recorded as a single sett.
