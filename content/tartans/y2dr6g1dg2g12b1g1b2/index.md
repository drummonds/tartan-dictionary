The **Manitoba** tartan is one sett proportion (`y2dr6g1dg2g12b1g1b2`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad. Its colour order is pattern [BGBGGGRY](/stripes/bgbgggry/); each scale
below is its own sett page.
