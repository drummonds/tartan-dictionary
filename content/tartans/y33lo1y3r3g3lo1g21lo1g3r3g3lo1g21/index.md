The **Terry** tartan is one sett proportion (`y33lo1y3r3g3lo1g21lo1g3r3g3lo1g21`) recorded at 2 scales — the
same cloth woven fine or broad, with close colours folded together. Its colour order is pattern
[GYGRGYGYGRGYG](/stripes/gygrgygygrgyg/); each scale below is its own sett page. First attested **1992**
— its oldest sett (★) anchors the tartan as the parent.
