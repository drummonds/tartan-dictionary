Its design is pattern [BKYRYRYWKGGKWGBGW](/stripes/bkyryrywkggkwgbgw/) — the page of every tartan sharing this colour sequence.

From the MacGregor-Hastie collection; obtained from Andersons in 1947, among designs produced 1930–1950 for Scottish septs and families.

The **York Puppet** tartan groups 2 setts — the same named design recorded as different cloths
(its kilt, Carpet, Child's…, or a transcription apart). The master sett (★) is the exemplar.
