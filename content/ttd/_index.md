The dictionary's pages record the tartans that exist. The TTD navigates the tartans that *could*
exist — the whole weavable space. Give it a thread count and a palette and it weaves the cloth in
your browser; from there, jog any colour a shade lighter, darker, or round the hue ring, and watch
the tartan change one step at a time.

Every tartan here has an address: the URL alone encodes the whole cloth, so anything you weave is
shareable. Below each weaving the TTD lists the **nearest existing tartans** by ΔTartan distance —
open a neighbour here in the TTD to keep exploring, or step out to its page in the dictionary —
and maps the corpus around it.

Arrive empty-handed and weave from scratch, or come from any tartan's page via *Edit this in the
TTD*.
