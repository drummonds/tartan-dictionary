The dictionary's pages record the tartans that exist. The TTD navigates the tartans that *could*
exist — the whole weavable space. Give the weaver a thread count and a palette and it weaves the
cloth in your browser; from there, jog any colour a shade lighter, darker, or round the hue ring,
and watch the tartan change one step at a time.

Every tartan here has an address: the URL alone encodes the whole cloth, so anything you weave is
shareable. Below each weaving the weaver lists the **nearest existing tartans** by ΔTartan
distance — open a neighbour to keep exploring, or step out to its page in the dictionary — and
maps the corpus around it.
