---
title: Tartans by type
date: 2023-03-18
---
Every tartan in the dictionary carries a type — the tradition it belongs to. Clan and family tartans also have their own [clan tier](/clan/).

- [Clan & Family](/type/clan-family/) — 1644 tartans. Tartans belonging to a Scottish clan or family — the worn identity of a kin group.
- [Personal](/type/personal/) — 1653 tartans. Tartans designed for an individual or single family, not a clan — treated here as families.
- [District](/type/district/) — 935 tartans. Tartans of a place — a region, town or area — rather than a kin group.
- [Corporate](/type/corporate/) — 2315 tartans. Tartans owned by a company or brand (e.g. the Burberry check), not a family.
- [Fashion](/type/fashion/) — 1987 tartans. Tartans designed for fashion or commercial cloth rather than identity.
- [Name](/type/name/) — 976 tartans. Tartans tied to a surname without a recognised clan structure.
- [Military](/type/military/) — 133 tartans. Regimental and military tartans (e.g. the Black Watch).
- [Royal](/type/royal/) — 44 tartans. Tartans of the royal household.
- [Commemorative](/type/commemorative/) — 238 tartans. Tartans marking an event, anniversary or memorial.
- [Other](/type/other/) — 1733 tartans. Tartans that do not fall under the other types.
