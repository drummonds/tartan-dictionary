Co-author, James Logan, describes the figure as wearing the 'regimental' tartan with a red line to distinguish the philabeg from the belted plaid. The Shaw tartan, it appears, had been derived from errors in the graphic illustration of the 'Black Watch'. For this reason John Shaw of Tordarroch, 22nd Chief, had a new Shaw tartan designed reflecting the genuine historical connections with Clan Mackintosh and Clan Chattan.
