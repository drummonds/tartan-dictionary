The following is an explanation of colours used in designing the tartan. Blue: for the saltire-St Andrews flag. White: for the diagonal cross in the saltire flag. Green: for MacRae meadows where the games take place. Grey: for the granite of Grandfather Mtn that overlooks the games and from which the games derives its name.
