Dalgety Esq., of Forfar who in turn obtained it from the late James Cant of Dundee. There is a marked similarity with the Bruce tartan, but there is no historical link between the names. Bruce derives from Robert de Bruis (or Brus), whereas Bryce derives from St Bricius, a Gaulish saint from the 5th century. An old Lennox family of Bryce is known to have fought along side the MacFarlanes in 1619, and although the Lennoxes are acknowledged as a name within the clan, only descendants of this particular Bryce family could claim protection from Clan MacFarlane.
