## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1930 Nithsdale, the valley of the River Nith, stretches over 50 miles, north and south, through the the length of Dumfriesshire to the sea, an area with historic connections with the Johnstons and Maxwells. The tartan was designed by Arthur Galt of Messrs Hugh Galt and Sons Ltd., Barrhill, Glasgow, for Councillor John Hannay.
