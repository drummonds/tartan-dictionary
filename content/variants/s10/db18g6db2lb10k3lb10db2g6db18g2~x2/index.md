Part of the [Crombie House Check](/tartans/crombie-house-check/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2302

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1997 A Corporate tartan for a general merchandising company whose mills were at Langholm, Dumfriesshire. Dark green and royal blue called for but lighter colours used here to display the sett.

## Thread count
