This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/b/ba/baird-dress/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BKBKGKWBWB](/stripes/bkbkgkwbwb/).

Part of the [Baird Dress](/tartans/b/ba/baird-dress/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link [https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=170](https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=170)
