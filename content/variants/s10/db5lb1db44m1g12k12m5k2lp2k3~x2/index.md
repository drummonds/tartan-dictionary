This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/db5lb1db44m1g12k12m5k2lp2k3/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BWBRGKRKWK](/stripes/bwbrgkrkwk/).

Part of the [Heart of Scotland Fancy](/tartans/heart-of-scotland-fancy/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=4230

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1999 October 1999. Designed by Lochcarron of Scotland for 'Gavin' Kiltmaker. They use it for their hire kilts. Colour checked against sample.
