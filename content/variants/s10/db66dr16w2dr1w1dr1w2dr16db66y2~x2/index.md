## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2005 September Designed by Douglas Gregor of Tartanweb as a personal tartan for Margot Coogan of County Laois, Ireland. The colours reflect those in the Cougan coat of arms - deep red representing the red cross in the shield and the white lines representing the three silver oak leaves.
