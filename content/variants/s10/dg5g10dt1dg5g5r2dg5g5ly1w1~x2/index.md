This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/dg5g10dt1dg5g5r2dg5g5ly1w1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GGBGGRGGYW](/stripes/ggbggrggyw/).

Part of the [Mississippi](/tartans/mississippi/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=6789

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2005 Karen M Green of the Mississippi Gulf Coast Scottish Highland Games and Celtic Music Festival designed the tartan using the House of Tartan online tartan designer. She hopes to get the approval of the MS State Legislature to adopt the tartan as the Mississippi State tartan.

## Thread count
DG/10 G20 DB2 DG10 G10 R4 DG10 G10 Y2 LN/2

One full sett is **148 threads**.
