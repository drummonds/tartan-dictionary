This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/m/ma/macavoy/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GGKGWGBRBW](/stripes/ggkgwgbrbw/).

Part of the [MacAvoy](/tartans/m/ma/macavoy/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=6687](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=6687)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1975 The pattern was based on an existing weave about 30 year old which had been abandoned by the Macavoy clan. James Macavoy being the only member to wear a kilt of this design in the present day (2005). The origin and design of the original is obscure.
