This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/dy9k2dy2r2g6k1lo1k1g6r3/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GKGRGKYKGR](/stripes/gkgrgkykgr/).

Part of the [MacAart](/tartans/macaart/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).
