This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/g11r4g4r7g41dy11t4db41r4db8/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BRBBGGRGRG](/stripes/brbbggrgrg/).

Part of the [Stewart of Appin Hunting](/tartans/stewart-of-appin-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=430

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1930-50 There is extensive correspondence about the use of the terms 'ancient' and 'hunting' in relation to this sett in the Stewart files at the Scottish Tartan Society. The use of brown makes this sett proportionately similar to the count recorded by James Scarlett as early nineteenth century. He says that the brown was probably black originally. (No. 417, The Highland Textile, 1990)

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Stewart of Appin Htg
- Stewart of Appin, Ancient hunting
