## Provenance

Earliest known date: September 1996 Full name is Royal College of Physicians, Edinburgh. Designed for the Royal College of Physicians (founded in 1681 for post graduate students), by Donald Fraser Weavers as a corporate tartan. Woven by Lochcarron, May 1997. Silk sample in STA Johnston Collection.
