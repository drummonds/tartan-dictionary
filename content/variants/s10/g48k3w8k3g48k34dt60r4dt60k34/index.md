This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/g48k3w8k3g48k34dt60r4dt60k34/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GKWKGKBRBK](/stripes/gkwkgkbrbk/).

Part of the [MacPhail Hunting](/tartans/macphail-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2158

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1994 A modern interpretation of the MacPhail tartan in hunting colours produced for the whisky merchants, Gordon and MacPhail, by the weaving firm, Johnstons of Elgin.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- MacPhail Htg
- MacPhail Hunting #2
- MacPhail, hunting
