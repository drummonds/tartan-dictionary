This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/g4w13g3w4g3w3g40w3g2ly4/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GWGWGWGWGY](/stripes/gwgwgwgwgy/).

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=945

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1977 An alternative source gives this sett as having been produced by Thomas Gordon of Glasgow around 1973. There is (c.1982) a pipe band in New York that wears this tartan.

## Thread count
Y/8 G4 LN6 G80 LN6 G6 LN8 G6 LN26 G/8

One full sett is **300 threads**.
