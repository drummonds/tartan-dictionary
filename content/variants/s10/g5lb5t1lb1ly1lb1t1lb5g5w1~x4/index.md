This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/g5lb5t1lb1ly1lb1t1lb5g5w1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GWBWYWBWGW](/stripes/gwbwywbwgw/).

Part of the [MacGiboney](/tartans/macgiboney/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1999 Designed by Greg McGibonney from Fremont, California. The sett shown here was submitted as a woven sample by Greg McGibboney. Colours here are from the woven sample.
