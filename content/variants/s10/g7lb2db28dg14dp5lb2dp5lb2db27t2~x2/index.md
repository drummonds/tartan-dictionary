This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/g7lb2db28dg14dp5lb2dp5lb2db27t2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BBWBWBGBWG](/stripes/bbwbwbgbwg/).

Part of the [Head of The Lakes](/tartans/h/he/head-of-the-lakes/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link [https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=1639](https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=1639)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1996 Designed by Joan Forrester Troniak & Fiona Irwin as a District Tartan to comemorate the 25th anniversary of the Ontario city of Thunder Bay which has adopted it.
