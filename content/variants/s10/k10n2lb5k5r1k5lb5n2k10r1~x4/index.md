This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/k10n2lb5k5r1k5lb5n2k10r1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [KBWKRKWBKR](/stripes/kbwkrkwbkr/).

Part of the [Callaway](/tartans/callaway/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2313

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1997 Designed by Arthur Bell (Scotch Tweeds) for a golf course and golf merchandising company in California.

## Thread count
K/40 Na8 N20 K20 DR4 K20 N20 Na8 K40 DR/4

One full sett is **324 threads**.
