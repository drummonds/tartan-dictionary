provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/c/co/collister/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [KBKBKBWBBY](/stripes/kbkbkbwbby/).

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).
