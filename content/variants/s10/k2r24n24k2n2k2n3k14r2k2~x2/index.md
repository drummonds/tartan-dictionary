This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/k2r24n24k2n2k2n3k14r2k2/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [KRBKBKBKRK](/stripes/krbkbkbkrk/).

Part of the [Douglas](/tartans/douglas/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).
