(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [KWKWKBKBKY](/stripes/kwkwkbkbky/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1991 Designed in 1991 by Dr. J.C.(Pat) Little of Morton Rigg, Dumfries, for the newly organized Clan Little Society. Registered with TECA 12 June1992. Incorporates elements of the Wallace and Shepherd setts. from Pat Little via JCT. Sample in STA's Johnston Collection.

## Thread count
K/16 LB16 K16 LB16 K16 DR32 K8 DR32 K32 LO/4

One full sett is **356 threads**.
