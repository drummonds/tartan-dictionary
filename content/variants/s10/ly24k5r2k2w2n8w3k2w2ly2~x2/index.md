This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/s/st/stewart-fawn/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [YKRKWBWKWY](/stripes/ykrkwbwkwy/).

Part of the [Stewart Fawn](/tartans/s/st/stewart-fawn/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1738](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1738)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1985 A very attractive sett based on the Royal Stewart tartan but using the colours of the Burberry Check. See Thompson Camel tartan.
