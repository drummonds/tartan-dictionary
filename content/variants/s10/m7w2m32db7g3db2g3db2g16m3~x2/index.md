This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/m7w2m32db7g3db2g3db2g16m3/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [RGBGBGBRWR](/stripes/rgbgbgbrwr/).

Part of the [Chisholm Hunting](/tartans/chisholm-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1457

## Provenance

Earliest known date: c.1815 Possibly the true source of the Vestiarium Scoticum sett with crimson changed to red.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Chisholm
- Chisholm D
- Chisholm hunting
- Chisholm, The
- Chisholm, hunting

## Thread count
R/14 LN4 R64 DB14 G6 DB4 G6 DB4 G32 R/6

One full sett is **288 threads**.
