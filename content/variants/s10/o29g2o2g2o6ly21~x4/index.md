## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2005 The first Scottish Maguire as recorded with Lord Lyon. The design is based on MacQuarrie and was created by FE Maguire at the House of Tartan in Comrie, Perthshire. The tartan is intended for the use of all Scottish Maguires.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Maguire Clan
- Maguire, Black
