## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1970 From Reis of New Haven, 1970 Same sett as #4490. Ref to Reis reads: ""Our exclusive English hand-blocked silk foulards are created by Reis craftsmen of only the purest unweighted silks, hand-sewn and fully lined. When you tie one on, you'll know it's a Reis." Reis were based in New Haven, Connecticut.
