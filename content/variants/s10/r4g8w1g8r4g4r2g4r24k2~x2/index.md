## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1842 John, Lord of Badenoch - the Red Comyn, fought Robert the Bruce for the Scottish throne, and died in the attempt. The Comyns of Altyre became Chiefs of the Clan. The true origins of the tartan are unknown as the claims of antiquity made in the Vestiarium Scoticum, where this version of the tartan was first recorded, are unreliable. Ref: The Setts No 32.
