This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/w1g10gi1k2gi2k1gi30g1k1ly1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [WGGKGKGGKY](/stripes/wggkgkggky/).

Part of the [Celtic F.C.](/tartans/celtic-f-c/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2080

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1989 Launched at Parkhead on November 30th 1989 by Billy McNeil. The tartan appeared the following year at the World Cup finals in Italy.

## Thread count
