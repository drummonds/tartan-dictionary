This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/b/bu/burns-check/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GGGWKWKWKW](/stripes/gggwkwkwkw/).

Part of the [Burns Check](/tartans/b/bu/burns-check/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [10 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes10/).
