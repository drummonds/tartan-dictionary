This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/db18k2t2db9k4g9r4db9t2k2ly1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BKBBKGRBBKY](/stripes/bkbbkgrbbky/).

Part of the [Amarillo](/tartans/amarillo/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2190

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1996 Designed for the city of Amarillo in Texas, USA, by Dr. Phil Smith, at West Chester University in Penn. The tartan has been adopted by the city authorities.

## Thread count
DB/72 K8 B8 DB36 K16 G36 R16 DB36 B8 K8 Y/4

One full sett is **420 threads**.
