This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/db3dy14dg14dy2db14dy2db14dy2dg14dy14r3/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BGGGBGBGGGR](/stripes/bgggbgbgggr/).

Part of the [Buchanan Hunting](/tartans/buchanan-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).
