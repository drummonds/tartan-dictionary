Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=5161

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2002 Designed and woven by Peter McDonald for a member of Perth Royal Scottish Country Dance Society (RSCDS) who wished it for a Swedish friend. Not intended as "the" Swedish tartan, but to represent a Scottish-Swedish connection and available for any who may wish to use it.

## Thread count
DB/80 LO1 K4 LO4 K4 LO4 K22 G36 DB4 B6 LB/2

One full sett is **252 threads**.
