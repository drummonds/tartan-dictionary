This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/dg12oi4o4n4o4oi4dg18g5dg5g5n4/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BGGGGRRBRRG](/stripes/bggggrrbrrg/).

Part of the [Ralston](/tartans/ralston/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=6109

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2003 The tartan commemorates the 60th birthday of Michael R. Ralstin of Scioto Co Ohio and provides a tartan design for the name Ralston and all its spelling variations.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Ralston Personal

## Thread count
DG/24 N8 R8 B8 R8 N8 DG36 G10 DG10 G10 B/8

One full sett is **244 threads**.
