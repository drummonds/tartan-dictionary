provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/g/ge/gemmell-dress/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GRBWKBRKGBW](/stripes/grbwkbrkgbw/).
