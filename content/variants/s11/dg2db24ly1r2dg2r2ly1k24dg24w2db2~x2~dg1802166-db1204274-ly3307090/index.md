This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/dg2db24ly1r2dg2r2ly1k24dg24w2db2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BWGKYRGRYBG](/stripes/bwgkyrgrybg/).

Part of the [Smithsonian](/tartans/s/sm/smithsonian-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=5813](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=5813)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: April 2003 Designed by Alistair Buchan of Lochcarron to support Scotland's and Lochcarron's participation in the Smithsonian Institute's For Life Festival in Washington DC, USA June 25th - July 6th 2003.
