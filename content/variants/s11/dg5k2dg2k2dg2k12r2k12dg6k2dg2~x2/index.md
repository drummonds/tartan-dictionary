## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1990 Mr Paul MacLoughlin described his reason for wanting a tartan of his own as, 'daughters with eccentric tastes'. This sett which is based on the Black Watch, has the red stripe from the MacLachlan. This is a private family tartan.
