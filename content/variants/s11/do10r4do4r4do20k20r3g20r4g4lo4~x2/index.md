This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/do10r4do4r4do20k20r3g20r4g4lo4/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BRBRBKRGRGY](/stripes/brbrbkrgrgy/).

Part of the [Cameron of Erracht](/tartans/cameron-of-erracht/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

Original link https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=5258
