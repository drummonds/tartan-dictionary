This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/dt3k2dt22k9g2lp2g2lp2g8k2w3/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BKBKGWGWGKW](/stripes/bkbkgwgwgkw/).

Part of the [Scottish Rugby Union](/tartans/scottish-rugby-union/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2101

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1990 S.R.U. requested that the Navy of their jersey should be prominent, including the green and lilac of the thistle, and the white of the shorts. This version is approved by the Scottish Rugby Union.

## Thread count
