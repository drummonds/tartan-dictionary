## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2006 This tartan was designed by William C. (Rocky) Roeger III of usakilts.com to honor anyone with Irish Heritage. It is a fashion tartan designed for ANYONE to wear, regardless of clan affiliation or nationality. The color green is to represent people of Irish heritage. The color black is for the strife that Ireland has endured. The color white is for the bright future of Ireland.
