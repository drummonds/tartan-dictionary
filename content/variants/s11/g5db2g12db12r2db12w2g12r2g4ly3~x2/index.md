## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1985 Authorized by Clan Society 1985. Chiefship of the Clan Hunter passed to the Cochrane-Patrick family who have changed their name to Hunter of Hunterston. Hunterston House belongs to British Nuclear Fuels.
