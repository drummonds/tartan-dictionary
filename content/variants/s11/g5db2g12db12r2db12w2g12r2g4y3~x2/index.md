This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/h/hu/hunter-of-hunterston/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GBGBRBWGRGG](/stripes/gbgbrbwgrgg/).

Part of the [Hunter of Hunterston](/tartans/h/hu/hunter-of-hunterston/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=719](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=719)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1985 Authorized by Clan Society 1985. Chiefship of the Clan Hunter passed to the Cochrane-Patrick family who have changed their name to Hunter of Hunterston. Hunterston House belongs to British Nuclear Fuels.
