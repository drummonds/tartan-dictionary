## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1819 This design comes from the pattern books of the old firm of weavers, Wilson's of Bannockburn. It has been adopted by the Scottish Tartans Authority as the tartan of their corporate identity, in line with general policy of the time, to avoid the proliferation of tartans.
