Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1985 A kilt in this material was brought into the Scottish Tartans Museum in Comrie which could be dated to before 1930. It belonged to a member of the Stewart Society at that time. The threadcount and the name were documented by Mackinlay, who studied and collected tartans between 1930 and 1950.
