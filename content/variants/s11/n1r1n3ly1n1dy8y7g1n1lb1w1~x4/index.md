provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/p/po/porcupine-2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BRBYBGGGBWW](/stripes/brbybgggbww/).

Part of the [Porcupine](/tartans/p/po/porcupine-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).
