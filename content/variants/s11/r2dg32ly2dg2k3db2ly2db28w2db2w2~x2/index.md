This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/r2dg32ly2dg2k3db2ly2db28w2db2w2/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [RGYGKBYBWBW](/stripes/rgygkbybwbw/).

Part of the [Pringle](/tartans/pringle/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=5447

## Provenance

Earliest known date: c.1998 Dalgleish of Selkirk designed and wove this circa 1998 as a personal tartan for someone of the name, Pringle.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Pringle #2
- Pringle Personal

## Thread count
DRa/4 G64 Y4 G4 K6 DBa4 Y4 DBa56 LN4 DBa4 LN/4

One full sett is **308 threads**.
