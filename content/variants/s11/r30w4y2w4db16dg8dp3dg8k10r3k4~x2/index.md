## Provenance

Earliest known date: 16/07/2012 Designed to commemorate Filipino-Americans, their traditions and their values. The colours of this tartan combine the Philippine Flag and the various colours of the warrior clan "Bahags" or loin cloths worn by the more than 160 tribes in the Philippine Islands. Developed for weaving by A Trivett on behalf of the Scottish Tartans Authority.
