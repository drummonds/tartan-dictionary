## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1989 First of a new range of football club tartans designed by the Glasgow kiltmakers, Messrs John MacGregor, who formed a new company, Tartan Sportswear, to develop the idea. The Glasgow Rangers Tartan was launched at September first's game with Ally McCoist in full Highland Dress.
