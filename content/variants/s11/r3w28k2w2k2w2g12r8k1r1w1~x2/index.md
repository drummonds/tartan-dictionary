## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1842 The spelling of the name Stuart does not neccessarily indicate the branch of the Stewart Clan. It is the spelling adopted by Mary, Queen of Scots, to accomodate the French alphabet, but does not imply Royal lineage. The Sobieski Stuart brothers used this spelling in the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842). The sett shows some minor variations to the usual pattern.
