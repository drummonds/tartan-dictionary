This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/r5lp2y24lr2y2lr2y6lr8r6lr8ly4/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [RWGYGYGYRYY](/stripes/rwgygygyryy/).

Part of the [Tasmanian](/tartans/tasmanian/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).
