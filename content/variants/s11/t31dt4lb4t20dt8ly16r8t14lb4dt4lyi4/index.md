## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1983 Registered with the Chief Herald of Ireland in 1983. Normally woven in ancient colours. Registered with TECA 01 July 1992 by Eli F.J. O'Carroll, chief of Clan Cian of Ely, Stockton, CA, USA.
