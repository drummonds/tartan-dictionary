## Provenance

<figure class="logan-scan">
<a href="/sources/logan-1831/setts/macneil.png"><img src="/sources/logan-1831/setts/macneil.png" alt="MacNeil: Logan's printed table listing, scanned"></a>
<figcaption>Logan, The Scottish Gaël (1831), vol. II p. 406 — page-scan crop (890,1228)–(1144,2075), (1156,471)–(1410,758)</figcaption>
</figure>

James Logan recorded the **MacNeil** sett in 1831, on page 406 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> 1 white · 6 smalt · 6 black · 6 green · 2½ black · ½ yellow · 2½ black · 6 green · 6 black · 6 smalt · ½ white

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `W/8 B48 K48 G48 K20 Y4 K20 G48 K48 B48 W/4`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
