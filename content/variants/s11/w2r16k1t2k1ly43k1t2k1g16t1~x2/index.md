This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/w2r16k1t2k1ly43k1t2k1g16t1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BGKBKYKBKRW](/stripes/bgkbkykbkrw/).

Part of the [Baxter](/tartans/baxter/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=175

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1856 A discription of this sett is given in The Baronage of Angus and Mearns (1856).

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Baxter of Balgavies
- Buchanan
- Buchanan 5

## Thread count
LN/4 R32 K2 B4 K2 Y86 K2 B4 K2 G32 B/2
