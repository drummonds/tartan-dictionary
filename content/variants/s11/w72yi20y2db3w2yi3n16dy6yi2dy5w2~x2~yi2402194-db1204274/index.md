This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/w72yi20y2db3w2yi3n16dy6yi2dy5w2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [WGGBWGBGGGW](/stripes/wggbwgbgggw/).

Part of the [Stewart Blue Dress](/tartans/s/st/stewart-blue-dress/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [11 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes11/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1794](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1794)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1977 May have originated at Peter MacArthurs in the 1970s, but was very popular at Kinloch Andersons shop in Leith. Information from Colin Hutcheson.
