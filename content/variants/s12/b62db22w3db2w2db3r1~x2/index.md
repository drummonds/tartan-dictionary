This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/b62db22w3db2w2db3r1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BBWBWBRBWBWB](/stripes/bbwbwbrbwbwb/).

Part of the [Tyneside](/tartans/tyneside/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2351

## Provenance

Earliest known date: July 1996 Designed for Pipe Band uniforms. Based on the Earl of St Andrews tartan with an additional red in the over check linking with the McGregor tartan which the band had worn since its foundation in 1958.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- North Tyneside
- North Tyneside Pipe Band
- Tyneside Blue
- Tyneside Blue, North Tyneside Pipe Band
- Tyneside, Scottish
