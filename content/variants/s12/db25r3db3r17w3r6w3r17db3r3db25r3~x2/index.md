This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/b/bo/bon-accord-corporate-com/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BRBRWRWRBRBR](/stripes/brbrwrwrbrbr/).

Part of the [Bon Accord Corporate Com](/tartans/b/bo/bon-accord-corporate-com/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2229](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2229)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1995 Designed by Michael King of Philip King Ltd. A tartan for the City of Aberdeen. Approved by the City council, launched at the Aberdeen Highland Games in June 1995.
