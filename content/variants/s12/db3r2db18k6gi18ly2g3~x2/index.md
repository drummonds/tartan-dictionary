## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1997 STS notes: Designed for a Mr McComb to play himself in as club captain of a golf club. It is the MacThomas with the over check changed to the clubs colours. Design by Donald Fraser (Oct 2002) of Berwick upon Tweed.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- McComb
