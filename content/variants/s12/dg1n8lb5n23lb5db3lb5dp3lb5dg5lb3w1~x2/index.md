## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2012 James Hand designed this tartan to celebrate his marriage to Miss Gail Wheatley. Colours: green for the Hand family name which originates from Ireland; blue and purple to signify a Scottish connection; charcoal and granite, colours which reflect a modern tartan. Weavers note: the granite and charcoal colours are woven with melange (blended) yarns of grey and black. Developed for weaving by House of Tartan.
