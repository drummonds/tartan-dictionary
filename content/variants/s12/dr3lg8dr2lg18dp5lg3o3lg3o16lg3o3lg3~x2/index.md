provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/dr3lg8dr2lg18dp5lg3o3lg3o16lg3o3lg3/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BYBYBYRYRYRY](/stripes/bybybyryryry/).

Part of the [Dublin Irish County](/tartans/d/du/dublin-irish-county/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2250](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2250)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1996 One of a series of Irish District tartans designed by Polly Wittering of the House of Edgar, with colours reminiscent of the Country with soft warm colours dominating.
