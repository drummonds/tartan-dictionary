provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/s/sc/scottish-spirit-2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BRBGKGKGKRKW](/stripes/brbgkgkgkrkw/).

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).
