This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/dt16g4dt3g3lo2g24r2/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BGBGYGRGYGBG](/stripes/bgbgygrgygbg/).

Part of the [St Andrews Links](/tartans/st-andrews-links/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2391

## Provenance

Earliest known date: May 1997 Designed by Polly Wittering of the House of Edgar 1997. Corporate tartan for use on merchandise. Green lightened here to show the sett.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- St. Andrews Links

## Thread count
DN/32 G8 DN6 G6 DY4 G48 DR4 G48 DY4 G6 DN6 G/8

One full sett is **320 threads**.
