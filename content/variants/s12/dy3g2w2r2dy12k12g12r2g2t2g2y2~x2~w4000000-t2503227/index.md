## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1995 Designed by Phil Smith and proclaimed by Governor Symington in December 1995. Green is for the forest that covers half the state; brown for the desert; azure for copper, white for silver; yellow for gold; red for the Native Americans and the red, white and green stripes for the Mexican population.
