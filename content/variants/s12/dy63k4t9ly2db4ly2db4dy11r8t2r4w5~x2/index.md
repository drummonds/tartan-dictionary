(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GKBYBYBGRBRW](/stripes/gkbybybgrbrw/).

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).

Earliest known date: 1978 The design is based on the Sillers connection with the Isle of Arran and the Clan Stewart.
