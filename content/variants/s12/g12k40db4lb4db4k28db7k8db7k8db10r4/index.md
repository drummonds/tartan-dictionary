## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2002 A modern Welsh tartan with a Dress theme for weddings and evening functions, commercially designed and woven in Wales at The Cambrian Woollen Mill, Llanwrtyd Wells, Powys. A differing warp & weft create a series of vertical stripes in this particular tartan or plaid. Woven for Wales Tartan Centres, Swansea.
