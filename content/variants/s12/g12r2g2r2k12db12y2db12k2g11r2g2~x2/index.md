This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/g12r2g2r2k12db12y2db12k2g11r2g2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GRGKBGBKRGRG](/stripes/grgkbgbkrgrg/).

Part of the [Akins Hunting](/tartans/a/ak/akins-hunting/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).
