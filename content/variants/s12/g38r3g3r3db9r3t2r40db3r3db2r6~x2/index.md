This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/g38r3g3r3db9r3t2r40db3r3db2r6/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GRGRBRBRBRBR](/stripes/grgrbrbrbrbr/).

Part of the [MacLintock](/tartans/maclintock/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).
