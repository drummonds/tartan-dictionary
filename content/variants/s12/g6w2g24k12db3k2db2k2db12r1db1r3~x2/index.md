## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1842 (1618) The earlier date refers to a letter from Gordon Earl of Sutherland instructing Murray of Dulrossie to 'remove the red and white lines from the plaids of the men so as to bring their dress into harmony with that of the other Septs.' This sett is also recorded by Smibert in 1850. Smiberts work lends greater authority to the accuracy of the tartan. Worn by the City of Nunawading Highland pipe band (Australia) (Strathallan 1994)
