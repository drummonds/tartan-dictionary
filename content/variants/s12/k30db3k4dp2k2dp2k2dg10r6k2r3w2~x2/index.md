## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1995 Braveheart tartan commemorates the making of the film by the same name, which tells the story of one of Scotland's greatest heros, William Wallace. Originally designed for Ronnie Watt.

## Thread count
K/60 DB6 K8 DP4 K4 DP4 K4 DG20 R12 K4 R6 W/4

One full sett is **208 threads**.
