This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/k3dt15k9do3k2do14k2do3k9dt13k2dt3/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BKBKBKBKBKBK](/stripes/bkbkbkbkbkbk/).

Part of the [McWilliams](/tartans/mcwilliams/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).
