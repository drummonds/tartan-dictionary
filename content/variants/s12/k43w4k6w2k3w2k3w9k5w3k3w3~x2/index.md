## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1880 'Clan Originaux' was published in Paris in 1880 by J. Claude Fres Et Cie. It contains the earliest known record of a number of Irish tartans and many variations of Scottish Clan tartans. The only copy known to exist was discovered recently in America and is now in the possession of Pendleton Mills in Portland, Oregon.
