This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/ly4dt2r7dt15r4dt3r4dt7r28dg7r6dg2/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GRGRBRBRBRBY](/stripes/grgrbrbrbrby/).

Part of the [Walker](/tartans/walker/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [12 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes12/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2068

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1991 R.W.Hawks submitted this tartan via Phil Smith in January 1991. Hawks advised Oct. 1993 that he is agreeable for any Walkers to use this tartan. Marroon Red.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Walker, Evening

## Thread count
Y/4 DB2 DR7 DB15 DR4 DB3 DR4 DB7 DR28 DG7 DR6 DG/2

One full sett is **172 threads**.
