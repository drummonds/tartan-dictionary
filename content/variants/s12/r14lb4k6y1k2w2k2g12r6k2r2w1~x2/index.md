## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1810-15 Also known as the Earl of Moray. A piece exists in the Highland Society of London collection (c.1815) in which the Yellow and White are in silk. The Prince' sett is identical with the Royal Stewart but for the much reduced red square. D.C.Stewart says, 'the tartan becomes richer to the point of congestion.' It is reputed to have been worn by the Prince at Holyrood. Worn by Kirkliston, Bowhill and Seafield, Kinross and District, Strathclyde Fire Brigade and Strathkelvin pipe bands.
