## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1985 Based on an unnamed tartan from South Uist, the yellow represents the gold and red of the original sett form the colours of the 'Mair' coat of arms registered with Lord Lyon. Head of the family, Duine Usail, Robert George Alexander Mair esq. F.S.A. Scot, Sunshine, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. He is the son of Alexander Smith Mair who arrived in Australia in 1924 from Govan, Glasgow.
