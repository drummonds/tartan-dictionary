## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1984 Designed by Gordon Kirkbright now (2002) of Fraser & Kirkbright, Vancouver, when he worked with West Coast Woolen Mill. Orange sometimes given as Red. Often woven in reprocolours where black becomes a dark brown.
