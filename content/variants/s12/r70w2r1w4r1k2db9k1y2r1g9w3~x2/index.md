## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2005 March Designed for the British Lions rugby team and unveiled in New York in April at the 2005 Tartan Day celebrations. Originally called Lion's Pride this tartan has a shield and other emblems woven into the red squares./Threadcount and colours aren't 100% original. Generated manually./
