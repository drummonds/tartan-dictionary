## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1993 The Chatahoochie tartan was conceived by Scottish Borders Enterprize and developed with Lochcarron of Scotland, to signify the spirit of co-operation between Fulton County in Atlanta, Georgia and the Scottish Borders. The red, white and black are the colours of the Atlanta Falcons.
