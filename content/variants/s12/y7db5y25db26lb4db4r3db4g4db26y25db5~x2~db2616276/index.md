provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/o/o/o-savanao/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BGBGBRBWBGBG](/stripes/bgbgbrbwbgbg/).
