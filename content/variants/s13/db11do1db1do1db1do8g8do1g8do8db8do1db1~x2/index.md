Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=593](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=593)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1924 Tyneside Scottish was originally intended to be a Regimental tartan but War Office (Ministry of Defence) declined to sanction the proposal. The tartan has come into use as a District tartan in much the same way as the Sutherland District tartan.
