This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/m/ma/macdevitt/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BKBKBKBKBKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkbkbkbkb/).

Part of the [MacDevitt](/tartans/m/ma/macdevitt/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from tartans-authority.  It is a [13 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes13/).
