This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/db18dy4db3dy3db3dy18g18r10g18dy18db18dy3r10/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BGBGBGGRGGBGR](/stripes/bgbgbggrggbgr/).

Part of the [Murray of Atholl](/tartans/murray-of-atholl/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [13 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes13/).

Original link https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=3061
