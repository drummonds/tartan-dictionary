## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2003 Designed by Councillor Linda Gorn of Keith who was instrumental in having a tartan museum established in Keith circa 1998 under the auspices of the Scottish Tartans Society. Keith is also home to Macnaughton Group's weaving mill (Isla Mill). The House of Edgar who formalised this design is also part of the same group.
