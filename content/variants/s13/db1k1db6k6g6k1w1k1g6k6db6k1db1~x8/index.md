## Provenance

<figure class="logan-scan">
<a href="/sources/logan-1831/setts/forbes.png"><img src="/sources/logan-1831/setts/forbes.png" alt="Forbes: Logan's printed table listing, scanned"></a>

James Logan recorded the **Forbes** sett in 1831, on page 403 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> 1 blue · 1 black · 6 blue · 6 black · 6 green · 1 black · 1 white · 1 black · 6 green · 6 black · 6 blue · 1 black · 1 blue

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `B/8 K8 B48 K48 G48 K8 W8 K8 G48 K48 B48 K8 B/8`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
