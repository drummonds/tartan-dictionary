Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=489](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=489)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: c. 1715 A variant of the MacDonell of Glengarry said to have been adopted by Lord Southesk who was active in the 1715 rebellion. The Glengarry white becomes yellow in the Carnegie. It is possible that this minor difference was caused by the passage of time.
