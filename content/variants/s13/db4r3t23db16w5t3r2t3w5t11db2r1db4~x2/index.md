Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [13 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes13/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1991 A philanthropic society founded by Scots around 1840. The tartan was designed to mark the 150th anniversary. The colours represent the State of Illinois Flag, the Chicago sports teams and the St Andrew's flag.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Illinois St Andrews Society
