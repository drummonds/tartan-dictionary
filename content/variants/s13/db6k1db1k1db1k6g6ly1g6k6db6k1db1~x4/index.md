## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1793 Source references: Cockburn Collection No 10. Logan. Smibert No: 46. Smith No 35. Grant No: 17. Bain. The Setts No: 64. Wilson advertised a range of different quality Gordon tartans in the same colours. e.g. Sergt's Plaids 56 8 8 8 8 58 54 10 54 58 54 8 8. Forsythe, it is said, produced samples with one, two and three yellow stripes. The Duke chose the single stripe.
