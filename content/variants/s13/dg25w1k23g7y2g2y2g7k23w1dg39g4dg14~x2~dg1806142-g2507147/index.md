## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1995 Designed by G. Duffy and Connell Reid, the kiltmaker from Blairgowrie in Perthshire, Scotland. The colours were chosen to accord with the Duffy crest which is a yellow lion on a green field. The lion is outlined in black. The new tartan was recorded by the Scottish Tartan Society in 1995.
