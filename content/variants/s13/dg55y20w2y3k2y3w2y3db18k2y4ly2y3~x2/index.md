This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/dg55y20w2y3k2y3w2y3db18k2y4ly2y3/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GGWGKGWGBKGYG](/stripes/ggwgkgwgbkgyg/).

Part of the [Knox](/tartans/knox/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from tartans-authority.  It is a [13 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes13/).

Original link http://www.tartansauthority.com/tartan-ferret/display/2516/

## Provenance

Earliest known date: Feb 1998 Based on the MacFarlane (with whom Knox was associated) and designed by Keith Lumsden when with the Scottish Tartans Society for a Mr D L Knox of Lancashire. (details from Keith Lumsden 20.3.2008). This was originally numbered 7555 but that was changed to 2516 on inspection of the Tartan Society Tartans Explorer 3 floppy disk in June 2009. What WAS 2516 was then renumbered 7555.
