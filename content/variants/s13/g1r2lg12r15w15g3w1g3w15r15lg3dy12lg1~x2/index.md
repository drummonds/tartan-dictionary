## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1st Jan. 2010 The Saint John New Brunswick tartan was commissioned by Claudia MacLean of ScotDance New Brunswick as a legacy gift to the City of Saint John, New Brunswick, Canada, following the Highland Games held there in 2006. This tartan can only be woven with the permission of the City of St John New Brunswick.
