## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1822 Granted by charter in 1822 to Currie of Balilone, by MacDonald, Lord of the Isles. The sett is based on the Lord of the Isles and the design is attributed to the Chief. The tartan is available to all members of the Clan Currie Society.
