## Provenance

<figure class="logan-scan">
<a href="/sources/logan-1831/setts/mackay.png"><img src="/sources/logan-1831/setts/mackay.png" alt="MacKay: Logan's printed table listing, scanned"></a>

James Logan recorded the **MacKay** sett in 1831, on page 405 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> ¼ green · 7 corbeau · 1 green · 7 black · 7 green · 1½ black · 7 green · 1½ black · 7 green · 7 black · 1 green · 7 corbeau · 1½ green

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `G/2 AK56 G8 K56 G56 K12 G56 K12 G56 K56 G8 AK56 G/12`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
