This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/gi25w1k23g7ly2g2ly2g7k23w1gi39g4gi14/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GGGWKGYGYGKWG](/stripes/gggwkgygygkwg/).

Part of the [Duffy](/tartans/duffy/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [13 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes13/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2172

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1995 Designed by G. Duffy and Connell Reid, the kiltmaker from Blairgowrie in Perthshire, Scotland. The colours were chosen to accord with the Duffy crest which is a yellow lion on a green field. The lion is outlined in black. The new tartan was recorded by the Scottish Tartan Society in 1995.
