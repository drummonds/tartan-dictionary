## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1997 Designed by Peter MacDoanld in 1997 for Alan Greenshields, Glasgow as a family tartan. Based by request of the customer on the MacNichol given by both Ross-Craven and Robert Bain but using yellow form the MacLeod.
