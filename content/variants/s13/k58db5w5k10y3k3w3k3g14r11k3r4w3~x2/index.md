## Provenance

Earliest known date: c.1830 This sett appears in Paton's collection housed in the Scottish Tartans Museum, Tolbooth, Stirling (1995). The samples are undated but the collection is known to have been put together around the 1830's, with some additions during the Victorian period. The tartan is worn by the Royal Scots Dragoon Guards pipe band and the Grampian Police pipe band.
