provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/lb25dp4lb4dp4lb4dp23lr23w4lr23dp23lb23dp4lb4/) (the scale-free proportion — the

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2008 One of four colourways for corporate tartans for professional golfer Ian Poulter's fashion range. Woven in polyster/viscose. Count and sample from Lochcarron.
