## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2nd Nov. 2009 This tartan is for the use of the Stanners family who lived in Grangepans, Bo'ness, West Lothian, for 200 years. It is for family members, relatives and anyone linked to the family by marriage. Developed for weaving by House of Tartan.
