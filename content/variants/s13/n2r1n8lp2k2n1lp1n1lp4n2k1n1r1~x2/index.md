## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1860 The Balmoral tartan is not produced as an article of commerce. D.W.Stewart wrote in his book, 'Old and Rare Scottish Tartans' (1893), "Her Majesty the Queen has not only granted permission for its publication here, but has also graciously afforded information concerning its inception in the early years of the reign, when the sett was designed by the Prince Consort." The grey threads are flecked to give an impression of granite.

## Thread count
N/4 R2 N16 LP4 K4 N2 LP2 N2 LP8 N4 K2 N2 R/2
