Part of the [Grant of Monymusk](/tartans/grant-of-monymusk/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [13 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes13/).

Original link https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=1509

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1810-15 An old tartan in the Cockburn Collection housed in the Mitchell Library in Glasgow. D.C.Stewart comments that it is made up of elements of the Huntly group of tartans.
