This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/r12g16r3g16r3g16r4db16r5k9r12g3r12/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [RGRGRGRBRKRGR](/stripes/rgrgrgrbrkrgr/).

Part of the [Grant of Monymusk](/tartans/g/gr/grant-of-monymusk/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [13 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes13/).

Original link [https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=1509](https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=1509)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1810-15 An old tartan in the Cockburn Collection housed in the Mitchell Library in Glasgow. D.C.Stewart comments that it is made up of elements of the Huntly group of tartans.
