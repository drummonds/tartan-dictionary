## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1930 MacKinlay (Fractional scale). Meaning 'gentle' (from the french) or `Bona res...' A good thing, this reputedly spoken by the King of France after a very un-gentle act of war on the part of Guilhen de Bonares as he was called thereafter. (Guilhen de Bonares is recorded in Perthshire c.1200) Coulson Bonnar was a tatan collecter c1930-1950.
