This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/r1k1g6k6db6k1db1k1db6k6g6k1ly1/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [RKGKBKBKBKGKY](/stripes/rkgkbkbkbkgky/).

Part of the [MacEwen](/tartans/m/ma/macewen-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [13 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes13/).
