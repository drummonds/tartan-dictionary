## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1995 Designed by Kinloch Anderson & John Bain of Peter MacArthur for the Tall Ships event in 1995. The design is based on the Robertson tartan in honour of Henry Robb - Leith shipbuilders. The red line marks Leith's strong links with the Claret Trade. The tartan was later formally adopted as the Leith District tartan. Worn by staff at the Royal Yacht Britannia and is sometimes misnamed as that attraction's tartan.
