## Provenance

Earliest known date: 11 October 2012 Gifted by Aberdeen University to Hong Kong University on the occasion of Hong Kong University's centenary in 2012. Granted in 1913, the University Arms are reflected in this centenary tartan: the red and gold represent the lion on its field of red; azure and light green symbolise Hong Kong's island status; the narrow black lines on white denote the open book in the Arms and the University's role in learning and knowledge.
