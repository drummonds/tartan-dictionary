## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1996 Designed by Lochcarron for Scottish Power using the main corporate colours and taking care to produce a sett that could be seen as pipe band kilts at a distance. Scottish Power say (9.12.02) that Kinloch Anderson deal with this tartan and that permission to order/wear it is required from Scottish Power, Corporate Communications, 0141 566 4856. .
