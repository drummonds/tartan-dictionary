(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BGRKRKRKRKRGBG](/stripes/bgrkrkrkrkrgbg/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2654

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1999 Commissioned by a Mr A.J. Singh to celebrate the 50th anniversary of his family's arrival in Scotland. Can be worn by anyone of the Sikh faith. It was also to celebrate the 300th (??) anniversary of the founding of the Sikh religion that falls in the year Two Thousand.

## Thread count
DT/40 G40 O6 K8 O6 K8 O60 K8 O6 K8 O6 G40 DT40 Y/8

One full sett is **520 threads**.
