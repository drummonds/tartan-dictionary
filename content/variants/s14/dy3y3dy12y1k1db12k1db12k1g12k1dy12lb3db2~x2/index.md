provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/b/ba/balmaha/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BWGKGKBKBKGGGG](/stripes/bwgkgkbkbkgggg/).
