## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2004 Iowa has a rich history of Scottish influence in the towns and cities. The Iowa Scottish Heritage Society desired to give to the people of Iowa a tartan that symbolizes the state. Blue for the sky, rivers & lakes, Green for the fields our farmers plant. Black for the rich soil for which we are blessed. White for the snow. Red for the barns and the wild rose. Brown for the earth. Yellow for corn and the Goldfinch. Adopted by the State of Iowa General Assembly, resolution No 149 by Heaton & Whitaker.
