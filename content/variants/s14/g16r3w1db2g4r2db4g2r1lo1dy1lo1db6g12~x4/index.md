## Provenance

Earliest known date: May 2002 Designed by Phil Heneghan for his family in San Francisco, Maryland and Alaska. The whole family took part in finalizing the design in preparation for parents 50th wedding anniversary celebration.

## Thread count
G/64 R12 W4 DB8 G16 R8 DB16 G8 R4 LO4 DY4 LO4 DB24 G/48

One full sett is **336 threads**.
