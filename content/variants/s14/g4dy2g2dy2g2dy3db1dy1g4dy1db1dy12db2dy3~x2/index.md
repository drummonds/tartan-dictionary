This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/g4dy2g2dy2g2dy3db1dy1g4dy1db1dy12db2dy3/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GBGBGGGBGGGGGG](/stripes/gbgbgggbgggggg/).

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [14 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes14/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=910](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=910)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1984 This version of the MacAlister of Glenbarr tartan is the same as the MacGillivray hunting tartan. This sample was taken from a piece woven by Lochcarron Weavers around 1984.
