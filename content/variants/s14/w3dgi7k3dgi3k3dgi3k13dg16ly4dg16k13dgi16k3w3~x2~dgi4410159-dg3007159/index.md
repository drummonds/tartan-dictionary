This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/t/te/terre-de-ecosse/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [WGKGKGKGYGKGKW](/stripes/wgkgkgkgygkgkw/).

Part of the [Terre De'Ecosse](/tartans/t/te/terre-de-ecosse/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [14 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes14/).
