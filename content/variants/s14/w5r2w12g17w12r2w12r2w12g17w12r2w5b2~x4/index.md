## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2004 A dance version of #634 designed by Dr. Phil Smith of Georgia for a Highland dance teacher. Prior to the threadcount shown here (received from the designer Dr Phil Smith) the sett shown was P/8 W20 LB8 W48 G68 Y2 W48 LB8 W/48. The present sett shown here misses out the yellow and replaces light blue with red.
