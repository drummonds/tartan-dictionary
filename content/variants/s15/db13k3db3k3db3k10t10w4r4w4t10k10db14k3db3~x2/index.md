This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/db13k3db3k3db3k10t10w4r4w4t10k10db14k3db3/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BKBKBKBWRWBKBKB](/stripes/bkbkbkbwrwbkbkb/).

Part of the [Palatine Union](/tartans/palatine-union/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [15 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes15/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=6802

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2004 Designed as a unique tartan for the wedding of Traepischke Graves (Trapper Graves) and Steve Lalor in Seattle. Palatine is an old Scottish name and also a district in Seattle.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Palatine Union Personal
