## Provenance

Earliest known date: Unknown Designed for surnames Enyon and Benyon at The Wales Tartan Centre in Cardiff, by Sheila Daniel of Cambrian Woollen Mill, Powys. They are unusual in that almost all of them incorporate odd numbered threads and have quite different warp and weft, both in thread numbers and sometimes colours. There is no known tradition of tartan in Wales.
