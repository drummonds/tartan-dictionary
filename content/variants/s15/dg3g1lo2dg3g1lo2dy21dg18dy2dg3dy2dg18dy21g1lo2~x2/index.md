(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GGYGGYGGGGGGGGY](/stripes/ggyggyggggggggy/).

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [15 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes15/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2125

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1930 Mackinlay suggests that David was the pet name of the Duke of Windsor when he was a boy and that the tartan was designed for his personal use.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Prince David
