This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/dy28r3do2o2do2r3o8lo2do8lo5do3w2do2lo3do1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BYBWBYBYRRBRBRG](/stripes/bybwbybyrrbrbrg/).

Part of the [Caithness](/tartans/caithness/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [15 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes15/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2466

## Provenance

Earliest known date: (Feb, 2001) Designed by Trudi Mann of Wick and incorporating colours of Caithness, including the unique blue grey Caithness flagstone.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- MacTavish
- Thompson/Thomson/MacTavish

## Thread count
T/56 R6 DR4 N4 DR4 R6 N16 LT4 DR16 LT10 DR6 LN4 DR4 LT6 DR/2
