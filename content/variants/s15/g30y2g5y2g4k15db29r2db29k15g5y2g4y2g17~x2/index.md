## Provenance

Earliest known date: c. 1945 Do you recognise this tartan. It was discovered by Robin Birch of Connell Reid Kiltmakers in Blairgowrie, attached to a Teddy Bear that he thought had a regimental connection dating from 1945. The unidentified sample was recorded here on 2nd February, 1995.
