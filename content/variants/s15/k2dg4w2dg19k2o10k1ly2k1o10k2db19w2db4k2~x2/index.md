Original link https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=2438

## Provenance

Earliest known date: June 2000 Designed by the late Donald Suttie Smith of Heraldic Graphics, Glasgow for Greg McGibonney of Fremont CA - descendant of a Scottish family that moved to the USA via Ireland in the 1760s. It is found in Pennsylvania, North Carolina, and Highlands of Tennessee. The tartan may be used by anyone of the name or its variants.
