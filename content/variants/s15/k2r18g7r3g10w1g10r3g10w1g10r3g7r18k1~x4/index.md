James Logan recorded the **MacAulay** sett in 1831, on page 404 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> ½ black · 9 red · 3½ green · 1½ red · 5 green · ½ white · 5 green · 1½ red · 5 green · ½ white · 5 green · 1½ red · 3½ green · 9 red · 1 black

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `K/4 R72 G28 R12 G40 W4 G40 R12 G40 W4 G40 R12 G28 R72 K/8`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
