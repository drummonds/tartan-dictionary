This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/lb30g8r2k8y2k8r2g8db11r2lb10r3lb2r2db3/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BRWRWRBGRKGKRGW](/stripes/brwrwrbgrkgkrgw/).

Part of the [Mungall](/tartans/m/mu/mungall/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [15 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes15/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=4070](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=4070)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2001 This tartan was first produced in ancient colours. It is a personal tartan to commemorate the signing of the Ragman's Roll by William de Mungall
