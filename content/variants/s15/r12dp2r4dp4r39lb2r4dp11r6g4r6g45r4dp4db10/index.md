provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/g/gr/grant-or-new-bruce/) (the scale-free proportion — the

Part of the [Grant or New Bruce](/tartans/g/gr/grant-or-new-bruce/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [15 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes15/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1385](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1385)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1819 As ordered by Patrick Grant of Redcastle, the chief of the Clan Grant. The large red and the large green squares have been reduced by a factor of 4 to allow display. The original sett was S15 P2 S4 P4 S156 LB2 S4 P42 S6 G4 S6 G178 S4 P4 S10 (HSHP half sett half pivot). This tartan was also adopted by the Drummonds.
