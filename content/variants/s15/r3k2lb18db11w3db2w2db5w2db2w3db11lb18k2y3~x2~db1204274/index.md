## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1980 Submitted to STS Monitoring Committee March 1982. South Carolina Military School. Ludovic Grant-Alexander possibly designer (Bob Marton) at that time, head of the Citadel Piping School. Woven by Dalgleish, 1980. Changed background to azure in 2007 following advice from C Adams.
