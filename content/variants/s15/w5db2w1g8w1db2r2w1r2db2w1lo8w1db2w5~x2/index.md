(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [WBWGWBRWRBWYWBW](/stripes/wbwgwbrwrbwywbw/).

Part of the [Wombles](/tartans/wombles/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [15 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes15/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1783

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2003 Wombles International, of Jacob's Well Mews, London, patented this design which is a variant of the Jacobite tartan. Wombles are television puppet characters.

## Thread count
