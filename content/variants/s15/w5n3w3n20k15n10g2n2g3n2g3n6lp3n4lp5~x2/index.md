provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/m/mi/midnight-thistle/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [WBWBGBGBGBKBWBW](/stripes/wbwbgbgbgbkbwbw/).

Part of the [Midnight Thistle](/tartans/m/mi/midnight-thistle/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [15 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes15/).
