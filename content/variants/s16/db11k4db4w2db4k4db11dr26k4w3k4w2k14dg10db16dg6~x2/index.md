## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2001 Andrew Stuart-Houghton wrote, I have included a design for which I would like as my personal tartan. it should be called: Stuart-Houghton, Andrew Terlach Eideard Seumas. I would also like to have it restricted, if possible. I would like to have it produced in heavyweight in order to make a kilt.
