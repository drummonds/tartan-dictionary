This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/db6dri2db2dri2db2do6g8do1w2do1g8dr2do4db8dri2db2/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BBBBBBGBWBGBBBBB](/stripes/bbbbbbgbwbgbbbbb/).

Part of the [Forbes of Druminnor](/tartans/forbes-of-druminnor/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [16 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes16/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=592

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1968 Inspired by an old rug belonging to Hon. Peggy Forbes Semphill. A sample was woven by A Stewart while acting as Director of Research at the Scottish Tartans Society in 1968.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Forbes of Druminnor Artifact
- Forbes, of Druminnor
- Glasgow Celtic Society

## Thread count
DB/12 DR4 DB4 DR4 DB4 T12 G16 T2 LN4 T2 G16 DRa4 T8 DB16 DR4 DB/4

One full sett is **216 threads**.
