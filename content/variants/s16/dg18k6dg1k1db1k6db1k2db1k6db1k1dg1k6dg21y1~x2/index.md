## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2002 This is not a general Masonic tartan but one designed for the Grand Lodge of Scotland which is custodian to the oldest Lodge Minutes in the world dating from 1599. Masons in other parts of the world wishing to obtain this tartan must, in the first instance, contact the Curator of the Grand Lodge of Scotland Museum, Robert L D Cooper
