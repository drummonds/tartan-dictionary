## Provenance

Earliest known date: Pre 1995 Deisgned by Kinloch Anderson for the Scottish Tourist Board trained US Travel Agents. Based on Davidson since the first President of the ASTA from 1931 - 1938 was Mr E. Irvine Davis.Designed to celebrate the ASTA 1997 world conference in Glasgow. Thread count estimated from illustration.
