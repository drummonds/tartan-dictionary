## Provenance

Earliest known date: c.1815 Hopetoun House, South Queensferry, home of the Marquesses of Linlithgow, was built by Robert Adam. The Highland Society of London collected specimens of tartan sealed with the signature of the Clan Chief or Head of the family, from 1815 onwards.
