This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/g5k24g12k16g26w4g24w4g26k16db4k4db4k4dbi28g3/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GBKBKBKGWGWGKGKG](/stripes/gbkbkbkgwgwgkgkg/).

Part of the [O'Conner](/tartans/o/o/o-conner/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [16 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes16/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2217](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2217)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1985 Designed by Jerry O'Connor of Keltic Klassics, Hillsdale, New Jersey who names it "Royal na Connaught" .Woven by House of Edgar who said they would call it O'Connor. Ref made to Lawrence & Gerald O'Connor - possibly customers of Macnaughtons of Pitlochry (part of same group as House of Edgar)
