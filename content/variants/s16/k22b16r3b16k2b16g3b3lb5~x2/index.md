This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/k22b16r3b16k2b16g3b3lb5/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BGBKBRBKBRBKBGBW](/stripes/bgbkbrbkbrbkbgbw/).

Part of the [Jethart](/tartans/jethart/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [16 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes16/).
