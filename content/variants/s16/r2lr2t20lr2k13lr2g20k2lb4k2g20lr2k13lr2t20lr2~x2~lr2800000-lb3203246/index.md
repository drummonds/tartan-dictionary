## Provenance

Earliest known date: c1998 One of three tartans designed by Tony Murray of Stirling for the Stirling Clan Society in 1998/1999. Internet poll conducted by Michael Jex and the winning one shown here was chosen by a large majority. Woven by Lochcarron. Clan Stirling society thought to be based in the USA - possibly California. www.clanstirling.org
