provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/r2lr2t20lr2k13lr2g20k2lb4k2g20lr2k13lr2t20lr2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [RYBYKYGKWKGYKYBY](/stripes/rybykygkwkgykyby/).

Part of the [Stirling](/tartans/stirling/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [16 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes16/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: c1998 One of three tartans designed by Tony Murray of Stirling for the Stirling Clan Society in 1998/1999. Internet poll conducted by Michael Jex and the winning one shown here was chosen by a large majority. Woven by Lochcarron. Clan Stirling society thought to be based in the USA - possibly California. www.clanstirling.org
