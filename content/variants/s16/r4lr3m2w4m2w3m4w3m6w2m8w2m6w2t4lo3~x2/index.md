## Provenance

Earliest known date: 12 November 2012 Designed to celebrate Amy Bradwell's 30th birthday and the relocation of the family to Scotland in 2012. The colours reflect Amy's personal preferences and the family's mixed Yorkshire and Scottish heritage.
