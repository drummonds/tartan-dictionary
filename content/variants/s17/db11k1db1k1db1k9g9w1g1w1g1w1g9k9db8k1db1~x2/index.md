## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1937 This sett was designed by Mr A.W. Geddes of Messrs William Anderson of Edinburgh in 1937. It is based on the MacKenzie tartan substituting three fine white lines on the green in place of the single stripe. It was designed for the Baillies of Polkemett. Geddes also produced a red sett for the Baillies around the same period.
