## Provenance

Earliest known date: c.1950 Warp is slightly larger. The Scottish Tartan Society supplied details of this sett to the 7th Cavalry in 1992 along with archival photographs and other details, so that the newly re-formed pipe band could have new uniforms made. The original sett had apparently been lost.

## Thread count
