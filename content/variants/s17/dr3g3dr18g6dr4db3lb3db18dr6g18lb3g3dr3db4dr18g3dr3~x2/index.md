This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/r/re/reid-3/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BGBBBGWGBBWBBGBGB](/stripes/bgbbbgwgbbwbbgbgb/).

Part of the [Reid](/tartans/r/re/reid-3/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [17 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes17/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=5415](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=5415)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1991 Found in Dalgleish swatch files 1991, but in use long before then. known to be worn by James Reid of Washington D.C. Modification of Robertson. see 1428 for "Reid - green" designed by Phil Smith 1991 for Wm. M. Reid, Jr. (perhaps without knowledge of the previously existing "Reid - red").
