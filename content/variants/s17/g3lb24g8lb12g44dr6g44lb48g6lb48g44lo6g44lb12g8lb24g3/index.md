This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/j/ju/jubilee/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GWGWGBGWGWGYGWGWG](/stripes/gwgwgbgwgwgygwgwg/).

Part of the [Jubilee](/tartans/j/ju/jubilee/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from tartans-authority.  It is a [17 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes17/).
