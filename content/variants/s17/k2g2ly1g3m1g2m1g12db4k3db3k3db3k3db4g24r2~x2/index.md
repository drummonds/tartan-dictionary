## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1847 The Earls of Cassilis built Culzean Castle on the site of an ancient Kennedy stronghold. Dunure Castle near Culzean on the Ayrshire coast, was also owned by the Kennedys. The tartan was first recorded by MacIan in his book 'The Clans of the Scottish Highlands' (1847) which he co-authored with James Logan.
