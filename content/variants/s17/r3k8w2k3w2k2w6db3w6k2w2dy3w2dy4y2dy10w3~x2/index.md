provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/r3k8w2k3w2k2w6db3w6k2w2dy3w2dy4y2dy10w3/) (the scale-free proportion — the

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [17 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes17/).
