## Provenance

<figure class="logan-scan">
<a href="/sources/logan-1831/setts/cumming.png"><img src="/sources/logan-1831/setts/cumming.png" alt="Cumming: Logan's printed table listing, scanned"></a>

</figure>

James Logan recorded the **Cumming** sett in 1831, on page 403 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> 1 azure · 1 black · 2 azure · 5 black · ¼ orange · 5 green · 2 red · ¼ white · 2 red · ¼ white · 2 red · 5 green · ¼ orange · 5 black · 2 azure · 1 black · 2 azure

Rendered at 8 threads to the eighth-inch that is `A/8 K8 A16 K40 O2 G40 R16 W2 R16 W2 R16 G40 O2 K40 A16 K8 A/16` — the eighths are the captured data, and the threadcount is derived from them at that stated factor. How many threads an eighth of cloth held depends on the weave's density, so the factor is a display calibration, not Logan's count; the sett's identity lives in the proportions, which the eighths record directly. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
