This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/dp17dpi3g3dpi3dp4k18db17w4db17k18dp18dpi3g3dpi3dp18k18db17w4/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BBGBBKBWBKBBGBBKBW](/stripes/bbgbbkbwbkbbgbbkbw/).

Part of the [Heart of Scotland Fancy](/tartans/heart-of-scotland-fancy/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [18 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes18/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=3105

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2000 Designed by Ruthven Milne of Piob Mhor, Blairgowrie. Not recorded or registered with anyone so its existance and its 'duplicated' name were not known until September 2002.
