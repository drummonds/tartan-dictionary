## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1996 Designed by Gillian Kirkwood of House of Edgar in 1996 for Matthew Gloag & Son Ltd who were established in 1800 as a whisky blenders and makers of the Famous Grouse Whisky. The company is based in Perth, Scotland and now (2002) has a major new visitor attraction at Crieff, 17 miles west of Perth.
