This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/k6r7w2r7k6r1k4r1k64r1k4r1k6r7w2r7k6lb2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [KRWRKRKRKRKRKRWRKW](/stripes/krwrkrkrkrkrkrwrkw/).

Part of the [Noordermeer](/tartans/n/no/noordermeer/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [18 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes18/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=6357](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=6357)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2004 Alwin Noordermeer is the senior petroleum engineering advisor for Rhourde El Baguel in Algeria. He designed his own tartan using Tartan Software and help from Maxine Scott.
