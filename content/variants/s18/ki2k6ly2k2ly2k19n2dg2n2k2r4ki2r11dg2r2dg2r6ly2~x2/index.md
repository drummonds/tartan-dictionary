(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [KKYKYKBGBKRKRGRGRY](/stripes/kkykykbgbkrkrgrgry/).

Part of the [Harmon](/tartans/harmon/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [18 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes18/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=7792

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2006 The original design had no black stripe though the red. Mr Harmon designed the tartan because, as he said, there does not appear to be any Harmon tartan, nor do the Harmons in Scotland appear to have any historical clan affiliation.
