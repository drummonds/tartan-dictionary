## Provenance

<figure class="logan-scan">
<a href="/sources/logan-1831/setts/ross.png"><img src="/sources/logan-1831/setts/ross.png" alt="Ross: Logan's printed table listing, scanned"></a>

James Logan recorded the **Ross** sett in 1831, on page 408 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> 4½ green · 1 red · 9 green · 9 red · 1 green · 2 red · 1 green · 9 red · 9 blue · 1 red · 9 blue · 9 red · ½ blue · ½ red · 1 blue · ½ red · ½ blue · 9 red

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `G/36 R8 G72 R72 G8 R16 G8 R72 B72 R8 B72 R72 B4 R4 B8 R4 B4 R/72`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
