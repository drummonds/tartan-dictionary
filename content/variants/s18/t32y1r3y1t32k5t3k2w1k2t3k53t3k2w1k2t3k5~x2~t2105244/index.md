## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2005 September The Estonian National Tartan designed by Kirsty Anderson of the MacNaughton Group, Perth. Organised by Shaw Marketing of Edinburgh (0131 557 5663) and to be launched during Estonian Week at the end of October. Colours taken from the Estonian national Flag.
