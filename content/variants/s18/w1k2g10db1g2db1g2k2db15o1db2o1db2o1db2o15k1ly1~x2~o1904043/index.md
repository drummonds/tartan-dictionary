provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/d/da/daniel-melrose/) (the scale-free proportion — the

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2008 Daniel Melrose says, "This Tartan is in memory of our ancestors who lived in the Newbigging and Dunsyre area for over 200 years." The tartan was designed to be woven in Ancient colours. (Corrects the 2 yellow stripes error. It should only be one yellow.)
