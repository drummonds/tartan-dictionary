provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/m/ma/macbean/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GWRBGBRWKGKWKBWBKWR](/stripes/gwrbgbrwkgkwkbwbkwr/).

Part of the [MacBean](/tartans/m/ma/macbean/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [19 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes19/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=952](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=952)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1963 The MacBains are closely associated with Mackintosh and this is apparent in the design of the tartan. A slightly different version is recorded by Lord Lyon (P.R.A. 43/108 - 8th March 1960) under the name MacBain. This version is available from the House of Edgar Old and Rare range.
