## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1982 Proposed by the Caribou Islands District Fire Hall (Ladies Auxilary). Among the suggestions for the symbolic meaning of the colours it says, "Red for our sunsets, our lobsters, and our Fire Trucks". Caribou is in Pictou County, Nova Scotia.
