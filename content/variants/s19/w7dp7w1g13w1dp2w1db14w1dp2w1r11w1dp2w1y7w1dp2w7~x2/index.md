## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2007 October While drawn from old earth religions, this was designed without any beliefe system in mind but to tie the greater whole together. Yellow - air/east. Red - fire/south. Blue - water/west. Green-earth/north. White - center/spirit & purple heart.
