## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1815 - 16 A specimen of the Rob Roy sett exists in the collection of the Highland Society of London, bearing the Seal of Arms of Sir John MacGregor Murray of MacGregor, Baronet, and signed John M. Murray. The specimens were collected during the period 1815-16.
