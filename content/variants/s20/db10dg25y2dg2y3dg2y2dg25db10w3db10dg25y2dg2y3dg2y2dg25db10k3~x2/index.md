## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2004 Designed by Carol Worthley of South Hiram, Maine for the Alma Mater of Stephen H Snell of Alexandria, Virginia - the College of William & Mary in VA. Stephen Snell has donated the tartan to the Earl Gregg Swem Library in that College to be sold as a fundraiser.
