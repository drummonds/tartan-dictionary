## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1900 Originally listed as Messrs Bolingbroke and Jones of Norwich, who were weavers around 1870. Count from a sample in the Museum of Antiquities, Edinburgh in which the W & Y are in silk. Ref: na1035 1959 909 ttb15(33-38). No. of 38 allocated after James Scarlett had seen the original. Very similar to the Cockburn in the Museum of Antiquities and very close to #798 the 71st Highland Regiment. This unnamed pattern has been adopted by the Ayre family.
