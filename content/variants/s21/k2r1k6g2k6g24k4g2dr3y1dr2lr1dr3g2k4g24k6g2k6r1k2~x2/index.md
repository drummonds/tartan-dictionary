## Provenance

Earliest known date: 13/10/92 Michael Jean George Pilette (Vlug) of Kinnear, Baron of Kinnear, commissioned the design of this new tartan. Based on the 'Duke of Fife' or Fife district tartan with an overcheck in the colours of the Kinnear Arms. The tartan was designed by Blair Urquhart for the Scottish Tartans Society and is intended for association with the bearer of the Kinnear Arms.
