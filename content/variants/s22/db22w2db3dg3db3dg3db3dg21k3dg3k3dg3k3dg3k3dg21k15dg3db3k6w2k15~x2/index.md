provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/w/wi/wilson-blyth-2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BWBGBGBGKGKGKGKGKGBKWK](/stripes/bwbgbgbgkgkgkgkgkgbkwk/).

Part of the [Wilson-Blyth](/tartans/w/wi/wilson-blyth-2/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [22 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes22/).

Original link [https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=11127](https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=11127)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 23/08/2014 The Wilson-Blyth tartan takes the set of the Clan Wilson tartan and uses the colours of the Clan Blyth (Tweedside District) tartan. It was designed for the marriage of Prof. Nancy J Devlin (nee Wilson) to Mr David R Blyth, in Rushden, Hertfordshire on 23rd August 2014, wherein both will take the new name Wilson-Blyth.
