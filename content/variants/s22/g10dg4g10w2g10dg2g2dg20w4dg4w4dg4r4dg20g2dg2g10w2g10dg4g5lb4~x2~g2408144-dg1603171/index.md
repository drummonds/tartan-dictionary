## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2002 Toshach is an early Celtic title given to minor territorial chiefs in Scotland (note Eire Prime Minister's official title is this). This sett was taken from a rug in the possession for many generations of a family whose forebear was reputed to be Sir Walter Scott's librarian. Green lightened to show sett.
