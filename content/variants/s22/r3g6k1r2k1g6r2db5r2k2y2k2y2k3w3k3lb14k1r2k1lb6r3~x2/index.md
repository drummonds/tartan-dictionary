This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/a/an/anderson-7/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [RGKRKGRBRKGKGKWKWKRKWR](/stripes/rgkrkgrbrkgkgkwkwkrkwr/).

Part of the [Anderson](/tartans/a/an/anderson-7/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [22 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes22/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2003 This sample comes from the MacGregor-Hastie collection which forms the basis of the cloth archive of the Scottish Tartans Society. Some of the samples, including this one, were unmarked. One can assume that the sample dates between 1930 and 1950.
