## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1810-15 Also recorded by Logan and Wilson in the Wilson's pattern books. The Hay Leith connection is believed to have come about through a marriage between the two families. At Delgattie Castle, Turriff, there is a Clan Hay centre and Leith Hall, home of the Leith-Hays, is owned by the National Trust for Scotland.
