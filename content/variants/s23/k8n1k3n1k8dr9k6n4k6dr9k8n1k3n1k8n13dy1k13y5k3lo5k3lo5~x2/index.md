This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/z/zi/zibrant/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [KBKBKBKBKBKBKBKBGKGKYKY](/stripes/kbkbkbkbkbkbkbkbgkgkyky/).

Part of the [Zibrant](/tartans/z/zi/zibrant/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [23 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes23/).
