## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1856 This Baxter tartan is recorded by Logan (c.1832) as "Buchanan". Logans complied his tartan list from the limited information available at the time. It appears in a description as Baxter in D. Macgregor Peter's Baronage of Angus & Mearns, 1856. The principal branch of the clan is the Baxters of Earlshall who live at Leuchars in north Fife.
