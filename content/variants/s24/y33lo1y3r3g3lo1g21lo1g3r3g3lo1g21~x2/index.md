## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1993 Designed by Thomas Terry of Geneseo, IL, USA. Registered with the Scottish Tartans Society 9-15-93. Info from Sue Miller, April 1995. With no evidence to the contrary it is assumed that all of the name 'Terry' can wear this tartan.
