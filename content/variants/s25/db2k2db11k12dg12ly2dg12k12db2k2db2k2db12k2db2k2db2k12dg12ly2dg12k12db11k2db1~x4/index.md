This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/db2k2db11k12dg12ly2dg12k12db2k2db2k2db12k2db2k2db2k12dg12ly2dg12k12db11k2db1/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BKBKGYGKBKBKBKBKBKGYGKBKB](/stripes/bkbkgygkbkbkbkbkbkgygkbkb/).

Part of the [Gordon of The Burn](/tartans/g/go/gordon-of-the-burn/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from peter-1856.  It is a [25 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes25/).

Original link [/posts/baronage-angus-mearns/](/posts/baronage-angus-mearns/)

## Provenance

David MacGregor Peter recorded the tartan of **Gordon of The Burn** in 1856, on page 137 of *The Baronage of Angus and Mearns* — a genealogy of the families of Angus and the Mearns whose entries carry their tartans in Logan's method: stripe depths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> ½ blue · 1 black · 5½ blue · 6 black · 6 green · 1 yellow · 6 green · 6 black · 1 blue · 1 black · 1 blue · 1 black · 6 blue · 1 black · 1 blue · 1 black · 1 blue · 6 black · 6 green · 1 yellow · 6 green · 6 black · 5½ blue · 1 black · 1 blue

Rendered at 8 threads to the eighth-inch that is `B/4 K8 B44 K48 G48 Y8 G48 K48 B8 K8 B8 K8 B48 K8 B8 K8 B8 K48 G48 Y8 G48 K48 B44 K8 B/8` — the eighths are the captured data, and the threadcount is derived from them at that stated factor (the same display calibration as Logan 1831, whose method the book borrows). Peter named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [The Baronage of Angus and Mearns](/posts/baronage-angus-mearns/) for the book, its method and every entry.
