## Provenance

Earliest known date: 01/01/2005 One of a series of Irish name tartans designed by Scotch Corner of Gateshead, England. Estimated threadcount. n asymmetric tartan woven by Marton Mills (JR554T) for Naxos Fashion & Schoolwear of Wood Green, London. This tartan comes from Scotch Corner of Gateshead who have produced various Irish surname tartans over the years .
