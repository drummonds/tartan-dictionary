## Provenance

<figure class="logan-scan">
<a href="/sources/logan-1831/setts/macpherson.png"><img src="/sources/logan-1831/setts/macpherson.png" alt="MacPherson: Logan's printed table listing, scanned"></a>
<figcaption>Logan, The Scottish Gaël (1831), vol. II p. 406 — page-scan crop (1156,746)–(1410,1859)</figcaption>
</figure>

James Logan recorded the **MacPherson** sett in 1831, on page 406 of the *Table of Clan Tartans* in *The Scottish Gaël* — the earliest systematic published collection of clan setts. Logan gives the stripe widths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> ¼ red · ¼ black · ¼ white · 5½ red · 2 azure · ½ black · ½ azure · ½ black · 2 azure · 3 black · ½ yellow · 4 green · 5½ red · 1 azure · 5½ red · 1 azure · 5½ red · 4 green · ½ yellow · 3 black · 2 azure · ½ black · ½ azure · ½ black · 2 azure · 5½ red · ½ white · ½ black · ½ red

In threads (at 8 to the eighth-inch) that is `R/2 K2 W2 R44 A16 K4 A4 K4 A16 K24 Y4 G32 R44 A8 R44 A8 R44 G32 Y4 K24 A16 K4 A4 K4 A16 R44 W4 K4 R/4`. Logan named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [Logan's Scottish Gaël](/posts/logans-scottish-gael/) for the full table and method.
