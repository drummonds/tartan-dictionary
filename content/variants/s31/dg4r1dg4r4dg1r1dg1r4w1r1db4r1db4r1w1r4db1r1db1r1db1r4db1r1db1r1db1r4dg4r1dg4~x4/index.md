## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1850 This plate is taken from the manuscript of William and Andrew Smith's 'Authenticated Tartans of the Clans and Families of Scotland', with a minor error corrected in the count given by D.C.Stewart in 'The Setts of the Scottish Tartans' (1950). The Smith's sources included the findings of George Hunter, who toured the Highlands in search of old tartans prior to 1822.
