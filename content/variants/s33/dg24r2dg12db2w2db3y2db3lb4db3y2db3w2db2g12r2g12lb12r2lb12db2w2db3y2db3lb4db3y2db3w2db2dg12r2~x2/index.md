## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1996 Designed by Andrew Elliot of Andrew Elliot Ltd, Forest Mill, Galashiels, Scotland. Appears to be a variation on Cockburn. Launched at a civic reception in Hawick as the offocial district tartan in February (14th) 1996.
