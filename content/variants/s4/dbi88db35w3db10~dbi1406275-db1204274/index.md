## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1990 This is in fact the second Scottish Tourist Board tartan and was designed in Navy, White and Blue in keeping with the new logo adopted this year by the Scottish Tourist Board. This supercedes the first tartan which was designed many years ago and reflected different colourings.
