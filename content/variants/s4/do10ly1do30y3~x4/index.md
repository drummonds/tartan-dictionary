This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/do10ly1do30y3/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BYBG](/stripes/bybg/).

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [4 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes4/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=7094

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1836 The pattern is taken from a shawl or cloak which appears in a portrait of Louie Pasteur's mother. The portrait was drawn in pastels when Pasteur was just 13 years old. The information came Marie-Claude Fortier researching the life of Pasteur.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Pasteur

## Thread count
K/40 LR4 K120 LG/12

One full sett is **300 threads**.
