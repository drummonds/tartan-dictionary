## Provenance

Earliest known date: 15/12/2012 Designed by Steven Randall Wothke as the official tartan for his traditional piping and drumming band, The Rogues, formed in Texas in 1994. Colours: black is reminiscent of formal attire; red, yellow and blue were inspired by the first kilt Mr Wothke owned.
