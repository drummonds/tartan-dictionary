## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1996 This is the dress version of McKerrell of Hillhouse Hunting (#1758) and designed by Madam McKerrell of Hillhouse in 1996 and recorded in the Lyon Court Books (LCB 123) on 20th November 2001. As shown here, red is used in the warp in place of yellow. The McKerrell of Hillhouse Dress can be worn by all those with the name McKerrell and its spelling variations by seeking the permission of McKerrell of Hillhouse
