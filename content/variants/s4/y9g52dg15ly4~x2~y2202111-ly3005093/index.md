## Provenance

Earliest known date: 16/01/2012 Inspired by the MacNeil of Colonsay tartan (STR ref #2688), as the surname McGuigan is considered a sept of MacNeil of Colonsay and Gigha. Using the green, red and white stripes of the MacNeil of Colonsay tartan, the colours have been toned down to create a softer palette.
