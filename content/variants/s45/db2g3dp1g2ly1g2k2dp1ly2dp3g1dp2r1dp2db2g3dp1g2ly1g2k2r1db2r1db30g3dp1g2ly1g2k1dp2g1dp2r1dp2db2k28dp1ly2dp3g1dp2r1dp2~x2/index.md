Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [45 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes45/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2002 Designed by Claire Donaldson of House of Edgar for Harrisons of Hamilton for their sole use in their retail outlet and kilt hire operation. Very difficult to match colours. The black shown here should be a very dark blue verging on black.
