provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/g/ga/gamba/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GRGBW](/stripes/grgbw/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 02/08/2012 Designed by Catherine Gamba to commemorate the golden wedding of Joseph and Catherine Gamba, whose roots are in Tuscany and Fife. The colours are taken from the Scottish and Italian flags.
