## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2001, June Designed in 2003 by Paul Gwynn of Texas. A Gwynn family tartan based on the Welsh national and incorporating Gwynn family colours. Can be worn by all of the name Gwynn. See also Gwyn (Welsh).
