provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/d/de/denholme/) (the scale-free proportion — the

## Provenance

Earliest known date: Unknown This tartan is remarkably similar to the Durham sett designed by Wilson's of Bannockburn around 1819. The variation in proportions may point to a deliberate modification suggested by the links between the names.
