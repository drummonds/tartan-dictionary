This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/r/ri/riley-s-theme/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BKGBWB](/stripes/bkgbwb/).

Part of the [Riley's Theme](/tartans/r/ri/riley-s-theme/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [6 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes6/).

Original link [https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=10657](https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=10657)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 04/07/2012 This tartan was created for Riley Hagel as a thank-you for helping the designer recover from two reconstructive surgeries, one in 2006 and one in 2007.
