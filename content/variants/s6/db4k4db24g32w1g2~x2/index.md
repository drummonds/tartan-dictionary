## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1842 Also The Setts No: 210. W & A K Johnston, 1906. Often referred to as 'Oliphant and Melville'. There is a similar pattern listed under 'Melville' which is also worn by the Oliphants. There is no definitive provenance to distinguish one from the other, though the Vestiarium has proved unreliable in many cases. See MELVILLE.
