## Provenance

Earliest known date: 12/10/12 This tartan was designed to celebrate the 25th anniversary of the formation of the Old Brigade an informal fraternal society dedicated to good fellowship, formed in 1987. The colours relate to the regiment in which the founding members of the society were serving at the time, with the addition of a gold stripe for excellence.
