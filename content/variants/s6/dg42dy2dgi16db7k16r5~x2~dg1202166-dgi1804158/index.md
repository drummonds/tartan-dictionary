This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/w/wa/waterford-irish-county/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GGGBKR](/stripes/gggbkr/).

Part of the [Waterford Irish County](/tartans/w/wa/waterford-irish-county/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [6 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes6/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1993 One of a series of Irish District tartans designed by Polly Wittering of the House of Edgar, with colours reminiscent of the Country with soft warm colours dominating.
