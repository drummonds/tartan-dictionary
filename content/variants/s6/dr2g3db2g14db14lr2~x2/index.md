## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1993 April 1993. Designed by Dr. J. Bruce Irving -- a colour variation of Irvine (733) - and regarded as a Personal tartan, not to be confused with the Clan/Family tartan 'Irving of Bonshaw' (2609) which predates this.
