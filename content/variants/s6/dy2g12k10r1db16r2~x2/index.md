## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1880 Mentioned in the pattern book 'Clans Originaux' produced in Paris in 1880 by J. Claude Fres Et Cie. It is similar in style and colour to the MacKay tartan both from Sutherland in the North of Scotland
