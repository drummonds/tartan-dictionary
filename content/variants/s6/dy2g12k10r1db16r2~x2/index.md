This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/dy2g12k10r1db16r2/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GGKRBR](/stripes/ggkrbr/).

Part of the [MacWilliam](/tartans/macwilliam/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [6 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes6/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1417

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1880 Mentioned in the pattern book 'Clans Originaux' produced in Paris in 1880 by J. Claude Fres Et Cie. It is similar in style and colour to the MacKay tartan both from Sutherland in the North of Scotland

## Thread count
R/4 DB32 R2 K20 G24 T/4

One full sett is **164 threads**.
