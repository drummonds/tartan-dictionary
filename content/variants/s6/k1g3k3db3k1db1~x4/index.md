This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/k1g3k3db3k1db1/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BKBKGK](/stripes/bkbkgk/).

Part of the [Sutherland 42nd](/tartans/s/su/sutherland-42nd/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [6 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes6/).
