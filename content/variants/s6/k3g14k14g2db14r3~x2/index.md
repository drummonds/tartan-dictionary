## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1908 The Clan Morrison have strong links with the MacKays and this is evident in the similarity of the tartans. Morrison has an added red stripe. Lord Lyon recorded a new sett in 1968 based on a fragment of Morrison tartan dated 1747.
