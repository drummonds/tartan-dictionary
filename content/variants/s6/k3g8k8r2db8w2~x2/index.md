This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/k3g8k8r2db8w2/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [KGKRBW](/stripes/kgkrbw/).

Part of the [Mitchell](/tartans/mitchell/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [6 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes6/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=2142

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1816-20 Named in honour of General Billy Mitchell when it was adopted as the tartan of the United States Air Force pipe band. The sett is also known as Russell, Hunter and Galbraith. The earliest reference to the tartan is in the collection of the Highland Society of London where it is labelled Galbraith.

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Galbraith
- Hunter
- Russell
- Russell, or Mitchell or Hunter or Galbraith
