## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2006 Corporate colours for Sonsub Ltd, Bridge of Don, Aberdeen. Sonsub is a well known provider of remote subsea systems technology, and provides services to the North Atlantic, Mediterranean, Africa, Caspian and Middle East.
