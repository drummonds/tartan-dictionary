This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/n6k16n6k16n45k4/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BKBKBK](/stripes/bkbkbk/).

Part of the [Grey Spirit](/tartans/g/gr/grey-spirit/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [6 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes6/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=6594](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=6594)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 01/03/2005 A fashion tartan from ACS Clothing of Glasgow for use in their kilt hire business. Woven by Lochcarron. The grey is actually a grey/black marl (mixture) which can't be shown graphically.
