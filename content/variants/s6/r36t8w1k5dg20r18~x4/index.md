provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/r36t8w1k5dg20r18/) (the scale-free proportion — the

Part of the [Sinclair of Auldbar](/tartans/s/si/sinclair-of-auldbar/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from peter-1856.  It is a [6 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes6/).

Original link [/posts/baronage-angus-mearns/](/posts/baronage-angus-mearns/)

## Provenance

David MacGregor Peter recorded the tartan of **Sinclair of Auldbar** in 1856, on page 309 of *The Baronage of Angus and Mearns* — a genealogy of the families of Angus and the Mearns whose entries carry their tartans in Logan's method: stripe depths in eighths of an inch, measured across the cloth and reflected about each end (a half-sett):

> 9 red · 10 green · 2½ black · ½ white · 4 azure · 18 red

Rendered at 8 threads to the eighth-inch that is `R/72 G80 K20 W4 A32 R/144` — the eighths are the captured data, and the threadcount is derived from them at that stated factor (the same display calibration as Logan 1831, whose method the book borrows). Peter named his colours rather than dyeing to a standard, so the palette here is the Dictionary's modern reading of his names.

See [The Baronage of Angus and Mearns](/posts/baronage-angus-mearns/) for the book, its method and every entry.
