## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1880 circa A tartan worn by a John Skinner in 1941. He was born in Dundee 1873. His grandson (John H Beech?) wished the tartan identified and recorded. It is a Wallace variant, with a blue substituted for the black overcheck.. Safe enough now for wear by all Skinners.
