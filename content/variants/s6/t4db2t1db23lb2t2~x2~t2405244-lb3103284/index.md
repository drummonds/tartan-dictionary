## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2008 Covenant College is a four-year Christian college that emphasizes the liberal arts. Founded in 1955 in Pasadena, California, the college moved its campus to Lookout Mountain, Georgia in 1965. It is an educational institution of the Presbyterian Church in America.
