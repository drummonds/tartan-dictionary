## Provenance

Earliest known date: 19 September 2012 Robert Reekie designed this tartan for his sister to thank her for her dedication and commitment to the study of Reekie heraldry. Colours: magenta is for dedication; yellow is one of the family colours; green is for patience; red represents a passion for heraldry; black ties the family together; white is for compassion and purity.
