## Provenance

Earliest known date: 8th July 2010 The period was the 1860s, a rather attractive time for men, all frock coats and top hats. Narrow-leg trousers and checks were fashionable. I accentuated Daniel's height and slenderness by extending his top hat, making his trousers thinner and his shoes longer. The other members of his gang were dressed along the same lines but not so finely: no one had the same presence as Bill the Butcher.
