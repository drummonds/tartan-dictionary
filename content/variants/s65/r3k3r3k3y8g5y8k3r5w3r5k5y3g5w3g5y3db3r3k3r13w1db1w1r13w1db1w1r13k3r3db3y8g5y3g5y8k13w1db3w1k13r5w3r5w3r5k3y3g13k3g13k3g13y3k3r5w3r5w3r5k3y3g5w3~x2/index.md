## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1812 This sett is also known as Ogilvie or Ogilvy. The full sett cannot be displayed in this medium. The Drummond of Strathallan tartan was adopted by the 6th Earl of Airlie on his marriage to Clementina Drummond in 1812. Weavers count available from STS archives.
