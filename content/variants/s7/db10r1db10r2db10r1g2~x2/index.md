## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2003 The design 'Heather Tartan' has been produced at the request of the Royal Society for Prevention of Cruelty to Children, as the Society's corporate tartan. The colours of the design are taken from the Society's badge and lettrhead. Dark Pink Mix, Light Pink Mix, Kingfisher and Emerald.
