## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1819 Also known as 'Madder'. Marroon red is used in this illustration to convey what Wilson describes as madder. This colour, produced from plant dyes, is a dull shade of red. Rock and Wheel refers to an old form of manufacture. Worn by the City of Glasgow pp
