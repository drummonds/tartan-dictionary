(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BGBGBKB](/stripes/bgbgbkb/).

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [7 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes7/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=3202

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2000 One of a set of three similar designs for Laurie, Lawrie and Lowry, designed by Peter MacDonald for Iain Laurie.
