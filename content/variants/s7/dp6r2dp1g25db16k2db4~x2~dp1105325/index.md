provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/dp6r2dp1g25db16k2db4/) (the scale-free proportion — the

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2000 One of a set of three similar designs for Laurie, Lawrie and Lowry, designed by Peter MacDonald for Iain Laurie.
