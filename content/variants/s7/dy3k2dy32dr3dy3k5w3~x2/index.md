This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/dy3k2dy32dr3dy3k5w3/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GKGBGKW](/stripes/gkgbgkw/).

Part of the [Bro-Dreger](/tartans/b/br/bro-dreger/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [7 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes7/).
