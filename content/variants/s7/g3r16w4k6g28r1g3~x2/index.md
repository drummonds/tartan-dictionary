## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1980 Based on the Maxwell sett which in turn comes from the Vestiarium Scoticum (1842). Details of the Clan Society's design came from Rhys A Pollock in America. Pollocks were feudal dependents of the Maxwells who lived in the Scottish Lowlands.
