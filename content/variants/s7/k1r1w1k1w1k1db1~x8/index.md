## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1986 Originally anotated with 'Authorized by The Clan Bell of Lochmaben' but not now recognised by Chief Apparent, Benjamin. This tartan was known, possibly in error, as Bell of Blackethouse or Bell Blackethouse, and is now called 'Southern Bell'.
