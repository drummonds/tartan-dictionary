## Provenance

Earliest known date: 01/01/2002 The Scottish Odyssey tartan from Lochcarron is a 'celebration of the wonderful experiences to be gained' from a visit to Scotland and some of the most spectacularly contrasting landscapes to be seen in Europe.

## Thread count
K/14 DB22 K6 DB22 DY22 G44 DB/6

One full sett is **252 threads**.
