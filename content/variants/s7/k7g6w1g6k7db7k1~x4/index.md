## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1856 Same as Graham of Montrose. From Baronage of Angus & Mearns 1856. Dalgety Archives say "Family . . . of Glenquiech". Is this Glen Quaich in Perthshire which runs from Amulree up into the hills that lead down to Kenmore on Loch Tay?
