## Provenance

Earliest known date: 01/04/2012 This tartan is designed to commemorate the award of the Queens Diamond Jubilee Medal in 2012. The colours inspired by the Lachance Arms are: silver (‘argent’), nobility, peace and serenity; green (‘vert’), antiquity and strength; gold (‘or’), the light of the sun; blue (‘azure’), the eastern sky; brown (‘brun’) for the base of the trees.
