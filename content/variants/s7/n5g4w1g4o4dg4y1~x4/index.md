This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/n5g4w1g4o4dg4y1/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BGWGRGG](/stripes/bgwgrgg/).

Part of the [Devon Original](/tartans/d/de/devon-original/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [7 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes7/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1284](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1284)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1984 The Devon Original and Devon Companion owe their origin to the success of the Cornish St Piran sett, which was woven by Coldharbour Mill in the early 1980's. The accreditation certificate was presented to the Mayor of Barnstaple in 1991. In a poem describing the tartan, Miss M. Miles says, "So, in the mind, Devon's beauty is retrieved By contemplating Devon's tartan's weave."
