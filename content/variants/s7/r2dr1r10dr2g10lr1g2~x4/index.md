provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/r2dr1r10dr2g10lr1g2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GYGBRBR](/stripes/gygbrbr/).

Part of the [Lennox](/tartans/l/le/lennox/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.
