## Provenance

Earliest known date: c.1970 Originally called Hilton Champion, this tartan was designed by Kinloch Anderson for the officials and winning jacket for the Hilton-sponsored Heritage Classic golf tournament. The name was changed to Heritage Plaid in September 2000
