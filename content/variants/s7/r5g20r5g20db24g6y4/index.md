## Provenance

Earliest known date: 01/01/1940 Design close to Cameron Hunting which has two red lines shown in Vestiarium Scoticum. This design evolved in the 1940s by J G MacKay of Portree and was first put on show at the Cameron Gathering at Achnacarry in 1956. (original STA ref: 1535)
