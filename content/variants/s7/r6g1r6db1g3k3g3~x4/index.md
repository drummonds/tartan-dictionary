This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/r6g1r6db1g3k3g3/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GKGBRGR](/stripes/gkgbrgr/).

Part of the [MacTavish](/tartans/m/ma/mactavish/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [7 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes7/).

Original link [https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=2768](https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk/tartanDetails.aspx?ref=2768)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1850 This plate is taken from the manuscript of William and Andrew Smith's 'Authenticated Tartans of the Clans and Families of Scotland'. The Smith's sources included the findings of George Hunter, an Army clothier, who toured the Highlands in search of old tartans prior to 1822.
