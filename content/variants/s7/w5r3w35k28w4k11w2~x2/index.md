provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/w5r3w35k28w4k11w2/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [WKWKWRW](/stripes/wkwkwrw/).

Part of the [MacPherson of Cluny](/tartans/m/ma/macpherson-of-cluny-3/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [7 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes7/).
