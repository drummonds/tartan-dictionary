## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2002 Designed for Gavin Kiltmakers & Dress Hire of Fraserburgh (01346 516039) for their kilt hire range. Based on the Grampian tartan with Maroon and Lavender added for heather and thistles. Will supply the tartan to other kiltmakers.
