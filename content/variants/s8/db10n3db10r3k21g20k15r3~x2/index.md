provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/w/wi/williamson-smart/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BBBRKGKR](/stripes/bbbrkgkr/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 12 September 2012 Inspired by time spent in Lochaber and his Scottish heritage, Mr Smart designed this tartan for his wedding using the Scotweb Tartan Designer.
