## Provenance

Earliest known date: 11 April 2012 Designed by Doris Maria Kruenaegel-Schropp to commemorate the Prussian and Bavarian origins of the Kruenaegel-Schropp family. Colours: blue and white represent the flags of both countries; green represents the hills of the Allgäu region of South Germany where the family currently reside; red-violet represents wisdom and dignity.
