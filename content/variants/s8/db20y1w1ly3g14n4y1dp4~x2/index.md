## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1996 Commercial version with only one shade of green. Designed by Peter MacDonald for St.Columba's Church, Groline on Mull to commemorate the 1400th aniversary of St. Columba's death. Tartan Society entry states "Based on all the natural colours of Iona, this tartan was designed to raise money to restore a church roof."
