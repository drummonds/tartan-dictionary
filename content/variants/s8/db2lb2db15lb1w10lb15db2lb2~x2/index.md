## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1984 A fashion pattern originating the early 1970s. Other variants of the design including this one appeared up to 1984. No place or family of the name is known and the pattern has no association with Bannockburn, or famous battle of 1314. Donald Broun may have been a designer with Edinburgh Woollen Mills.
