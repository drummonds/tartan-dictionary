This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/b/ba/bannockbane-tan/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BGBGWWGW](/stripes/bgbgwwgw/).

Part of the [Bannockbane Tan](/tartans/b/ba/bannockbane-tan/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [8 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes8/).

The Bannockbane proved to be an exceptionally successful addition to modern tartan collections, which although originally produced as a fashion check, is now generally considered to have earned it's place as a Scottish tartan.
