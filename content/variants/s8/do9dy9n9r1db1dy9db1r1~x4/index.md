## Provenance

Earliest known date: c.1978 The chiefly house is Jardine of Applegirth, a baronetcy created in 1672. The Jardines of Castlemilk in Dumfriesshire settled there in the early fourteenth century. The tartan is approved by Col Jardine. The darker brown is recorded as "J.Br", and the lighter as "Olive Br" in the Lyon Books.
