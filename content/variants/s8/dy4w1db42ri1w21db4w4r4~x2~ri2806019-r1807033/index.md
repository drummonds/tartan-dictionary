provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/dy4w1db42ri1w21db4w4r4/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GWBRWBWR](/stripes/gwbrwbwr/).

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [8 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes8/).
