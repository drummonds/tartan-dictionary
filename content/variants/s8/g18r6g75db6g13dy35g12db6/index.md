This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/g/ga/gayre-bodyguard/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BGGGBGRG](/stripes/bgggbgrg/).

Part of the [Gayre Bodyguard](/tartans/g/ga/gayre-bodyguard/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [8 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes8/).
