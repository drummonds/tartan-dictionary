## Provenance

Earliest known date: circa 1992 Designed by Tony Murray for an Albert (Bert) Riddoch of Bearsden, Glasgow as a private and personal tartan initially but it was then changed to usage by anyone of the name. Bert Riddoch (2002) is a private investgator involved in covert drugs investigations and 'highly thought of by the police'. Tartan based on Campbell of Breadalbane.
