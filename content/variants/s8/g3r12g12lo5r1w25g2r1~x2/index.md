This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/g3r12g12lo5r1w25g2r1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [GRGYRWGR](/stripes/grgyrwgr/).

Part of the [Dogwood](/tartans/dogwood/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [8 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes8/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=913

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1968 The registered tartan of the Dinwiddie Clan. Dinwiddies are normally associated with the Maxwells, but Lord Lyon stated, in 1988, that Dinwiddies were a sept of no other clan.
