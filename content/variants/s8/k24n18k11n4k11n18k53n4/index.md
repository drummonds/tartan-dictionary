## Provenance

Earliest known date: 20th May 2010 A modern day plaid, designed and woven in Wales to commemorate Owain Glyn Dwr, crowned Welsh Prince 1406. Colours represent the slates and dark waters of Mid and North Wales, Glyndwrs homeland. Woven by the Cambrian Woollen Mill, Mid Wales exclusively for Wales Tartan Centres, Swansea.
