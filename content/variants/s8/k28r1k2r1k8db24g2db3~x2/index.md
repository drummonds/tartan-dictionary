provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/h/ho/home/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BGBKRKRK](/stripes/bgbkrkrk/).

Part of the [Home](/tartans/h/ho/home/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from register-of-tartans.  It is a [8 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes8/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1842 D.W.Stewart says, "The tartan of the ancient and notable family of Home, though differing in colour, has the same scheme as the Grey Douglas. Both first appear in the Vestiarium Scoticum." Border 'Clans' are mentioned in an Act of the Scottish Parliament in 1587, but no evidence of a Clan tartan exists before the earliest date given here.
