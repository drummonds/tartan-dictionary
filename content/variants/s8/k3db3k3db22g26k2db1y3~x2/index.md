This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/j/jo/johnstone/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GBKGBKBK](/stripes/gbkgbkbk/).

Part of the [Johnstone](/tartans/j/jo/johnstone/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [8 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes8/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1062](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1062)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2003 This sett appears in Paton's collection which is housed at the Scottish Tartans Museum, Comrie in Perthshire, Scotland. The samples are undated but the collection is known to have been put together around the 1830's, with some additions during the Victorian period.
