## Provenance

Earliest known date: 13 August 2012 Designed by Charles Lambert, using the Scotweb Tartan Designer, for his family, to celebrate their Irish ancestry. Mr Lambert has also designed the Lambert (Front Royal) Greer tartan (STR #10673) using the same geometry.
