provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/setts/lb1n12r1db1r1db2r5lb1/) (the scale-free proportion — the

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1996 May 1996 for Tenmaya Department Store Ltd in Okayama, Japan. Sample in STA Johnston Collection. Designed for British promotion October/November 1996 which demonstrated weaving and woven by Archie Snmall from Selkirk who was sent to Fukuoka to demonstrate weaving.
