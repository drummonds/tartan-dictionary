## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1831 Holden Monaro GTS 1978 car seats. Reproduction colours. Warp 162 threads Weft 202 threads. Last weaving went through with slight difference. Sett size = 4 3/8th - 4 3/4 inch (112mm - 120mm)
