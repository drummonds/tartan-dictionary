## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2005 For a new House of Edgar Collection in wedding gray. This is the same sett and colours as 6822 but as in Dark Island, the sett has been highlighted in the weaving process by with parts of the patern being woven 'face up' and the remainder 'back up'. To further accentuate the sett, the weft is slightly darker than the warp. Theoretically only one of these setts should be included but we have stretched a point to provide dated documentation for the weaver.
