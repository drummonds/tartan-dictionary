## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1831 The sett used by James Logan to illustrate his method of recording the threads and colours of tartan patterns. It appears in the first edition of 'The Scottish Gael' and is therefore the first published illustration of a tartan sett. The problems of printing tartan were very much apparent and the illustration showed differences in each volume produced.
