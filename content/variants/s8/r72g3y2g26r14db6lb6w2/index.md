## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1745 This tartan is almost identical to the Drummond of Perth. It was one of the valueable relics of the '45, treasured by the Murray-Threipland family of Fingask. It was said to be the pattern of a cloak of Prince Charles Edward, who left it at Fingask after staying there at the time of the '45.
