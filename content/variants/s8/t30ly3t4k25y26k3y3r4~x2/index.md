(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [BYBKGKGR](/stripes/bybkgkgr/).

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 2000 Samples in STA Dalgety Collection labelled "Restricted, Hunting Ogilvie, Family Only". However, the major weavers have this in their swatch books so the restriction mentioned above seems to have been lifted at some stage.
