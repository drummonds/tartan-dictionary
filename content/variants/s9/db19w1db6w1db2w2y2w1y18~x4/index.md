## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2013 Based on 5191 Highland Park High School (Texas) Pipe Band tartan, reducing the number of white stripes on the blue. Designed to reflect our school's heritage and reputation for being the Highland Park High School 'fighting Scots'. Our school colors are Old Gold and True Navy and these are the colors reflected in the design we have created.
