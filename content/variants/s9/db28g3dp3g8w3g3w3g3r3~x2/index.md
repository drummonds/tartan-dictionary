## Provenance

Earliest known date: 2000 Variation of Tweedside Green Hunting with the black pivot changed to red. Produced for Gregory V Todd and registered with TECA 10th August 1996. Anyone of the name can wear io. D C Dalgliesh was the weaver. Todd is Border dialect for fox - hence the red.
