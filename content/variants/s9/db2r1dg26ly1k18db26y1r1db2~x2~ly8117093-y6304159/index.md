This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/r/ro/robb/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [BRGBKYGRB](/stripes/brgbkygrb/).

Part of the [Robb](/tartans/r/ro/robb/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [9 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes9/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=3157](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=3157)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1994 Designed by Peter MacDonald for Martin Robb of Carroglen, Comrie, Perthshire. Can be worn by anyone of the name but Martin Robb would appreciate being advised.
