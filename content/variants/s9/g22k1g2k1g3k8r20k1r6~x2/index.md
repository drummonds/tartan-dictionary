This is one **variant** — a specific cloth: this exact thread count and colourway, with its own
provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/s/st/stewart-of-athol/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [GKGKGKRKR](/stripes/gkgkgkrkr/).

Part of the [Stewart of Athol](/tartans/s/st/stewart-of-athol/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [9 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes9/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=802](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=802)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: pre 1842 (1745) Prepared for the Vestiarium Scoticum but not included in the published version. It was claimed to be the sett of a 'relic from the '45' and published as such by D.W.Stewart in his book 'Old and Rare Scottish Tartans' in 1893. It has become more widely known in recent times. There is also an Atholl district tartan.
