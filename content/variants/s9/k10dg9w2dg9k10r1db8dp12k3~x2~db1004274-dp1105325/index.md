## Provenance

Earliest known date: April 2009 Designed by and commemorating the wedding of Adeline Hunter and Stuart Graham. The tartan was created by combining the setts of the Graham of Montrose and the Hunter tartans. No restrictions on weaving or wearing. Organised through the House of Tartan, Comrie.
