This page is one **sett** — a single exact thread-count. It belongs to the [tartan](/setts/k1g15dy1ly2dy1w6r1db3k1/)
(the same proportion at any scale), whose colour order is pattern [KBRWGYGGK](/stripes/kbrwgyggk/).

Part of the [Nor Westers](/tartans/nor-westers/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [9 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes9/).

Original link http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1069

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1963 Named after the Nor Westers Mountain Range in Ontario. Designed by Miss Evelyn B Halliday in February 1963 to commemorate the naming of the range in that year

## Also known as

This cloth is also recorded under:

- Nor Westers Commemorative

## Thread count
K/2 G30 T2 Y4 T2 LN12 R2 DB6 K/2

One full sett is **120 threads**.
