## Provenance

Earliest known date: 05/05/1998 Designed by Violet Holmes, B.C. Canada and adopted as the official RCMP tartan. Weft differs in having red in place of orange so making 6 weft colours. Sample presented to the RCMP by Priness Anne during a visit to New Brunswick on 25th June 1998. Lochcarron records say December 1997. Different weft - orange missing. Original count had dark blue in place of the black shown here in the Lochcarron swatch.
