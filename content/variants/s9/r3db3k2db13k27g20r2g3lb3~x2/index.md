## Provenance

Earliest known date: 25 October 2012 A tartan for the area around Loch Freuchie near Crieff. The tartan is based on the Murray of Atholl and Breadalbane setts, with differences, to relate the tartan to the particular Loch Freuchie area. Mr MacArthur-Fox has created a tartan for anyone to wear without fear of offending another person or group.
