## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1996 Designed by Iain Semple of Tamworth for anyone of the name. Very similar design to Sempill # 2420. See the Semple Family website (www.semple.org). Clan/Family Chief is Lord Semple who lives in Leith, Edinburgh.
