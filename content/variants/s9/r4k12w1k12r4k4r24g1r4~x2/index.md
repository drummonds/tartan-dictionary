provenance below. It is one weaving of the [sett](/tartans/w/we/wemyss/) (the scale-free proportion — the
same cloth at any scale or shade), whose colour order is pattern [RGRKRKWKR](/stripes/rgrkrkwkr/).

Part of the [Wemyss](/tartans/w/we/wemyss/) tartan — the named design grouping this sett with its other cloths.

Sourced from house-of-tartan.  It is a [9 stripe tartan](/stripes/stripes9/).

Original link [http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1512](http://www.house-of-tartan.scotland.net/house/TartanViewjs.asp?colr=Def&tnam=1512)

## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1842 Wemyss means cave and probably originates from the caves beneath MacDuff castle. There is a long and interesting article on the family of Wemyss in William Anderson's 'The Scottish Nation' published by A Fullarton in 1874.
