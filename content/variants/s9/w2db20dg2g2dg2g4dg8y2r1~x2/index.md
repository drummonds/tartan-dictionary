## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1956 Mrs Douglas Murray designed a panel for a historical display showing a shepherd tending his flock on the hills of Cape Breton. To avoid trouble amongst the clansfolk of Nova Scotia she designed a new tartan for the shepherds kilt.
