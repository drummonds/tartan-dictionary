## Provenance

Earliest known date: 1906 Registered by Lord Lyon on August 29th, 1991 and approved by the Chief. The chief also approved a black and white MacPhee after consultation with the Scottish Tartans Society. MacPhees, MacFie, or MacDuffie held lands on the Isle of Colonsay until the mid seventeenth century but were later scattered by the Clearances. They were the hereditary Keeper of the Records to the Lords of the Isles. The present chief, Sandy MacFie, lives in Queensland, Australia.
