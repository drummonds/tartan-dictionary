## Mission

**Support and help sustain the living Scottish Celtic tartan tradition** — the line that runs
from Hallstatt through to today. The Tartan Dictionary is a welcoming front door to that
tradition: somewhere to understand tartans, trace a family or clan tradition, explore the wider
pattern world, and find your way to actually owning tartan.

## Aims

1. **Support the tradition** *(primary)* — promote and preserve the Scottish Celtic tartan
   tradition.
2. **See tartans as patterns, not thread counts** — the Dictionary's distinctive, deliberately
   *delicate* departure from the weavers' world. Weavers specify a tartan as an exact
   thread-count recipe; the Dictionary works one level up, at the **pattern** — the design
   abstracted from exact counts and shades — so that the many weavings of one design are
   recognised as one design. Drummond of Megginch is the worked example. See
   [thesis.md](thesis.md).
3. **Support family traditions, and be welcoming** — help people find, understand and celebrate
   their family's or clan's tartan, and make everyone welcome however they come to it.
4. **As a public good, connect people to makers** — support weavers and manufacturers by
   signposting visitors to them, so people can obtain tartan-themed goods from a choice of
   suppliers. The Dictionary does not sell; it makes it easier to get your own — kilt, cloth, or
   whatever.

## Who it is for

Anyone with a connection to, or curiosity about, tartan — families tracing their own tradition,
enthusiasts and researchers, the curious arriving from a search, and people who simply want to
get tartan goods. The stance is welcoming and inclusive, not gate-keeping.

## What it is — and isn't

- **Is:** a guide to the tartan tradition organised by **pattern**; a place that supports family
  traditions; a welcoming front door; a public-good signpost to makers; and an explorer of the
  wider pattern space.
- **Isn't:** an official registry; a weavers' thread-count database (it sits a level above that,
  on purpose); or a shop (it connects to suppliers, it doesn't sell). Its core is the Scottish
  Celtic tradition of 2/2-twill, symmetric setts (see [thesis.md](thesis.md)).

## Name

**Tartan Dictionary** — kept.

## North star

A welcoming Go web app that serves the tradition: browse and explore tartans **by pattern**,
with a curated, enriched set of clan/family pages as the lasting, indexable heart; render any
sett; and signpost to makers. Most of the near-infinite pattern space is computed on the fly and
explorable. Built incrementally, with the current Hugo site live until cutover (see
[architecture.md](architecture.md), [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md)).

## Voice

Welcoming, scholarly but personal, and plain. UK English. Confident about the pattern-level
method; honest about the alpha state and about what is uncertain (colour, dating, attribution).
